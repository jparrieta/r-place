                                                                                                                                                                                               during a key step of inductive theorizing—pattern detection. ML can facilitate algorithm
                                                                                                                                                                                               supported induction, yielding conclusions about patterns in data that are likely to be robustly
                                                                                                                                                                                               replicable by other analysts and in other samples from the same population. These patterns
                                                                                                                                                                                               can then be used as inputs to abductive reasoning for building or developing theories that
                                                                                                                                                                                               explain them. We propose that algorithm-supported induction is valuable for researchers
                                                                                                                                                                                               interested in using quantitative data to both develop and test theories in a transparent and
                                                                                                                                                                                               reproducible manner, and we illustrate our arguments using simulations.
