## What I could not tell

The sample could not establish stable social roles for high-count human accounts other than roles made explicit in opened posts, and frequency alone does not identify expertise or authority. (header)

The evidence could not establish whether `Boofin' Bartards` referred to a wider running gag beyond its one opened thread. The window ended at 16:00 UTC on March 31, so it also could not establish the AMA's later course or campaign outcome. (header)

## Data problems

Coverage was necessarily partial: 370 complete items from 75 threads were opened out of 26,447 items and 2,424 threads. All seven days were touched, but depth was uneven by day, from 15 opened items on d2 to 111 on d1. Claims of absence, uniqueness, and recurrence are therefore bounded to the opened sample unless they use header counts. (state)

The archive contains 2,699 deleted or removed items and 811 unresolved parents among 24,273 recorded parent pointers. Those gaps block reconstruction of some exchanges and removed claims. (header)

All explicit partial-item continuations were completed before the evidence receipt was generated. (state)
