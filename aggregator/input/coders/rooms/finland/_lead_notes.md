## Before counter-reading search

Working reading: r/finland served mainly as a public negotiation and repair point around a Finnish flag whose expansion participants repeatedly described as uncontrolled; people entertained alliances and designs, defended or repaired flags, and moderators concentrated the traffic, but the record does not show a command structure directing the expansion.

Competing account: the expanding Finnish flag was a coordinated, broadly backed campaign, and the disclaimers about not knowing who controlled it merely hid or understated that coordination. If the working reading is wrong, the corpus should contain people owning an expansion plan, giving or following orders for it, naming a responsible group, or celebrating organised expansion as their shared work rather than describing it as contrary to organised efforts.

## Counter-reading result and step 6 handoff

Most hits for “plan” and “order” belonged to ordinary-life threads; the event hits reproduced the contrast already in the coding: one title called to “defend and expand,” while multiple speakers said expansion violated an agreement or organised efforts and that they did not know who was responsible. A narrower search for the case's own pro-expansion vocabulary returned five items; I opened each. The strongest contrary item is `62wtel`, because it couples expansion with defence and coordinates. The newly surfaced `dfrgpe7` called to take over Norway's flag, so I coded it at step 5 and added it to the defence/attack theme. These calls establish support for expansion, but neither names a directing group nor shows compliance. The working reading therefore survives with a sharper qualification: some participants openly recruited expansion, while visible organising venues opposed or disowned the eastward expansion that generated complaints.
