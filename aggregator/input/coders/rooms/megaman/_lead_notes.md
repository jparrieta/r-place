# Lead working notes

## Before the counter-reading search

Provisional reading: r/megaman repeatedly rallied to build and repair recognisable figures, but as the beam met neighbours and rivals, the room spent much of its effort bargaining over routes and denying control of attacks it could not consistently direct.

Competing account: the apparent fragmentation is only surface noise, and a recognised organiser or shared command venue actually directed a settled target, template, and beam policy. If the provisional reading is wrong, the corpus should contain participants identifying that organiser or venue, treating its directions as binding, and claiming collective control for attacks or redirections rather than repeatedly disclaiming it.

The alternative uses this case's own words—organize, plan, Discord, control, attack, redirect—and will be tested for positive contrary material rather than inferred from an absence.
