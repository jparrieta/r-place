# Lead running notes

Before counter-search, the working reading is: r/iceland mostly built, corrected and guarded its flags by making practical bargains with neighbours; expansion and raiding were visible but secondary and often playful or disputed.

The most consequential competing account is that this was chiefly an outward expansion campaign, with neighbourly deals serving the raids. If the working reading is wrong, the record should contain repeated first-person claims of organised attacks, refusals of neighbours' requests to stop, or celebration specifically of destroying others—not merely outsiders accusing Iceland or isolated proposals to raid.

The initial grouping review separated correcting the flag, guarding it, negotiating with neighbours, and moving coordination elsewhere because adjacency and a shared flag vocabulary did not make those the same practice.
