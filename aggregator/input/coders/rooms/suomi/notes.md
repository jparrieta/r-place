## Branch and procedure

The gate fixed branch B with 12 whole-thread slices. I read the briefing and openings before the gate. During the fleet phase I did not read or search raw threads. After each collection I reviewed the additions to `timeline.md` and `lexicon.md`; none was struck because each timeline line remained an item-bound fact or attributed report and each lexicon line was an unglossed term with its first-use item. The pooled/staged audit and forest gate passed before themes were made.

No reader holds the whole week. One coder read each slice, so there is no coder-agreement measure. Slice boundaries are arbitrary with respect to exchanges, and a boundary-spanning exchange may have been seen in parts by different coders. The within-case relations were assembled from slice cases and the forest and are therefore weaker than a single reader’s whole-week account. The forest preserves reply sequence but does not turn adjacency into causation. No strict causal relation in the final account depends only on adjacency; the only cross-slice Kekkonen continuation is qualified as an identified continuation after a direct same-thread response.

## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between an observed sequence, a participant’s own explanation, and a supported response relation. Argentina’s failed-communication account is preserved as the speaker’s explanation; the sub-hour start of retrospective posting is only a sequence; explicit answers naming the earlier proposal or agreement support the stronger links. It was also difficult to keep the room’s Suomi 100 styling and other April Fools jokes separate from canvas work when both used patriotic language in the same hours.

Image-led posts and deleted bodies often left only a title plus replies. I coded surviving titles and words, never missing images or removed text. I nearly treated repeated redirects as proof that the sticky caused later coordination, but the text supports a recurring moderation practice, not that causal chain. I also resisted treating the pxls.space proposal as movement: it is only a proposal.

## Data problems

The record cannot show the live canvas or linked images directly. Deleted and removed bodies locally hide proposals and rationales. Discord, r/FinlandFlag, r/Finland, image hosts, and pxls.space are visible only where people point to them or report something back; their internal discussion and activity are unavailable. The opening of `5zw2eg` lies outside the record, so the praised update cannot be identified. Some coded items contain substantial uncoded stretches, visible as `na` in the forest; the account does not use those stretches as evidence.

All 12 batches’ dispositions and citations passed collection checks. The complete-record audit confirmed that every coded thread had live codes and that every qualifying read exclusion had its exact citation. Late codes required rebuilding the forest before final validation.

## Anomalies and leads

The moderator metathread is assigned to one slice but receives replies across much of the remaining active event, making it a partial bridge across otherwise arbitrary slicing. The room continued opening separate Place threads even after repeated redirects; consolidation and dispersion coexisted. Post-closure material likewise combines thanks, data requests, a proposed successor canvas, fatigue, and removal of outside thanks rather than one uniform ending.

The final slice contains no visible event practice, while a large retrospective thread opened earlier still has later replies. “Return to ordinary business” therefore describes the later opening stream and served windows, not an exact moment when every retrospective stopped.

## How the reading held up

I never worked from memory of a raw thread: branch B withheld that whole-room reading, and the account was built from pooled codes, the forest, facts files, and slice cases. Earlier slice details became indistinct while later slices were being processed, especially batches 01–04, so I went back to their case files and the relevant forest passages before writing relations and chronology. I also reread the returned counter-items because their parents were needed to interpret short replies.

The run was interrupted and resumed after the scheduler’s fleet checkpoint, and the conversation was compacted during the long run. I recovered by rereading `out/_lead_notes.md`, `out/ledger.md`, the slice cases, the complete forest sections needed for the account, and the gate record. I did not substitute remembered wording for instrument text. Later material was sharper initially; rereading restored the earlier episodes sufficiently for the bounded claims made here.

## Complaints

Branch B usefully preserves coverage but asks the lead to make a whole-case account no single reader has experienced. The forest helps with sequence, yet the split-reader design cannot supply inter-coder agreement or a genuinely continuous reading. External coordination is also structurally under-observed: the record can show that people moved discussion and what they reported back, but not the decisions inside those venues. These limits are manageable only by keeping causal claims narrow and qualifications claim-specific.
