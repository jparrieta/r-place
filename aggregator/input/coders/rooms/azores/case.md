## Counter-reading

My reading is that this is one brief episode in which participants work out who made a linked piece, claim individual and collective contributions, and invite others into the Discord associated with the collective claim.

If that reading were wrong, the record would instead contain a denial or correction of authorship, language calling the claim a joke, or a statement that nobody coordinated or that Discord was unrelated to making it.

The strongest contrary item is “Eu vi e contribui com 1pixel ;)” (`dfrvu1r`): it supports participation but only at the scale of one pixel, and therefore prevents the collective claim from being generalized to substantial participation by the subreddit.
