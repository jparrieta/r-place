> Realistic battle player wants to get into Simulator battles and needs tips (`61xxkv`)

> Happend in my first real simulator battle (accidenlty pressed SB instead of RB) (`dfi88nz`)

`BR` appears beside a decimal number, while `up tiered` describes being matched against a higher number.

> The m47 BR at 7.3 means that half of the time it got up tiered to fight 8.0s (`dfeu6n9`)

`Spaded` appears as a modifier for a vehicle presented as ready for a performance test. The opened text does not define which upgrades it includes.

## Register

In the opened simulator-help thread, the newcomer received an offer of help and an explicit welcome.

> I'm always happy to help new SB players. (`dfi7ugn`)

> Happy Hunting and welcome to SB. (`dfi7ugn`)

Disagreement can also be blunt and profane, especially over vehicle performance and player skill.

> Thats bullshit, go look at lvl 100 accounts and how often they take out T2/3 planes in air rb (`dflwb7e`)

Technical disputes can turn quickly into teasing. After two users agreed to test a claim in a custom battle, a third replied:

> Did you guys bang? (`dfm5gan`)

The evidence therefore supports a mixed register within opened threads: patient coaching, terse correction, profanity, and jokes. It does not support a single tone for every newcomer or topic. (state)
