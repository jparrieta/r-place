## Grouping review

I revisited all six themes after writing the within-case account and after the counter-reading. I did not regroup them. The two late codes `c0070` and `c0071` extended `t04`, “arguing over taking, sharing and leaving other flags alone”; they did not require a new theme. The two themes that nearly merged were “starting again and moving the Faroese flag until it sticks” and “joining in to build, fix and defend the flags.” They stayed apart because the first is about finding a viable place and restarting after failure, while the second is about supplying labour, tools and upkeep once a build is underway.

## Evidence and relation limits

Reply adjacency was used to reconstruct sequence, never by itself as evidence that one turn caused another. Relations in the account rest on explicit answer wording, conditional language, or a participant's own explanation. The “not being left out” account is labelled as that participant's explanation; the Philippines request-to-completion passage is labelled as an observed sequence; the post/comment differences are descriptive contrasts and do not claim that the community changed its mind.
