# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: participants repeatedly relocated and repaired small Faroese marks, drew help from nearby communities, negotiated shared hearts, and afterward made a grateful record of what survived.

Competing account: the case was primarily opportunistic takeover and territorial conflict, while the friendship hearts were secondary. If that account were right, the corpus should contain repeated calls to erase or seize neighbours' work, refusals of shared hearts, or celebration of attacks, rather than one contested episode and proposals checked by other participants.
