# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: r/coldplay built a small run of album covers by repeatedly adapting the design and location, moving coordination to Discord, and negotiating coexistence with neighbours; conflict existed but did not define the project.

Competing account: the project advanced mainly by taking or wrecking neighbours' space rather than by adaptive building and negotiated coexistence.

If the provisional reading is wrong, the corpus should contain explicit calls to attack, overwrite, or refuse compromise, or participants' accounts that deliberate destruction—not agreements and relocation—carried the build forward.
