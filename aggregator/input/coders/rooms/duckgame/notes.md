## Grouping review

I regrouped after reviewing the within-case account. “Keeping the duck and allies alive” split into “sounding the alarm and keeping the duck alive” versus “rebuilding and defending each other's pictures,” because reciprocal work across the duck, flag, and Yoshi is a different practice from defending the duck itself. “Remembering the duck after it ended” split into mourning finality versus saving and checking pictures, because using an image as a record or repair reference is not the same practice as grieving. These splits are recorded in the affected themes' `regrouped_from` fields. I reviewed the remaining assignments after the account and left them unchanged.

The two themes that nearly merged were “making a sombrero deal with Mexico” and “making friends through the sombrero duck”; explicit offer, terms, invitation, and acceptance kept the deal apart from later cheering, thanks, honour, and promised friendship.
