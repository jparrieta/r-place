## Chronology

The record opens with ordinary r/romania business. From `62czcb` at H+0.02 to `62jo84` at H+0.44, people continued pre-window discussions of taxis, dog attacks, politics, health care and journalism; no r/place conduct appeared in that opening slice. The first canvas episode begins at d0 03-31 18:26 with `62n1dz`: “Roddit! Hai sa facem steagul Romaniei pe /r/place!” Contributors chose coordinates, corrected the colour order, resized the tricolour and allocated building and repair work. By d0 04-01 11:54, `dfotq0y` told them to keep discussion in one thread and organize live on Discord, then accumulated ten edits covering the border, official proportions, an eagle, text, maintenance, limits on expansion, a Hungarian heart and Dracula. This episode runs from `62n1dz` to `dfotq0y`; it establishes a visible plan but also moves some deliberation outside the record.

A separate April Fools episode runs from `62o8hp` to `dfqgl0x`. Posts offered fake resignations, arrests and death stories; comments checked calendars, thumbnails and Inspect Element. Fake-ban recipients tested whether they could still post, asked that explanatory threads be deleted so later recipients would still be fooled, and retold how they had “mușcat” the bait. Moderator `dfozw2a` said “nu e de la noi” and told recipients to inspect the sender, so the surviving record contradicts the claim that the moderator team sent the message but does not identify its actual sender. This joke overlapped the flag work without becoming part of it.

From `62txqw` to `dfq5m0v`, the flag became both a worksite and a disputed national claim. Posts proposed moving it, enlarging it, recolouring Belgium, turning the sleeping Americans’ flag into a tricolour and attacking Hungary. Comments supplied coordinates and repair reports, but also declined help requests, warned that new space could not be held, reported outside complaints, and played with the lettering as “Pomania,” “HOMONIA” and “RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII.” A moderator’s d1 04-02 11:00 reply, `dfq3a2a`, said the majority opposed an attack because “Ne-am face mai mult rău decât bine.” Different speakers therefore advanced attack and restraint at the same time; the evidence does not show the community changing its mind as a unit.

The planning-and-diplomacy episode from `62z7uk` to `631u0t` made that tension sharper. One post told people not to edit without Discord; commenters rejected that venue even as the poster said unannounced makers erased one another. A Bulgarian non-aggression proposal in `62zsd4` drew offers of help. A Hungarian-agreed heart in `62ztm3` drew both refusal and participation, then celebration as peace. The conquest post `6307h6` was answered by `dfqar8r` with a link to the agreement from “Literalmente acum o ora.” During the Dracula build, `dfqff0k` admitted erasing it because they had not seen the thread and thought somebody was mocking the flag; `dfqfmvr` asked that the design be put in the moderator sticky so it would not look like damage. Completion was claimed, while proposals for further figures and monuments continued.

From `6322yy` to `dfrkgwh`, r/place language spread into unrelated talk while practical work dispersed across repair calls, outside requests, hearts and additional flags. Contributors answered “we need help” with “We are on our way,” explained the five-minute pixel rule to newcomers, and reported repairing the hearts. The counter-reading later found the compact exchange `dfqs2mp`–`dfqs683`: “Nu e planificat?” was answered with “Nu am gasit niciun post legat de asta, deci presupun ca nu e planificat.” It makes the visibility problem explicit. Later, from `635o5f` to `dfrmn24`, moderators redirected small design proposals to the inherited central thread; the supplied threads do not show whether those proposals were adopted.

The final large canvas episode runs from `6371tw` to `dfs01y5`. “operațiunea Snoo Vampir” began at d2 04-03 14:18. Defenders initially erased the new drawing, then waited for the plan, made a grid, repaired the image and supplied coordinates; `dfsa4d9` later said the flag and vampire were at (735, 635). At d3 04-03 17:12, `dfs01y5` reported “S-a terminat...” and linked the ending announcement. The retrospective in `63879r` then credited constructors and “janitors,” recounted last-pixel repairs and answered a visitor from r/hungary who thanked the room for cooperation and mutual non-attack.

After closure, the result became memory and material. From `639w64` to `dftgwh2`, contributors celebrated that “Vampirul nostru a supravietuit,” offered an r/place-inspired application, recreated Snoo Vampir in Perler beads, explained the craft and supplied vector files for a laptop sticker. Around that small afterlife, ordinary news and discussion again dominated. The final assigned stretch, `63ed2p` to `63f661`, contains no visible r/place conduct; that is a bounded observation about the last slice, not proof that nobody in the community mentioned it elsewhere.

## Relations — what set off what

Recruitment prompted instruction: `dfod8xr` asks “cum functioneaza asta?”, and its direct answer `dfodbse` says “Click pe culoare si apoi pe coordonatele dorite.” The later turn explicitly answers the earlier question.

Conquest was checked by an existing agreement: the post `6307h6` calls “Haideti sa cucerim Ungaria!”, while reply `dfqar8r` links the Hungarian heart design and says “Literalmente acum o ora.” The passage itself names the prior agreement as the answer, rather than the relation resting on reply adjacency.

Poor plan visibility prompted corrective routing. `dfqff0k` says the speaker erased Dracula because they had not seen the thread and thought somebody was mocking the flag; `dfqfmvr` consequently asks for the design in the fixed moderator thread so people will stop mistaking it for dirt. These are participants’ own explanations of their actions, not a general causal claim about all accidental erasure. The later exchange `dfqs2mp`–`dfqs683` similarly records a question about whether work was planned and an answer inferring it was not because no post could be found. [crosses 05|06]

The Snoo Vampir mistake produced an explicit recognition rather than a causal inference from nesting. `dfrstep` asks people to wait because “Eu făceam janitoring peste ce lucrați voi”; `dfrt682` answers “Si eu ... Credeam ca isi bate cineva joc!” The words establish that both speakers had erased planned work because they read it as damage.

The vector request produced two derivatives. `dftakbz` asks “Sa îl facă cineva vector” for a laptop sticker, and `dftds93` answers “Done!” with a file link; another answer offers a second file. Here the requested output and the answering artifact are explicit.

Cooperation is supported by testimony, not inferred from neighbouring flags. Visitor `dfsfq75` thanks r/romania “pentru cooperare si pentru ca nu ne-am atacat unii pe altii”; `dfsg38v` answers that “we've defended that common heart properly.” This establishes their stated account of cooperation. It does not prove that every account refrained from attacks, and the corpus does not establish that every damage report caused the repair that followed it.

## Posts against comments

[Batch 01] Posts and comments both concern ordinary community business; neither layer shows r/place in that bounded opening.

[Batch 02] Posts call for a flag or help, while comments show the less compact practice: correcting colours, assigning teams, guarding, bargaining over hearts and negotiating with neighbours. Comments also contain both sabotage proposals and the later plan’s restraint, a tension absent from the post titles.

[Batch 03] Flag posts recruit; comments teach placement and route people centrally. April Fools posts present bait as real or as moderator action, while comments check the date and `dfozw2a` denies moderator authorship.

[Batch 04] Posts use collective imperatives—move, save, mobilize, attack—but comments decline a France request, call a new location hard to hold, criticise the “castel de manelisti,” and say a majority opposes attack. Those are contrasting speakers, not evidence of collective conversion.

[Batch 05] Posts assert a plan—use Discord, stop expanding, make the heart, draw Dracula—while comments reject Discord, refuse the agreed design and admit mistaking planned art for vandalism. Comments also show Bulgarian help and Austrian participation that titles understate; the conquest title is checked by the agreement link.

[Batch 06] Posts call for repair, attack, another tricolour and a shared heart. Comments sometimes answer “On it” or “repar la greu,” but warn that expansion may cost the existing flag and say the proposed shared heart follows an invasion.

[Batch 07] Posts propose a person, “useful things” and joining hearts; comments mainly redirect planning to the central thread. No human reply establishes uptake of the hearts proposal.

[Batch 08] Posts cleanly declare “operațiunea Snoo Vampir” and “We did it, roddit!” Comments reveal mistaken counter-repair, grids, uneven contributions and retrospective claims about bots or multiple accounts. Those remain participant claims. Only comments describe Zimbabwe, Bulgaria and Hungarian cooperation.

[Batch 09] Posts say the vampire survived and r/place inspired an application; comments leave survival unchallenged but question what “inspirat” means while also supplying the application link.

[Batch 10] The post presents Snoo Vampir “în viața reală”; comments turn display into reuse by naming Perler Beading, explaining construction and producing vectors. A mascot objection and a “scary” reaction coexist with that practical uptake.

[Batch 11] Both layers return to ordinary politics, news and jokes. Their agreement supports only the bounded absence in those assigned threads.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/romania made the flag through recurrent coordination that remained contested and partly invisible off-platform: repair, pacts and restraint repeatedly checked attacks and overextension, and closure turned the resulting Snoo Vampir into a shared memory and remake.

If that reading were wrong, this corpus would instead contain participants describing a stable common plan that was broadly followed with little corrective erasure or dispute, or it would show successful conquest displacing the agreements, hearts and restraint described here.

The strongest contrary item is `dfotq0y`. It centralises discussion, directs live organization to Discord, specifies official proportions, and repeatedly updates a concrete plan through border, eagle, text, maintenance, a Hungarian heart and Dracula. That is substantial visible coordination, so the account must not reduce the work to confusion or individual improvisation. Searches for the case vocabulary around “Discord,” “planificat,” organization, agreements, peace, attacks and conquest also returned `dfq3a2a`, `62z7zi`, `6307h6` and `dfsfq75`; reading them showed a rejected attack, a stop-expansion instruction, an unsupported conquest call, and retrospective thanks for non-attack. The exact search for “planul oficial,” “plan comun,” or everyone following a plan returned no items; that null result is limited to those phrases in this corpus and cannot reveal the Discord conversation.

The most damaging evidence for a stable-plan account is internal rather than absent: `dfotq0y` really did serve as an evolving plan. What survives of my reading is the documented variation in access and compliance. `dfqff0k`, `dfqfmvr`, `dfrstep`, `dfrt682`, `dfqs2mp` and `dfqs683` explicitly describe missed plans and protective erasure; `dfq3a2a` rejects attack as a majority position, while `6307h6` still calls for conquest. The search therefore qualifies the reading—coordination was strong enough to produce and revise shared designs—but does not overturn the finding that it was contested and incompletely visible.

## Saturation

Practice saturation was late but visible. Batch 02 founded “calling Roddit to make the Romanian flag,” “choosing where and how the flag should look,” “organizing flag work in one thread and Discord,” “standing guard and repairing the flag,” “pushing into other flags,” “holding back attacks and overextension,” “trading help with neighbours,” “making hearts into alliances and refuge,” and “marking place as ended.” Batch 03 first added “showing newcomers how to place pixels,” “posting April Fools bait as news,” “checking whether April Fools bait is real,” “joking together in the holiday spirit,” “circulating and testing the fake-ban prank,” and “retelling pranks people fell for.” Batch 04 first added “turning flag rivalry into nationalist banter,” “claiming and celebrating what Roddit made,” “arguing over whose space is being griefed,” “turning ROMANIA into canvas wordplay,” and “putting room and national symbols on the flag.” Batch 05 first added “undoing planned art while trying to protect the flag”; the two step-5 codes extended that same practice. Batch 06 founded the single-code “bringing r/place into unrelated talk,” batch 07 the single-code “filtering low-karma r/place posts,” batch 08 “preserving and revisiting the finished canvas,” and batch 09 “carrying pixels into an app, beadwork and stickers.” Batch 10 extended that last practice; batch 11 brought no new practice. Thus the late slices still added post-event preservation and remaking, but the final two slices opened no new theme.

Record saturation is weaker. Each slice had one reader and no agreement measure; no reader held the full week. Discord carried some live planning that cannot be inspected, and linked images, grids, applications, vectors and timelapses are known only through participants’ descriptions. Deleted and removed bodies obscure local motives, while slice boundaries weaken cross-slice relations. The forest restores sequence but not missing talk or a single reader’s continuous view. The record is nevertheless sufficient for a full verdict because it shows calls, plans, corrective disputes, direct responses, closure and post-event remakes in surviving passages.

## Selection

The lead read the briefing and the complete openings table before the gate, so the room’s ordinary opening and return were visible before triage even though that reading is not in the raw-read log. The threshold assigned branch B. Eleven coders then read whole-thread slices in corpus order, keeping r/place conduct and the room’s separate April Fools practice and excluding ordinary news, politics, advice and entertainment with required citations. The lead did not read raw threads during fleet coding; after consolidation, the pooled codes, every slice case, the facts files and the complete forest were read in slice order. Raw item reading began only for the stated counter-reading. The relation carried across a slice boundary is the plan-visibility problem between the Dracula erasure in batch 05 and the “not planned” exchange in batch 06; it rests on `dfqff0k`, `dfqfmvr`, `dfqs2mp` and `dfqs683`, not merely their order.

## Other communities named

Other communities appear as neighbours, negotiating partners and targets rather than as a single outside bloc. In `62n1dz`, Portugal requests mutual cleaning and defence, while New Zealand and Malaysia ask about space in Romanian hearts. `62yyfu` and `62z7zi` frame r/roosterteeth as a neighbour whose Achievement Hunter logo expansion should not destroy. `62zsd4` brings a Bulgarian non-aggression proposal and reciprocal help. `62ztm3` and later `dfsfq75`–`dfsg38v` make r/hungary both the object of rejected conquest talk and a partner in a heart and claimed mutual non-attack. `632i0a` instead records hostile lettering work against r/wales. `dfrvxcr` reports that Zimbabwe users had “pus ochii” on one Romanian heart. These are r/romania-side passages; they do not supply the other communities’ full deliberations.

## Absence

Absence is uneven, not empty. Removed post bodies survive beside titles and moderator redirects in several r/place threads, and deleted comments interrupt the central flag, Dracula and vampire exchanges. Surviving replies still establish redirection, mistaken erasure, repair and completion claims, but not every missing proposal or motive. Discord, linked images, the application, grids, vectors and timelapses remain outside the textual record; their existence and described use are evidence, their unseen contents are not. The final batch’s 23 disposed threads show ordinary discussion and no r/place conduct, including a cited large political exclusion, but that small late slice cannot establish community-wide silence. Reply adjacency was used only as sequence unless the words explicitly answered, named or recalled the earlier turn.
