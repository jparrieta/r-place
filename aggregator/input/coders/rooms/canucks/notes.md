## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between a descriptive sequence and a supported relation. The attack post preceded the successful build by 7 hours 7 minutes, but no contemporaneous speaker says the raid caused the build. I therefore report sequence and separately attribute the later explanation—“started ... to attack theirs instead of building our own”—to `dfs4r9n`.

The separate Benning April Fools performance was easy to conflate with the simultaneous canvas event. I kept it because it is community conduct, but every theme and the case account state that it is not about the canvas.

Image-led claims were another persistent difficulty. Patterns, screenshots, videos, and the Alberta guide cannot be inspected under this instrument. I report only what titles and commenters say about them.

The forest restores the textual order and reply structure but not a common reader’s memory. Cross-boundary relations in the account are explicitly qualified.
