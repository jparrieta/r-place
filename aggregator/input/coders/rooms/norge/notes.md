## What was hard

The hardest boundary was between negotiating with neighbours and arguing over expansion. The same exchange could contain a takeover call, a relocation offer, and a complaint about destroyed art. I kept the themes apart by practice: making or accepting a deal belongs with neighbour negotiation; judging whether expansion was justified belongs with the expansion dispute; carrying out the push belongs with fighting and defending.

The corpus also makes “Discord coordination” easy to overstate. Reddit shows invitations, targets, reports, credits, and later criticism, but not most deliberation inside Discord. I treated participants' claims about Discord as their explanations and did not turn them into independently established causal relations.

The first `find` query failed because an inline regular-expression flag appeared twice. It returned no hits and contributed no evidence; the corrected query was logged and inspected. One attempted pre-gate `read header` call was refused by the branch-B lock and exposed no corpus material.

## Data problems

Deleted and removed bodies are common in event posts and in the large megathread. Surviving titles support narrow claims, but unseen images, removed instructions, and motives were not reconstructed. The supplied archive itself cannot prove historical completeness.

Discord, IRC, and successor canvases are only partly visible. Pointers record what participants sent out and what returned, but leadership, voting, and much off-platform activity remain unavailable. Slice boundaries also divide a few exchanges; no reader held the whole record, and there is no inter-coder agreement measure.

## How the reading held up

This branch-B lead did not work from memory of raw threads: before consolidation I read openings, facts files, ledger entries, and slice accounts, while coders read the threads. I did not go back to a raw thread because I could not remember it. During consolidation, earlier slices did not become indistinct relative to later ones because `codes_by_thread.md`, the slice cases, the ledger, and `_lead_notes.md` were reread in prescribed order.

The run was intentionally interrupted for scheduler checkpoints after each dispatch and after the final collect. I resumed from on-disk gate, ledger, journal, pooled files, and lead notes rather than restarting or recollecting. At the final checkpoint, all seven batches were already collected; batch 07 was not collected again.

The counter-reading used `find` only after step 4 and read returned relevant items through `read item`. It added four in-window codes at step 5. The strongest contrary in-window phrase was “r/place er kill or be killed”; the strongest leadership passage was after the window and was reported only as context. Search nulls are limited to the supplied, partly removed Reddit record.
