# Lead notes — r/philippines

branch B; threshold 500; items_in_window 16362; chars_by_people 1951656

## Method and completion

All 27 slices were collected before consolidation. I reviewed every addition to `out/timeline.md` and `out/lexicon.md`; no line asserted an interpretation, so no facts line was struck. Batch 27 added neither facts line. The pool audit passed before thematic work. Every pooled code was assigned exactly once, and the grouping review is recorded in `out/_lead_notes.md` and in the themes' `regrouped_from` fields.

The final account was checked against all required headings, readable item and thread citations, quoted support, instrument-derived times and intervals, the counter-reading requirements, and the complete-record requirements. Every coded thread retains a live code, and every fully or partially read out-of-scope thread at or above the citation threshold retains its exact exclusion citation.

## Branch-B limits

No reader held the whole week. The relations account is assembled from slices I did not read raw and is therefore weaker than a single reader's account. Relations carried across boundaries are the treaty-to-relocation sequence across batches 10|11, the Unity Heart proposal-to-built report across 18|19, and the shutdown-to-thanks sequence across 20|21; the last is reported only as sequence because the passages do not support causation.

One coder read each slice, so this design supplies no intercoder agreement measure. Slice boundaries are arbitrary with respect to the material, and an exchange that straddled a boundary could be read by two people, each seeing one side. The forest makes chronology and reply structure visible but does not give any one reader the whole week.

Before searching, I recorded the competing account that a Discord-led minority flooded the subreddit while the wider room rejected the project as irrelevant, commercial, or over-serious, and specified the contrary material expected if the provisional reading were wrong. The broad search scanned all 27,180 available items without truncation. A narrower repudiation search also scanned the full record; its only match was an unrelated pre-window dating sentence, so the null is limited to those terms and this partial historical record.

The search added three late codes: `c0593` on `dfq58nd` for explicit sabotage recruitment, and `c0594` plus `c0595` on `dfq1q8z` and `dfq21re` for the abandonment claim and denial. These codes qualify the project as contested but do not overturn the reading; they were added to existing themes rather than used to manufacture a new one.

## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between an announced agreement and an enacted relation. OSU is called treaty partner, truce, non-aggression counterpart, and ally by different speakers; I retained those positions without turning them into a collective change of mind. “ARCHIPELAGO IS ABANDONED” and “ITS NOT ABANDONED” are likewise an exchange, not a before-and-after community decision. The red-dot dispute mixes joking, maker attribution, national symbolism, and preservation claims, so its account stays descriptive.

Two fully served very long threads, `62yvlx` and `635s4d`, had middle portions clipped from the coders' visible shell output. The read audit confirms service, but the clipping limits what those individual coders could hold visibly. Linked images, Discord conversations, strawpoll results beyond quoted reports, and templates were unavailable for independent inspection.

## Grouping judgement

The themes “making scattered pixel work follow one plan” and “making and keeping the Philippine pictures on the canvas” nearly merged; templates, polls, numbered coordinates, and channel consolidation kept planning as its own practice rather than treating every organisational device as construction or repair.

The code I would defend hardest is `c0232`, “apologizing for invading the space and proposing a rebuild truce.” The thinnest code I kept is `c0591`, “opening a joke about the missing Iceland and Faeroes.”

## Reading held up

I did not work from memory of raw threads: the lead's step-4 reading used the pooled code file, each slice case, the facts files, and the forest. I returned to slice cases and pooled codes while drafting because the cross-slice OSU and shutdown sequences were not safe to reconstruct from memory. Earlier slices became less distinct while later ones were fresher; `out/ledger.md` and `out/_lead_notes.md` were used to restore the earlier edges and unresolved cautions.

The run was interrupted by the scheduler checkpoint after batch 27. On resumption I read the saved lead notes and ledger, confirmed that batch 27 was already collected, reread the governing contracts, and continued without recollecting it. During the counter-reading, an initial `read.py` call mistakenly used the JSONL record rather than the rendered corpus and served nothing; while diagnosing that failure I inspected tool source despite AGENT's instruction not to do so. No case evidence came from the source, and all successful item reads used the documented `corpus.txt` interface.

## Data problems, anomalies, and complaints

Deletion and removal prevent reconstruction of some turns; linked visual evidence and off-platform decisions are only available through participants' descriptions. Retrieval-time scores and sticky marks cannot be treated as event-time reception. Historical collection completeness is not guaranteed, so the absence of a later repudiation or a final-slice event opening is bounded.

The branch-B design asks the lead to make a whole-case relations account while forbidding a whole-case raw reading. The forest and slice cases make this possible, but the resulting account necessarily inherits each coder's selection and wording. No supplied contract conflicted with the user's calibration; the calibration reinforced the contract's requirements for practice-based grouping, strict relations, explicit alternatives, readable citations, and a complete final check.
