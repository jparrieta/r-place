## Theme boundaries and grouping review

The two themes that nearly merged were “drawing borders without battling the neighbours” and “trying and then dropping writing r/parrots.” They stayed apart because the first works out the bird's edge and coexistence with surrounding work, while the second works out whether and how to label the room beneath it. The instrument records this split in `regrouped_from`.

I also split celebration from defense: “We did it” and successful-repair turns mark an outcome, whereas loss reports, calls, repairs, attack claims, and rebukes keep the bird present against damage. After writing and the counter-reading, I reviewed all 15 themes again and made no further regrouping. Batch 02 extended the settled defense and celebration themes and opened three genuinely new practices rather than forcing them into shared-vocabulary bins.
