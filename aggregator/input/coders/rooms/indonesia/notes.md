## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between successful local coordination and stable coordination across the event. `dfrfvzo` plainly praises pakde's coordination, and agreed relocations do trigger practical script changes. Those passages had to remain contrary evidence rather than being absorbed into a disorder-only story. The later border questions, changing sites and mixed-command complaint support instability over time, not an absence of coordination.

The mock presidential and military register was also difficult. I kept “playing president, parliament and war command” separate from “asking for one leader and one current order” because the former performs a national office and the latter tries to make coordination usable.

Cross-slice relations were retained only when a passage named or answered the earlier action. Context openings located inherited edges but were not evidence.

Discord discussions, Pastebin and GitHub revision histories, linked images and the external communities' own accounts are unavailable.
