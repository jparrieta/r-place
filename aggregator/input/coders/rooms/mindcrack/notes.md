## What was hard

The main difficulty was separating a descriptive sequence from a supported response. The forest makes replies and elapsed time visible, but reply position alone does not establish that one practice caused another. Relations in the case account therefore use later words that answer, name, or recall the earlier passage; otherwise chronology says only that one episode followed another.

The Dutch/Czech material was also difficult because speakers distinguish organized groups, Discords, “rogues,” “randoms,” flags, and individual visitors inconsistently. Contrasting speakers do not establish that the community changed its mind. The account preserves the conflict as competing reports and attributes the causal explanation only where a participant explicitly recalls the Dutch experience.

The subreddit’s two April Fools contexts required separation. The code on the recap joke concerns the room’s own recurring practice, not the canvas, and the code on mistaking a takeover for that joke preserves the confusion without merging the events.
