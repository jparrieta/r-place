## What was hard

The main difficulty was separating several nearby canvas objects without turning each noun into a theme. Mushroom designs, a shrooms word, a yinyang, THC, and PSILOCYBIN share space and vocabulary, but the practices differ: recruiting, negotiating colors, teaching newcomers, persisting under replacement, shifting among jobs, answering calls, and recognizing closure.

The late “little shroom” resembles the earlier mushroom calls, but no passage links it to them. I resisted treating visual similarity and chronology as continuity. Likewise, the report “I got the THC under DMT” is a completion claim for THC only; it does not establish success for PSILOCYBIN or either mushroom.

## Counter-reading search

Before searching I recorded the competing account that the repeated calls produced a stable, successfully maintained mushroom or shrooms mark, and expected explicit reports that it was finished, still there, defended, intact, or won. The initial broad query returned 37 items and was noisy because ordinary drug and cultivation talk uses “shrooms,” “complete,” “done,” and “won.” I opened every returned item through `read item`, then used narrower combinations of the case’s target words and success terms and opened their hits as well. No hit reported a stable event mushroom or word. The strongest contrary item was the already-coded `dfq9pbe`, which claims completion of THC under DMT. No late code or thread reclassification was warranted.

The null is bounded to the supplied record and searched vocabulary. It cannot establish what linked images showed, what deleted replies said, or what participants may have done without discussing it here.
