## Vernacular

“Cubes” is shorthand for *Psilocybe cubensis* in identification replies; “Golden Teacher” is treated as a cubensis name or variety. 

> cubes (`dfd0bs1`)

> Looks Like psilocybin cubensis: Golden Teacher (`dfe0ptx`)

“Mushies” is a casual name for the dried mushrooms themselves.

> These are airhead sour belts, and when you wrap the mushies inside, the soft texture of the candy makes it extremely easy to chew up the dried mushrooms, and the sweet and sour taste almost completely masks the musky mushy taste. (`dfei47x`)

“Tek” names a procedure. “PF tek” belongs to cultivation talk, while “lemon tek” is an ingestion method discussed as changing the onset and intensity.

> Pf classic is a variety of cubensis. OP wasn't referring to doing pf tek. These are grain jars for a bulk grow. (`dfkra5i`)

> Not an extraction. The acid converts psilocybin to psilocin (the actual active drug in the brain). Normally your stomach does this slowly, lemon tek ramps it up. (`dfm253l`)

“Boomers” is another name for mushrooms in dose talk. “Ego death” names a state speakers describe as losing touch with reality.

> When it comes to boomers, 4 to 5 grams can very easily put you thru ego death, at that point you just go with it. (`dfj9mni`)

> I tripped really hard on 2g, like, almost total ego death, I was losing touch with reality and stuff. (`dfn46or`)
