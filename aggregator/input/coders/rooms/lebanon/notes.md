## What was hard

The central difficulty was keeping local success distinct from community-wide control. “Mission complete,” “Attack successfully thwarted,” and the accepted Austrian terms are supported outcomes. They coexist with explicit lone action, an empty Discord, treaty breaches and repeated losses. The account therefore describes partial centralization and bounded successes, not either total disorder or a disciplined army.

The two-versus-three-hearts contradiction could not be resolved. Live participants count two, three and further attempted hearts; a retrospective salutes three. Images and the canvas are outside the text, and the statements occur at different moments, so they remain time-bound participant reports.

`Bto2ta2` was the thinnest interpretive edge. The title is citable and visibly performs a reveal, but the record does not explain the linked video. I retained it as the room's own possibly April Fools conduct and explicitly separated it from the canvas.

No relation in the account rests on reply adjacency alone. I used a reply as relational evidence only where its words answer, name or recall the earlier turn. Elsewhere—especially across the fleet boundary—I state sequence only.
