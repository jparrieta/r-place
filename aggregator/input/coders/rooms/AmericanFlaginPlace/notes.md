## What was hard

The hardest judgment was separating what participants did from what they inferred about pixels. Ireland, Rainbow Road, the Void, The_Donald, scripts and unaffiliated placers were all named as causes; I retained accusations, denials and corrections as participant conduct without deciding external authorship. The second difficulty was distinguishing a proposal from a shared plan. Imperative post titles often sounded collective while replies, competing templates and old scripts showed fractured control.

Image and link posts sometimes left only a title. Those codes remain limited to the surviving words; I did not infer the image. The empty pre-week briefing could not support a baseline. I nearly treated repeated patriotic and conquest language as a single expansion practice, but kept rallying talk separate where it functioned to sustain placers rather than specify a spatial action.

I worked from the saved ledger and lead notes at each collect, then read the ten slice cases beside pooled codes in order and used the forest for the final account. I did not substitute memory of a raw thread for text served by `read`; in the counter-reading I opened every returned item through the prescribed tool.

Earlier slices did grow less verbally sharp while later slices remained fresh.
