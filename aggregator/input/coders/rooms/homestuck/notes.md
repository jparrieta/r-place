## What was hard

The hardest interpretive boundary was between real-time status reports that oriented the next placement and retrospective accounts that negotiated blame or meaning after placement ended. Another recurring difficulty was treating claims about alliances, attacks, finished drawings, and traffic as participants' reports rather than independently observed outcomes. Images were not evidence unless surviving words described them. Reply adjacency preserved sequence but was never sufficient by itself for causation.

No single reader held the whole week. The case lead assembled relations and chronology from six independently read slices, the timeline, and the forest. Exchanges and practices crossing arbitrary slice edges are therefore weaker evidence than within-slice links. One coder read each slice, so there is no coder-agreement measure.

## Data problems

Removed and deleted bodies, image-only posts, pruned uncoded stretches, and conversations continuing beyond the window make the record incomplete. Discord, Steam, group messages, Pastebin scripts, and other subreddits' venues expose only what participants brought back to Reddit. The forest shows item order and reply structure but cannot recover missing wording, unseen images, or off-platform decisions.

Every collected addition to `timeline.md` and `lexicon.md` was reviewed. No facts line was struck as a reading.
