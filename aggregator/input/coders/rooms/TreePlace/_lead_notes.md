# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: TreePlace made a small, simple tree stand for resisting the canvas's empires and repeatedly tried to preserve or restore it, while working out its decoration and location.

Competing account: the room was substantially another territorial fighting group, and its language of simplicity only dressed up ordinary canvas conflict. If the provisional reading is wrong, the corpus should contain participants calling for expansion, attacks, destruction, or taking others' space on the tree's behalf, and treating that fight—not keeping or restoring the tree—as their shared purpose.
