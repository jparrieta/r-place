# r/ainbowroad: building a road that nobody fully controlled

## Chronology

At H+2.77 the room opened by recruiting people to “construct a large rainbow on the Place” in post `62n66p`. Within the opening slice, people were already repairing colour order, negotiating with Blue Corner and Nyan Cat, and creating a Discord in `62o01j`. This was not simply a growth campaign. The first arguments asked whether the road should consume, respect, or incorporate encountered art, and whether a pact announced in one venue would restrain the people placing pixels.

The early road became a sequence of negotiated geometries. People proposed bounces, reflections, routes under Snek, and portals around Waldo. The portal post `62qjsm` told builders to place portals before extending the rainbow “so that we can avoid destroying” Waldo; its edit then reported, “It is done! Somehow the hivemind understood.” The weave was later celebrated as complete in `62r6oa`: “We have completed the weave!” These successes sat beside recurrent claims that other strands were rogue, unofficial, or outside the subreddit’s control.

By H+16.38 the moderator strategy post `62ritr` told participants to “focus on creating a strong rainbow core.” Expansion did not stop, but repair, protected art, and accommodation became increasingly explicit priorities. Discord, Reddit Live, templates, polls, coordinates, and sticky posts made work executable; they also fragmented access. A decision visible in Discord could look like vandalism to a Reddit repairer, and a public approval could be ignored by placers who saw neither venue.

The H+33.25 “OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT” in `62wwmq` directed all hands to defence and maintenance. Later, the H+42.99 plan in `62z2mr`, the simultaneous stop-building notice in `62z2od`, the H+46.80 guidelines in `63006a`, and the “indefinite moratorium” proposed in `630bsf` made the preservation turn public. This is an observed development in announced priorities, not proof that the community collectively changed its mind: calls to expand, attack, host more art, and build unofficially continued alongside it. The guidelines themselves called the rainbow “organised by the crowd for the crowd,” which describes both its participatory appeal and its control problem.

In later slices the road functioned as a host and permission desk. Other groups asked for small sprites, flag crossings, refuge, protection, labour, and recognition. Moderators approved some proposals, refused others, and repeatedly redirected effort to the core. Approval did not guarantee completion: the approved Star Citizen Hornet was later reported not to have made the finished canvas. Conversely, unofficial work could persist until moderators adopted it; one moderator comment `dfpg4j0` said an erstwhile unofficial strand had become official because “The hivemind has overpowered us.”

The American-flag episode concentrated the case’s competing accounts. Post `635ovh` asked, “Why is Rainbow Road trying to take over the American Flag?” Replies denied collective responsibility, called the incursion a false flag, attributed pixels to randoms or a hivemind, and reported that informed members were repairing the flag. At the same time, flag participants reported rainbow pixels replacing their repairs. Moderator comment `dfrlahd` declined an alliance and restated neutrality, while `dfrozgf` said an interference proposal had been rejected. These are simultaneous positions, not evidence that one community mind first attacked and then repented.

At H+73.02 post `6382oc` said, “As of 1PM EST, I can no longer place pixels.” What followed was a sequence of thanks, statistics, shirts, knitting, wallpapers, histories, and clone-canvas proposals. The H+77.05 sticky `639kla` retrospectively named the work—“creating pixel art, negotiating with other factions/art makers, organizing builds, creating to-do lists, maintaining the rainbow”—and said the community would not cease with Place. A later moderator comment `dft897m` limited the claim: clone roads could continue individually, most moderators were done, and Discord would remain open.

## Relations — what set off what

- An incursion into Blue territory prompted a pact reminder in `62nyxv`; the author of the earlier “pierced the Blue Corner” post later used `62o3wl` to say, “I want to apologize.” The wording names the earlier conflict, so this is a supported response rather than chronology alone. [crosses 01|02]
- A predicted collision with Waldo set off the portal plan in `62qjsm`, whose stated purpose was to avoid destroying Waldo and whose edit reported the portals in place. This supports an episode-specific relation from obstacle to rerouting, not a general claim that plans always controlled placement.
- A warning in `dfom07u` that destroying recognized art could make neighbours rebel changed the Snek caller’s stated position in `dfomk13`: “i am convinced” and “leave the SNEK alone.” Both endpoints explicitly name the issue.
- The moderator’s core-focus instruction in `62ritr` received the direct reply `dfopymw`, “all pixels to the Core now.” This supports immediate uptake by one participant; it does not measure compliance across the room.
- The Rocket League request in `62vvz8` received explicit approval in `dfpi1yp`, then the moderator post `62w2mw` publicly presented “Rocket League Co-Operation.” The sequence shows negotiation becoming a public plan, while later repair exchanges show that a shared template still was not universal. [crosses 09|10]
- Reports of Polish damage and contact attempts were followed by negotiated cooperation in `6304fd`; the edited report `dfq4cne` says the Polish Discord agreed to repair the rainbow and Snek while doubting that everyone would listen. The participant supplies the explanation; the record supports agreement and doubt, not durable compliance.
- The American-flag accusation in `635ovh` received the direct denial `dfriswd`, “We aren’t,” with a false-flag explanation; `dfrkfdq` later described “damage control.” Other replies pointed to expansion proposals. The exchange is causally linked as accusation and answer, but responsibility remains unresolved.
- The placement-stop report `6382oc` was followed by end announcements, keepsakes, and the retrospective sticky `639kla`. These items explicitly refer to Place ending, but the record does not show that one memorial project caused another; it supports an end-then-memory sequence.

## Posts against comments

- [batch 01] Posts announced pacts and limits; comments included both maintenance and “THIS MEANS WAR.”
- [batch 02] Posts offered sign, border, and Snek directives; comments exposed rogue widening, conflicting templates, and disagreement over Discord authority. The sign’s correction and completion aligned across layers.
- [batch 03] Posts framed urgent attacks and plans; comments described an uncontrollable “hivemind” and negotiated practical corrections.
- [batch 04] Posts said “WE’VE DECIDED”; comments distinguished Discord organisers, subreddit readers, stray helpers, and a hivemind. The weave was an exception in which both layers reported execution and completion.
- [batch 05] Posts called for joint art, defence, and a strong core; comments qualified who was official and whether neutrality or repair took priority.
- [batch 06] Posts announced alliances and routes; moderator comments narrowed them through neutrality, unauthorized-shard labels, and revised schematics.
- [batch 07] Posts asked for expansion or intervention; comments stressed thin resources, protected art, repair, and neutrality.
- [batch 08] Singular commands to destroy, save, or bounce were qualified in comments by prior claims, borders, and maintenance; many image proposals existed mainly in titles.
- [batch 09] Posts sounded categorical about war, peace, and plans; comments did the boundary work and distinguished organised plans from people who had not read Discord or Reddit Live.
- [batch 10] Cooperation posts looked decisive; comments showed competing templates and repeated undoing, especially around Rocket League and Helix.
- [batch 11] Urgent posts implied central direction; comments described unorganised members and inaccessible coordination, while both layers agreed maintenance was pressing.
- [batch 12] Posts treated “the road” as able to grant permission; comments said scripts, a hivemind, or people outside Discord might ignore it.
- [batch 13] Posts presented stop orders, cooperation, and a moratorium; comments said Discord agreements did not bind “randoms” and negotiated patterns were overwritten.
- [batch 14] Posts spoke for whole communities; comments separated Discord, subreddit, hivemind, unaffiliated builders, and silent readers, and emphasized hours of cleaning.
- [batch 15] Posts labelled new pixels attacks; comments sometimes identified approved Beatles or Yoshi work. Official-project posts were also erased by repairers seeking proof.
- [batch 16] Simple proposal posts hid overloaded moderators, broken links, and unseen decisions; comments contradicted claims that Mona Lisa crossings and Banana work were settled.
- [batch 17] Emergency and compromise posts were narrowed by protected intersections, an approval pause, and formal refusals in comments; one Discord check returned to Reddit with a repair instruction.
- [batch 18] Takeover and alliance posts were answered by unauthorized-incursion claims and neutrality; comments also documented approval and later failure of the Hornet.
- [batch 19] Posts accused the road of attacking America; comments both disowned the spread and reported rainbow replacing flag repairs. Gilbert Baker memorial posts and completion comments aligned.
- [batch 20] Defence, permission, and celebration posts coexisted with comments disputing authorization and whether accommodation protected or diluted the road.
- [batch 21] Posts proposed collective continuation; moderator comments said clone-canvas work was unofficial individual action. Retrospective comments added spying and false-flag accounts without displacing the celebratory story.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that Rainbow Road coordinated real projects and agreements, but authority remained partial: public plans, moderators, Discord organisers, repairers, visitors, and unaffiliated placers continually had to make one another’s pixels legible.

If that reading were wrong, the corpus would instead show recognized leaders ordering contested expansions, participants consistently following those orders, and approved projects repeatedly reaching completion without being assigned to rogues, randoms, or a hivemind.

The strongest contrary item is `62qjsm`. It announces an administration plan, says “Please build this before building the rainbow,” and later reports, “It is done! Somehow the hivemind understood and we got the portals into place.” The completed weave in `62r6oa` and completed Mario Kart reported in `dfosyp3` confirm that executable public plans sometimes coordinated successful builds.

The search covered the complete item record for completed, finished, or successful plans; approved or official builds followed or completed; following plans or orders; and the room’s contrary terms “hivemind,” “randoms,” “rogue,” “unsanctioned,” and “unofficial.” Returned candidates were read as items. The result narrows but does not overturn the reading. The strongest contrary item itself says “somehow” and credits the hivemind. Comment `dfp0con` distinguishes an official bounce from a through-route “built by hivemind”; `dfokm6o` says the hivemind “is not aware of our deals, and we cannot control them”; `dfrov1i` disowns expansion into the destroyed American flag while reporting informed members repairing it; and `dfpk7hz` says, “We only approved the sign, we did not say we would help build it.” Central direction was effective in some bounded episodes, not a settled command structure.

## Saturation

The practice themes founded in batch 01 were coordinating through venues and tools; setting priorities and authorizing projects; warning about and fighting attacks; repairing and defending; negotiating; routing around art; extending and building; celebrating and belonging; and arguing over what the road should be. Batch 02 first added disowning rogue/unofficial work and petitioning the road for room, help, or protection. Batch 03 first added reporting and checking canvas conditions. Across batches 04–19, new neighbours and projects varied the objects of these practices more than the practices themselves.

A genuinely new practice appeared in batch 20: remembering the ended canvas and carrying the road elsewhere through thanks, histories, keepsakes, and successor-canvas proposals. Batch 21 extended that theme and qualified official continuity. Thematic saturation is therefore strong for live-placement conduct by the middle and late slices, but the event ending necessarily opened a new post-event theme near the corpus edge.

Evidence saturation is weaker. Discord, Reddit Live, image hosts, documents, private messages, and voice channels are themselves part of the coordination practice, but their contents are only glimpsed when participants bring them back to Reddit. Deleted bodies and title-only or image-dependent posts also thin some episodes. No one reader held the whole week; cross-slice synthesis is less direct than a continuous reading.

## Selection

Branch B used a census of the in-window r/ainbowroad record split into 21 chronological, whole-thread slices. All 1,991 threads were disposed: 1,969 were coded and 22 were out of scope. Slice boundaries were imposed by the instrument’s item budget, not by substantive turning points. Opening windows from the neighbouring slice oriented coders at edges but were not coded as part of the wrong slice. The pooled account uses all live first-order codes, the slice cases, the factual timeline and lexicon, and the reply forest.

## Other communities named

Blue Corner was alternately ally, territorial rival, and repair partner. Nyan Cat, Snek, Lord Helix, Waldo, Mona Lisa, the Trans Flag, the American flag, Straya, Poland, France, Portugal, Denmark, Estonia, Rocket League, Formula One, Radiohead, Prequel Memes, OSU, Green Lattice, EraseThePlace, and the Void appeared as neighbours, petitioners, opponents, or negotiation partners. Smaller groups repeatedly approached the road as usable space or protective infrastructure. These names do not form one relationship category: what mattered was the practice—bargaining, routing, petitioning, defending, or repairing—in which each appeared.

## Absence

The record does not provide the full contents of Discord, Reddit Live, linked images, templates, Google documents, private messages, voice chats, or successor canvases. Deleted posts sometimes leave only titles and replies. The pre-week brief was empty, so there is no earlier local history against which to measure continuity. Absence from this Reddit corpus cannot establish that coordination or conflict did not occur elsewhere, and a short post-event tail cannot establish whether proposed clone roads endured.
