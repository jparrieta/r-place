## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/catalonia's visible r/place practice develops from repeated, contested flag-building into script-assisted coordination that also works out room for named neighbours and a legible design.

If that reading were wrong, and the script mainly hardened blanket territorial defence and expansion, the corpus would instead contain people refusing alliances or exceptions, urging attacks on named neighbouring drawings, or continuing to destroy them after the script coordination.

The strongest contrary item is the unequivocal title “We DO NOT accept any drawings on our flag in r/place” (`636p22`). A search scoped to the whole supplied record for `attack|destroy|respect|alliance|invad|accept` returned 11 items, all of which were read. The early thread does contain accusations of attack (`dfq34ns`), invasion (`dfq3aak`), and destruction of the Dreamcast logo (`dfqeay9`). Yet its strongest participant statement on expansion says “we shouldn't destroy other art just to make the flag bigger” (`dfqfivd`), while the later script thread repeatedly says to respect r/factorio (`635yxc`) and the asexual flag (`dfrpsff`; `dfrss2p`). The alliance hit is an offer rather than a refusal (`634e4p`), and the blanket title is followed by a title containing an r/Factorio exception (`636r04`).

The alternative therefore changes the account by making territorial defence a live, explicit strand rather than a discarded early position. What survives is the narrower claim that the script thread supports accommodation as well as defence. The search cannot establish consensus, and the deleted announcement bodies make the ten-minute contrast especially limited.
