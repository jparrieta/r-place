## What was hard

The hardest boundary was separating expansion by speakers in r/poland from actions attributed to “randoms” or people organising in r/Polska and Discord. I preserved those attributions as participants' explanations rather than treating them as verified membership or causation.

The title “Przyjaciele na zawsze!” (`632lph`) is event-adjacent only through its position and image; the full thread has just an admiring reply. I kept it because the selected event sequence and linked image context make the friendship celebration part of the r/place discussion, but I did not claim which communities the image joined.

## How the reading held up

This run was not interrupted or resumed. I did not substitute remembered content for unserved text. During coding I worked from the passages already served in corpus order and grounded every passage again through `cite`; for the counter-reading I reopened every returned item through `read item`. Earlier threads became less visually sharp once the long middle run of border disputes accumulated, so I used the rendered forest to review the complete coded sequence before writing the account. The opening coordinate and Austrian threads, the AMD settlement, and the late plan thread remained distinct; the smaller image-only threads were the least memorable without their titles in front of me.
