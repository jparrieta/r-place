# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: the room repeatedly tried to keep its pointer present by rebuilding it, bargaining with neighbours, and changing its position or borders; outside agreements were provisional means to that end rather than a replacement for it.

Most consequential competing account: the apparent cooperation instead shows the room giving up its own pointer and becoming labour for stronger neighbouring groups. If that account is right, the record should contain participants urging one another to stop or abandon the pointer, to replace it with another group's picture or logo, or to keep working for outsiders without tying that work to rebuilding or preserving the pointer.
