## Posts against comments

Posts tend to make urgent, room-wide declarations—“Give em what for” (`62r7bc`), “All is lost” (`62uh0r`), “WE WILL REBUILD” (`62vzm7`), and “All is found” (`62xp5z`). Comments complicate those declarations with bargaining and practical limits. Thus the posts say the pointer is simply lost or found; the comment layer says recovery is “for the moment” (`dfpucxo`), a proposed spot is occupied (`dfp7ndg`), an alliance mostly was not followed through (`dfpp0xo`), and a taskbar move may exceed available manpower (`dfr4cu6`).

The two layers also reinforce one another where posts propose a course and comments operationalize it. The negotiation post `62rhb4` asks for someone to bridge the gap; comments supply representatives, terms, a template, and starting pixels (`dfp0w9y`, `dfp0x8u`, `dfp3hhn`, `dfp3tdx`). Claims about coordination detail live mainly in comments, whereas declarations of collective status live mainly in posts.
