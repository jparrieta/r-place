## Fleet method and limitations

The four slices were read by four sequential fleet runs. No reader held the whole week, one coder read each slice, and there is no agreement measure. Slice boundaries follow thread openings rather than exchanges, so later replies can sit in earlier slices. The forest makes reply structure and sequence visible but does not make adjacency causal. I carried no response relation across a slice boundary: every relation in the final account rests on an answering or explicitly recalling passage within its thread-owned slice. Cross-slice chronology is descriptive and comes from the slice cases, timeline, forest and measured item times.

Planning moved to `r/ParahumansPlace`, Discord and IRC. The visible redirects are conduct; the unseen deliberation is a limit. The scheduler required a checkpoint after the final collect, so `notes.md` was started there and completed now rather than first being written only after the account.

## Fact-file review

At each collect I read the additions to `timeline.md` and `lexicon.md`. I struck no lines in any batch. Batch-specific reviews and carry-forward decisions are in `ledger.md` and `_lead_notes.md`.

## What was hard

The main difficulty was preserving three simultaneous readings: visible cooperation, genuine calls to take more space, and disagreement over whether promotion and newcomers were good for the room. Deleted image/body posts required narrow claims. It was also difficult to distinguish immediate reading uptake from sustained retention, which the window cannot show, and to separate the room's own April Fools games from canvas conduct.

Off-venue planning is unavailable. The batch-01-to-03 lead remarks were initially inserted under the first ledger marker by an imprecise hand patch; I repaired their placement before final validation without changing any coder or append-only research record.

## Complaints
