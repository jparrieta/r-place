## What was hard

The largest interpretive difficulty was keeping three things apart: a visible practice among active participants, a plan attributed to Discord organizers, and consent by the subreddit as a whole. “We,” alliances and finished-work claims were repeatedly challenged. The case therefore describes organizers, helpers, dissenters and neighbors rather than treating r/rocketleague as a single speaker.

The other persistent boundary was between the r/place canvas and Rocket League's own April Fools conduct. Vegetable ranks sometimes entered canvas proposals, but most Potato, Corn, Onion, Broccoli, Radish and Grand Eggplant talk concerned game ranks. Codes and themes keep those practices distinct.

## Data problems

No reader holds the whole week. Forty-seven fleet coders each read one arbitrary whole-thread slice; the lead assembled chronology and relations from pooled codes, facts files, the forest and slice cases. Cross-slice relations are weaker than relations read by one person, and this design supplies no measure of agreement across the room. The early invitation/working-thread relation crosses 07|08 and the working design/alliance account crosses 08|09; both were retained only because passages redirect or credit the earlier work.

Discord carries templates, plans and negotiation outside the visible record. Reddit shows what returned, not the discussion that decided it. Image and link contents were unavailable. Deleted and removed bodies frequently leave narrow title evidence. Large ordinary threads stopped at their prescribed triage windows, so absence claims are limited to what those windows could show.

The batch-05 thread `6166i3` was disposed out of scope although its in-window replies predict and confirm veggie ranks. Step-5 full-thread reading appended `c0855`–`c0859` and added them to `t02`; the earlier disposition was not revised, preserving the append-only record.

## Complaints

The slice system makes a week tractable but asks the lead to make relations no individual reader observed end to end. The forest preserves sequence, not causation, and the arbitrary boundaries can split the meaning of an exchange. The contract correctly limits those claims, but the resulting case cannot offer an agreement measure.

The first `emit.close()` printed an extremely large integrity structure dominated by the expected pre-theme list; terminal output was clipped even though the close completed. A concise first-close summary would be easier to audit. The shell also rejected heredoc temporary files, so required helper scripts had to be created explicitly inside the run.
