## Limits of inference

Reply adjacency was used to locate exchanges but never by itself to claim causation. Score and sticky marks are retrieval snapshots. Claims about agreement, force, brigading, Discord activity, and maintenance remain attributed to the speakers who made them. Different speakers' contrasting claims do not establish that the community changed its mind.
