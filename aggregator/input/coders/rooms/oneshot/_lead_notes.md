Provisional reading: r/oneshot turned an uncertain first attempt into a sustained Niko build by adapting the design, coordinating work, making room with neighbours, and defending the figure; after the canvas closed, participants celebrated that shared effort and welcomed people whom the art brought into the subreddit.

Competing account: Niko survived mainly because the top-right corner was lucky and drew little opposition, not because collective building, defence, or diplomacy materially mattered. If the provisional reading is wrong, the record should contain participants attributing survival to luck, few griefers or absent competitors, or denying that guarding and agreements did meaningful work.

Grouping review: reviewed all themes after outlining the within-case account; no regrouping was needed. Design/location decisions remained separate from hands-on building, neighbour agreements, defence, celebration, Discord coordination, help to other pictures, extra-art failures, canvas jokes, and newcomer welcome because each is a different practice despite recurring vocabulary.
