## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between reciprocal neighbor help and help constrained by the room’s own priorities. The record supports both: participants protect neighboring art and honor agreements, but also reserve pixels, decline fights, and in one instance abandon Kebble for Sakura Gakuin. I kept these as related but separate practices rather than smoothing the contrary passages into a single cooperative account.

Completion language was also difficult. “Finished,” “perfect,” and “zero blemish” recur while comments still repair, debate templates, plan an exit, and remember damage. I treated this as a descriptive post/comment contrast unless a participant supplied an explanation; I did not infer that the community changed its mind from different speakers’ positions.

The Fox Day bait-and-switch overlaps the event calendar but not the canvas. It remains coded conduct under the contract and is explicitly separated from r/place. Removed proposal bodies and image-led posts sometimes left titles or replies as the only citable evidence; I did not reconstruct what was missing.

During step 4 I re-read every slice case beside its slice’s pooled codes in order.
