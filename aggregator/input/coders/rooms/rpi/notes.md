Those themes were split and the reasons were recorded in their `regrouped_from` fields.

The main attack and bot threads contain many short turns. I treated a reply as evidence of reply structure only. A relation in the account was included only where the later words name the prompt or supply their own reason; no relation rests on adjacency alone.
