## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between collective plans and conduct attributed to uncontrolled or “rogue” users. Outsider posts addressed Croatia as a single destroyer, while participants explicitly separated Hulk attackers from flag makers and also admitted that some members did attack or sabotage. The account therefore preserves both the collective accusation and the internal qualification without turning either into a census.

Separating an observed sequence from a supported relation was also difficult around the many “We did it” posts, renewed maintenance, closure, and post-close blame. I treated sequence as sequence unless a later passage named the earlier action, supplied a reason, answered it directly, or stated the outcome. The tree records adjacency only; no relation rests on adjacency alone.

The case lead did not infer image contents. Image-only posts were usable through surviving titles and participants’ descriptions, which sometimes made the post/comment contrast legible but never made the linked image an observation.
