# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: r/colorado used many short posts and a few exchanges to recruit pixels, redirect people among several Colorado-flag projects, maintain territory (partly through Discord), and then celebrate and credit the finished flags.

Competing account: the apparent common effort was instead a set of duplicative projects that mostly produced spectatorship and created conflict with neighbors, rather than coordinated Colorado flag work.

What would be present if the provisional reading were wrong: participants would reject additional Colorado flags, describe competing Colorado projects working at cross-purposes, or object that Colorado was taking neighboring canvas territory. The strongest likely contrary vocabulary in this case is “fourth Colorado flag,” “wrath,” “get back on your side,” “randoms,” and “got rid of it.”

## Consolidation review

Compared codes across exchanges by practice, not shared vocabulary. Retained five boundaries: recruiting/building, redirecting among ongoing projects, defending/maintaining territory, explaining the canvas, and celebrating/crediting. The close boundary was between redirecting builders and maintaining territory; pointing to an existing project moves effort, while defending, maintenance, border warnings, and Discord coordination keep a spot in place.
