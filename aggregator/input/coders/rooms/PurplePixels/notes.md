## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between role-play and command. “Emperor,” “commandments,” and overseer titles are plainly the room's words, but their presence does not demonstrate obedience. I retained them as appointing roles and laying down rules, then used replies to describe where authority was checked.

“Purple Corner” alternates between a canvas territory and a separate subreddit or faction. I treated it as territory unless a passage explicitly named alliance, separation, or factional action. Lattice was also difficult because it was both advocated as survival and condemned as fewer purple pixels. Keeping those positions together under a concrete splitting-over-lattice theme preserved the dispute.

The strongest relation claims required restraint. A reply is only a reply until its words answer, name, or recall the earlier practice. I reported the later corner-loss statements as a sequence rather than claiming that Destiny's attack or leadership decisions caused the loss. Times and intervals in `case.md` come from `package/tree_index.json` through `_lead_times.py`, or directly from the slice account's measured record.

## Counter-reading

Before searching I recorded a competing account of stable, uncontested Emperor-led command, accepted lattice and shared artwork, and continuing control of the corner. Four searches tested the case's own vocabulary: command/following, solid-purple/lattice, r/2007scape/permission, and relocation/corner loss. I read every returned item. Some hits were lexical false positives such as “revolution” matching “evolution,” and automated previews were not treated as conduct.

The strongest contrary item was `6308qs`, which says “Purple lattice for survival!” It required the account to recognise lattice as a participant-backed survival alternative. It did not overturn the wider reading because other returned items explicitly break the PurpleCorner alliance, mock exceptions to “only purple,” report ignored permission, and say PurplePixels relocated to Discord. The command search returned an outside envoy's claim that leaders control followers, but no PurplePixels passage showing durable, uncontested obedience within that search scope. Because the archive is partial and coordination moved off-platform, that scoped null cannot establish general absence.

The search produced six late codes, all on threads the fleet had already read whole: two on attack-versus-home priorities, one mocking exceptions to the slogan, one reporting relocation to Discord, one accusing r/2007scape of ignoring refusal, and one calling a Voidbringer truce. No raw thread needed a new full-thread call.

## Data problems

The pre-week brief is harness-generated and empty, so it cannot independently establish which Button-era roles or phrases preceded r/place. Deleted and removed bodies obscure image posts and proposals; the post-event continuation in `63bf9w` cannot be identified because its opener is removed. Discord, shared chats, private messages, and Twitch carry decisions and participation the Reddit record only reports indirectly.

Branch B also weakens the account structurally. No reader held the whole week, one coder read each slice, there is no agreement measure, and an arbitrary boundary separates the Destiny dispute from later defence. I carried one cross-slice relation only as an explicit answer followed by later reports, marked `[crosses 01|02]`; I did not claim the later loss was caused by the earlier exchange.

The forest was built before counter-reading, as §2 orders. Six step-5 codes therefore rest on verified `cite` passages and full-thread fleet dispositions but were not present in the pre-step-4 forest rendering. This is a consequence of the prescribed order for late codes, not an attempt to infer uncoded text.

The shell environment repeatedly warned that it could not create an xcrun cache under `/tmp`; the Python tools nevertheless completed. An attempted heredoc at the start was rejected because the shell sought an external temporary file. No outside-run write succeeded, and all later work used direct foreground commands or files inside the run directory.

## Anomalies and leads

Discord is both an organising venue and an authority problem: the Chroma Convention is refused after disclosing Discord, a different Discord-made agreement is questioned on Reddit, and later battle rooms are role-restricted. The record shows orders and claims returning but not who followed them.

The reported suspected-bot episode moves from suspicion to moderator contact and a report of suspensions, but the record does not independently identify the accounts or verify the mechanism. The lattice split later yields a temporary Void truce, a useful example of rivals cooperating without resolving their underlying disagreement.

## Complaints

The required order builds and audits the forest before the counter-reading, while the counter-reading is expressly allowed to mint late codes. The contract does not prescribe rebuilding the forest afterward, so late codes cannot appear in the audited pre-step-4 forest. The final record remains citation-checkable, but an explicit rule about whether to rebuild would remove this ambiguity.

The fleet's generic title codes are much broader than its more detailed reply codes. Consolidation can group them by the practice named in the surviving title, but some removed-title codes remain necessarily thin. The single-code theme preserves that limit instead of pretending the missing request can be classified.
