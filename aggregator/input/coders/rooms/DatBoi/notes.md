Calls to save Dat Boi, instructions to rebuild him, weapon proposals, and arguments with OSU all name the same figure and sometimes Megaman, but they do different work. I separated recruiting from rebuilding and arming from neighbour negotiation. I kept peaceful and hostile OSU passages together only because each works out who may occupy or cross the same nearby space; their outcomes and positions remain explicit in the theme explanation and case account.

Image and link posts often leave only a title as citable text. I coded only what that title or a comment actually says, never the unseen image. For `631z6t` and `632u93`, the body is deleted and the code rests explicitly on the surviving title.
