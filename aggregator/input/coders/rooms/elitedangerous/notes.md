## What was hard

The difficult distinction was between a public subreddit practice and evidence of who actually placed pixels. The corpus supports public recruitment, grid and coordinate work, bargains, warnings, repair reports, scripts, and first-person labour accounts. It does not support treating every maintained pixel as manual, counting agreement across the room, or making Reddit stand in for Discord and hidden planning.

The early April Fools makeover and the later canvas activity overlap in calendar time but are not one practice. I kept the No Man's Sky styling, fake Elite announcements, and repeated challenge-image gag separate from r/place unless a passage named the canvas. I nearly treated the early failed marks as the origin of the later 675,252 logo, but no participant links them; the account states only that they precede it.

The late threat material was also difficult because posts announce certainty while comments correct it. I preserved “planned attack,” “ignored suggestion,” “no hit list,” and “bots vs bots” as competing participant positions rather than a community-level reversal.

## Data problems

No reader held the whole week. Sixteen coders read arbitrary whole-thread slices, and the lead assembled relations from their cases, pooled codes, facts files, and the forest. A relation crossing a slice boundary is weaker than a relation read by one person and is marked in `case.md`. One coder per slice also means there is no inter-reader agreement measure. Threads continuing across boundaries could be encountered as context by another reader, but the forest supplies sequence, not causation.

Linked images are unavailable as visual evidence. Deleted and removed bodies make several event posts title-only. Discord, a hidden planning channel, Pastebin-hosted scripts, pxls.space activity, and the Minecraft recreation are visible only through what participants brought back to Reddit. Those are observed venue choices or pointers, but their unseen contents and outcomes remain gaps.

The main `62xyoj` thread was logged as served whole by its slice coder although its display was clipped in that coder's interface. The coded portions, slice account, forest, and exact citations support the claims used here; uncoded middle material may contain detail not represented in the themes.
