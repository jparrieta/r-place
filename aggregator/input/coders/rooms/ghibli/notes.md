## What was hard

The main difficulty was separating a succession of overlapping Totoro and NoFace attempts without turning every new coordinate into a new practice. The clearest boundary came from what participants did after trouble: some passages redirected the picture or site, while others announced, explained, or mourned defeat.

`dfrz4he` was the most consequential ambiguity. Its “smallest white Totoro” may have been made by someone outside this room; the text supports recognising and proposing to maintain it, not claiming that r/ghibli created it.

The April Fools sequel and image threads were considered carefully. They show ordinary members reacting to jokes elsewhere, but not a subreddit-run joke or canvas conduct, so they were excluded rather than coded as this room's own April Fools practice.

## Data problems

Fourteen items have deleted or removed text. Several retained link posts survive only through titles, and two deleted comments in the long Totoro thread cannot be interpreted. Discord appears only through Reddit reports, so neither the opposing group's discussion nor the planned request to SwedenOnPlace is visible. Automated messenger and preview accounts were treated as machine-generated setting. Nallenbot and RedmondTheRobot were flagged by name but their brief items did not establish whether they were people; I coded nothing from them.

The briefing sampled a different week and explicitly left eight threads unopened. I used it only for orientation and did not treat its unsupported state summaries as case evidence.

## Complaints

The pointer rule requires both a cited phrase and a separate raw value; repeated names in one item require extending the cited phrase even when the raw name itself is unambiguous. This is defensible but easy to trip over. The instrument otherwise provided the timing and reply measurements needed to avoid inferring relations from adjacency.

## Theme boundary

“Switching pictures and moving to another spot” nearly merged with “calling the build gone when bigger groups take it.” I kept them apart because redirecting effort is an action that continues the project, whereas declaring or mourning loss assesses why an attempt ended. This regrouping is also recorded on the two themes through the instrument. After the counter-reading, I reviewed every theme again and kept the grouping: the small-Totoro maintenance proposal belongs with saying better coordination is needed because both scale the response to what the room can sustain.

## How the reading held up

I did not work from memory of a thread instead of served text. I did not return to an earlier thread because I had forgotten it; later item reads were the required counter-reading of search hits. Earlier threads did not become indistinct while later ones stayed sharp, nor the reverse: the openings table, ordered reading, and forest kept the sequence legible. The run was not interrupted or resumed. The only failed operations were local citation-shape checks while recording; because citations were resolved in batches before the affected records were emitted, they did not require re-reading or revising corpus interpretation.

The gate selected branch A at 179 in-window items against a threshold of 500. Grouping was reviewed after the forest and again after the counter-reading. The only regrouping split redirecting work from announcing or mourning defeat; no later code was added by the counter-reading.
