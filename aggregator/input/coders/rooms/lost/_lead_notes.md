# Lead working notes

## Before counter-reading

Provisional reading: r/lost moved from scattered calls into one small shared set of LOST numbers, then kept it present through repair and reciprocal peace, while using LOST jokes to make the canvas work its own; after the event, surviving mattered more than being perfect.

Competing account: this may instead have remained a fragmented project led by a few loud organisers, with rival locations and expansion wishes never truly converging on the maintained numbers. If the provisional reading is wrong, the record should contain participants rejecting the main site or its maintenance plan, pursuing another location after being redirected, preferring attacks or expansion over peace, or dismissing the finished mark as not representing them.
