# Notes — r/madokamagica

## What was hard

The hardest interpretive boundary was holding together visible cooperation and visible displacement without turning either into the whole case. “Armistice,” allied help, apologies, relocation offers, and later treaty memories support cooperation; r/Landry's loss and objections to event spam prevent describing it as universal or costless. The room's franchise language also crosses contexts: “Walpurgisnacht,” “meguca,” and “Homura did nothing wrong” can be ordinary fandom, the room's own April Fools conduct, or canvas conduct. The fleet coders kept these apart and did not infer image contents from links.

The strongest causal-looking sequences required restraint. A reply is only a reply. The forest establishes order and elapsed time; a relation was written only where the later words answer, name, or explain the earlier practice. In particular, the batch-01 megathread request preceded the moderator hub, but only the moderator's own “tired of the spam … so I created” passage supports a cause. The account does not claim that the particular request caused the moderator action.

## Data problems

Branch B was selected on 802 in-window items against the fixed threshold of 500; the gate also records 150858 characters by people and two whole-thread slices. No reader held the whole week, each slice had one coder, there is no agreement measure, and the boundary is arbitrary with respect to practice. Ten earlier openings in batch 02 were context only. Cross-boundary relations are therefore weaker than a single-reader account and are identified in `case.md`.

Discord carried substantial coordination. The record shows the choice to organise there and what returned to Reddit, but not most deliberation, live pixel work, or voice-chat relocation negotiation. Removed or deleted bodies obscure several event updates and neighbour approaches. Thread `63cntr` continues into post-window context, so the record cannot settle whether the Chatot mascot proposal became durable practice. No missing content was reconstructed.

## Grouping review

The ten-theme grouping was reviewed after the counter-reading and the return to `step4_themes` was recorded by the instrument. No regrouping was made. The five late codes fit existing practices of contesting the posting arrangement, bargaining over neighbours, and remembering loss.

The two themes that nearly merged were “defending, repairing, and keeping the design recognizable” and “making room, borders, and peace with neighbours.” They remained separate because the first maintains the group's own picture, while the second bargains over coexistence with other pictures and communities; a border can appear in either, but the practice and counterpart differ.

## How the reading held up

The case lead did not read raw threads while the fleet ran and did not work from memory of a thread. Each coder reported no memory substitution, no return caused by forgetting, and no earlier-thread indistinctness; both coder runs began fresh and were not resumed. The lead run itself was deliberately checkpointed after each batch and resumed from `out/ledger.md` and `out/_lead_notes.md`, not from conversational memory. At resumption, the lead reread both files before consolidation. Earlier slice detail remained available through the slice cases, pooled code order, and forest rather than an asserted memory of raw text.

## Complaints

The facts-file format asks for metadata-only lines, but both coders appended lines that mixed valid edit/moderator metadata with interpretive summaries. Striking the whole lines also discards useful metadata, because the instrument provides no documented way to retain the metadata while striking only the gloss.

The scheduler checkpoint explicitly required an interim `notes.md` before consolidation, while CONTRACT.md ordinarily places the one-time notes write at the end. The checkpoint was preserved in the running lead records and this final file replaces that interim shell with the complete notes. The research contract otherwise governed the work unchanged.

The branch-B chronology requires opening and closing on ordinary activity, but out-of-scope threads do not appear in the coded forest. The account therefore uses their citable surviving titles only as orientation and makes no relation rest on them.
