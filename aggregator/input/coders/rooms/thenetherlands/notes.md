## Fleet facts-file review

All fourteen batches were collected in slice order. After every collection I read the additions to `timeline.md` and `lexicon.md`, checked whether any line was interpretation rather than fact, and recorded the review under that batch in `ledger.md` and `_lead_notes.md`. I struck no line: timeline additions were direct moderator, automation, sticky, or retrieval-edit facts tied to item ids; lexicon additions were unglossed participant terms tied to item ids. Batch headings were organisational rather than readings. Batch 14 added no facts lines.

The timeline is appended by slice and is therefore not globally displayed in chronological order; its individual entries remain checkable through their explicit time and item id. I did not silently reorder this append-only record.

## What was hard

The hardest boundary was between taking canvas territory and negotiating across its borders. The same neighbours recur in both, but expansion codes concern acquiring space while diplomacy codes concern asking, answering, refusing, helping or agreeing. Image-only posts and removed bodies were kept narrowly when the title itself described conduct; I did not reconstruct the image or missing proposal. The retrospective also required keeping distinct a descriptive contrast (celebratory posts versus critical comments), participants’ explanations (“They just wanted to expand”), and causal claims. Different positions were not treated as evidence that the community changed its mind.

## Data problems

Organising visibly moved in part to Discord, r/cirkeltrek and r/PlaceNL, but the deliberation there is outside this record. The branch-B split means no reader held the whole week, one coder read each slice, no agreement measure exists, and arbitrary slice boundaries sometimes divided a continuing exchange. The forest supplies reply structure and instrument times, but a reply edge is not itself causal evidence.

## How the reading held up

I did not work from memory of raw threads. During the fleet I worked from each slice case, facts-file additions and the contemporaneous ledger; after consolidation I reread `codes_by_thread.md` slice by slice beside the cases. Earlier slices became less sharp as later slices arrived, so I returned to their case files and `_lead_notes.md` when assembling chronology and relations. This happened especially for batches 01–05 when comparing their overlapping heilzaam and early-canvas episodes with batches 12–14. The run was interrupted by scheduler checkpoints after batch collection and resumed after batch 14; I picked it up by rereading the contracts, journal tail, gate, ledger, lead notes, pooled-code boundaries and slice cases. Raw corpus reading began only after `step5_counter` and was limited to returned counter-reading items and supporting items used to verify their wording.
