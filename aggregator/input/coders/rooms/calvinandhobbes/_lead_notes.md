# Lead working notes

## Before the counter-reading search

Provisional reading: members turned affection for Calvin and Hobbes into a coordinated build, partly moved coordination to Discord, negotiated neighbouring space, then maintained and celebrated the picture.

Competing account and failure condition: the picture survived mainly because outsiders already liked it and vandalised it little, not because this community's coordination and upkeep mattered. If the provisional reading is wrong, the record should contain explicit abandonment, refusal to coordinate, or retrospective attribution of survival to passive goodwill rather than repair.
