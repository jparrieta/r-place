The instrument was clear. The opening-only `635gvy` also shows why an unavailable linked image cannot safely carry an event inference.

## Grouping review

The initial broad group “building and keeping it up” was split into building, coordinating, neighbour negotiation, and upkeep because codes across different exchanges performed different work despite sharing canvas vocabulary. The heart dispute was lifted out of upkeep because it contests the intended design rather than simply restoring it. The retrospective material was split between appraising the final picture and explaining why recognisable shared art works. These regroupings are recorded on the themes. After the counter-reading I reviewed all seven themes again; no further regrouping was warranted.

The two themes that nearly merged were “building Calvin and Hobbes pixel by pixel” and “keeping Calvin and Hobbes clean and recognisable.” What kept them apart was the object of the action: making and finishing new features versus watching and restoring an existing image.

## How the reading held up

I did not work from memory of a thread instead of what `read` served. I did not return to a thread because I had forgotten it; the only repeat item reads were required inspections of search hits. Earlier threads did not become indistinct relative to later ones: the openings table and chronological reading kept the ordinary first half distinct from the r/place cluster beginning at `62xbqf`. The run was not interrupted and resumed.

The counter-reading searches returned several lexical false positives in pre-window, out-of-scope discussions; I read each returned item through `read item` before excluding it from the test. Three relevant late passages were coded at step 5 and added to existing themes.
