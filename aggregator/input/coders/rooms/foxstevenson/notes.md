## Method notes

The briefing was used only for orientation and not as evidence. The counter-reading produced no late codes. Reply adjacency was used to locate exchanges, never by itself to claim that one turn caused another. The grouping is based on shared practice: joke-making, recruiting, designing, relocating, negotiating space, allying and directing helpers, or accepting a partial result—not on shared vocabulary or mere adjacency.
