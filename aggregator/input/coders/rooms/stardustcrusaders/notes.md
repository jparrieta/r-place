## Complaints

The branch-B workflow forces synthesis across cases written by different readers while prohibiting a lead from reading the corpus until counter-reading. That makes cross-slice causal claims appropriately difficult but also makes apparently simple chronology depend on careful handoff prose. The output transport truncation for a fully served long thread is especially awkward: the read audit can be correct while the analyst cannot inspect every displayed byte. The append-only theme model also means a mistaken early grouping cannot be moved later, so the required incremental order makes conservative single-code themes safer than premature merging.
