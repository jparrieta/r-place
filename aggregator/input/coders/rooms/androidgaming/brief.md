## Moderation and its visible traces

Moderation is conspicuous in the supplied archive: 235 items are deleted or removed, 15 are sticky-marked, and 116 are moderator-distinguished. (header)

The visible workflow combines automated filtering with human explanations. `AutoModerator` flags account age or karma and mentions spam, self-promotion, and sidebar rules; human moderators then give category-specific removal reasons.

> Due to your account being new to reddit (or having low karma), your post has been filtered to be reviewed by the moderators. (`dfll62s`)

> This post has been removed because it is a game request. (`dflllp1`)

Self-promotion rules are stated as participation requirements, and at least one directly replying poster acknowledges learning the rule rather than contesting the removal.

> Wow, thanks for the heads up, wasn't aware of that rule (`dfdnh7c`)

Removed bodies remain `[removed]` or `[deleted]` in the record; the brief does not reconstruct them. The 304 unresolved parent pointers also limit what can be said about reactions when the parent was not served or is absent. (header)
