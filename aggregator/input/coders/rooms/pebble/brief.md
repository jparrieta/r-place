## Vernacular

Model codes carry much of the talk. `P2`, `PT`, and `PTS` name hardware, while `aplite`, `basalt`, and `diorite` name platforms; `OG` means the original series.

> In my experience, you can't load a watchface or app on a P2 which was compiled only for "basalt" (PT/PTS hardware). (`dffgpg4`)

> It has to be built for aplite (OG series) or diorite (P2), with B/W resources that work on those platforms. (`dffgpg4`)

`Rebble` names the team expected to preserve the Pebble ecosystem after official support ends.

> provided the Rebble team can keep the ecosystem alive after 2017, I'd be happy to continue using a Pebble for years. (`61hemt`)

People call the watch they wear most their `daily driver`; another device is a `backup`.

> I think the P2 will become my daily driver, and the PTS more for suit situations like interviews or what have you. (`dfdj4mj`)
