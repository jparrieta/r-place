## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between participants' national-war vocabulary and the actual practice it organized. I retained their words—`invade`, `attack`, `troops`, `diplomat`, `border`, `griefers`—without turning them into an outside category. The Dutch relation was especially difficult because warnings, insults, votes, protection and joint art coexist. Different speakers' contrasting positions were treated as simultaneous differences unless a speaker or direct answer explicitly linked one turn to another.

The openings and many posts point to images whose content is not text evidence. I treated participant descriptions as what those participants said and did not infer unseen plans from an image. Removed-body titles were codeable; the missing bodies were not reconstructed. The Mapy.cz April Fools map was kept separate from the canvas event.
