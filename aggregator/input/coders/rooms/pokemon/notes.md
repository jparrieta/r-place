## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between the subreddit’s Team Skull April Fools takeover and r/place conduct. They overlap in time and canvas callers sometimes address “grunts,” but the takeover changes r/pokemon’s rules, voice, art, and recurring formats, while canvas conduct asks for pixels, defense, space, or planning. I retained both as community conduct and kept the distinction explicit in themes and the account.

The other difficult boundary was between proposing, defending, centralizing, and remembering canvas work. Shared Pokémon names or reply adjacency did not establish one practice. A proposal asks people to make something; defense answers visible damage or pressure; hub-building chooses where plans and updates should go; retrospective posts catalogue or explain work after it is over.

No late code was needed. Every relevant counter-reading passage already had a live fleet code.
