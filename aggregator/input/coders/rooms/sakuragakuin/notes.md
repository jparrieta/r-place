## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between coexistence and defence. Several turns about the snek tongue change SG's own border to accommodate a neighbour, while later turns protect SG/BM from attacks. They can share vocabulary about borders and overwriting without being the same practice, so they remain separate themes.

The complaint in `dfpicdk` made it impossible to describe accommodation as consistently preventive. The replies explicitly apologize and offer repair, which supports a repaired-cooperation reading; reply adjacency by itself was not treated as evidence.

Completion was also ambiguous. Posts announce the banner or project finished, while comments immediately start a proper logo, continue repairs, or describe attacks. I treated these as stage claims and a post/comment contrast, not as proof that the community changed its mind.

The term `murial` is the participants' repeated spelling in the ending thread and is preserved where labels closely follow their language. `Spica` and `Spoony` remain unglossed lexicon terms; their relationship is described only where the cited exchange supports it.
