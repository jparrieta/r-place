# Lead working notes

Before counter-search: the provisional reading is that the canvas practice was a small, fragile mark made by one person, briefly watched, then treated as permanent and worthwhile after it drew a newcomer. The strongest plausible alternative comes from the post's own "Protect it": the mark may instead have become sustained collective defence or expansion. If that alternative is right, the record should contain people assigning pixels or shifts, reporting attacks and repairs, or naming allies and enemies.

Grouping review before search: five themes were retained after comparing practices across exchanges. Match celebration was separated from the explicit April Fools reversal; uncertain making/watching was separated from later claims of permanence; memorialising the mark and welcoming the newcomer were merged because the participants explicitly treated the arrival as the mark having "paid off".
