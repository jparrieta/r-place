## What was hard

The hardest interpretive problem was preserving the coexistence of attack, restraint, repair, and familiar rivalry around Ohio State. Shared references to the O or to “Fuck Ohio” did not make these passages one practice. I separated attacking and altering rivals’ pictures from calling off attacks and making peace, and treated later rivalry sayings as talk rather than evidence of continued organised canvas attack.

The second difficulty was separating making a design from getting teammates onto its current version. Border proposals, letter shapes, and a BIG M make or remake the picture; coordinates, corrections, stickies, private messages, votes, and warnings about obsolete bots align contributors. Automation overlaps both, but the script exchanges form a further practice because participants build, distribute, update, and constrain the placement machinery itself.

Deleted-title and image-only posts sometimes show a visible call without its reasoning or result. I kept the surviving action where the title itself states it and did not infer what an unseen image depicted. The Ann Arbor/Touhou moderator title and several deleted template titles remain particularly thin.

## Data problems

Branch B divides the week between two readers. No reader holds the whole raw record, no agreement measure exists, and a slice boundary can divide an episode. Relations across that boundary are therefore weaker; the final account carries inherited context but makes no causal claim across 01|02.

Deleted or removed bodies and unresolved parent pointers leave local gaps. External recruitment, polls, images, code, and templates also hide what happened away from Reddit. The record shows what people linked and what returned, not who voted, who joined after recruitment, or how repository content changed.

The runtime repeatedly warned that it could not create an xcrun cache under /tmp, but every instrument call completed. An early shell heredoc failed because it tried to create a temporary file outside the permitted run directory; subsequent state used foreground one-line Python or files inside this run directory.

## Complaints

The fleet design necessarily asks the lead to make a whole-case relation account without having read the raw week. The forest preserves sequence but cannot repair the absence of a single reader or make reply adjacency causal. The contract’s explicit warning is essential and is retained in case.md.

The timeline rewrite warning was unsettling because the required facts file is described as append-only. The collector produced a complete merged lead copy, but the warning should remain auditable.

Search results include lexical false positives from ordinary community life. Reading every hit correctly prevents the hit count from masquerading as counter-evidence, but the harness’s FindResult summary does not itself print ids; the result had to be iterated before items could be opened.

## Theme boundary

The themes “attacking and altering rivals’ pictures” and “calling off attacks and making peace with Ohio State” nearly merged because both repeatedly concern the O; they stayed apart because overwriting or threatening it and withdrawing, bargaining, or repairing it are opposing shared practices, not variations of one doing.

## How the reading held up

I did not work from memory of raw fleet threads: before consolidation the case lead read openings, facts, ledger entries, and slice outputs, not raw threads. In step 4 I read codes_by_thread one slice at a time with that slice’s case beside it. Earlier slice details did not need to remain sharp as unaided memory because the ledger, slice case, forest, and pooled code order carried them.

I did not go back to a raw thread because I could not remember it. After step 4, I opened every item returned by the counter-reading searches and additional cited items needed for the account, which was the prescribed step-5 reading rather than a memory repair. Some items were opened again when they supported both the search assessment and the written account; the reason was quotation checking.

This run was deliberately checkpointed after batch 02 was collected. On resumption I used the existing journal and the already reviewed ledger/_lead_notes state, did not recollect either batch, and continued with consolidation. No earlier or later portion became indistinct in a way that shaped coding: the fleet coders owned first-order reading, while the lead’s consolidation remained anchored to the ordered files.
