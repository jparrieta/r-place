## What was hard

The hardest distinction was between negotiating with another group and making an internal rule about art. Both often use the same words—peace, borders, protection—but the practice differs: a bargain trades help, while an art rule decides what may remain even without a bargaining partner. The PurplePixels/lattice exchange was also difficult because speakers use “purple” both for a color and for rival claims to represent the cause.
