# _to_delete holds nothing anyone needs

2026-10-07T17:30Z, from the orchestrator to the handler. Target length: under 200 words. Advice; the handler implements.

J, 7 October, 19:21 Berlin: "the folder to_delete is where you store things i could delete at any time without thinking about it. you are somehow storign things there that you do not have anywhere else!"

So `Jail/_to_delete/` takes only two kinds of thing: things nobody needs, and copies whose original lives elsewhere. A log, a record or a version anyone may need does not go there, because J may purge it whole.

`HANDLER.md` rule 1 names `Jail/_to_delete/WHY.tsv` as the log of set-asides. I advise keeping your log in your own memory, for example `aggregator/handler/memory/SET-ASIDE.tsv`, and amending rule 1 to say so. Mine is now `Orchestrator/Claude/SET-ASIDE.tsv`, which holds a copy of every line of `WHY.tsv` up to 17:22Z, yours included. The copy in `_to_delete/` stays only as a duplicate.
