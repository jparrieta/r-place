- In the sample, at least one fresh reader placed or listed 40 of the 80 kept themes. So about 1,400 kept themes across the corpus would get no fresh reader's support.

**A cheap rule does not give the estimate.** The concept counts my B1 grouping recorded predict support only weakly. Kept themes with three or more B1 concepts were supported in 17 of 28 cases. **How to get the real figure.** Two fresh DeepSeek readers on the class 2 items alone. That is about 4,000 readings. Arm 1 cost $3.46 for 1,370 readings, so roughly $10.

Where the primary itself splits, the boundaries are T3-010 against T3-149, T3-084 against T3-062 and T3-011, and T3-011 or T3-012 against T3-010. In order, here is what I would do with each sentence:

1. **T3-010's `must_show`: rewrite.** It says "borders agreed or asked for, shifts of a few pixels, overlaps accepted or refused, space shared". That swallows its siblings' acts: asking (T3-035), granting (T3-012) and refusing (T3-115). I would keep "agreed between the two rooms" and drop "asked for" and "accepted or refused". This sentence already failed at adjudication on "overlaps accepted or refused".
2. **The T3-010 against T3-149 discriminator: rewrite to name the evidence.** If the words name an attack, an overwrite or a war between the two rooms before the deal, it is T3-149. In de t05 the words were "Blitzkrieg us" and "stop trying to swallow our flag".
3. **The T3-084 against T3-062 discriminator: rewrite.** "Another sub's membership" against "another canvas room as a group" asks for something readers cannot see, because most rooms are both. I would give a visible sign: a thread posted in another sub goes to T3-062; a request to a room's builders or megathread goes to T3-084.
4. **T3-010 and T3-011: add a discriminator.** They have none between them. Adjudication packet 5 already showed 7 splits on this pair.
5. **A family `decide_when_both` field: new sentence.** When one exchange holds a request, a grant, a trade and a border, say which act leads: the agreement the exchange ends in. The others become second placements only on concepts of their own.

I see no case for splitting a G14 theme. The evidence calls for narrowing T3-010, drawing one missing pair, and giving readers a rule for which act leads.
