What can I see and talk to?
Handler
Your separate Codex chat for requests, cards and coordination. It reads study state and keeps short decisions on disk. Historical website handlers remain readable.
Stage / leg
The research aggregator for that leg. Talk to the current leg while it works. Closed legs are read-only; open their saved state as an interview.
Fleet readers and checks
Separate workers controlled by the fleet. You can inspect their activity. Use a saved interview to question a reader without editing a live dispatch.
Saved agent
A stored snapshot of conversation and files, not an agent working in the background. One agent can have several snapshots. Choose Saved snapshots for past states, or Tests and checks for test agents. “Resume as interview” creates an independent branch. “Continue saved handler” restores its operational role and is checked by the controller.
Research files
Files stay with each agent and its checkpoint. Ask the agent about them here. This panel does not yet browse files.
