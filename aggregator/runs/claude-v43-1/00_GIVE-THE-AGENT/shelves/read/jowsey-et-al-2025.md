                                                                 Abstract
                                                                 Four hundred and nineteen experienced qualitative researchers from 32 countries invite readers of Qualitative Inquiry to
                                                                 consider their position on use of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) for qualitative research. We hold the position
                                                                 that analytic approaches such as reflexive thematic analysis are human research practices requiring a subjective, positioned,
                                                                 and reflexive researcher and therefore the use of GenAI in such approaches is not methodologically congruent. We
                                                                 additionally reject GenAI for reflexive qualitative approaches on the grounds of social and environmental justice.

                                                                 Keywords
                                                                 generative, artificial intelligence, GenAI, reflexive, reflexive thematic analysis
