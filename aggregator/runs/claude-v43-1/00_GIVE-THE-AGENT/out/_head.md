## The method question, in one sentence

Can independently reconstructed case histories be composed into a shared dynamic account that supports explanations at successive levels of specificity, from population-wide patterns, through forms and subforms, to particular organizational histories, while keeping access to the evidence from which those explanations were developed?

The theory question is unchanged: when strangers organize on a clock they did not set, with no way to compel anyone and no goal given to them, what forms does organizing take, how do they differ in their functioning, and which of them keep the work alive?

## How the B1 walk goes (the stage file, kept here for after a compaction)

- **Legs.** One stretch of the order per leg, about 1 MB (`out/_analysis/b1_stretch_sizes.py <first> <last>`), declared in the diary when the leg opens. Then B1c (the saturation test), B1d (the second pass), B1e (the scheme standing, handback, interview cut).
- **The next room is always `mem.py next --stage B1`**, at every leg start and after every compaction. It follows the order and the stopping rule. Never a room from memory.
- **Startup packet each leg:** this head, the stage file, the last five diary entries, `mem.py scheme --brief` (paged at 80 lines: `--offset 80`, 160), `validate.py --open B1`.
- **Per room.** Read the A1 note (`mem.py note`) and every theme's note and concept labels. Read the room on its own first, then against rooms already read that it is tied to. An episode needs both records to tell it, by one thread or one artwork in the same hours; a member writing in both rooms is strong evidence (`b1_shared_many.py` on dumps from `b1_room_codes.py` and `b1_dump_rooms.py`; `b1_grep_rooms.py` reads matches room by room; `b1_show_codes.py <sub> c0001,c0002` checks cited ids; `b1_room_placements.py` gives a partner's kinds). A request or mention that only one record holds is not an episode. Mark "same": "thread" only when both rooms' codes sit in that thread; otherwise "artwork". A member's name goes on a line only from an untruncated display (`cite.py` gives it whole). A room is done when every second-order theme is placed or listed in `unplaced-B1.jsonl` (Edit tool, one line `{"sub", "second_order", "theme_id", "label", "leg", "why"}`); unplaced is only for a theme with no doing to read (a bare title or link). A theme that stands alone goes on the rare tier, not out.
- **The tie search.** Every `mem.py` listing stops at 80 lines. Count with `b1_listing_pages.py <read_rooms_file> "label=regex"`, list with `mem.py codes --grep "<re>" --by-room`, intersect with the read rooms (refresh the list from `saturation.py order` after each room's placements), read every match in full. An unread room's record is a lead, never an episode. The store's grep is case-blind; `\b` bounds a short name (an unbounded short word matches inside others). `mem.py diary --grep` takes a plain phrase; it does not take alternation.
- **Episodes.** One line per event, listing every room that tells it; a room joining an existing line is added, with "Added at B1b-NN" at the start of the evidence and the end of the why. List only kinds a listed room holds; when an edited older line holds a kind no listed room holds, take it off and say so in the why. Before closing, run `b1_episode_read_check.py <read_rooms_file>` and `b1_episode_theme_check.py <first> <last>`, and read the check's last line before logging its count. Quote a member only through `cite.py`; a coder's label is never shown as a quote; members take they and them.
- **Order of work in a room:** read every theme; log ideas and mint the room's new kinds first; then place the rest with `python3 out/_analysis/b1_place.py <sub> t01=T3-002 t02=T3-004,T3-103 ...` (timeout 600,000 ms). Before choosing kinds for an unfamiliar theme, `out/_analysis/b1_label_grep.py "<regex>"` shows how earlier rooms' themes with similar labels were placed.
- **Rule 0, single placement.** One third-order theme per second-order theme, the one whose gate it meets best. A second or third only where a concept of its own supports it; a one-code theme takes one kind. Write the leg's table as a `.psv` with the Edit tool (header `sub|theme_id|primary|additional|codes|why_not_one`, new lines before the `END|t00|` placeholder; cite only codes under that theme), convert the whole table with `grep -v "^END|" ... | tr '|' '\t'` (the tools write only lines not yet in the log), dry-run `b1_multiplace_write3.py <tsv> --leg <leg>` then add `--write` (timeout 500,000 ms). Beyond three kinds: place once and record a bag through `b1_bags_from_file.py <tsv> <leg> <bagfile> [--write]` (bag lines `sub:tNN:T3-kept:T3-a,T3-b:why`, exactly four colons), which also writes the table's new lines. After bags, `b1_check_bags.py <leg> <sub>`.
- **Reading a room (the handler, 7 October):** read the room's whole print before placing any of its themes; grep finds things and does not replace reading. `python3 out/_analysis/b1_room.py <sub> > $TMPDIR/x.txt`, printed with `cut -c1-200` in parts under 30 KB with `sed -n`; check the line count. The Read tool cannot open `$TMPDIR`.
- **Verbs, never by hand.** `mem.py idea "<label>" --trigger <sub>:<tNN>`; `mem.py mint --label "..." --trigger <sub>:<tNN>`; `mem.py place`; `mem.py unplace <sub>:<tNN> --theme T3-NNN`. A merge or split of a theme with more than twenty members: `log --kind move` and `python3 tools/snap.py B1 move-<move>-<n>`.
- **Grouping.** Gioia's first-order consolidation: kinds, not sizes; never split by degree; when in doubt keep two. No ceiling (prior §1: 250 to 450). A theme holding more than one in ten is read again for kinds inside it. Names 2 to 5 words, gerund-led, never a construct's name.
- **The floor:** a kind seen in two rooms unlike each other (size, side of the contrast, or form) enters the main scheme; one room only, the rare tier. Rooms telling one episode count as one for the themes the episode lists.
- **The stopping rule** (`saturation.py status --stage B1`, `--json` for the rounds; never count by hand): rounds begin after 10 average rooms and 10 rooms without a new kind; marker 1 (the first stop) and marker 2 after three empty rounds each; then the draw (marker 3); then the report and `done.sh B1 --pause`. A round predicted longer than 10 average rooms is logged when it begins. The decision after the check-in or marker 3 is the PI's alone (the decision record is never written by me; the guard refuses it, and refuses a log line that names the command).
- **Reads allowed** are listed in the stage file; quotes through `cite.py` only. Forbidden: cutting fourth-order themes or dimensions; citing a note.
- **Safe stops:** after each room's themes are placed and logged; `ls .pause`.
