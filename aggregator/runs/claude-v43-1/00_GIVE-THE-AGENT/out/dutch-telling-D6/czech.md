## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 14 changes, 8 restrict, 3 add a condition, 2 add a step and 1 links. They sit in Critical mass 2, Coordination without command 5, Order without law 5, Imprinting 1 and Contention 1.

- **The call.** Two changes sit at T3-002, calling members in to place (T4-005 Mobilization). It adds a condition to P16 (help): one member offers to repair neighbours only when the room's own flag is in good shape. It restricts P25 (newcomers): seeing the flag brings a member to place, without that member finding the room through it.
- **The design.** Three changes sit at T3-004, settling the mark's design (T4-006 Collective representation).
  - It restricts A11 (a template settles a design); the arrow fails here: a posted pattern does not settle the design while members dispute whether it is usable and right.
  - It adds a condition to P04 (templates): placing from a current view of the canvas is a condition of coordination, since instructions to refresh followed reports of wasted work.
  - It restricts P05 (upkeep): friendly misrepair and corrections are shown, but erased lettering alone does not show keepers who did not know a change.

  In the A1 telling a member specified that "the slope should be 2/3" (`dfoysvh`) for the flag's wedge.
- **Scripts and the chat.**
  - It adds a step to P14 (scripts and spare accounts) at T3-028, running scripts for the work (T4-009 Workarounds): members propose changed bot targets while they begin a design. This is form B's change, and a "robust" one. In the A1 telling a member said "many people here just running selfrepairing bots over night" (`dfr4ype`).
  - It restricts P28 (a room formed around a mark) at T3-014, moving planning into a chat (T4-010 Backstage decision-making): a national canvas sub and chat are offered, without a new collective shown forming around the mark.
- **The neighbours.** Five changes sit at T3-010, settling borders with neighbours, T3-149, agreeing truces with rival rooms (T4-016 Negotiated order), and T3-084, asking other rooms for help (T4-017 Reciprocity).
  - It restricts A04 (keeping brings border talk); the arrow fails here: talk with neighbours followed the room's own expansion crowding them, not the erosion of its own work.
  - It links and adds a step to P08 (border terms): expansion over neighbours' work brings appeals to spare it and bargaining over another direction; and telling organised allies from hostile placers comes before treating damage as a broken pact. The step is form B's change.
  - It adds a condition to P17 (coalitions): friendship with one room does not let the room give that partner's ground to another ally's mark.
  - It restricts A13 (keeping under strain brings help); the line states a condition: help depends on what is owed and on spare hands, not on the receiver's shortage alone.
