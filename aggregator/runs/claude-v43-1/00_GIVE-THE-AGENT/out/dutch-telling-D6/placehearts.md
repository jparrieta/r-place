  - It adds a step to P06 (working out who changed the work): a member's admission identifies deliberate counter-placing in a repair dispute, telling opposed wishes apart from mistakes.

  In the A1 telling members argued at the close over the last pink pixel in the first heart (`6383gl`, `6386qs`).
