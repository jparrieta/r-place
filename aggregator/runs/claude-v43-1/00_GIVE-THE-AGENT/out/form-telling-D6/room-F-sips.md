- **It did not move.** The form's name is moving for the spot, and the move and the arrow to it are absent here. The room asked, waited, built a small sign in another place, and lost it. The form's account notes that its record runs to the close with the spot still unsettled.

- **Its result rests partly on another room.** The A1 telling records a member of a neighbouring room who kept the room's name on the canvas.
