# r/romania: the model as instantiated in one room of form D (the play, kept)

Written at D6a-14. The figure is `room-D-romania.png` (and `.svg`). The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 15 selected changes (the D5 lines of form D; `_analysis/d6a14_facts_D.json`) and its A1 telling (`package/romania/case.md`), cited below for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/romania stands for D.2.2, the play, kept. D.2.2 is the smallest part of the form, and only two of its rooms lie in the nearer half of D's defining distances and nearer D's centre than any other. Of the two, r/romania has the more selected changes: 15, against the form's median of 11, and 270 coded concepts. Its distance to D's centre is 0.8987, 45th nearest of 96. In the A1 telling the canvas work begins with "Roddit! Hai sa facem steagul Romaniei pe /r/place!" (`62n1dz`), let us make Romania's flag. Members then built and kept the flag, argued over attack and restraint, made hearts with neighbours, built a vampire figure late in the event, and afterwards remade it in beads and as a sticker. Beside the flag, the sub ran its own April Fools play.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 15 changes, 7 restrict, 6 add a step, 1 adds a condition and 1 links. They sit in Coordination without command 1, Maintenance 7, Collective identity 1, Host framing 5 and Imprinting 1.

- **The design and its keepers.** Six changes sit at T3-170, mistaking friendly work for vandalism (T4-019 Attribution).
  - The room restricts A11 (a template settles a design): a posted template does not settle agreement among defenders who have not seen or recognised the revision.
  - It restricts A03 (building then keeping): repair and building overlap while the design is revised.
  - It adds a step to P06 (working out who changed the work): finding no public plan supports a provisional judgement that a change is unplanned, before members commit to repair it, and does not prove that no plan exists. In the A1 telling a member asked "Nu e planificat?", is it not planned, and was answered "Nu am gasit niciun post legat de asta, deci presupun ca nu e planificat." (`dfqs2mp`, `dfqs683`): I found no post about it, so I assume it is not planned.
  - It links P05 (upkeep): a provisional judgement that a change is unplanned is followed by commitments to repair, while elsewhere in the thread members find the organisation behind it.
  - It restricts P05: friendly fire, once confirmed, does not show a change lost or given up, when completion is reported and the reach of the correcting message is unknown. In the A1 telling a member admitted erasing the Dracula figure because they had not seen the thread (`dfqff0k`), and another asked that the design be put in the moderators' sticky (`dfqfmvr`). During the vampire figure two members found they had both erased planned work: "Eu făceam janitoring peste ce lucrați voi" (`dfrstep`), I was janitoring over what you were working on.
  - It adds a condition to P23 (the chat): coordination in the chat must return a design that the defenders in the public thread can recognise, since otherwise they erase planned work they do not know. In the A1 telling the organising post kept discussion in one thread and sent live organising to Discord (`dfotq0y`).
- **Keeping.** It restricts P14 (scripts and spare accounts) at T3-021, watching and repairing the work (T4-013 Repair work): members' suspicions, drawn from instant repairs, are kept apart from the report of a member who ran the accounts. This is form D's defining change, and a "robust" one. The A1 telling keeps the claims about bots or multiple accounts as participants' claims.
- **The neighbours.** It restricts P19 (a guest's work under a host's rule) at T3-079, joining flags with a heart (T4-022 Flagging the nation): one member's willingness to share does not show the room's approval, a completed transfer or a host's rule. This is form D's defining change. In the A1 telling a heart agreed with Hungary drew both refusal and participation (`62ztm3`). A later call to conquer Hungary was answered with a link to the agreement, made "Literalmente acum o ora" (`dfqar8r`), literally an hour ago.
- **The host's frame and the sub's play.** Five changes sit in Host framing.
  - It restricts C01 (decided order) at T3-015, routing canvas posts into megathreads (T4-002 Boundary work): teams proposed, discussion rules enacted and command over placements must be told apart.
  - It changes K01 (the April Fools frame) four times at T3-001, staging April Fools fake news (T4-001 Keying). This is form D's defining change, held mostly by D.2's rooms. It adds three steps: recipients test the frame by date, source and thumbnail; an apparent ban is believed, tested and kept from spoilers; members make being fooled into shared stories and delay a reveal. It restricts once: a prank's apparent authorship by the moderators is not its authorship, when the moderators deny sending it. In the A1 telling a moderator said of the fake ban "nu e de la noi" (`dfozw2a`), it is not from us.
- **The mark's joke.** It adds a step to P26 (jokes) at T3-113, drawing national emblems (T4-006 Collective representation): members put a pun into their own mark, which others remember repairing. In the A1 telling members played with the lettering as "Pomania" and "HOMONIA".
- **Carrying on.** It adds a step to P13 (what the room keeps) at T3-057, remaking canvas art elsewhere (T4-028 Transition): carrying on includes challenging a claimed inspiration and teaching how to make the work by hand. This is form D's defining change, and a "robust" one. In the A1 telling members remade the vampire in Perler beads. A request "Sa îl facă cineva vector" (`dftakbz`), for a laptop sticker, was answered "Done!" (`dftds93`).

- **Its carrying on came after the close.** It adds to what the room keeps (P13), but the record carried on (A09) is absent, and none of its changes is new work begun before the close.
