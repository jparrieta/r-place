# Form C, The design reopened: the form's telling

Written at D6a-13, on J's instruction (27 October, 06:41 to 06:58 Berlin, through the handler). The form is form C of the D4a-08c cut, which does not change. It has 125 rooms, 96 of them defining. The figure is `form-C.png` (and `form-C.svg`). The counts come from `fractal-forms-D6.jsonl`, `form-cells-sensitivity-D6.tsv`, `form-cells-size-D6.tsv`, the allocation file `room-forms-D4a08c.jsonl`, the D5 lines of the form's defining rooms and the form's account (`room-form-C-account.md`); `_analysis/d6a11_form_telling.py` reads them. Local validation is not established: each room shows a pattern in its ruled lines, not in a validated account of the room.

**How a change is written here.** A change is an op on a unit of the model: an element or an arrow. The op is add-step, add-condition, link or restrict. The change is placed at the third-order theme (T3) where the room's own theme sits, and so at a fourth-order theme (T4) and a dimension. The T3 and T4 given for each change are the most common placements of the form's lines for it.

## What defines the form against the global model

**The change signature.** One fourth-order theme is changed by this form's defining rooms at 1.5 times the population's share or more, by a third of them or more (the allocation's own columns): Collective representation (T4-006, Coordination without command), the theme of what the mark shows. 98 per cent of the defining rooms change it, 2.63 times the population.

**The changed cells.** Read against the 66 elements and arrows by the rule of D6a-01, the form changes 10 units. They follow the model's own course, from the call to the help between rooms.

- **The call.** P01, the room's calls for hands, is specified by link and add-step in 14 defining rooms (1.40 times the share). Its lines sit most often at T3-002, calling members in to place, and by fourth-order theme most often at Collective representation (T4-006, D02). The call returns within the work: a finished figure, a granted space or a member's report of a part done becomes the premise of the next call for hands.
- **The design.** P02, settling what the mark will show, is specified by add-step in 21 rooms (1.94) and by link in 19 (1.70). Its lines sit at T3-004, settling the mark's design (T4-006, D02), and T3-009, celebrating the finished work (T4-026 Completion ritual, D07). The design is settled for a time and reopened. Members make a provisional choice overnight, place rough lettering to hold a gap, follow a finished mark with the next addition, and turn outside help into claims to label the mark.
- **The template.** P04, templates and coordinates, is specified by add-step in 22 rooms (1.22), at T3-004 (T4-006, D02). The template is kept current: builders find mistaken blocks, retire outdated posts, announce changed locations and message the members who overwrite the design.
- **The arrow from the design to the template.** A11, P02 to P04 (a template settles a design), is restricted in 58 rooms (1.27), most often at T3-004 (T4-006, D02). In 40 of its 58 lines the arrow fails as stated: colours, sizes and spacing stayed open, a redesign was repaired away as damage, or a dispute was settled by pointing to an image already in circulation. In 16 more, the template settles the design only for the placers who recognise its current version.
- **Decided order.** C01 is restricted in 12 rooms (1.27), at T3-004 and T3-015, routing canvas posts into megathreads (T4-006, D02). Members divided outline and lettering, endorsed a template and assigned pixels or relay roles, without a formal office. The lines do not test how often rooms decided their order.
- **Keeping.** P05, upkeep, is restricted in 46 rooms (1.30), most often at T3-004 (T4-006, D02); the element sits in Maintenance (D03). The lines restrict P05's telling clause: informed members refused changes, or the record cannot tell ignorance from dissent. None of the 47 lines shows a change lost only for want of telling.
- **The neighbours.**
  - P07, sparing, is specified by link in 10 rooms (1.38), at T3-010, settling borders with neighbours (T4-015 Live and let live, D04). Restraint is linked to a reason the room states: an apology after a complaint, help or space received from the neighbour, or the room's own identity.
  - P08, border talk and deals, is specified by add-condition in 10 rooms (1.42), at T3-010 (T4-016 Negotiated order, D04). Agreements are kept apart from compliance: members report attacks after announced terms, and a truce needs a counterpart able to keep its terms.
  - P16, help between rooms, is specified by add-step in 12 rooms (1.34), at T3-010 (T4-017 Reciprocity, D04). Help is part of the room's own building: helpers alternate placements between the two works, and requests and offers are kept apart from delivered help.
  - A13, P05 to P16 (a room keeping its work under strain turns to other rooms for help), is restricted in 30 rooms (1.20), at T3-010 (T4-016, D04). Help does not follow a shortage of hands: donors help from completed or well-kept work, and help comes with construction and overlap repair.

**How the changes follow one another.** The form's rooms start and build as the model says. They leave R01, P03 and the arrows A01 to A03 untouched at the form level, and the marking and the record too (P11, P12, P13, A07 to A09). The changes gather where the model has the room settle its mark. The design is settled (P02), a template lays it out (P04), and the template does not end the design (A11). Keeping restores the known form (P05), and the keepers who undo a change may know of it and refuse it. Decided order (C01) appears where members divide the design's parts. A finished part is then the start of the next: the finished mark is followed by a new addition (P02, by link at T3-009) and by a new call for hands (P01). The model draws no arrow from the marking back to the design, and the form's lines show this return as description, not as a tested arrow. With the neighbours, help comes from the room's own work, not from its shortage (A13, P16), and sparing and terms come with reasons and limits (P07, P08). In short, the form's rooms settle what the mark shows for a time, reopen it after each finish, and keep it against keepers who may know of a change and refuse it.

## What the marks do to the form's claim

Each change carries two marks: one from the rule's thresholds (D6a-09) and one from the size-held test (D6a-10).
- **Marked "robust": 1.** P02, the design, keeps its relation at ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent. It survives size matching on both measures (1.88 on coded concepts, 2.39 on canvas-work concepts).
- **Size-dependent: 1.** P04, the template kept current, fails when matched on canvas-work concepts (1.18).
- **Marginal and not size-dependent: 8.** P01 keeps its relation at ratio 1.5 with the 10 per cent floor. A11, P05 and A13 keep it with the 15 per cent floor at ratio 1.2: many rooms hold them, but their lean over the population is small. C01, P07, P08 and P16 lean only at the rule's own thresholds.

So form C's claim rests on one sharp change: the design is settled for a time and reopened (P02). It is one of the 20 cells, in all six forms, that survive the stricter rule. The restrictions that carry the form's account are broad but shallow. The template that does not settle the design (A11) and the keepers who refuse a change they know of (P05) are held by 58 and 46 of the 96 defining rooms. The population restricts both too, and C's rooms do so only 1.27 and 1.30 times as often. The template kept current (P04) does not keep its lean once C's rooms are compared with rooms of the same canvas-work size. The neighbour cells are held by 10 to 12 rooms each. Form C is best read as the rooms where the design is changed most often and reopened after each finish. The template's failure and the keepers' refusal are the population's restrictions, held here a little more often.

**The held proposals (QD4-001) bear on this form.** The A11 proposal would withdraw the arrow form C restricts in 58 rooms, and the P05 proposal would bound the telling clause it restricts in 46. Both are stated in full in the head and in the form's account, section 6. Neither is part of the model told here.

**The restrict lines on arrows.** Of the 301 restrict lines on arrows held by C's defining rooms, 172 say the arrow fails here (the form's account, section 7; one reader's sort).
