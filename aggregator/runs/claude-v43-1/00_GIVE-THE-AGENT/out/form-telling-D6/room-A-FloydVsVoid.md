- **It fights a rival room as well as dealing with neighbours.** Its changes on the contest (A16, A17, P09) and the call that returns after a defeat (P01) are not form A's. Form A leaves the call, the contest and the move as the model states them.
- **It changes Collective efficacy and Territoriality** (A10, P32, P27), which the form does not.
- **Most of its changes restrict** (21 of 26), while form A's changed cells are mostly specifications.

It is the nearest room to the form's centre because it holds the form's plan, keeping and terms. Its own course is a contest lost, then a call renewed with allies, and then terms with each neighbour.
