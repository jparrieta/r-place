The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/nbaspurs stands for the third act of form F: building again at the new spot. It holds all three acts, the only room of the three that does. It is in the triple with the most selected changes: 21, against the form's defining median of 11, and 243 coded concepts. Members then built a small Spur, lost it four times, moved, bargained with neighbours, rejected scripts and kept the last Spur into the final snapshot. Beside the canvas, the sub ran its own April Fools joke and changed its voting buttons.

  - It links P27 (choosing the spot): an objection about an occupied site is answered with an agreement to move, which joins restraint to choosing a site.

## Where it departs from its form

- **It refused scripts.** Its scripts lines say the work was manual and that scripts were against the rules. Form F specifies scripts kept in step across accounts.
- **It had help from a rival's room.** The A1 telling records the edited post thanking another team's sub "for helping us and working together" (`633aov`). Help between rooms is form D's ground, and form F leaves it as stated.
- **It did not carry the work on.** Carrying on is absent, and its last work is the celebration of a Spur that survived.

Seen through the model, the room started, called, designed and built as the model says, and its contest with rivals ran in talk as the model says. Its own course was all of F: it bargained for space while it built, lost its spot and moved, and built again at each new spot until one Spur held to the close. Beside the work, its sub's own April Fools joke was argued and switched off.
