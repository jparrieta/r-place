## Its changes, along the model's course

  - The room restricts A02 (the start before the call) at T3-041, extending the room's work (T4-006 Collective representation). The starter's renewed invitation gathers hands around a proposal already made, but an earlier call means the start does not always come first.
  - It adds a step to P01 (the call) at T3-015, routing canvas posts into megathreads (T4-002 Boundary work): a project given its own thread draws members' attention before the host's line becomes the issue. This is form C's defining change. In the A1 telling a member asked for the work to have its own thread, and the answer was "Tein itse" (`dfosvi8`), I made it myself.
  - It restricts A12 (the bare answer to a call) at T3-004, settling the mark's design (T4-006): bare assent and bare refusal answer the same proposal, so a refusal does not always come with a reason.

- **It changes the host's frame and the record** (P20, P28, A08, P12, P24, K06), which form C leaves as stated.
