Written at D6a-12. The figure is `room-B-uofm.png` (and `.svg`). The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 12 selected changes (the D5 lines of form B; `_analysis/d6a12_facts_B.json`) and its A1 telling (`package/uofm/case.md`), cited below for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/uofm stands for B's scripts facet. It holds the scripts (Workarounds) and neither the chat nor the raids. Among the six such rooms in the nearer half of B's defining distances, and nearer B's centre than any other, it has the most selected changes: 12, against the form's median of 10. Its distance to B's centre is 0.855, 22nd nearest of 113, and it has 246 coded concepts. In the A1 telling the event becomes collective work with "Let's make a Michigan M! I started around (81,817) with a yellow pixel." (`62ml8z`). Members then built an M, enlarged it to GO BLUE, redesigned it as a BIG M and kept it to the close.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 12 changes, 4 add a condition, 3 restrict, 3 link and 2 add a step. They sit in Coordination without command 5, Maintenance 2, Order without law 1, Collective identity 2, Host framing 1 and Contention 1.

- **The design and the plan.**
  - The room restricts A11 (a template settles a design) at T3-091, keeping everyone on one template (T4-008 Standardization of output). A published template settles the work only as far as the keepers and the machines' targets are brought into line, since keepers who were not reached go on restoring the old form.
  - It changes P04 (templates) twice. Bringing a revision into line includes checking accounts and messaging friendly placers who keep the old design (an added step). Automation makes the design depend on updating the machines' targets as well as on telling the members (a link at T3-028, running scripts for the work).
  - It adds a step to P14 (scripts) at T3-028: updating the machines' targets includes asking for and supplying a template that can be updated remotely. This is form B's defining change, and the room's scripts facet. The A1 telling records the conflict over the plan: "Please follow the template" (`dfoft0k`) is answered that it is "too late" (`dfog5lv`). It records a request to tell casual members that they were keeping the old logo (`dfr4j1l`), and the template made remotely updateable.
- **Building and keeping.** It adds a condition to A03 (a built mark becomes a mark to keep): keeping overlaps building and can interrupt a celebration. It restricts P10 (the move): after the work was overrun, a proposal to move was dropped when the work recovered, so weighing a move is not moving.
- **The neighbours and the rival.**
  - It adds a condition to A05 (a settled border is a line members keep) at T3-149, agreeing truces with rival rooms (T4-016 Negotiated order): restraint depends on the other side's restraint, not on an agreement binding every placer. This is form B's defining change.
  - It restricts A18 (an attack is carried on in taunts) at T3-050, trading taunts with rivals (T4-021 Rivalry): attack and victory carry on talk about a rival the room already had. In the A1 telling a joint plan with a neighbouring school proposed "Together we could crush OSU and create peace in our time" (`62unef`).
- **The host's frame.** It adds a condition to A12 (the bare answer to a call) at T3-037, arguing the canvas's worth (T4-002 Boundary work): members join while judging the event poorly and finding it hard to stop.
- **The marking, the record and carrying on.**
  - It adds a condition to A08 (what is celebrated is kept on record): recording is planned during building.
  - It links P11 (celebration) and P13 (what the room keeps) at T3-031, playing the room's in-jokes (T4-004 Frame bridging). A misspelling in the damaged lettering becomes a way to show affection for the shared work, and the same joke gives the wording of a proposed keepsake. In the A1 telling members found "GO BLOE" and one recast it as "old French for 'BLUE'" (`638l52`).

## Form B's defining changes in this room

The room shows two of the form's seven changes. P14 is "robust" and survives size matching. A05 is marginal and size-dependent. The room carries sparing, border terms, stepping away and the call turned on a rival as the model states them (P07, P08, P32, A15). The chat (P23) is absent.

## Where it departs from its form

- **It holds one facet only.** The chat is absent, and the raids' units are carried as the model states them.
- **Its changes gather on the template and the machines' targets** (A11, P04, P14), and on keeping a plan in step with the placers. That is form A's ground as much as B's.
- **It changes the record and carrying on** (A08, P11, P13) through an in-joke made of the damage.

Seen through the model, the room started, called, built and kept as the model says, and fought its rival as the model says. Its own course was a design revised more than once. Each revision had to reach both the members and the machines before it held.
