**How a change is written here.** A change is an op on a unit of the model: an element or an arrow. The op is add-step, add-condition, link or restrict. The T3 and T4 given for each change are the most common placements of the form's lines for it.

They leave the design and the template (P02, P04, A11), building (P03), border talk (P08, A04) and the contest (P09, A15 to A18) untouched at the form level. The model draws no arrow from carrying on back to the call, and the form's lines show this return as description, not as a tested arrow. In short, the form's rooms keep their work by shared and checked watches, send help when their own work is safe, and begin carrying the work on before the close; help and carrying on are done by different rooms.

## What the marks do to the form's claim

Each change carries two marks: one from the rule's thresholds (D6a-09) and one from the size-held test (D6a-10).
- **Marked "robust": 6.** P01, P05, P14, P20, P13 and A09 keep their relation at ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent, and each survives size matching on both measures.
- **Size-dependent: none.** Form D is the only form all of whose changed cells survive size matching.
- **Marginal: 11.** A01, A06, P16, A13, K01 and P11 keep their relation with the 15 per cent floor at ratio 1.2. A02, P07, P19, A05 and A20 lean only at the rule's own thresholds.

So form D's claim is among the sharpest of the six. Six of the 20 cells that survive the stricter rule are D's; only form F has more, with 7. The sharp changes are keeping shared and checked (P05), carrying on begun before the close (P13, A09), the call returning from carrying on and loss (P01), the host's room for canvas talk (P20), and scripts offered and not shown run (P14). The help in the form's name is not sharp. Help under strain (A13) is held by 33 rooms, the most of any of D's cells, but its lean is 1.32 times the population; help on the helper's terms (P16) leans 1.42 times. So the form's "carrying on" rests on a sharp change and its "help" on broad, shallow ones, and the two are done by different rooms. Form D is best read as keeping shared and checked, with two further patterns held by different rooms: help sent from a safe room, and carrying on begun before the close.
