# r/prequelmemes: the model as instantiated in one room of form A

Written at D6a-11. The figure is `room-A-prequelmemes.png` (and `.svg`). The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 25 selected changes (the D5 lines of form A; `_analysis/d6a11_facts_A.json`) and its A1 telling (`package/prequelmemes/case.md`), cited below for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/prequelmemes is the 13th nearest of form A's 135 defining rooms to the form's centre (distance 0.777). It has 25 selected changes, the second most among the candidates, against the form's defining median of 10. It has 736 coded concepts. In the A1 telling the room's work begins at H+1.94 with an invitation to "write the story of darth plagueis the wise" (`62mu00`). Its members then wrote, kept and altered a long quotation for the rest of the event.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 25 changes, 17 restrict, 5 add a step and 3 add a condition. They sit in six dimensions: Coordination without command 10, Maintenance 1, Order without law 5, Host framing 1, Imprinting 6 and Contention 2.

- **The plan.**
  - The room adds a step to P04 (templates) at T3-014, moving planning into a chat (T4-010 Backstage decision-making). The standards are tied to progress reports and to routing into the chat, in the same public instructions. This is one of form A's defining changes.
  - It restricts A11 (a template settles a design): a font image answered criticism of the design, and its maker later changed the font.
  - It restricts P23 (the chat): invitations and returned decisions show the routing, not how the chat agreed.
  - It restricts P28 (a room formed around a mark): a venue made inside an existing community is not a new body.
- **Building.** It adds a step to P03 (building) at T3-021, watching and repairing the work: construction and repair overlap before the quotation is declared complete.
- **Keeping.**
  - It adds a condition to P05 (upkeep) at T3-192, griefing the work for fun (T4-020 Repertoire of contention). Repair follows the keeper's idea of the right form, and that idea can hold the spelling error being restored. That is form A's change.
  - It adds a step to P26 (jokes made of the work), at the same T3. Members alter the text for a joke and repair it, restoring their own change and becoming its keepers. The A1 telling records "power" changed to "boner" and "dark" to "dank".
  - It restricts P14 and A06 (scripts): a preservation script is offered and refused, and bots are debated, not shown placing. P14 is form A's change.
  - It restricts K06 (the site's faults): reports about timers and refreshing do not show a fault.
  - It restricts C01 (decided order): rules and credited jobs exist, and the population's count cannot be tested in one room. That is form A's change.
- **Loss and the move.**
  - It restricts A10: too few hands justified holding the current work and refusing to expand, not leaving the spot.
  - It changes P10 (the move) twice. Repeated undoing brings an unresolved choice between moving and holding on (restrict). Contraction can mean dropping a figure that asked too much while the quotation is kept (an added step).
- **The neighbours and rivals.**
  - It restricts P19 (hosting): a request for guest space inside the mark is recorded, but not acceptance.
  - It restricts C02 and K03 (the tie that names the rival; the quarrel carried in): naming a rival does not reconstruct an earlier dispute, and a group credited with helping is not treated as a rival.
  - It restricts K07 (members of both rooms): teasing over divided support does not show dual membership.
  - It restricts P33 (plans kept from rivals): watching a rival's public thread is not infiltration.
- **The host's frame.** It adds a condition to P25 (newcomers through the work) at T3-075, objecting to the canvas flood (T4-002 Boundary work). Newcomers are met with rejection or with the joke explained.
- **The marking, the record and carrying on.**
  - It restricts A07: pride came before the whole work was finished.
  - It changes P13 (what the room keeps) three times. Remaking in another medium begins while the canvas is live (an added step). Continuing is contested, since another member judges repeating the quotation not worthwhile (an added condition). The close is scoped to the original canvas, since a successor is made after it (restrict). In the A1 telling, at H+96.31, a post asks the room to rebuild the story on another canvas (`63f477`).
  - It restricts A09: affection for the drawing, not a saved record, moves members to remake it.

## Form A's defining changes in this room

The room shows four of the form's eleven changes: P04, P05, P14 and C01. P04, P05 and P14 are marginal and not size-dependent; C01 is size-dependent. It does not show the others. It carries sparing, border terms and the settled border as stated (P07, P08, A05). It has no line for help after a settlement (A14), which is absent. It restricts the chat rather than specifying it (P23). It carries the marking and the record as stated (P11, P12).

## Where it departs from its form

- **Its border work is the model's own.** Form A's terms and their reach do not show in its lines, and it is the farthest of the three rooms from the form's centre.
- **Its changes gather where form A's do not:**
  - the upkeep and alteration of a long text (P03, P05, P26);
  - the move held back (A10, P10);
  - carrying on after the close (P13, A09);
  - the elements of identity and rivalry (C02, K03, K07, P33).
- **It changes one unit of host framing (P25)**, which form A leaves as stated.

Seen through the model, the room called, built and kept as the model says. It kept the plan current and argued its scripts, as form A's rooms do. Its own work was a text its members kept, altered for jokes, defended to the close and tried to rebuild afterwards.
