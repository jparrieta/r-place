# r/theexpanse: the model as instantiated in one room of form A

Written at D6a-11. The figure is `room-A-theexpanse.png` (and `.svg`). The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 24 selected changes (the D5 lines of form A; `_analysis/d6a11_facts_A.json`) and its A1 telling (`package/theexpanse/case.md`), cited below for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/theexpanse is the 5th nearest of form A's 135 defining rooms to the form's centre (distance 0.737). It has 24 selected changes, the third most among the candidates, against the form's defining median of 10. It has 153 coded concepts. In the A1 telling the event becomes a project at H+7.23. Members then built a sequence of marks: the MCRN logo, the subreddit's name, the Roci, and the OPA and Tycho marks.

## What it carries as the model states it

Eighteen units are instantiated with no change:
- **The spine from the start to keeping:** the starter (R01), the call (P01), the start before the call (A02), building (P03) and building then keeping (A03).
- **The bare answer and celebration:** P15 and P11.
- **The neighbours and the room's ties:** sparing (P07), border terms (P08), help (P16) and coalitions (P17).
- **Other mediators:** working out who changed the work (P06), explaining the canvas (P24), jokes made of the work (P26) and the work turned to outsiders (P30).
- **Conditions:** K02, K04 and K05.

Thirteen units are absent, among them the host's arrow (A20) and who may commit the room (P21). Five units have no line: decided order (C01), scripts (P14), the host's line (P20), the envoy (R02) and the marking of a finished mark (A07). Six more carry change lines in the ledger view that are not among the selected changes (A09, A14, A15, A19, K01, P09). The figure draws them apart, and this telling does not count them.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 24 changes, 16 restrict, 4 add a condition, 3 add a step and 1 links. They sit in six dimensions: Critical mass 2, Coordination without command 5, Maintenance 1, Order without law 8, Collective identity 1 and Imprinting 7.

- **The call.** The room restricts A01 (calls bring builders): calls and work reports show a local sequence, not who answered. It restricts A12 (the bare answer): a brief offer answers an explanation.
- **The design and the plan.**
  - It adds a condition to P02 (settling what the mark will show) at T3-021, watching and repairing the work (T4-013 Repair work). The repairs and upkeep a redesign would need can defeat it, even where accuracy to the books favours revising.
  - It restricts A11 (a template settles a design): the visible standards do not show the conversation that settled the design.
  - It links P04 (templates) at T3-014, the chat: a member outside the chat depends on design information coming back.
  - It adds a step to P23 (the chat) at the same T3: returning decisions to open threads lets people without chat access follow design and alliance changes. This is one of form A's defining changes. In the A1 telling a central post gathers the official version, the current plan, the chat invitation and the formal alliances (`62vkes`). A reply says: "As long as the information filters down here I'll be able to keep tabs on alliances and design changes" (`dfpvkh4`).
- **Keeping.**
  - It adds a condition to P05 (upkeep): a member explains the room's success by hand through a good group, an early start and not being targeted. That is form A's change.
  - It restricts A06 (scripts under strain): repeated upkeep did not bring scripts. The A1 telling records the claim "Doing this all as a team, with no scripts or bots" (`dfs18xt`).
  - It restricts K06 (the site's faults).
- **The neighbours.**
  - It restricts A05 (a settled border is kept): restraint came from recognising others' work and from ethical objections, with no treaty before it. That is form A's change. In the A1 telling the room's first episode ends with the room yielding to the makers of an existing Diego rather than overwriting them.
  - It adds a step to P27 (the spot chosen before any loss) at T3-010, settling borders with neighbours: recognising an existing maker explains yielding the spot.
  - It adds a condition to P19 (hosting): permission to occupy guest space requires the requesting makers to keep their own work.
  - It restricts A04 (keeping brings border talk): an alarm meets a report of diplomacy already done.
  - It restricts A13 (short of hands, the room turns to other rooms): reported allied defence does not show a shortage bringing the help.
  - It restricts A16 and A17 (an attack call refused, or met with a truce). Its record holds an outside maker's request about ownership, and an offer to share that belongs to a mockup, not a truce.
  - It restricts P31 (whether covering is fair): members state different criteria for covering, and no settled ethic.
  - It restricts C02 (the tie that names the rival): an identity from fiction and playful antagonism do not name a rival room.
- **Loss.** It restricts A10 (too few hands, then the move): a doubt about capacity is answered with a report that the plan has started.
- **The marking, the record and carrying on.**
  - It adds a step to P12 (records) at T3-031, playing the room's in-jokes (T4-004 Frame bridging): a small memorial artwork remembers a destroyed neighbour. That is form A's change.
  - It restricts A08 (what is celebrated is kept on record): celebration and records overlap, with no line showing one bringing the other.
  - It restricts P22 (which version is kept): correcting an understanding of the design is not a dispute over copies.
  - It restricts P25 (newcomers through the work): interest and arrivals are reported, but not retention.
  - It adds a condition to P13 (what the room keeps): proposals for a next canvas are held back by concerns about upkeep and by members' different predictions about scripts. In the A1 telling, after the close, a post asks, "Can we all agree that if r/place comes back, this is what we build next?" (`63bjq5`).

## Form A's defining changes in this room

The room shows four of the form's eleven changes: P23, P05, A05 and P12. P05 and A05 are marginal and not size-dependent; P23 and P12 are size-dependent. It does not show the others:
- it links the template rather than adding a step to it (P04);
- it has no line on scripts or decided order (P14, C01);
- it carries sparing and border terms as stated (P07, P08);
- its line on help after a settlement is not selected (A14);
- it carries the marking as stated (P11).

## Where it departs from its form

- **It changes the model's border arrows that form A leaves as stated:** keeping before talk (A04), help from shortage (A13), and the attack call refused or answered with a truce (A16, A17).
- **It changes the design and the spot**, as forms C and F do: upkeep conditions a redesign (P02), and yielding a spot to an earlier maker (P27).
- **It changes Imprinting widely** (P12, P13, P22, P25, A08).

Seen through the model, the room called, built and kept as the model says. It carried its border terms as stated. It held to form A in three ways: decisions brought back from the chat to the thread, restraint without a treaty, and keeping on conditions. Its own course was a sequence of marks, each finished, guarded and followed by the next.
