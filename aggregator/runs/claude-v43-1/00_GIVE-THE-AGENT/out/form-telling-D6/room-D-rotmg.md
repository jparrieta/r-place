## Where it departs from its form

- **Its keeping runs through loss and moving** (A10, P10, and P01 and P04 at T3-022). Form D leaves the move as stated; it is the ground of form F, moving for the spot.
- **It carries the sub's play as stated** (K01). The A1 telling records the sub's anime makeover beside the canvas work and keeps the two apart. That fits D.1, the part of the form without the play.
- **It has no scripts.** Scripts and the arrow to them are absent, where form D's sharp change restricts them.

Seen through the model, the room started, called and built as the model says, and it kept its work on terms with its neighbours. Its own course was the form's in D.1's way: each loss led back to a call, and each new figure was kept by watches and bargains. Its help came from shared exposure, not from shortage. Before and after the close it carried the work on, to a pixel made into a game idea and to a replacement canvas where the work did not register.
