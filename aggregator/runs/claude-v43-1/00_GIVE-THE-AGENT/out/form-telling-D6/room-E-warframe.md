## Where it departs from its form

- **It has no contest.** The attack calls and every arrow from them are absent.
- **Its canvas work ran through one neighbour.** Eight of its changes sit at settling borders with neighbours: permission, reciprocal upkeep, help after the logo was legible and a denied authority. Help from a room whose own work is safe is form D's ground, and the terms are form A's.
- **It carries the call to building as stated** (A01), where form E restricts it. The form's account (section 8) reads r/warframe as a room where building came before one call, but the room's selected lines do not restrict the arrow.

Seen through the model, the room started, called, built and celebrated as the model says. Its sub's play was the larger part of its record: another game's theme borrowed for the day, a further theme bought by votes, styling repaired where it broke, and an end set by the clock. Its canvas course was a logo lost twice and then built inside a neighbour's lattice, kept by reciprocal upkeep, and written into an atlas after the close.
