Written at D6a-13. The figure is `room-C-bardmains.png` (and `.svg`). The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 16 selected changes (the D5 lines of form C; `_analysis/d6a13_facts_C.json`) and its A1 telling (`package/bardmains/case.md`), cited below for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/bardmains is the third of form C's 21 candidates. It has 16 selected changes, against the form's defining median of 10, and 85 coded concepts. Its distance to C's centre is 0.7325, 2nd nearest of 96. In the A1 telling the first proposal was a bare call: "Can bard mains work together to create a pixel art bard mask at (312,60)" (`62qaab`). Members then built a small mask, argued its colours and lettering, gathered the posts into a megathread, let a neighbour's beam pass through the mask, and found a red point in its eye at the close.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 16 changes, 8 restrict, 6 add a condition and 2 add a step. They sit in Critical mass 2, Coordination without command 3, Maintenance 3, Order without law 2, Host framing 3 and Imprinting 3.

- **The call.**
  - The room restricts A12 (the bare answer to a call) at T3-002, calling members in to place (T4-005 Mobilization): bare commitments come beside offers with reasons and acceptances with objections to the design.
  - It adds a step to P02 (settling the design) at T3-002: rough lettering can hold a gap before it is refined, because another picture might fill the space. This is form C's "robust" change. In the A1 telling members tried lettering above the face (`62xb2n`), then below it when "the one above isn't going to work" (`62yr3z`).
- **The design and the plan.**
  - It adds a condition to P02 at T3-004, settling the mark's design (T4-006 Collective representation): a colour patch already on the canvas and the cost of changing finished work limit which designs the room can still choose. In the A1 telling a helper asked about a darker orange (`dfpkygj`) and was told "Way too late to switch colors" (`dfpl1ha`), since a yellow blob was there when they started.
  - It restricts A11 (a template settles a design) at T3-004: plans circulated, and members kept their objections to the colours and left the choice to the placers at large. This is form C's defining change. In the A1 telling members disputed a white or a colourful background (`dfq6iax`) and whether the colourful version was more "bard-like" (`dfq5zq8`).
- **Keeping.**
  - It restricts P05 (upkeep) at T3-004: members repair, but the lines do not trace the disputed pixels to an agreed change or to a telling that failed. This is form C's defining change. In the A1 telling members were unsure whether yellow changes were "us" (`dfqi4f5`).
  - It restricts A13 (keeping under strain brings help) at T3-005, defending the work against attackers (T4-013 Repair work): outside help is seen, without an account of the room asking another room because its defenders ran short. This is form C's defining change. In the A1 telling a Kiwi said the beam should stop and helped "stop this laser malarkey" (`dfrdcqy`).
  - It adds a condition to P16 (help) at T3-005: a helper's stated affiliation does not show a mandate from the room or an exchange of help.
  - It restricts P33 (plans kept from rivals) at T3-005: a member's charge of spies shows concern that the room's thread is public, not that rivals entered it. The A1 telling keeps "spies" (`dfq1u1p`) as the members' word.
- **The neighbours.**
  - It restricts A05 (a settled border is a line members keep) at T3-010, settling borders with neighbours (T4-016 Negotiated order): restraint is seen, but not that it rests on a settled border, since a member waited for direction while the beam remained. In the A1 telling the moderator proposed letting the beam pass through holes in the mask (`dfqtzol`) and accepted its passage while asking to keep the mask "somewhat intact" (`dfqv8r0`). Members waited for direction so as not to "offend the kiwis" (`dfrlh1v`).
  - It adds a condition to K07 (members of both rooms) at T3-010: a member who says they belong to both shows their own position, not that they speak for either room.
- **The host's frame.**
  - It adds a step to P20 (the host's line) at T3-015, routing canvas posts into megathreads (T4-002 Boundary work): the public megathread runs beside a separate chat, so gathering the posts does not put every design decision in it. In the A1 telling the moderator wrote "I want to keep it all in one thread" (`631z6v`), and a later post was removed because "we currently have a megathread for r/place" (`dfqp2wp`).
  - It adds a condition to C01 (decided order) at T3-015: a moderator's authority over posts is not authority over placements.
  - It adds a condition to P23 (the chat) at T3-015: an invitation to the Discord shows the intent to coordinate there, not how the chat decides or who takes part.
- **The marking, the record and carrying on.**
  - It restricts A08 (what is celebrated is kept on record) at T3-009, celebrating the finished work (T4-026 Completion ritual): reports of the close and pride come together, without showing that celebration brought the record. ## Form C's defining changes in this room

The room shows four of the form's ten changes: the design (P02, "robust"), and the template that does not settle the design, upkeep and help under strain (A11, P05, A13, all marginal). It changes two more of the form's units by another op than the form's: it adds a condition to decided order (C01), which form C restricts, and to help (P16), which form C specifies by add-step. It carries the call (P01), the template (P04), sparing (P07) and border terms (P08) as the model states them.

## Where it departs from its form

- **It has no contest.** The attack calls and the arrows from them are absent. Its trouble with a neighbour was a beam that crossed the mask, met with passage and with help.
- **Its host drew the line** (P20, C01, P23): a moderator's megathread and a Discord channel. Form C leaves the host's line and the chat as stated.
- **It changes the record and carrying on** (A08, A09, P13), which form C leaves as stated.

Seen through the model, the room started, called, built and kept as the model says. Its own course was the form's at a small size. The design was chosen to fit few hands and the pixels already there, and it was argued and changed while it was built. It was kept by members who could not always tell their own changes from damage. A neighbour gave it two pixels, and it let a neighbour's beam pass through. At the close it kept its pride and let a successor go.
