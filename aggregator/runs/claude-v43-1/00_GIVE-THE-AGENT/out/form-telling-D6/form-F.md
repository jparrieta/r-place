# Form F, Moving for the spot: the form's telling

Written at D6a-16, on J's instruction (27 October, 06:41 to 06:58 Berlin, through the handler), with J's ruling of 12:25 on how the rooms are chosen. The form is form F of the D4a-08c cut, which does not change. It has 56 rooms, 34 of them defining, the fewest of the six forms, and its rooms have the shortest records. The figure is `form-F.png` (and `form-F.svg`). The counts come from `fractal-forms-D6.jsonl`, `form-cells-sensitivity-D6.tsv`, `form-cells-size-D6.tsv`, the allocation file `room-forms-D4a08c.jsonl`, the D5 lines of the form's defining rooms and the form's account (`room-form-F-account.md`); `_analysis/d6a11_form_telling.py` reads them. Local validation is not established: each room shows a pattern in its ruled lines, not in a validated account of the room.

**How a change is written here.** A change is an op on a unit of the model: an element or an arrow. The op is add-step, add-condition, link or restrict. The change is placed at the third-order theme (T3) where the room's own theme sits, and so at a fourth-order theme (T4) and a dimension. The T3 and T4 given for each change are the most common placements of the form's lines for it. In form F each change rests on 5 to 16 rooms.

## What defines the form against the global model

**The change signature.** Three columns are changed by this form's defining rooms at 1.5 times the population's share or more, by a third of them or more (the allocation's own columns):
- Territoriality (T4-014, Order without law), the theme of space and claims to it, changed by 32 of the 34 defining rooms (94 per cent), 5.36 times the population, the highest ratio of any form's signature;
- Mobilization (T4-005, Critical mass), the theme of calls, 15 rooms, 1.67 times;
- the link from Repair work to Negotiated order, lines that join keeping the mark to terms with neighbours, 14 rooms, 1.55 times.

The call and the terms are held by the same rooms more than chance gives (8 against 6.2), and every room that holds either holds territoriality (the form's account, section 14). F keeps no split below it.

**The changed cells.** Read against the 66 elements and arrows by the rule of D6a-01, the form changes 14 units. They follow the model's own course, and they gather on the three acts the form's name gives: the room asks for space before it builds, moves when the spot is lost, taken or claimed, and builds again at the new spot.

- **The call.**
  - A12, P01 to P15 (the bare answer to a call), is restricted in 10 defining rooms (1.27 times the share), at T3-002, calling members in to place (T4-005 Mobilization, D01). Answers to a call often ask where or how to help, or give a reason, so the bare one-line join is one answer among several.
- **Asking first.**
  - A04, P05 to P08 (keeping brings border talk), is restricted in 13 rooms (1.67), at T3-035, asking a host for space, and T3-010, settling borders with neighbours (T4-014 Territoriality, D04). Border talk comes before keeping: rooms ask neighbours for permission or access while they look for a spot or build, before any mark exists to keep.
  - P19, a guest's work under a host's rule, is restricted in 6 rooms (2.05), at T3-035 (T4-014, D04). Hosting shows only at the request stage: a room applies, or asks a larger mark for shelter, and no grant, protection or residence under the host's rules is shown.
  - P27, choosing the spot before any loss, is restricted in 5 rooms (2.28), at T3-022, relocating after losing the spot, and T3-020, choosing a canvas spot (T4-014, D04). The choice of a spot also comes after a loss, so the bound "before any loss" holds for one site decision, not for the room's whole history.
- **Building with scripts, and keeping early.**
  - P14, scripts and spare accounts, is specified by add-step in 6 rooms (1.26), at T3-028, running scripts for the work (T4-009 Workarounds, D02). Scripts and overlays are kept in step across accounts, and members switch automation off when they prefer to place by hand.
  - A06, P05 to P14 (upkeep under strain brings scripts), is restricted in 9 rooms (1.55), at T3-028 (T4-009, D02). Scripts came with the building, not after upkeep strained.
  - P05, upkeep, is specified by add-step in 7 rooms (1.49), at T3-021, watching and repairing the work (T4-013 Repair work, D03). Keeping begins before the mark is finished: a border reserves the space, and defenders are planned before a stable work exists.
- **Moving for the spot.**
  - A10, K02 to P10 (a room with too few hands moves), is restricted in 16 rooms (1.40), most often at T3-020 (T4-014, D04). The move answers the spot more than the hands: a taken spot or a lack of permitted space moves the room, and where too few hands are named, the move is proposed or hesitant.
- **Building again.**
  - P03, building, is specified by link in 12 rooms (1.56), at T3-022 and T3-020 (T4-014; the element sits in Coordination without command, D02). Building returns after a move: a new spot or a new plan is followed by a new start and, in some rooms, a smaller finished mark.
  - P04, templates and coordinates, is specified by add-step and link in 9 rooms (1.41), at T3-039, yielding space when asked, and T3-020 (T4-014; D02). The template moves with the spot: a new site gets an anchor coordinate, and scarce hands make an exact first placement matter.
  - P01, the room's calls for hands, is specified by link in 6 rooms (1.70), at T3-038, building and reporting the mark, and T3-022 (T4-014; D01). The call returns after a lost, taken or cleared spot.
- **The marking and the record.**
  - A07, P03 to P11 (a finished mark is marked), is restricted in 7 rooms (1.85), at T3-009, T3-043 and T3-073 (T4-026 Completion ritual and T4-027 Collective memory, D07). The marking answers a partial or traced work: rooms celebrate visible traces, mark a partial result at the close, and a success announcement meets objections and a new move.
  - P12, records, is specified by link in 5 rooms (3.16), at T3-016, T3-029 and T3-020 (T4-014; D07). The record links to the live talk and to failure: a question about the location draws a report of the work's fate, and unfinished building at the close leads straight to a tally and mourning, with no celebration between.
- **The sub's play.** K01, the April Fools frame, is specified by add-step in 9 rooms (1.43), at T3-001, staging April Fools fake news, and T3-032, objecting to April Fools posts (T4-001 Keying, D06). Members argue the play's limits more than they run it: they contest a painful prank, demand consequences for its maker and test what is real.

**How the changes follow one another.** The form's rooms start and settle a design as the model says. They leave R01, P02, border terms (P08), sparing (P07), the contest (P09, A15 to A18), help (P16, A13, A14) and carrying on (P13, A09) untouched at the form level. They also leave the move itself (P10) as stated: what F changes is what brings the move. The course runs as follows. Before the room builds, it looks for a spot and asks the neighbour or the host for it (A04, P19). The record holds the request more than the answer. A call goes out, and the answers ask where to help (A12). Scripts come with the building (A06, P14), and keeping begins before the mark is finished (P05). When the spot is taken, claimed or lost, the room moves for the spot's reasons, not its hands (A10). It chooses a spot again after the loss (P27), and it calls, lays out a template and builds again at the new spot (P01, P04, P03). In some rooms the cycle repeats. The marking answers a partial work (A07), and the record runs from the fate of the work, often its failure (P12). The model draws no arrow from the move back to building, and the form's lines show this return as description, not as a tested arrow. In short, the form's rooms spend their work on the spot: asking for it, losing it, moving and building again.

## What the marks do to the form's claim

Each change carries two marks: one from the rule's thresholds (D6a-09) and one from the size-held test (D6a-10).
- **Marked "robust": 7.** P01, P03, P04, A06, P19, A04 and A07 keep their relation at ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent, and each survives size matching. Form F has the most such cells of the six.
- **Size-dependent: 1.** A12 fails when matched on coded concepts (1.16).
- **Marginal and not size-dependent: 6.** P27 and P12 keep their relation at ratio 1.5 with the 10 per cent floor. P14, P05, A10 and K01 keep it with the 15 per cent floor at ratio 1.2.

So form F's claim is sharp but small. Seven of the 20 cells that survive the stricter rule are F's, and they are the cells of its course: asking first (A04, P19), building again (P03, P04, P01), scripts with the building (A06) and the marking of a partial work (A07). They survive when F's short-recorded rooms are compared with rooms of the same size, so they are not an artefact of the short records. But each rests on 6 to 13 rooms, and the form's whole claim rests on 34. The move itself is marginal: A10 is held by 16 rooms, the most of any F cell, but leans only 1.40 times the population. So the form's name rests less on the move than on what surrounds it, the asking before and the building after. Form F is best read as the rooms whose record is the search for space: asking first, moving for the spot, and building again.

**The restrict lines on arrows.** Of the 114 restrict lines on arrows held by F's defining rooms, 72 say the arrow fails here, 63 per cent, the highest share of the six forms (the form's account, section 7; one reader's sort).

**The held proposals (QD4-001).** Form F's cells do not touch them at the form level: F specifies keeping (P05) by when it begins, not by its telling clause, and leaves A11 untouched. Both are stated in full in the head.
