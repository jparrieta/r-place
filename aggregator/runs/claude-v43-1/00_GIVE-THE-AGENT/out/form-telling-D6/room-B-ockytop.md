The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** By the rule logged before any room was read, r/ockytop stands for B's raids facet. It holds the raids (Repertoire of contention) and neither the chat nor the scripts. It is the only room that holds the raids alone within the nearer half of B's defining distances and lies nearer B's centre than any other form's. In the A1 telling the event begins with "We need to create a Power T" (`62mork`). Members then built and expanded a Power T, fought a Viking ship and planned raids, and kept and rebuilt the T to the close.

## Its changes, along the model's course

Of the 9 changes, 4 add a condition, 3 restrict, 1 adds a step and 1 links. They sit in Coordination without command 3, Order without law 1, Host framing 1, Imprinting 2 and Contention 2.

- **The design and the plan.**
  - The room restricts A11 (a template settles a design) at T3-091, keeping everyone on one template (T4-008 Standardization of output). Published instructions to stop settle a design only if placers adopt them; overwrites go on, and members find it hard to get agreement.
  - It adds a step to P04 (templates): members find friendly overwriters and message them privately, to bring their placing into line with the published redesign.
  - It restricts P05 (upkeep) at the same T3: telling the keepers is not shown to be enough, since some dislike the change and cannot be persuaded.

  This is the model's telling clause met by refusal. In the A1 telling defenders repeatedly undid redesigns. The episode ended by conceding to "actually do the original plan... a real power T only" (`dfq400e`), a T a reply called "smaller and easier to defend" (`dfq4w9d`).
- **Keeping.** It links P05 at T3-009, celebrating the finished work (T4-026 Completion ritual): a reply turns a celebration straight back into keeping.
- **The neighbours and the raids.**
  - It adds a condition to A05 (a settled border is a line members keep) at T3-010, settling borders with neighbours (T4-016 Negotiated order): a proposed peace line must be accepted before it can hold. This is form B's defining change. In the A1 telling a delegate of the Viking ship offered to "end hostilities" if the room stopped at TN (`dfpnmu3`), and the reply was edited from a compromise into "WAR WAR WAR" (`dfpo6tq`).
  - It restricts P09 (attack calls) at T3-006, calling to deface rivals' work (T4-020 Repertoire of contention). Checkerboarding counts as an attack when it is aimed at another work; around the room's own mark it builds. This is the room's raids facet. In the A1 telling a raid on a rival school's mark was scheduled and then called off in favour of "fixing our area and then defending" (`dfr342d`).
  - It adds a condition to P19 (hosting) at the same T3: a proposal of hosted space is shown at the request stage only.
- **The record and carrying on.**
  - It adds a condition to P12 (records) at T3-037, arguing the canvas's worth (T4-002 Boundary work). The wish to preserve the work is held by the members who speak, not by all; one never expected the letters to last.
  - It adds a condition to P13 (what the room keeps) at T3-054, carrying work to new canvases (T4-028 Transition): an invitation to restart on another canvas shows the intent, but access and usability limit who joins. In the A1 telling the restart post says "No bots allowed here!" (`63bo3o`), and a reply says it did not work on mobile (`dftgb0z`).

- **Its changes gather on the plan refused by its keepers** (A11, P04, P05). That is form C's ground (the design reopened, keepers refusing a change they know of) as much as B's.
- **It changes the record and carrying on** (P12, P13), which B leaves as stated.
