# Form E, The sub's play, run as work: the form's telling

Written at D6a-15, on J's instruction (27 October, 06:41 to 06:58 Berlin, through the handler). The form is form E of the D4a-08c cut, which does not change. It has 98 rooms, 84 of them defining. The figure is `form-E.png` (and `form-E.svg`). The counts come from `fractal-forms-D6.jsonl`, `form-cells-sensitivity-D6.tsv`, `form-cells-size-D6.tsv`, the allocation file `room-forms-D4a08c.jsonl`, the D5 lines of the form's defining rooms and the form's account (`room-form-E-account.md`); `_analysis/d6a11_form_telling.py` reads them. Local validation is not established: each room shows a pattern in its ruled lines, not in a validated account of the room.

**How a change is written here.** A change is an op on a unit of the model: an element or an arrow. The op is add-step, add-condition, link or restrict. The change is placed at the third-order theme (T3) where the room's own theme sits, and so at a fourth-order theme (T4) and a dimension. The T3 and T4 given for each change are the most common placements of the form's lines for it.

**How the changes follow one another.** The form's rooms start, build, keep and talk with their neighbours as the model says. They leave R01, P01, P03, keeping (P05), border talk (P08), the template (P04, A11) and the arrows around keeping and the neighbours (A03 to A06, A13, A14, A19) untouched at the form level. Beside all of it runs the sub's own April Fools play, organised like canvas work (K01). The canvas work enters the threads as calls and as reports of the mark's state. So the record holds the call and the report but not the hands between them (A01). It holds the start and the call in one post (A02), and the attack talk inside the recruiting (A15). The design is fitted to the host and the means (P02). Scripts help on conditions (P14), and the chat returns claims, not deliberation (P23). A loss ends one part of the work, not the room's place (A10), and restraint answers harm or overlap (A16). The marking does not wait for a finished mark (A07), and the record does not wait for the marking (A08). Carrying on is offered after the record, and is not shown taken up (A09, P13). In short, the form's rooms run their sub's play as work, and their canvas work shows in the record as calls, reports, celebrations and records, with the work between them thinner.

## What the marks do to the form's claim

Each change carries two marks: one from the rule's thresholds (D6a-09) and one from the size-held test (D6a-10).
- **Marked "robust": 4.** K01, A01, A07 and A15 keep their relation at ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent, and each survives size matching.
- **Size-dependent: 3.** A02 fails when matched on canvas-work concepts (1.12). A10 (1.16) and A09 (0.96) fail when matched on coded concepts.
- **Marginal and not size-dependent: 6.** P02 and P13 keep their relation at ratio 1.5 with the 10 per cent floor. A16 and A08 keep it with the 15 per cent floor at ratio 1.2. P14 and P23 lean only at the rule's own thresholds.

So form E's claim rests on the sub's play, run as work (K01). It is held by 56 of the 84 defining rooms at 3.61 times the share, the highest lean of the 72 changed cells in all six forms. Matched on size, it keeps 5.0 times the share on coded concepts and 8.5 times on canvas-work concepts. The form's other sharp changes are restrictions on arrows that say what the record cannot show: the call and the building apart (A01), the attack talk inside the recruiting (A15), and the marking before a finished mark (A07). They describe what these rooms' records hold, the play and the canvas work as calls and reports, more than a different way of doing the canvas work. The size-dependent cells (A02, A10, A09) are restrictions that rooms of the same size make as often. Form E is best read as the rooms whose record is the sub's play, run as work, with the canvas work thinner beside it. The form's account names the limit: the form's lean is on the same key that the branch F01 was cut on, so the two restate each other in part.

**The restrict lines on arrows.** Of the 304 restrict lines on arrows held by E's defining rooms, the arrow fails here in 130 and the record cannot show it in 113 (37 per cent), more than in any of forms A to D (the form's account, section 7; one reader's sort).

**The held proposals (QD4-001).** Form E's cells do not touch them at the form level: it leaves keeping (P05) and the template's arrow (A11) untouched. Both are stated in full in the head.
