# Form A, Border terms and the announced plan: the form's telling

Written at D6a-11, on J's instruction (27 October, 06:41 to 06:58 Berlin, through the handler). The form is form A of the D4a-08c cut, which does not change. It has 166 rooms, 135 of them defining. The figure is `form-A.png` (and `form-A.svg`). The counts come from `fractal-forms-D6.jsonl`, `form-cells-sensitivity-D6.tsv`, `form-cells-size-D6.tsv`, the allocation file `room-forms-D4a08c.jsonl` and the D5 lines of the form's defining rooms; `_analysis/d6a11_form_telling.py` reads them. Local validation is not established: each room shows a pattern in its ruled lines, not in a validated account of the room.

**How a change is written here.** A change is an op on a unit of the model: an element (P, K, C, R) or an arrow (A). The op is add-step, add-condition, link or restrict. The change is placed at the third-order theme (T3) where the room's own theme sits, and so at a fourth-order theme (T4) and a dimension. A change on sparing (P07, under Order without law) can therefore sit at a T3 of repair work. The T3 and T4 given for each change below are the most common placements of the form's lines for it.

## What defines the form against the global model

**The change signature.** Three fourth-order themes are changed by this form's defining rooms at 1.5 times the population's share or more, by a third of them or more (the allocation's own columns):
- Negotiated order (T4-016, Order without law), changed by 87 per cent, 1.75 times the population;
- Completion ritual (T4-026, Imprinting), 51 per cent, 1.58 times;
- Standardization of output (T4-008, Coordination without command), 37 per cent, 2.06 times.

**The changed cells.** Read against the 66 elements and arrows by the rule of D6a-01, the form changes 11 units. They follow the model's own course, from the plan to the record.
- **The plan.** P04, templates and coordinates, is specified by add-step in 32 defining rooms (1.26 times the share), at T3-070 sharing coordinates, templates and how-to (T4-008 Standardization of output, D02). Members keep the template in step with what is placed: they correct references against the canvas and replace stale plans.
- **The chat.** P23 is specified by add-step in 16 rooms (1.22), at T3-014 moving planning into a chat (T4-010 Backstage decision-making, D02). Expired invitations are replaced, and decisions are brought back to members outside the chat.
- **Machine hands.** P14 is restricted in 17 rooms (1.21), most often at T3-027 arguing whether scripts are fair (T4-009 Workarounds, D02). Scripts are offered, debated and refused, and the record shows the offer more than any placing.
- **Decided order.** C01 is restricted in 17 rooms (1.28), placed at T3-010 settling borders and T3-014, the chat. Rooms divide the work, name representatives and assign defenders, but the record does not show the division kept.
- **Keeping.** P05 is specified by add-condition and link in 37 rooms (1.43), at T3-009 celebrating the finished work and T3-005 defending the work (T4-013 Repair work, D03). Keeping takes conditions from the neighbours: protecting a neighbour can mean undoing the room's own overextension. A finished mark is followed by renewed defence.
- **The neighbours.** P08, border talk and deals, is specified by add-step, link and add-condition in 21 rooms (1.28), at T3-010 settling borders with neighbours (T4-016 Negotiated order, D04). Members prepare a deal inside the room, check the counterpart and cede background pixels before picture pixels. P07, sparing, is specified by add-condition in 16 rooms (1.20), mostly at T3-005 defending the work. Sparing rests on overlapping membership, valued art or fear of retaliation.
- **The arrows from the border.** A05, P08 to P07 (a settled border is a line members keep), is restricted in 39 rooms (1.34), at T3-010. Restraint often comes before any settlement, and a treaty holds for the members who learn and accept it. A14, P08 to P16 (a settled border opens a channel for help), is restricted in 15 rooms (1.72), at T3-010. Help is part of the bargain more than its result.
- **The marking and the record.** P11, celebration, is specified by add-step in 21 rooms (1.47), at T3-043 crediting who made the work (T4-026 Completion ritual, D07). Credit is disputed and corrected while the work goes on. P12, records, is specified by add-step in 16 rooms (1.30), at T3-073 reporting how the work fared (T4-027 Collective memory, D07). The record holds mourning, memorials and imagined versions.

**How the changes follow one another.** The form's rooms run the model's spine as the model states it. They start, call and build, and the form leaves R01, P01, P03 and their arrows untouched. Its changes come in two places on the spine. The first is the plan kept current while building (P04, with P23 and P14 beside it). The second is the meeting with neighbours, where keeping (P05) brings border talk (P08). The model's arrow from keeping to border talk (A04) is not changed at the form level. The form's account found that rooms here often talk with neighbours before they have a mark to keep, which A04's bound already allows (`room-form-A-account.md`, section 7). From the border, the form changes both arrows that leave it: the terms bind the placers they reach (A05), and help is offered as part of the terms (A14). Then the marking and the record (P11, P12) come with the credit argued and the work's fate reported. In short, the form's rooms make terms while they build, keep a plan in step with the canvas, and find that both bind only the placers they reach.

## What the marks do to the form's claim

Each change carries two marks: one from the rule's thresholds (D6a-09) and one from the size-held test (D6a-10).
- **Kept at the stricter thresholds:** none. All 11 cells lean only at the rule's own thresholds (ratio 1.2, floor 10 per cent). At ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent form A keeps none, and at ratio 1.5 with the 10 per cent floor it keeps 1.
- **Size-dependent:** 4. C01 (decided order) and P23 (the chat) fail when matched on canvas-work concepts, P12 (the record) on record size, and P07 (sparing) on both.
- **Marginal and not size-dependent:** 7. These are P04, P05, P08, P11, P14, A05 and A14.

So form A's claim is the weakest of the six. Its rooms lean, at the rule's thresholds, toward the terms, the plan kept current, keeping linked to the neighbours, and the marking. That lean holds with record size held fixed, but no single change of it is sharp. The chat, decided order, sparing and the record do not keep their lean once A's rooms are compared with rooms of the same size. The form's account called it "a pole of the continuum and a lean of the population, not one shared change", with a stability of about 0.21 on matched subsamples. The marks agree. Form A is best read as where the negotiated order and the plan are most often changed, not as a type of room set apart from the others.
