# r/gamindustri: the model as instantiated in one room of form D (the good old room)

Written at D6a-17. The figure is `room-D-gamindustri.png` (and `.svg`), drawn as `README.md` describes. The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 20 selected changes (the D5 lines of form D) and its A1 telling (`package/gamindustri/case.md`), cited for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room is the good old one.** The good-old rule takes the rooms that hold every column of the form's change signature and lie nearer the form's centre than any other form's. For D that means a room that holds keeping (Repair work), carrying on (Transition) and help (Reciprocity) together. In most of D's rooms help and carrying on are held by different rooms, at chance; eight rooms hold all three. r/gamindustri shows six of D's cells, as many as any of the eight, and it is the nearer of the two that show six: its distance to D's centre is 0.7939, 2nd nearest of 96. It has 20 selected changes, against the form's defining median of 11, and 181 coded concepts. It lies in D.2.1, the play carried on, and the form's account names it among D.2's nearest rooms and quotes it in its opening narrative. What makes it the plain case is that it does all three of D's things at once: it keeps its work against attack, trades help with its neighbours, and carries the work on after the close.

In the A1 telling the canvas work begins with a post asking the sub to "immortalize itself in that canvas" (`62w1ms`). Members then built a small logo, defended it against a neighbour, built a second logo with a host's approval, fought in the game's own war talk, and after the close began again on another canvas. Beside the canvas, the sub ran its own April Fools jokes.

## Why it is the plain case

Form D is keeping, with help or carrying on (`../form-telling-D6/form-D.md`), and its help and its carrying on are usually done by different rooms. r/gamindustri does both beside its keeping. It defends a logo with exact instructions, trades help with neighbours and waits for a host's approval, and when the canvas closes it carries the work to the next one. It also runs the sub's own play beside the work, as D.2's rooms do. Its keeping itself is in the ledger view more than in its selected lines, which is the one way it falls short of the whole form.

Seen through the model, the room started, called, designed and celebrated as the model says. Its own course was D's in full: a logo kept against a neighbour, help traded and a host's approval sought, and the work begun again after the close.
