Written at D6a-17. The figure is `room-E-paladins.png` (and `.svg`), drawn as `README.md` describes. The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 25 selected changes (the D5 lines of form E) and its A1 telling (`package/paladins/case.md`), cited for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** The round-2 rule chose the three rooms of form E that together show the most of E's thirteen changed cells, ranking rooms by the cells each shows. r/paladins shows nine, the most of any of E's 84 defining rooms. Three are "robust": the sub's play run as work (K01), the call and the building apart (A01) and the attack talk inside the recruiting (A15). Five are marginal: the design fitted to the means (P02), the chat returning claims (P23), restraint answering harm (A16), carrying on offered more than taken up (P13) and the record not waiting for the celebration (A08). One is size-dependent: a loss ending one part of the work (A10). It has 25 selected changes, against the form's defining median of 11, and 317 coded concepts. Its distance to E's centre is 0.8601, 25th nearest of 84. In the A1 telling the first canvas post said "Your kings demand a spot in /r/place" (`62stxf`). Members then built a crystal logo by agreement with a large neighbour, defended it, moved it by agreement with another, and after the close argued whether the canvas had a point. Beside the canvas ran the sub's April Fools play and an Easter egg hunt.
