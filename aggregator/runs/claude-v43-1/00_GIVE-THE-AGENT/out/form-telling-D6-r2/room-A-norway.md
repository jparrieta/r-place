# r/norway: the model as instantiated in one room of form A (round 2)

Written at D6a-17. The figure is `room-A-norway.png` (and `.svg`), drawn as `README.md` describes. The sources are the room's ops on every unit of the model (`_views/rooms-x-elements.tsv`), its 13 selected changes (the D5 lines of form A) and its A1 telling (`package/norway/case.md`), cited for moments. The room's account is unresolved, as every room's is; what follows rests on its ruled lines.

**Why this room.** The round-2 rule chose the three rooms of form A that together show the most of A's eleven changed cells. r/norway supplies the three that the other two rooms lack: sparing (P07), border terms (P08) and help after a settlement (A14). It has 13 selected changes, against the form's defining median of 10, and 111 coded concepts. It is on A's edge: its distance to A's centre is 1.1052, 124th nearest of 135, and it lies nearer form B's centre than A's. The rule did not limit distance, and this room is the price of covering the form's cells. The A1 telling's first canvas post called "let's destroy the swedes on /r/place!" (`62nvdi`). Members then fixed a Norwegian flag, and visitors from many rooms came to the sub to ask for space or for an end to attacks.

## Where it departs from its form

- **It is a host more than a builder.** Its lines are about visitors arriving with appeals and being sent to the room that built the flag. That is the host's line (P20), which form A leaves as stated.
- **It lies nearer form B's centre than A's**, and its record holds none of the spine's arrows.
- **Its terms are made by others' appeals**, answered with pacts, refusals and a reported vote, more than made by the room while it builds.

Seen through the model, the room called, designed and built a flag as the model says, and its contest with neighbours ran in talk. Its own course was the border seen from the receiving side: visitors asked, the sub answered, and the work and the terms were settled somewhere else.
