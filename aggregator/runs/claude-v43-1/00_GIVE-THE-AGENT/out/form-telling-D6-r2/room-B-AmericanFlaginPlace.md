- **It holds two facets, not one.** It is the chat's room in this round, and it holds the scripts as well; the raids are absent.
- **Its design was argued as a question of ownership** (P02, P16 at Symbolic boundaries): who could sign the flag, and whether help made a helper an owner. That is form C's ground, the design, as much as B's.
