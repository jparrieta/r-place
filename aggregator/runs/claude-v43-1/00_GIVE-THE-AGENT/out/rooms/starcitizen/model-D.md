## D02 — Coordination without command

Representation was proposed rather than given: members offered a logo, pixel alternatives and a concrete suggestion, and later someone proposed a Carrack or Connie instead of the Hornet; the response said the Hornet was already agreed and space was scarce. [P02] [c0157] [c0161] [c0162] [c0333] [c0354]

Coordinates, a selected icon and requests for a grid made a proposal followable, but [A11] needs qualification: choosing a template did not itself settle implementation, and the Hornet required corrected coordinates, color instructions and refreshing the display before placing.
