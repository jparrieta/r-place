## D01 Critical mass

Calling people in is initially a small, uncertain accomplishment: “A call for action over at r/place” draws “Huh?”, followed by an explanation naming Reddit's “new thingie” [P01] [c0001] [c0002] [c0003]. Recruiting therefore includes teaching the venue, timer and mobile access, not just finding willing hands; in the later mobile exchange, the explanation of the giant canvas receives “Gonna check it out when I get home” [P01] [c0332] [c0333] [c0335] [c0336]. This is an added instructional step within mobilisation, also represented by the ladder's sensemaking node [P01] [T4-003] [c0002] [c0003].

Calls receive both placement reports and bare commitments: the presence-building post receives “I've contributed my pixel,” while another request receives “I'm down to help!” [P03] [P15] [A01] [c0160] [c0161] [c0103] [c0105]. The call–bare-answer pairing is locally visible, including “I gotchu,” but the stronger claim that every reader owes an answer, or that silent readers accepted by placing, cannot be tested from these written responses [A12] [P15] [c0570] [c0571]. A competing response directs effort to preserving Bender, so acceptance of one project is not room-wide assent to every call [P01] [c0036] [c0040] [c0041].

Looking is also an activity in its own right: someone invites a music-festival-like tour, and another describes looking and forgetting what life is; neither turn reports a placement commitment, which does not establish whether the speakers placed elsewhere [new: touring the canvas without reporting a commitment to build] [T4-003] [t07] [c0090] [c0030].

Participants explicitly doubt their “entpower,” and one predicts that too few contributors mean the pineapple cannot be restored [K02] [c0393] [c0408]. Yet “Cant give up the fight” and a proposal for a “modest 420 where we had it” show that scarcity can shrink the design while preserving the site, rather than end defence [K02] [P10] [A10] [c0393] [c0408].

## D02 Coordination without command

Proposals give recruitment a concrete object: a pineapple construction, a pot leaf, and a 420 at the symbolically matching coordinate [R01] [P02] [P01] [c0034] [c0036] [c0108]. Starting and calling are often combined in the same post, so a separate start-causes-calls transition is not demonstrable here [absent A02] [c0034] [c0036].

Builders share coordinates, ask for a form to follow, circulate leaf and pineapple templates, and report both progress and loss [P02] [P03] [P04] [c0165] [c0173] [c0289] [c0303]. Templates do not by themselves settle design: someone trying to change the pineapple says everyone keeps “fixing” it, and later helpers preserve an obsolete 420 while a larger template is being executed [P04] [A11] [c0351] [c0539]. Restricting the settlement mechanism to a template recognised as current explains these failures better than treating posting a template as agreement [P04] [A11] [c0496] [c0498] [c0539].

## D03 Maintenance

Repeated repairs, a thicker border and enlarging 420 are distinct ways of maintaining recognisability rather than simply expanding territory [P05] [c0295] [c0491] [c0522]. Overnight demolition and later rebuilding are reported, but their order alone does not make one particular report the cause of another person's call [P03] [P05] [c0306] [c0307].

Attribution is indispensable here, not an optional gloss on defence: a participant retrospectively says “no one was fighting us at all on this,” only ents making mistakes [P06] [c0528] [c0529] [c0530].
