# r/mexico: an adapted room model

## D01 Critical mass

The flag effort recruited by showing something already begun: “Vamos a hacer la bandera de México… comencé en la coordenada 270, 360” simultaneously offered a work, called for hands and located it [R01][P01][P04][c0037]. A respondent switched from another post to support this effort, and another reported contributing a pixel; these are visible answers to the call, not a measure of all recruited placers [A01][c0081][c0045]. Starting and calling are bundled in the same opening rather than two demonstrably separate stages, so A02 needs that local restriction [A02][c0037].

Smallness was relative to particular neighbours: one member calculated that twenty-seven columns could survive beside a blue line, while another proposed expanding downward because they could not beat TF2 above [K02][c0044][c0201]. Neither judgment establishes that the room subsequently abandoned its spot because its hands were too few; that causal version of the shortage-to-move arrow is not testable here [absent A10][c0044][c0201][c0324].
