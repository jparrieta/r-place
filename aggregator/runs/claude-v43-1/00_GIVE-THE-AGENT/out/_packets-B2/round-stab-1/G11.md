# Family G11  scripts, bots and extra accounts

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-027  arguing whether scripts are fair

gloss: Members argue whether scripts and bots are fair: cheating or common practice, against the spirit of the canvas or the only way to hold ground.
must_show: Argument about scripts or bots as such: called cheating, embarrassing or beneath the room; defended as what every large community does; judged to spoil the canvas; bot rumours, and claims that a defence was scripts and not people.
must_not_show: Running or sharing a script with no argument over its fairness (T3-028); placing beyond the limit with extra accounts (T3-088).
decide_when_both: Running and arguing: T3-028 primary when the scripts are run, T3-027 added when the argument has concepts of its own. A bot rumour, or saying a defence was scripts rather than people, goes here when fairness is in question.
varies: From one jab at a rival's cheating to long threads on whether automation is moral; pride in hand work; rumours that a rival is botting.
is_not: A member sharing a script and how to run it (T3-028).
vs T3-028: Are scripts run (T3-028), or is their fairness argued (T3-027)?
vs T3-088: Are extra accounts used (T3-088), or the fairness of automation argued (T3-027)?
vs T3-179: Is the fairness of scripts argued (T3-027), or a political ideology argued from the canvas (T3-179)?
vs T3-037: Is the canvas's worth argued (T3-037), or the fairness of its scripts (T3-027)?

## T3-028  running scripts for the work

gloss: Members write, share and run scripts or bots that place or keep the room's work.
must_show: Scripts or bots made, shared, installed or run for the room's work: links and instructions, the script repairing the mark, errors and updates.
must_not_show: Arguing whether scripts are fair (T3-027); extra accounts with no script (T3-088); stale scripts switched off (T3-089).
decide_when_both: Running and arguing: see T3-027. Stale scripts switched off in concepts of their own add T3-089; extra accounts add T3-088.
varies: From one member offering a way to draw automatically to sub-wide bot armies with install guides; scripts that repair a mark overnight.
is_not: A template posted for placing by hand (T3-070).
vs T3-027: Are scripts run (T3-028), or is their fairness argued (T3-027)?
vs T3-088: Is placing automated (T3-028), or multiplied by extra accounts (T3-088)?
vs T3-089: Are scripts run for the plan (T3-028), or stopped because they fight it (T3-089)?
vs T3-108: Is the bot the room's own (T3-028), or one target pooled with other rooms (T3-108)?
vs T3-070: Is a template shared for placing by hand (T3-070), or a script that places (T3-028)?
vs T3-021: Is it kept by hand (T3-021), or by a script (T3-028)?
vs T3-120: Do members run the script on their own accounts (T3-028), or lend accounts for someone else's bot (T3-120)?
vs T3-176: Is the script run for the room's own work (T3-028), or slipped to a rival against it (T3-176)?

## T3-088  placing beyond the pixel limit

gloss: Members place more than the one-pixel limit allows: extra accounts, alts, throwaway accounts, glitches used to place more.
must_show: Placing beyond the limit by any means: alts and throwaway accounts made or used, many accounts per member, glitches exploited to place more.
must_not_show: Scripts as such (T3-028) unless they run on extra accounts; coping with the limit while building (T3-074); accounts lent to another's bot (T3-120).
decide_when_both: A script run on extra accounts: T3-028 for the script, T3-088 added when the accounts have concepts of their own. Glitches: using one to place beyond the limit is T3-088, coping with one that stops or delays placing is T3-074, explaining one is T3-016.
varies: From a reminder that alt accounts work too to plans for multi-account scripts; members insisting the work is manual despite many accounts.
is_not: Working within the cooldown while building (T3-074).
vs T3-028: Is placing automated (T3-028), or multiplied by extra accounts or a glitch (T3-088)?
vs T3-074: Is the limit worked within (T3-074), or broken (T3-088)?
vs T3-120: Does a member place more with their own accounts (T3-088), or lend them to another's bot (T3-120)?
vs T3-110: Are extra placements made (T3-088), or a tile offered as payment (T3-110)?
vs T3-027: Are extra accounts used (T3-088), or the fairness of automation argued (T3-027)?

## T3-089  switching off stale scripts

gloss: Members find the room's own scripts fighting its plan (old versions, wrong templates) and switch them off or update them.
must_show: The room's scripts or bots working against its own current plan: old versions overwriting, bots fighting each other or a neighbour's agreed space, calls to stop or update them.
must_not_show: Running scripts with no conflict (T3-028); a script edited to spare a neighbour as an agreed change (T3-168).
decide_when_both: Running and switching off in one theme: T3-028 primary, T3-089 added when the switching off has concepts of its own.
varies: From one member stopping an old script that overwrote a flag to rooms finding their bots fighting each other after a template changed.
is_not: A script revised to leave a neighbour's border alone (T3-168).
vs T3-028: Are scripts run for the plan (T3-028), or stopped because they fight it (T3-089)?
vs T3-168: Is a script stopped because it fights the room's own plan (T3-089), or edited to spare a neighbour (T3-168)?
vs T3-091: Are hand placers held to the current plan (T3-091), or scripts brought into line with it (T3-089)?

## T3-108  sharing one bot target

gloss: Rooms pool one bot map or target so that a shared script keeps several rooms' works.
must_show: A bot's target or map shared across rooms: other rooms' logos added to it, a target shared with another nation's room, scripts merged so another room can use them.
must_not_show: A room's own script (T3-028); members lending accounts to another room's bot (T3-120).
decide_when_both: A shared target agreed in a deal: T3-151 when the trade of help leads, T3-108 when the pooled target does.
varies: From kindred Linux rooms on one bitmap to Italy's script target shared with the English.
is_not: A member lending their accounts to another room's bot (T3-120).
vs T3-028: Is the bot's target the room's alone (T3-028), or pooled with other rooms (T3-108)?
vs T3-120: Do rooms share one target (T3-108), or do members lend their accounts to another's bot (T3-120)?
vs T3-151: Is help traded between rooms (T3-151), or one bot target pooled (T3-108)?

## T3-120  lending accounts to a bot

gloss: Members lend their accounts or machines to a bot, another room's or their own room's.
must_show: Accounts lent to a bot: members asked to run another's script or join a botnet, accounts handed over for someone else to run, manpower traded for a script.
must_not_show: Members running their own room's script on their own accounts (T3-028); a target shared among rooms (T3-108).
decide_when_both: Lending accounts in a trade between rooms: T3-151 when the trade leads, T3-120 added when the lending has concepts of its own.
varies: From one member offering to set up lent accounts to a whole room trading its manpower for another's flag script.
is_not: A room's members running its script on their own machines (T3-028).
vs T3-028: Do members run the script on their own accounts (T3-028), or lend accounts for someone else's bot (T3-120)?
vs T3-108: Do rooms share one target (T3-108), or do members lend accounts (T3-120)?
vs T3-088: Does a member place more with their own accounts (T3-088), or lend them to a bot (T3-120)?

## T3-168  editing scripts to spare neighbours

gloss: Members change the room's script so it spares a neighbour: a border left untouched, overlapping pixels removed, a shared image.
must_show: A script or bot edited, or asked to be edited, to leave a neighbour's work alone or keep an agreed border.
must_not_show: Turning off stale scripts that fight the room's own plan (T3-089); restraint kept by hand with no script (T3-024).
decide_when_both: A border agreed and written into the script: T3-010 for the agreement, T3-168 added when the script change has concepts of its own.
varies: From one pixel of a neighbour's border left alone to rooms checking that their scripts share one image.
is_not: Stopping an outdated script that overwrites the room's own work (T3-089).
vs T3-089: Is a script stopped because it fights the room's own plan (T3-089), or edited to spare a neighbour (T3-168)?
vs T3-024: Is the restraint written into a script (T3-168), or kept by hand (T3-024)?
vs T3-010: Is the border agreed (T3-010), or the script changed to keep it (T3-168)?

## T3-176  planting disguised scripts on rivals

gloss: Members plant a script on a rival that secretly works against it: the rival's own script modified to cover its logo, a psyop to sow distrust.
must_show: A script given or passed to a rival that harms it while seeming to help, and the telling of it.
must_not_show: Calls to deface a rival by hand (T3-006); running the room's own scripts (T3-028).
decide_when_both: A disguised script used in an attack the room called for: T3-006 for the call, T3-176 for the planted script.
varies: From a rival's logo script modified to paint over it to a psyop that sowed distrust of all scripts.
is_not: Calling members to cover a rival's logo by hand (T3-006).
vs T3-006: Is the rival's work defaced by members placing (T3-006), or by a script planted on the rival (T3-176)?
vs T3-028: Is the script run for the room's own work (T3-028), or slipped to a rival against it (T3-176)?
vs T3-132: Is the rival watched from inside (T3-132), or harmed from inside by a script (T3-176)?
