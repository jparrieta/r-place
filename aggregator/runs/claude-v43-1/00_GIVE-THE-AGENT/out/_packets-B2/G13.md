# Family G13  holding back from others' work

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-024  refusing to cover others' work

gloss: Members keep the room's work off others' art: going around it, sparing it, sizing the work so it does not cover a neighbour.
must_show: Restraint toward others' work kept by the room's own choice: going around a neighbour's design, refusing to overwrite it, sizing or placing the work to spare it, respect for others' effort stated.
must_not_show: Moving because a neighbour asked (T3-039); reining in the room's members who are already attacking (T3-076); restraint written into a script (T3-168); an agreed border (T3-010).
decide_when_both: Restraint inside a border deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-024 added when the restraint has concepts of its own. Sparing others' work by the room's own choice is T3-024; agreeing lines with a neighbour is T3-010.
varies: From going around someone's chequered tiles to a room's rule of peace toward its neighbours' mascots.
is_not: A room moving its work after a neighbour asks for the space (T3-039).
vs T3-010: Is a line or space agreed with a neighbour (T3-010), or others' work spared by the room's own choice (T3-024)?
vs T3-039: Does the room yield when asked (T3-039), or keep off others' work unasked (T3-024)?
vs T3-076: Is an attack under way stopped (T3-076), or others' work spared before any attack (T3-024)?
vs T3-046: Is the fairness of covering argued (T3-046), or covering simply refused (T3-024)?
vs T3-168: Is the restraint kept by hand (T3-024), or written into a script (T3-168)?

## T3-039  yielding space when asked

gloss: The room gives ground when a neighbour asks: moving, shifting or shrinking its work, giving a spot back.
must_show: A neighbour's request for space and the room yielding: moving the work over, shifting it, shrinking it, letting the neighbour take the spot back, forfeiting an attempt.
must_not_show: Refusing to yield (T3-123); moving after the work was lost to an attack (T3-022); restraint with no request (T3-024).
decide_when_both: Yielding inside a border deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-039 added when the yielding has concepts of its own.
varies: From a sign moved a few pixels left to a whole attempt forfeited to another room.
is_not: Moving the work after an attack destroyed it (T3-022).
vs T3-123: Does the room yield the space (T3-039), or refuse (T3-123)?
vs T3-022: Does the room move because a neighbour asked (T3-039), or because its work was lost (T3-022)?
vs T3-024: Does the room yield to a request (T3-039), or spare others' work unasked (T3-024)?
vs T3-012: Does the room give up its own space (T3-039), or grant a neighbour room on its work (T3-012)?

## T3-040  apologizing to neighbours for damage

gloss: Members apologise to a neighbouring room for damage the room or its members did to its work, often offering to help repair it.
must_show: An apology to another room for damage to its work by the room or people from it: 'sorry about that', apologies made in the other room's thread, offers to help redraw or rebuild.
must_not_show: Owning a mistake inside the room's own or an ally's work (T3-170); mourning art the room covered with no apology to its owners (T3-183).
decide_when_both: Apology inside a deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-040 added when the apology has concepts of its own.
varies: From a one-line 'I'm so sorry' to offers to redraw the character nearby; apologies made on behalf of a nation's members.
is_not: A member admitting a misclick in the room's own work (T3-170).
vs T3-170: Is damage to a neighbour's work apologised for (T3-040), or a friendly mistake inside the room's own or allied work owned (T3-170)?
vs T3-183: Is the apology made to the neighbour (T3-040), or the covered art mourned among members (T3-183)?
vs T3-076: Is the damage apologised for (T3-040), or the attackers stopped (T3-076)?

## T3-046  arguing whether covering is fair

gloss: Members argue whether covering others' work is fair: an open canvas where anything goes, or a wrong done to others' effort.
must_show: Argument over covering others' work as such: 'it's an open canvas', 'that's the whole point', 'they put in passion, respect it', whether a neighbour may use the room's background.
must_not_show: Refusing to cover with no argument (T3-024); pleading to have one's own work spared (T3-139); arguing who had the spot first (T3-045).
decide_when_both: An argument that ends in restraint: T3-046 when the argument leads, T3-024 when the restraint does. Stopping the room's own attack is T3-076; arguing whether such covering is fair is T3-046.
varies: From a Void member calling erasure the point of the experiment to members weighing whether a neighbour may use their blank background.
is_not: A member pleading that a rival spare their room's logo (T3-139).
vs T3-024: Is covering refused (T3-024), or its fairness argued (T3-046)?
vs T3-076: Is the room's attack stopped (T3-076), or the fairness of covering argued (T3-046)?
vs T3-139: Does the speaker plead for their own work (T3-139), or argue whether covering is fair (T3-046)?
vs T3-045: Is who came first argued (T3-045), or whether covering is fair at all (T3-046)?

## T3-076  reining in the room's attackers

gloss: Members tell their own room's attackers to stop: no more attacks, leave the neighbours alone, stop vandalising.
must_show: The room's own members told to stop attacking or vandalising others' work, or an attack called off after an objection; rules against new wars.
must_not_show: Restraint before any attack (T3-024); pleading with another room's attackers (T3-139); excluding members as a sanction (T3-121); members acting against such a call (T3-102).
decide_when_both: An attack called for and then reined in: T3-006 primary when the attacking leads, T3-076 when the reining in does (rule 0 pairs them both ways).
varies: From one 'stop messing with other schools' to moderators' rules against new wars; attacks withdrawn after an objection.
is_not: A member asking another room's raiders to stop (T3-139).
vs T3-024: Is an attack under way stopped (T3-076), or others' work spared before any attack (T3-024)?
vs T3-139: Are the room's own attackers stopped (T3-076), or another room's attackers asked to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-102: Is the room's call to hold back made (T3-076), or defied (T3-102)?
vs T3-188: Does the room rein in its own attackers (T3-076), or say the attackers are not its own (T3-188)?
vs T3-046: Is the attack stopped (T3-076), or the fairness of covering argued (T3-046)?
vs T3-195: Does the room stop its own attackers (T3-076), or hold another room to an agreement (T3-195)?

## T3-102  defying the call for restraint

gloss: A member goes on attacking or breaks a treaty after the room told them to hold back.
must_show: A member refusing the room's restraint: attacking after being told no, calling to break the room's treaty, refusing to stop drawing over a neighbour.
must_not_show: The room's call to hold back itself (T3-076); attacks the room calls for (T3-006).
decide_when_both: A call to hold back and its defiance in one theme: the side with more concepts is primary, the other under rule 0.
varies: From 'Nah I'm going to hit the Bills' to a call to break a treaty.
is_not: A room calling for an attack on a rival (T3-006).
vs T3-076: Is the call to hold back made (T3-076), or defied (T3-102)?
vs T3-006: Is the attack called for by the room (T3-006), or pressed by a member against the room's word (T3-102)?
vs T3-195: Is a room held to its agreement (T3-195), or does a member break it (T3-102)?

## T3-123  refusing to give up space

gloss: Members refuse to give up the room's space when a neighbour asks or presses: 'this is our space', 'we were here first', 'it stays'.
must_show: A refusal to move or yield ground to a neighbour: claims of first arrival, of a prior agreement or of work done; promises to defend the spot against the one asking.
must_not_show: Yielding (T3-039); arguing who had the spot first with no refusal (T3-045); fighting off an attack with no request to yield (T3-005).
decide_when_both: A refusal inside a border deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-123 added when the refusal has concepts of its own.
varies: From a polite 'we'd rather stay' to promises to defend the space against a named neighbour.
is_not: A room moving after a neighbour asks (T3-039).
vs T3-039: Does the room yield the space (T3-039), or refuse (T3-123)?
vs T3-045: Is who came first argued (T3-045), or is the space refused on any ground (T3-123)?
vs T3-005: Is an attack fought off (T3-005), or a request to yield refused (T3-123)?
vs T3-124: Is fighting set against moving (T3-124), or is moving refused outright (T3-123)?

## T3-183  mourning art the room covered

gloss: Members mourn or regret art that their own room's work covered.
must_show: Grief or regret among members for another's art the room's own work displaced: 'RIP Blonde', a memorial post for a neighbour the room's expansion left no room for, members torn between the two works.
must_not_show: Apologising to the neighbour (T3-040); mourning the room's own lost work (T3-029).
decide_when_both: Mourning and apology together: T3-040 when the apology is addressed to the neighbour, T3-183 when the mourning stays among members.
varies: From a one-line 'RIP' to a member who worked on both the flag and the tree it covered.
is_not: Members mourning their own room's erased logo (T3-029).
vs T3-029: Is the room's own lost work mourned (T3-029), or others' art the room covered (T3-183)?
vs T3-040: Is the regret addressed to the neighbour as an apology (T3-040), or voiced among members (T3-183)?
