# Family G15  pacts and envoys between rooms

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-030  disputing how an alliance went

gloss: Members dispute an alliance's conduct or worth, often after the close: who kept their word, betrayal, never again.
must_show: Argument over an alliance's or deal's conduct or worth: broken word alleged, betrayal warned of, collaboration renounced.
must_not_show: Holding a room to its agreement so the work can go on (T3-195); arguing whether cooperation is surrender (T3-193); blaming an attacker for a loss (T3-058).
decide_when_both: A breach named mid-work: T3-195; the alliance judged as a whole: T3-030.
varies: From 'that's the last time we collaborate with them' to warnings that a colour faction will betray its partner again.
is_not: Reminding a neighbour mid-work of the border agreed (T3-195).
vs T3-195: Is the agreement invoked so the work goes on (T3-195), or the alliance's conduct or worth disputed (T3-030)?
vs T3-193: Is cooperation as such called surrender (T3-193), or one alliance's conduct disputed (T3-030)?
vs T3-058: Is a rival blamed for a loss (T3-058), or an ally's conduct disputed (T3-030)?
