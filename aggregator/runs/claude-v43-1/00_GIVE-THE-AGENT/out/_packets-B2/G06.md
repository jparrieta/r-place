# Family G06  strain, doubt and giving up

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-036  judging the canvas too full

gloss: Members judge that the canvas is too full, or too far gone, for the room to join.
must_show: Statements that there is no space left, or that it is too late, to put the room's work on the canvas: 'no space for us now', 'the chart is too full', the canvas closed before the room began.
must_not_show: Searching for a spot (T3-020); doubting the room's strength (T3-136); asking a host room for space (T3-035).
decide_when_both: Searching for a spot while judging there is none: T3-020 when the search leads, T3-036 when the judgement does.
varies: From a sad 'no space for us now' to a proposal to try a second canvas instead.
is_not: A room giving up a spot it held under attack (T3-059, T3-181).
vs T3-020: Is a spot looked for (T3-020), or is the canvas judged to have none (T3-036)?
vs T3-136: Is the canvas too full (T3-036), or the room too weak (T3-136)?
vs T3-035: Is space asked of a host room (T3-035), or is the canvas judged full (T3-036)?

## T3-059  urging others to give up

gloss: Members tell the room, or its defenders, to give the work up: it is lost, over, not worth the fight.
must_show: Calls on others in the room to stop, let the work go or accept the loss: 'it's over, just give up', 'let it go', 'burn it', 'a lost cause'.
must_not_show: A member quitting the effort themselves (T3-181); mourning the loss (T3-029); weighing whether to fight or move (T3-124).
decide_when_both: Urging and refusing in one theme: the side with more concepts is primary; refusing to yield the space to a neighbour is T3-123.
varies: From a gentle 'we made a good effort, let's have a beer' to scorn for a flag's last defenders.
is_not: Members mourning a lost work after it is gone (T3-029).
vs T3-181: Are others told to give up (T3-059), or does the speaker quit (T3-181)?
vs T3-123: Is giving up urged (T3-059), or refused (T3-123)?
vs T3-029: Is the loss mourned (T3-029), or is giving up urged (T3-059)?

## T3-074  working around the timer

gloss: Members work around the one-pixel cooldown, too few hands and placing troubles while they build.
must_show: The pixel limit, the cooldown, too few placers or placing troubles worked around while building: what the limit lets the room do, designs scaled to the hands at hand, broken timers, mobile trouble, glitches that stop or delay placing.
must_not_show: Doubting that the room can manage a work at all (T3-136); placing beyond the limit by extra accounts or any other means (T3-088); explaining what r/place is (T3-016).
decide_when_both: Doubting whether the room can manage a work at all, even during the work, goes to T3-136; working around the timer and too few hands while building goes to T3-074. Glitches: explaining how the canvas or a glitch works is T3-016; using a glitch to place beyond the limit is T3-088; coping with a glitch that stops or delays placing is T3-074.
varies: From one member's broken timer to a room scaling its panda to the few who place; finals, sleep and phones fitted around the five minutes.
is_not: A member saying the room has too few people to make the logo at all (T3-136).
vs T3-136: Is the room's capacity for the work doubted (T3-136), or are the timer and few hands worked around while building (T3-074)?
vs T3-088: Is the limit worked within (T3-074), or broken by extra accounts or a glitch to place more (T3-088)?
vs T3-016: Is the cooldown explained as how r/place works (T3-016), or worked around for the room's build (T3-074)?

## T3-098  questioning the room's canvas presence

gloss: Members ask whether and how the room shows on the canvas: why it is absent, whether a work counts as the room's, how it compares with other rooms.
must_show: Questions about the room's presence: why the room has no work on the canvas, whether a picture counts as the room's, comparisons with other subs' marks, whether a duplicate or a small mark represents the room well.
must_not_show: A mark proposed (T3-033); the canvas judged worthless (T3-037); the room's strength doubted (T3-136).
decide_when_both: A question that ends in a proposal: T3-033 when the proposal leads, T3-098 added when the questioning has concepts of its own.
varies: From 'why don't we have a quaggan?' to arguing whether a game's pictures count as the sub's participation.
is_not: A member saying r/place is a waste of time (T3-037).
vs T3-033: Is a mark proposed (T3-033), or the room's presence or absence questioned (T3-098)?
vs T3-037: Is the canvas itself judged (T3-037), or the room's showing on it (T3-098)?
vs T3-085: Is the room's presence questioned (T3-098), or the room weighed against the nations on the canvas (T3-085)?

## T3-124  weighing fighting against moving

gloss: Members weigh, after a loss or under pressure, whether to fight for the spot, rebuild it there, or move elsewhere.
must_show: A choice put between fighting and moving, or between rebuilding in place and relocating: 'restore it where it was or move?', 'not this time, we fight back', a move proposed against holding on.
must_not_show: Moving after an attack as a thing done (T3-022); refusing a neighbour's request to yield (T3-123); urging the room to give up (T3-059).
decide_when_both: The weighing is T3-124 and the move made after it T3-022; when both have concepts of their own, rule 0.
varies: From a quick 'move or restore?' to long arguments over raiding a neighbour or holding ground.
is_not: Relocating the work once it has been decided (T3-022).
vs T3-022: Is the move weighed (T3-124), or made (T3-022)?
vs T3-123: Is moving one option weighed (T3-124), or refused outright to a neighbour (T3-123)?
vs T3-059: Is the room's next step weighed (T3-124), or giving up urged (T3-059)?
vs T3-005: Is the attack fought off (T3-005), or is fighting one option weighed against another (T3-124)?

## T3-136  doubting the room's strength

gloss: Members doubt that the room has the people or strength for a work: too few, too small, outnumbered, the work impossible.
must_show: Doubt that the room can make or hold a work at all: too few people, a small sub against a large one, estimates of the hands a work needs, a design judged impossible; doubt during the work counts.
must_not_show: Working around the timer and few hands while building (T3-074); urging the room to give up (T3-059).
decide_when_both: Doubting whether the room can manage a work at all, even mid-work, goes to T3-136; working around the timer and too few hands while building goes to T3-074.
varies: From a hopeful 'now all we need is enough people' to a small sub conceding it cannot fight a larger one.
is_not: A member explaining how few pixels the timer allows while the room builds (T3-074).
vs T3-074: Is the room's capacity for the work doubted (T3-136), or are the timer and few hands worked around (T3-074)?
vs T3-059: Is the room's strength doubted (T3-136), or giving up urged (T3-059)?
vs T3-036: Is the room too weak (T3-136), or the canvas too full (T3-036)?
vs T3-049: Is the room too few or too weak (T3-136), or too scattered (T3-049)?

## T3-181  quitting the effort under strain

gloss: Members quit the canvas work themselves: tired, stressed, beaten by scripts or rivals, or at odds with the room.
must_show: A member or members stopping their own part in the work: 'I give up', 'I'm done', 'they got tired of it', leaving over scripts or over a split in the room.
must_not_show: Telling others to give up (T3-059); mourning the lost work (T3-029); a faction splitting over doctrine (T3-190).
decide_when_both: Quitting and mourning in one theme: the larger part primary, the other under rule 0.
varies: From an embarrassed admission of giving up hours ago to workers quitting because it was too stressful; leaving because scripts took the fun out.
is_not: A defender telling others that the work is a lost cause (T3-059).
vs T3-059: Does the speaker quit (T3-181), or tell others to give up (T3-059)?
vs T3-029: Is the loss mourned (T3-029), or the effort abandoned (T3-181)?
vs T3-190: Does a faction split over doctrine (T3-190), or does a member leave the effort (T3-181)?
