# Family G10  finding who changed the work

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-058  blaming attackers after the close

gloss: After the canvas closes, members blame the room that destroyed or attacked their work, and keep the grievance alive.
must_show: Blame laid after the close (hour 73 on) on a named room or group for damage to the work: accounts of who destroyed it, curses, grievances kept up.
must_not_show: Tracing an attacker while placement goes on (T3-087); blaming moderators or admins (T3-118); mourning with no blame (T3-029).
decide_when_both: Blame and mourning after the close: T3-029 when the loss leads, T3-058 when the blame does.
varies: From a curse on a nation's sub kept alive as a meme to careful accounts of who ruined what in the last hour.
is_not: Members working out during placement who is changing their pixels (T3-087).
vs T3-087: Is the culprit sought during placement (T3-087), or blamed after the close (T3-058)?
vs T3-029: Is the lost work mourned (T3-029), or a room blamed for it (T3-058)?
vs T3-118: Is an attacking room blamed (T3-058), or the moderators or admins (T3-118)?
vs T3-050: Are taunts traded with a rival as sport (T3-050), or blame laid for a loss (T3-058)?

## T3-087  tracing who changed the work

gloss: Members work out who changed their work or who sent a call: checking placers, naming rooms, reading signs of bots or scripts.
must_show: Finding out who is altering the work or who is behind a call: asking who keeps changing it, checking placers' posting histories, naming the room or bot responsible, members saying the damage is not the room's own.
must_not_show: Fighting the attack (T3-005); blame laid after the close (T3-058); an organised room setting its plan apart from placers it does not control (T3-188).
decide_when_both: Tracing with defence: T3-005 primary when the fight leads, T3-087 added when the tracing has concepts of its own. Members doubting that a damage was the room's own are tracing it (T3-087); an organised room telling others its plan does not cover such placers is T3-188.
varies: From 'who keeps putting green in our logo?' to reading usernames and posting histories; bots and scripts suspected; who sent a call traced.
is_not: An organised room telling a neighbour that placers in its name are not under its control (T3-188).
vs T3-005: Is the attack fought (T3-005), or its source traced (T3-087)?
vs T3-058: Is the culprit sought during placement (T3-087), or blamed after the close (T3-058)?
vs T3-188: Do members work out who did it (T3-087), or does an organised room tell others that such placers are not under its control (T3-188)?
vs T3-170: Is the culprit sought (T3-087), or found to be a friend or one's own mistake (T3-170)?
vs T3-132: Is the source traced from the pixels and what others say (T3-087), or learned from inside a rival's chat (T3-132)?

## T3-118  blaming moderators for a loss

gloss: Members blame the sub's moderators, or the site's admins, for a loss: their prank, their removals, help they denied, a rigged game.
must_show: Blame for the room's loss or failure laid on moderators or admins: their April Fools change, their removals, denied requests for help, the canvas said to be rigged.
must_not_show: Blame laid on an attacking room (T3-058); objections to a removal with no loss in view (T3-063, T3-185).
decide_when_both: A loss blamed on both a room and the moderators: the target with more concepts decides.
varies: From a joke that a defeat will be the moderators' fault to suspicions that admins added touches to the canvas.
is_not: Contesting a single removal under the sub's rules (T3-063).
vs T3-058: Is an attacking room blamed (T3-058), or the moderators or admins (T3-118)?
vs T3-063: Is a removal contested (T3-063), or a loss laid at the moderators' door (T3-118)?
vs T3-032: Is the moderators' joke objected to as a joke (T3-032), or blamed for a loss (T3-118)?

## T3-121  excluding the room's own vandals

gloss: The room sanctions its own members who damage the work or the room by exclusion: bans from the chat or sub, kicking, shunning, calling them traitors.
must_show: Exclusion used as a sanction against the room's own members: bans from the Discord or the sub, kicking, shunning a 'traitor', members reported so moderators throw them out, such a ban challenged or reversed.
must_not_show: Messaging placers to stop (T3-008); posts removed under the sub's rules (T3-063); bigotry shut down in chat (T3-182).
decide_when_both: A ban for bigoted talk: T3-182 when the bigotry leads, T3-121 when the exclusion does.
varies: From shunning a single traitor to a three-day cooling-off; a ban from the room's Discord challenged and reversed.
is_not: A moderator removing a post that breaks the sub's rules (T3-063).
vs T3-008: Is the placer asked to stop (T3-008), or excluded (T3-121)?
vs T3-063: Is a post removed under the sub's rules (T3-063), or a member excluded as a sanction (T3-121)?
vs T3-182: Is bigotry in chat shut down (T3-182), or a member sanctioned by exclusion for damage to the work or the room (T3-121)?
vs T3-076: Is the room's attack reined in by words (T3-076), or a member excluded (T3-121)?

## T3-126  disputing who speaks for rooms

gloss: Members dispute who may speak or decide for a room toward others: false claims to be its moderator or representative, denials of being one, outsiders told not to decide for its work, no one yet able to speak for the placers.
must_show: Contest or statement about who represents a room toward others: a claimed representative or moderator challenged; someone denying they speak for the room; outsiders told they cannot decide for the room's work; members saying no group yet exists to speak for the placers.
must_not_show: Disputes over the room's own chat's authority inside the room (T3-114); an organised room disowning placers it cannot control (T3-188).
decide_when_both: Separating the organised plan from placers it does not control is T3-188; who may speak for the room is T3-126.
varies: From a false moderator claim exposed to a random member denying they are the official mediator; a room telling other Discords not to decide for its art; a pattern's repeaters with no one to speak for them.
is_not: A room telling a neighbour that rogue placers act against its plan (T3-188).
vs T3-114: Is the room's own chat's power inside the room disputed (T3-114), or who speaks for the room to others (T3-126)?
vs T3-188: Is the right to speak for the room disputed (T3-126), or placers outside the plan disowned (T3-188)?
vs T3-194: Does a member act as the room's envoy (T3-194), or is the right to do so disputed or denied (T3-126)?
vs T3-101: Is someone put in charge of the work (T3-101), or is it disputed who speaks for the room outward (T3-126)?

## T3-170  mistaking friendly work for vandalism

gloss: Members find that what looked like vandalism was friendly work or their own mistake, and own or forgive it.
must_show: Damage that turns out friendly: an ally's or neighbour's work taken for vandalism and then recognised; members' own misclicks or misplaced pixels owned and forgiven; help that went wrong.
must_not_show: Tracing a hostile attacker (T3-087); apologising to a neighbour for damage the room did to their work (T3-040); damage done for fun (T3-192).
decide_when_both: Tracing that ends in finding a friend: T3-087 when the search leads, T3-170 when the recognition and the owning do.
varies: From a community's lettering undone as vandalism and then recognised to a newcomer's accidental pixel apologised for.
is_not: Apologising to a neighbouring room for covering its art (T3-040).
vs T3-087: Is the culprit sought (T3-087), or found to be a friend or one's own mistake (T3-170)?
vs T3-040: Is the friendly mistake inside the room's own or allied work (T3-170), or damage the room did to a neighbour's work (T3-040)?
vs T3-192: Was the damage a mistake (T3-170), or done for fun (T3-192)?

## T3-188  disowning placers outside the plan

gloss: An organised room tells others that placers acting in its name or against its plan are not under its control: rogues, the hivemind, individuals outside its chat.
must_show: A room, or its organisers, setting its plan apart from placers it does not control: 'we are not for that person', 'there are rogues outside our Discord', 'the hivemind works in mysterious ways', offers to repair what the rogues did.
must_not_show: A room saying it has no plan at all (T3-125); moderators disowning the canvas work as a whole (T3-140); members of a room doubting a damage was theirs, or a bare denial pointing at another room (T3-087).
decide_when_both: An outside room's denial counts when it separates its organised members from placers acting in its name (a fandom room's Danish members, a crusader room's Norwegian ally); a bare 'it was not us' that points to another room is tracing (T3-087). Separating the plan from such placers is T3-188; who may speak for the room is T3-126.
varies: From one disowned rogue in a chat to national rooms saying their plans do not control every placer of their nation; offers to repair the rogues' damage.
is_not: A room that says it has no plan and wings it (T3-125).
vs T3-087: Does an organised room say the placers are not under its control (T3-188), or do members work out who did it, the room's own members doubting it was theirs included (T3-087)?
vs T3-125: Does the room set its plan apart from placers outside it (T3-188), or say it has no plan (T3-125)?
vs T3-126: Are placers outside the plan disowned (T3-188), or is the right to speak for the room disputed (T3-126)?
vs T3-076: Does the room rein in its own attackers (T3-076), or say the placers are not its own (T3-188)?
vs T3-140: Do moderators say the sub has no canvas project at all (T3-140), or does the room set its project apart from rogues (T3-188)?
vs T3-195: Does an organised room say it does not control placers acting in its name (T3-188), or hold another room to an agreement (T3-195)?

## T3-192  griefing the work for fun

gloss: Members grief a work for fun, the room's own or another's, and own it or joke about it.
must_show: Damage done for amusement: letters or numbers changed into jokes, a dot kept in a logo to annoy defenders, griefing to watch others react; admissions and jokes about doing it, the room's own work included.
must_not_show: Attacks made for the room's ends (T3-006, T3-061); mistakes owned (T3-170).
decide_when_both: A joke defacement called for as the room's attack on a rival: T3-006; done by a member for amusement: T3-192.
varies: From turning 60 into 69 on the room's own logo to griefing a rival's logo to hear its fans complain.
is_not: A member admitting a misclick (T3-170).
vs T3-006: Is a rival's work defaced for the room (T3-006), or any work, the room's own included, griefed for fun (T3-192)?
vs T3-170: Was the damage a mistake (T3-170), or done for fun (T3-192)?
vs T3-031: Is the joke played by damaging the work (T3-192), or told in talk (T3-031)?
