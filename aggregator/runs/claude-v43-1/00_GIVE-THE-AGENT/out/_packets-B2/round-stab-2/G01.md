# Family G01  April Fools in the sub

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-001  staging April Fools fake news

gloss: Members post their own April Fools fakes in the sub as if true (news, leaks, patches, releases, claims) and play along with them, fall for them or reveal them.
must_show: Members of the room themselves post something false as true for April Fools (a fake news story, leak, patch, release, announcement, trade or personal claim), or reply inside such a fake: playing along, being caught, revealing it.
must_not_show: A change to the sub itself staged by the moderators or the sub (T3-023); a joke made by the company, creator or organisation the sub follows (T3-048); members testing a claim the theme did not stage (T3-095).
decide_when_both: When a theme both stages fakes and tests claims from elsewhere, T3-001 if most concepts make or play inside the room's own fakes, T3-095 if most weigh an outside claim; objecting to the jokes in three or more concepts adds T3-032.
varies: From one fake headline to a run of fake patch notes and leaks; from members who stay in character to members who reveal at once; from fooling the room to readers admitting they were caught.
is_not: Members proposing pranks to play on friends away from the site: nothing is staged in the room, so it is the sub's usual talk (T3-017).
vs T3-095: Did members of this room stage the claim? Yes: T3-001, the replies inside it included. It came from the moderators, the company, a public figure, the news or another room: T3-095.
vs T3-023: Is the joke the sub's own changed state (its name, look, rules, subject or a staged takeover)? T3-023. Is it a member's post claiming something false inside the sub? T3-001.
vs T3-048: Whose joke is it: the room's members (T3-001), or the game's company, the show's creator, an official channel or organisation (T3-048)?
vs T3-031: Is the joke a fake posted as true for April Fools (T3-001), or the room's ordinary in-jokes and memes on any day (T3-031)?
vs T3-017: Is anything posted as a fake for April Fools (T3-001), or are the posts ordinary (T3-017)?

## T3-023  running an April Fools takeover

gloss: The sub is turned into something else for April Fools, by its moderators or by the sub as a body (renamed, restyled, given new rules or a new subject, or one sub's joke raid on another), and members play inside it.
must_show: A change made to a whole sub for April Fools, announced or staged by its moderators or by a sub acting together: a new name, look, subject, rules or flairs, a fake sale or takeover by a character, company or rival sub, or one sub taking over another for the day; and members playing along inside it.
must_not_show: Members' own fake posts in an unchanged sub (T3-001); working out whether the change is a joke (T3-095); only complaining about the jokes (T3-032) or turning the styling off (T3-097).
decide_when_both: Playing along inside the takeover stays T3-023. Working out what the change was in three or more concepts adds T3-095; objecting in three or more adds T3-032; turning off or working around the look adds T3-097; asking to keep it after the day adds T3-100.
varies: From a one-day styling swap to a full change of subject with removals enforced; from the moderators alone to a whole sub raiding another sub as a joke.
is_not: A moderator removing canvas posts under the sub's ordinary rules on 1 April (T3-063): nothing about the sub was changed for the joke.
vs T3-001: Is the joke the sub's changed state (T3-023), or a member's fake post inside the sub (T3-001)?
vs T3-095: Are members playing inside the change (T3-023), or trying to tell whether the change is real and what it is (T3-095)?
vs T3-097: Is the look being staged or played with (T3-023), or turned off, worked around or asked away (T3-097)?
vs T3-093: Are moderator powers handed to every member and played with (T3-093), or is it any other takeover (T3-023)?
vs T3-064: Is the target sub changed as a whole, as the joke's stage (T3-023), or do members carry the room's joke into other places one post at a time (T3-064)?
vs T3-048: Is the joke the maker's (T3-048), or the sub's own moderators' change to the sub, even when they play the maker (T3-023)?
vs T3-133: Is the change a joke the moderators staged (T3-023), or a real compromise they repair (T3-133)?
vs T3-185: Is the rule change a joke for the day (T3-023), or meant to hold (T3-185)?
vs T3-032: Is the sub turned into something else for the day and played inside (T3-023), or are the sub's April Fools jokes objected to and asked to stop (T3-032)?
vs T3-100: Is the sub turned into something else for the day (T3-023), or is it asked that an April Fools change outlive the day (T3-100)?

## T3-032  objecting to April Fools posts

gloss: Members object to the April Fools jokes in their sub as unfunny, stale, hurtful or in the way, and ask for them to stop or come down.
must_show: Objection to the sub's April Fools content carried by three or more concepts: calling the jokes unfunny, stale, hurtful or disruptive; asking members or moderators to stop them or take them down; warning others not to post them.
must_not_show: One or two passing complaints inside a theme about the jokes (named in the room note, not placed); complaints that the prank styling is hard to read (T3-097); objections to canvas posts (T3-075).
decide_when_both: A theme that stages or plays the jokes and objects to them keeps the staging kind as primary and adds T3-032 when the objecting has three or more concepts.
varies: From a sigh at a stale joke to calls for the moderators to undo a takeover; hurt at a joke about a nation, a language or a death.
is_not: A member disliking the game company's April Fools feature (T3-048): the objection is to the maker's joke, not to posts in the sub.
vs T3-075: Are the posts objected to April Fools jokes (T3-032) or canvas posts flooding the sub (T3-075)?
vs T3-097: Are the jokes themselves bad or unwelcome (T3-032), or is the joke look in the way of reading and voting (T3-097)?
vs T3-063: Are members objecting (T3-032), or moderators removing posts under the sub's rules (T3-063)?
vs T3-048: Is the objection to posts in the sub (T3-032), or a verdict on the company's or creator's own joke (T3-048)?
vs T3-118: Is the moderators' joke objected to as a joke (T3-032), or blamed for a loss (T3-118)?
vs T3-143: Are April Fools posts objected to (T3-032), or a canvas joke's limits argued (T3-143)?
vs T3-023: Is the sub turned into something else for the day and played inside (T3-023), or are the sub's April Fools jokes objected to and asked to stop (T3-032)?

## T3-048  reacting to official April Fools

gloss: Members react to the April Fools jokes of the company, creator, channel or organisation the sub follows: waiting for them, judging them, comparing years, missing them.
must_show: Talk about an April Fools joke made by the maker the sub is about (a game company's patch, trailer, store item or mode; a creator's or channel's video; a club's, school's or organisation's post): anticipating it, judging it, comparing it with other years, wishing for one, missing one.
must_not_show: An open question whether an announcement is a joke at all (T3-095); members' own fakes (T3-001); asking for the joke feature to stay after the day (T3-100).
decide_when_both: Judging the official joke stays T3-048. When most concepts ask whether an announcement is real, T3-095; asking to keep the joke feature in three or more concepts adds T3-100.
varies: From disappointment that a company made no joke this year to praise for a well-made fake update; companies, streamers, channels, clubs and universities.
is_not: The sub's moderators staging a fake takeover by the company (T3-023): the moderators are not the company.
vs T3-095: Is it still open whether the announcement is a joke (T3-095), or is the joke known and members judge it (T3-048)?
vs T3-001: Is the joke the official maker's (T3-048) or the room's members' (T3-001)?
vs T3-100: Reacting to the joke on the day (T3-048), or asking for it to stay or be made real after the day (T3-100)?
vs T3-023: Is the joke the maker's (T3-048), or the sub's own moderators' change to the sub, even when they play the maker (T3-023)?
vs T3-032: Is the objection to posts in the sub (T3-032), or a verdict on the company's or creator's own joke (T3-048)?
vs T3-106: Do members only judge the maker's joke (T3-048), or call for action against the maker (T3-106)?

## T3-064  spreading the room's joke elsewhere

gloss: Members carry their sub's joke or in-joke into other subs or sites, or show another sub what the room is doing.
must_show: The room's own joke (an April Fools takeover, an in-joke, a meme, a birthday flood) taken by members into another sub or site: posting it there, linking it into other subs, showing another sub what the room is doing, comparing the room's joke with other subs'.
must_not_show: A takeover of the room's own sub (T3-023); calls to other subs for canvas help (T3-062).
decide_when_both: One sub changing another sub as a whole for the day is T3-023; members carrying the joke into other places post by post is T3-064.
varies: From one link to another sub to a coordinated flood of birthday wishes across subs; from pride in the joke's reach to worry that another sub's joke is everywhere.
is_not: Recruiting kindred subs to help with the canvas (T3-062): no joke is carried.
vs T3-023: Is another sub taken over as the joke's stage (T3-023), or do members carry the joke into other places (T3-064)?
vs T3-031: Is the in-joke played inside the room (T3-031), or taken out to other subs (T3-064)?
vs T3-062: Does the message to another sub carry the room's joke (T3-064), or ask that sub for help on the canvas (T3-062)?

## T3-093  playing with granted moderator powers  [rare]

gloss: Every member is made a moderator for April Fools, and members play with the powers and with losing them.
must_show: Moderator status handed to every member (or to many) for the day, and members playing with it: threats to ban or sticky, jokes about ruling, talk when the powers are withdrawn.
must_not_show: Any other takeover in which the moderators keep their powers (T3-023).
decide_when_both: The moderators' staging of the handout sits with T3-023; members' play with the powers goes to T3-093.
varies: From jokes about banning the real moderators to a mock mutiny once the powers end.
is_not: A real election or appointment of moderators for the canvas work (T3-173, T3-101).
vs T3-023: Do members hold moderator powers and play with them (T3-093), or do they play inside a change the moderators made (T3-023)?
vs T3-133: Members playing with powers handed out for the day (T3-093), or a stolen account undone (T3-133)?

## T3-095  testing others' April Fools claims

gloss: Members work out whether a claim they did not make is an April Fools joke: a moderators' or company's announcement, a public figure's post, a news story, another room's claim, or real news taken for a joke.
must_show: Members asking or settling whether something the theme did not stage is real or a joke: checking the date and the source, pointing to evidence, warning others, confirming or exposing.
must_not_show: The room's own fakes and the replies inside them, readers caught by them included (T3-001); judging an official joke already known to be one (T3-048).
decide_when_both: If the room's members staged the claim, T3-001 even when readers test it; otherwise T3-095. Where a theme holds both, the kind with more concepts is primary and the other is added under rule 0.
varies: From a single 'April Fools?' to a thread tracing a leak to its source; claims by companies, moderators, players, news sites, public figures and other rooms; real news first taken for a joke.
is_not: Readers falling for a fake that members of the same room posted (T3-001).
vs T3-001: Did members of this room stage the claim being tested (T3-001), or did it come from elsewhere, the moderators, a company, a public figure, the news, another room (T3-095)?
vs T3-048: Is it still open whether it is a joke (T3-095), or do members judge an official joke they know is one (T3-048)?
vs T3-023: Are members working out what the sub's change is and whether it is real (T3-095), or playing inside it (T3-023)?
vs T3-016: Is the claim tested whether r/place itself is Reddit's April Fools joke, answered by explaining what r/place is (T3-016), or a claim about anything else (T3-095)?

## T3-097  switching off the prank styling

gloss: Members get past the sub's April Fools look or rules: turning the style off, working around it, reporting what it breaks, asking for it gone, or the moderators switching it back.
must_show: The sub's April Fools styling or rule change treated as something to get past: turning off the subreddit style, browser and CSS workarounds, reports that it breaks reading, voting or links, requests to switch back, the moderators ending it.
must_not_show: Playing inside the changed look (T3-023); objecting to the joke posts as unfunny (T3-032); asking to keep the change (T3-100).
decide_when_both: A theme that stages the takeover and switches it off keeps T3-023 as primary and adds T3-097 when switching off or working around has a concept of its own.
varies: From a reader's browser trick to the moderators restoring the old theme; contrast, fonts, flairs, hidden links and buttons that stop working.
is_not: Moderators changing the sub's posting rules for good after the day (T3-185).
vs T3-100: Do members want the change gone or worked around (T3-097), or kept after the day (T3-100)?
vs T3-032: Is the look in the way (T3-097), or are the jokes themselves unwelcome (T3-032)?
vs T3-023: Is the change being staged or played (T3-023), or got rid of (T3-097)?

## T3-100  keeping April Fools changes afterwards

gloss: Members ask that something made for April Fools outlive the day: the sub's flairs, styling or name, or a company's joke feature made real.
must_show: A request or decision that an April Fools change stay after the day: flairs, a theme, a name, a game mode, a rank set or a joke item; petitions, polls and settings asked for so that it can stay.
must_not_show: Reacting to the joke on the day (T3-048, T3-023); getting rid of the change (T3-097).
decide_when_both: Asking to keep a company's joke feature is T3-100 when the asking has a concept of its own, beside T3-048 for judging the joke.
varies: From a flair kept a few more days to petitions with thousands of votes asking a company to make a joke rank permanent.
is_not: Asking to carry the canvas work to a successor canvas (T3-054): the canvas is no April Fools change.
vs T3-097: Is the change wanted gone (T3-097) or kept (T3-100)?
vs T3-048: Judging the official joke (T3-048), or asking that it stay or be made real (T3-100)?
vs T3-105: Is the canvas work saved in screenshots and timelapses (T3-105), or an April Fools change kept in use (T3-100)?
vs T3-023: Is the sub turned into something else for the day (T3-023), or is it asked that an April Fools change outlive the day (T3-100)?
