# Family G14  space and help between rooms

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-010  settling borders with neighbours

gloss: Neighbouring rooms settle lines, overlaps, moves and spots between their works: borders drawn, a shift of a pixel or two, space shared.
must_show: A deal over lines and space between neighbouring works with no fight to end: borders agreed or asked for, shifts of a few pixels, overlaps accepted or refused, space shared.
must_not_show: A pact that ends or forestalls a fight (T3-149); an alliance for joint defence or a joint fight (T3-150); help traded (T3-151); an envoy's errand (T3-194); an agreement already made invoked (T3-195); restraint by the room's own choice (T3-024).
decide_when_both: A pact ending or forestalling a fight between rooms already in contact goes to T3-149; an agreement drawing borders or sharing space where no fight began goes to T3-010. A theme that holds several kinds of deal keeps the leading one as primary.
varies: From a request to shift a pixel or two to borders drawn with several neighbours at once; overlaps accepted with grumbling.
is_not: A truce ending raids between two rooms (T3-149).
vs T3-149: Does the deal end or forestall a fight between rooms in contact (T3-149), or settle lines and space where no fight began (T3-010)?
vs T3-150: Do the rooms join for defence or a fight (T3-150), or settle where their works meet (T3-010)?
vs T3-151: Is help given in return for help (T3-151), or a line or space settled (T3-010)?
vs T3-194: Is a member acting as the room's envoy the doing (T3-194), or the line agreed (T3-010)?
vs T3-195: Is a line settled now (T3-010), or an agreement already made invoked (T3-195)?
vs T3-024: Is the line agreed with the neighbour (T3-010), or others' work spared by the room's own choice (T3-024)?

## T3-011  helping another room's work

gloss: Members send their pixels to another room's work: helping build, finish or defend it, with nothing asked in return.
must_show: The room's members helping another room's work, or called to: 'come help the farm at r/X', 'I'm helping r/Y', pixels sent to a neighbour's defence.
must_not_show: Help traded for help (T3-151); asking for help (T3-084); an alliance for joint defence (T3-150); joining another group's build as one's own (T3-137).
decide_when_both: Help given in return is T3-151; help given with nothing asked is T3-011.
varies: From one member placing for a neighbour to a room sending its spare pixels to a neighbour's defence.
is_not: Help given in return for help received (T3-151).
vs T3-151: Is help given in return for help (T3-151), or with nothing asked (T3-011)?
vs T3-084: Is help asked for (T3-084), or sent (T3-011)?
vs T3-150: Are the rooms allied for joint defence (T3-150), or is help sent to another's work (T3-011)?
vs T3-137: Does the member help another's work while keeping the room's own (T3-011), or join another group's build as theirs (T3-137)?

## T3-012  granting a neighbour room

gloss: The room grants a neighbour space on or beside its work: permission to build in its area, room made in its design.
must_show: A neighbour allowed onto the room's work or area: 'go for it', 'do what you want with that part', room made inside the design for another's art.
must_not_show: The room yielding its own space and moving (T3-039); a host ruling on many requests by its rules (T3-162).
decide_when_both: A grant inside a border deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-012 added when the grant has concepts of its own.
varies: From permission for a rival school's logo beside the room's to orange space assigned to displaced groups.
is_not: A room moving its work to give a neighbour its spot (T3-039).
vs T3-039: Does the room move off and give up its spot (T3-039), or let a neighbour build on or beside its work (T3-012)?
vs T3-162: Does a host rule on requests by its standing rules (T3-162), or does the room grant one neighbour room (T3-012)?
vs T3-035: Is room asked for (T3-035), or granted (T3-012)?

## T3-035  asking a host for space

vs T3-020: Is space found on the open canvas (T3-020), or asked of the room that holds it (T3-035)?
vs T3-084: Is space asked for (T3-035), or hands to help the work (T3-084)?
vs T3-139: Is space asked for (T3-035), or an attack asked to stop (T3-139)?

varies: From 'the blue note was definitely first' to timestamped images offered as proof.
is_not: A room refusing to move because of work done or an agreement (T3-123).
vs T3-123: Is the space refused (T3-123), or is who came first argued (T3-045)?

## T3-099  relying on a bigger neighbour

vs T3-150: Do rooms of like size join for defence (T3-150), or does a small room rely on a bigger one (T3-099)?
