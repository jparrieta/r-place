# Family G17  nations and their flags

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-065  building the homeland's flag

gloss: The room makes the flag of its nation, state or region its mark: calling hands to it, building, sizing and fixing it.
must_show: The flag of the room's own land (a nation, a state, a region or county) built as the room's mark: calls to make it, its proportions, its spot, its repair.
must_not_show: Calls for hands to any other picture (T3-002); emblems and pictures drawn beside or inside the flag (T3-113); helping another nation's flag (T3-078).
decide_when_both: The flag of the room's land as its mark is T3-065, calls for hands to any other picture T3-002. A flag with emblems drawn into it: T3-065 for the flag, T3-113 when the emblems have concepts of their own.
varies: From nation states to the flags of a US state, a French region or an English county; proportions argued; flags rebuilt after attacks.
is_not: A room adding its nation's emblems inside a flag already built (T3-113).
vs T3-002: Is the work the flag of the room's land (T3-065), or any other picture (T3-002)?
vs T3-113: Is the flag itself built (T3-065), or emblems drawn beside or inside it (T3-113)?
vs T3-078: Is the room's own flag built (T3-065), or a kin nation's helped (T3-078)?
vs T3-145: Is the flag built (T3-065), or national feeling voiced through it (T3-145)?

## T3-078  helping kin nations' flags

gloss: Members help the flags of kindred nations or regions: Baltic brothers, neighbours, a diaspora's homeland.
must_show: Help for another nation's or region's flag felt as kin: helping Baltic brothers restore their flags, asking a kin nation's sub for help, defending a brother nation's flag.
must_not_show: Help to any other room's work (T3-011); the room's own flag (T3-065).
decide_when_both: Help to a kin nation traded for help: T3-151 when the trade leads, T3-078 when kinship does.
varies: From Lithuanians restoring the Estonian flag instead of making a third of their own to Dominicans asking help for Puerto Rican brothers.
is_not: A sports sub helping a neighbouring logo (T3-011).
vs T3-011: Is help sent to any room's work (T3-011), or to a kin nation's flag as kin (T3-078)?
vs T3-065: Is the room's own flag built (T3-065), or a kin nation's helped (T3-078)?
vs T3-079: Is a kin nation's flag helped (T3-078), or two flags joined with a heart (T3-079)?

## T3-079  joining flags with a heart

gloss: Rooms join their national flags with a heart or another sign of friendship.
must_show: Hearts or friendship signs made between national flags: proposals, half-and-half hearts, hearts kept for alliances, friendship between nations drawn on the canvas.
must_not_show: Other joint marks (T3-056); debating friendship with a historic enemy (T3-165).
decide_when_both: A heart between historic enemies argued over: T3-165 when the argument leads, T3-079 when the heart is made.
varies: From one Faroese-Welsh heart to hearts traded as tokens of alliance among Balkan and Baltic flags.
is_not: Two fandoms finishing each other's lettering (T3-056).
vs T3-056: Are national flags joined with a heart (T3-079), or another joint mark made (T3-056)?
vs T3-165: Is the heart made (T3-079), or friendship with a historic enemy argued (T3-165)?
vs T3-150: Is friendship drawn between flags (T3-079), or an alliance for defence formed (T3-150)?

## T3-085  weighing the room against nations

gloss: Members weigh the room's art against the nations' flags on the canvas: flags called bland or too big, a nation's size claimed as a right to space.
must_show: The room's mark set against national flags: flags called boring or bland, a portrait called a bigger show of power than a flag, a nation's population claimed as a right to a big flag.
must_not_show: Questioning the room's own presence (T3-098); attacks on flags (T3-006).
decide_when_both: A judgement on flags that leads to attacking them: T3-006 for the attack, T3-085 for the weighing when it has concepts of its own.
varies: From 'a flag doesn't need to be that big and is kind of boring' to a billion-person nation's claim to a big flag.
is_not: A room asking why it has no logo on the canvas (T3-098).
vs T3-098: Is the room's own presence questioned (T3-098), or its art weighed against nations' flags (T3-085)?
vs T3-006: Are flags attacked (T3-006), or weighed against the room's art (T3-085)?
vs T3-037: Is the whole canvas judged (T3-037), or nations' flags against the room's art (T3-085)?

## T3-113  drawing national emblems

gloss: Members draw their nation's emblems, figures and symbols beside or inside the flag: monuments, animals, the king's name, a coat of arms.
must_show: National emblems and pictures added to or beside the flag: figures of the nation's history or folklore, animals, monuments, the monarch's name, a coat of arms, national words.
must_not_show: The flag itself (T3-065); political symbols argued over (T3-080).
decide_when_both: A flag with emblems: see T3-065. A political emblem argued over: T3-080 when the argument leads. Veiled fascist emblems drawn into a national room's work sit here.
varies: From Dracula on the Romanian flag to the Dutch king's full name beside the coat of arms; cedars, kiwis, roos.
is_not: Members arguing whether a hammer and sickle belongs on the canvas (T3-080).
vs T3-065: Is the flag built (T3-065), or the nation's emblems added to it (T3-113)?
vs T3-080: Is an emblem added (T3-113), or a political symbol argued over (T3-080)?
vs T3-041: Is a further piece added to any work (T3-041), or a national emblem to the flag (T3-113)?

## T3-145  feeling national through the canvas

gloss: Members feel and voice national belonging through the canvas work: pride in the flag, a nation standing together, fighting for it from abroad.
must_show: National feeling voiced through the work: pride and tears at the flag, the nation said to stand together, members abroad or by marriage fighting for it.
must_not_show: The flag built (T3-065); the finished work celebrated with no national feeling (T3-009); national politics read into the canvas (T3-130).
decide_when_both: A celebration in national terms: T3-009 when the work is celebrated, T3-145 when national belonging is voiced.
varies: From a tear in the eye at the Irish flag to a member defending Latvia's flag in honour of a spouse.
is_not: A team sub celebrating its finished logo (T3-009).
vs T3-009: Is the finished work celebrated (T3-009), or national belonging voiced (T3-145)?
vs T3-065: Is the flag built (T3-065), or feeling for the nation voiced through it (T3-145)?
vs T3-130: Is national politics read into the canvas (T3-130), or belonging felt (T3-145)?

## T3-165  debating friendship with historic enemies

gloss: Members argue whether canvas friendship with a historic national enemy means anything, or should exist at all.
must_show: Debate over friendship or coexistence on the canvas with a nation the room counts a historic enemy: Greece and Turkey, Korea and Japan, Palestine and Israel.
must_not_show: A heart made with no such argument (T3-079); cooperation argued as surrender in general (T3-193).
decide_when_both: See T3-079.
varies: From Greeks thanking Turkey as a good neighbour to Palestinians asking why anyone supported flags side by side with Israel.
is_not: A heart drawn between two friendly nations (T3-079).
vs T3-079: Is the heart made (T3-079), or friendship with a historic enemy argued (T3-165)?
vs T3-193: Is cooperation as such called surrender (T3-193), or friendship with a historic national enemy debated (T3-165)?
vs T3-130: Is national politics read into the canvas generally (T3-130), or friendship with one historic enemy debated (T3-165)?

## T3-180  disputing national belonging

gloss: Members dispute who belongs to the nation or region and who may speak or act for it on the canvas.
must_show: Contest over national or regional belonging: who counts as Korean, whether only Arabs may support Arabs, whether a university's attackers count as Texas, whether Quebec's flag is Canada's to overwrite.
must_not_show: Who speaks for a room (T3-126); national politics read into the canvas generally (T3-130).
decide_when_both: A dispute over who speaks for a national room: T3-126 when the room's voice is in question, T3-180 when membership in the nation is.
varies: From K-pop fans called koreaboos to Texans disowning a university's takeover.
is_not: Members disputing who may speak for a sub (T3-126).
vs T3-126: Is it disputed who speaks for the room (T3-126), or who belongs to the nation (T3-180)?
vs T3-130: Is national politics read into the canvas (T3-130), or belonging to the nation disputed (T3-180)?
