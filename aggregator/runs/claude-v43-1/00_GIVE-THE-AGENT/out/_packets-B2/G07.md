# Family G07  choosing what to make and where

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-004  settling the mark's design

gloss: Members work out what the mark should look like (picture, colours, size, lettering, details) and correct it against the plan.
must_show: Argument or work on the mark's look: which picture or version, colours, letters, fonts, size, outlines, details; corrections to make the placed work match the design.
must_not_show: Proposing that the room make a mark at all (T3-033); choosing where it goes (T3-020); holding everyone to a chosen template (T3-091); reshaping the mark against tampering (T3-055); a bigger or second piece (T3-041).
decide_when_both: Design argued and spot chosen in one theme: the larger part primary and the other under rule 0. A design argument settled by a vote adds T3-066 when the vote has concepts of its own.
varies: From a one-pixel correction to long arguments over which picture represents the room; letters, fonts, colours and outlines.
is_not: Holding placers to the agreed design against those who stray (T3-091).
vs T3-033: Is a mark proposed to the room (T3-033), or is its look worked out (T3-004)?
vs T3-020: Is the question what it looks like (T3-004), or where it goes (T3-020)?
vs T3-091: Is the design being settled (T3-004), or the settled design enforced against strays (T3-091)?
vs T3-055: Is the look changed for its own sake (T3-004), or to make the mark harder to tamper with (T3-055)?
vs T3-041: Is the mark's own design settled (T3-004), or a bigger or second piece added (T3-041)?

## T3-020  choosing a canvas spot

gloss: Members look for and choose where on the canvas the mark goes: open space, a corner, beside kindred marks, a new location.
must_show: Where the mark should go: free space found, corners and neighbours weighed, a location announced or changed before or while building.
must_not_show: Moving the work after an attack (T3-022); asking a host room for space (T3-035); judging there is no space left (T3-036).
decide_when_both: A new spot chosen after an attack is T3-022; a spot chosen in the first place, or for a further piece, is T3-020.
varies: From a quick 'top right corner!' to long searches across a full canvas; spots beside kindred works; striking where the defence is weak.
is_not: Relocating the work once attackers have taken its spot (T3-022).
vs T3-022: Is a spot chosen in the first place (T3-020), or a new one found after an attack (T3-022)?
vs T3-035: Is space found on the open canvas (T3-020), or asked of a room that holds it (T3-035)?
vs T3-036: Is a spot found (T3-020), or the canvas judged to have none (T3-036)?
vs T3-004: Is the question where it goes (T3-020), or what it looks like (T3-004)?

## T3-033  proposing the room's mark

gloss: A member offers the room an idea of what to make on the canvas, and calls the room to make it.
must_show: An idea of what the room should put on the canvas, offered to the room: 'we should put X on r/place', 'let's make a ...', a proposal with or without a picture; slogans and symbols proposed as the room's mark included.
must_not_show: Calls for hands to a mark already chosen (T3-002); the design of a chosen mark worked out (T3-004).
decide_when_both: A theme whose lead is recruiting hands to a picture is T3-002; one whose lead is offering the room an idea of what to make is T3-033.
varies: From one member's wish to put a symbol on the canvas to a series of picture proposals; political slogans proposed as such sit here.
is_not: A call for help on a picture already started (T3-002).
vs T3-002: Is the lead an idea of what to make (T3-033), or hands called to a picture already chosen (T3-002)?
vs T3-004: Is a mark proposed (T3-033), or a chosen mark's look worked out (T3-004)?
vs T3-098: Is a mark proposed (T3-033), or the room's absence from the canvas questioned (T3-098)?
vs T3-041: Is the room's first mark proposed (T3-033), or a second piece beside an existing one (T3-041)?

## T3-055  reshaping the mark against tampering

gloss: Members change the mark's shape to make it harder to tamper with: borders, boxes, outlines, enlarging or compacting it, dropping parts easily griefed.
must_show: A design change made for protection: a border or box around the mark, an outline, the mark enlarged or compacted, a vulnerable part (a name, letters) dropped or replaced.
must_not_show: Repairing the mark as it was (T3-021); design changes for their own sake (T3-004); fighting off attackers (T3-005).
decide_when_both: Repair and protective reshaping in one theme: T3-021 primary when the repair leads, T3-055 added when the reshaping has concepts of its own.
varies: From a green box around a mushroom to a name dropped as too easily griefed and a lattice built around the figure instead.
is_not: Re-placing the pixels attackers changed (T3-021).
vs T3-021: Is the mark restored as it was (T3-021), or changed in shape so it resists tampering (T3-055)?
vs T3-004: Is the change made for the look (T3-004), or for protection (T3-055)?
vs T3-005: Are attackers fought off (T3-005), or the mark's shape changed against them (T3-055)?
vs T3-042: Is the mark reshaped to resist tampering (T3-055), or expansion dropped for upkeep (T3-042)?

## T3-077  animating the mark in timelapse  [rare]

gloss: Members plan a mark that moves or flashes when the canvas is watched as a timelapse.
must_show: A plan to change the mark over time so that it animates in the timelapse: flashing colours, a walking figure, a scripted animation.
must_not_show: Saving the work in screenshots and timelapses (T3-105).
decide_when_both: A plan that animates the mark and saves the timelapse: T3-077 for the plan, T3-105 for the saving.
varies: From a toadstool flashing red and green to a scripted animation.
is_not: Sharing a timelapse of the finished work (T3-105).
vs T3-105: Is the timelapse used to keep the work (T3-105), or the work designed to move in it (T3-077)?
vs T3-004: Is a still design settled (T3-004), or a change over time planned (T3-077)?

## T3-092  making a tribute alone

gloss: One member makes a small mark for the room, or for someone they admire, on their own and reports it, often asking others to keep it.
must_show: A single member making a small piece alone and saying so: 'I wrote a small X, protect it', 'I added a little something for my bias', 'I decided to make a tribute'.
must_not_show: Members reporting their share of the room's work (T3-003); proposing a mark to the room (T3-033).
decide_when_both: A lone piece that the room then takes up: T3-092 for the lone making, T3-038 for the room's build.
varies: From a tiny tribute made before a long drive to a small logo one member keeps alive.
is_not: A member reporting how many pixels they placed on the room's flag (T3-003).
vs T3-003: Is the work one member's own small piece (T3-092), or a share in the room's work (T3-003)?
vs T3-033: Is a mark proposed for the room to make (T3-033), or made alone and reported (T3-092)?

## T3-160  building memorials to the dead

gloss: Members make or protect memorials on the canvas to people who died: musicians, drivers, relatives, the victims of a disaster.
must_show: A work made, proposed or defended as a memorial to the dead, or an existing work recast as one.
must_not_show: Mourning the lost canvas work (T3-029); tributes to the living (T3-092).
decide_when_both: A memorial defended against neighbours: T3-160 primary when the memorial is the work's point, T3-005 added for the defence.
varies: From a road renamed for a flag designer who had died to drivers' and musicians' memorials defended against neighbours.
is_not: Members mourning their own erased work (T3-029).
vs T3-029: Is a dead person honoured by the work (T3-160), or the lost work mourned (T3-029)?
vs T3-092: Is the tribute to someone who died (T3-160), or a lone member's tribute to anyone (T3-092)?

## T3-169  signing the sub's name

gloss: Members put the sub's name on or beside the work: r/name, a sign, a label.
must_show: The sub's name, or a short form of it, written on the canvas; the choice whether to write it; keeping it there.
must_not_show: Advertising the sub to bring visitors in, when that is the stated point (T3-044); leaving the name off on purpose (T3-191).
decide_when_both: Writing the sub's name is T3-169's doing; a call to write it is its form, and one concept carries one placement.
varies: From a short tag under a logo to arguments over which short name fits the space.
is_not: Dropping the sub's name so that the work is no target (T3-191).
vs T3-044: Is the name written to sign the work (T3-169), or to bring visitors to the sub (T3-044)?
vs T3-191: Is the name put on (T3-169), or left off on purpose (T3-191)?
vs T3-004: Is the mark's design settled (T3-004), or its signature added (T3-169)?

## T3-191  leaving the work unsigned  [rare]

gloss: Members leave the sub's name off the work, so that it draws no attackers or because the builders are not the sub's.
must_show: A decision to drop or omit the sub's name or label from the work, with its reason.
must_not_show: Writing the name on (T3-169).
decide_when_both: A theme that argues both: the side that prevails in the concepts decides.
varies: From dropping a label that makes the room a target to saying the builders are not the sub's.
is_not: Writing the sub's name beside the work (T3-169).
vs T3-169: Is the name put on (T3-169), or left off (T3-191)?
vs T3-144: Is the recruiting kept quiet (T3-144), or the name kept off the work (T3-191)?

## T3-197  heeding the creator's wishes  [rare]

gloss: The room shapes its canvas work around what the creator of its subject wants: the creator's name kept off, canvas talk moved out of the creator's way.
must_show: The wishes of the person the sub is about (an author, an artist) passed on and followed in the canvas work: not using their name, keeping canvas talk out of their sub, not pinging them.
must_not_show: Moderators' rules for canvas posts with no creator's wish behind them (T3-063, T3-015).
decide_when_both: Canvas talk moved to a new sub for the creator's sake: T3-090 for the move, T3-197 for the reason when it has concepts of its own.
varies: From passing on that the author wants his name kept out to asking members not to ping him.
is_not: Moderators gathering canvas talk into a megathread for order (T3-015).
vs T3-063: Are canvas posts handled under the sub's rules (T3-063), or shaped by the creator's wish (T3-197)?
vs T3-090: Is a canvas sub opened for order (T3-090), or to keep the work out of the creator's way (T3-197)?
vs T3-140: Do the moderators disown the work (T3-140), or does the room heed its creator (T3-197)?
