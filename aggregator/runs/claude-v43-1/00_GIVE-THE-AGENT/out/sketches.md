# Sketches, pass 1 (C5a-01)

Target length: about 1,100 words. Three working arrangements of the same model (`model-C4.md`; 25 standing elements, 20 arrows, three tentative entries from the field). Each comes with what it showed and what it hid. These are working drawings; section 10 of the stage file specifies the figure.

## 1. By time on the canvas clock

```
hour 0 ........ 24 ........ 48 ........ 73 | 73 ........ 97
R01 start, P01 calls, P15 one-line answers
P02 what to make, P04 templates
K01 April Fools play (hours 8 to 32 in most rooms)
P20 the host sub's line around canvas talk
        P03 build reports, P05 upkeep, P14 scripts

        P09 attack calls, P18 contest in talk
                    P17 coalitions, K02 doubt, P10 moves
          P11 celebration ............................ P11
                                           placement stops
                                           P12 records and curation (P22 tentative)
                                           P13 carrying on
                                           P18 rivalry in talk
```

**What it showed.** One terminus for every room: placement stopped at hour 73, and after it members can celebrate, record, curate, carry on and keep a rivalry in talk, and nothing else. The drawing puts curation after the close, apart from carrying on, as J ruled.

**What it hid.** Every order in the model is counted inside each room, from that room's first concept of one element to its first concept of another. The drawing puts the steps on one shared clock, and neither source supports that. My early and late rooms run the steps at different hours (A04's bound: 6 of 15 late rooms keep its order). The field's analyst could not find one progression shared by all groups. P11 and P18 each sit at two places on the clock: celebration during placement and after it, and contest talk during the fight and after the close.

**What it showed.** The model has a middle column, and it is wide: 18 elements, each named for what members do. The conditions are thin. K02 carries one thin arrow (A10), and K01 carries none. The claims stand on findings and carry no arrow. So the figure can show mediators, but the conditions side holds almost nothing a reader can follow.

**What it hid.** It hid order and time, and it hid that P05 does two jobs. In this drawing P05 is a mediator: upkeep leads to border talk, help, scripts and coalitions. In the first drawing, upkeep is what follows building. That is the same element in two places, as stage C5 section 3 warns. I read it as the spine passing through P05, not as two jobs, because both readings are one counted chain (A03 into P05, and A04, A06, A13 and A19 out of it). I examine it again in pass 2. Three standing elements have no arrow: P06, K01 and R02. They are candidates for the simplification of section 4.

## 3. By trajectory: one room's path, then two that differ

```
day9           R01 h14.7 -> P08 terms h33.9 -> loss h48.0 -> K02/P10 move h49.7-50.7
               -> P03 new work finished h53.1 -> R02 diplomat h55 -> P12 recount h74.1
ubc            P01 h17.4 -> P04 anchor h25.1 -> P11 "We did it!" h35.7 -> P08 neighbours h40-64
               -> P07 refusal h55.8 -> P14 spare accounts h65.7 -> P11/P12 at the close
ultrahardcore  K01 h7.7-40 -> R01 h57.6 -> P02/P04 h57.9-58.1 -> P09/P07 h57.9-58.0
               -> P11 "We almost did it!" h73.1
```

**What it showed.** The three rooms run the same start, and their paths differ after it. day9 talks with a neighbour before any loss (P08 at h33.9), loses its spot, moves, and builds again. ubc celebrates a first finished piece (h35.7) before its neighbours arrive, and later keeps a border by agreement. ultrahardcore runs proposal, template, attack call and refusal inside one thread in under half an hour, and never meets a neighbour.

**What it hid.** It hid the counts: a path shows what one room did, not how many rooms did it. It also hid two elements doing two jobs.
- **P08 in day9** comes before any loss, as an offer of terms ("We only require 35x35 pixels"). A04 holds it as talk that follows erosion. The same element serves both. A04's bound already names neighbours present from a room's first post, and the field holds an early buffer offered in r/place. P08 is a candidate for a split between terms offered before any damage and talk after it. I test that in pass 2, by reading P08's first concepts in the rooms where A04's order fails.
- **P10 in day9** is both the end of a fight and the start of new work. In the tier drawing it is a mediator that leads nowhere. No arrow runs from the move to building again, so the drawing shows a path the model cannot count.

## What the three drawings agree on

All three keep the spine from the start through calls, building and keeping, then the neighbours, the contest, the close at hour 73 and what follows it. The account rests on the second arrangement, by tier, with the clock of the first written into its last section. The third arrangement supplies the rooms told upward.
