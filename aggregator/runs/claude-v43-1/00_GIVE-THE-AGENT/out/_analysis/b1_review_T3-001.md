# Review of T3-001 staging April Fools fake news (B1b-20)

Working file for the PI's request of 9 October. Every member printed whole with
b1_theme_members.py ($TMPDIR/k001.txt, 377 KB), cut into 16 parts by b1_split_print.py, and read.
227 members in 171 rooms; 135 placed here only; co-placed with T3-095 working out whether it is an
April Fool in 67, T3-023 the moderators' takeover in 14, T3-032 objecting to April Fools posts in
13, T3-048 the company's own April Fools in 9.
B1b-02's line: staging a joke and seeing through one are two doings, whoever made the joke;
members who both stage and see through their own jokes stay in T3-001, and B1e's gate decides.
Letters, added as found:
f a member's fake post or news staged (a fake trade, leak, ban, announcement, rule);
t testing, calling out or explaining a fake (T3-095);
m the moderators' frame (T3-023);
o objecting to the jokes (T3-032);
c the company's own April Fools (T3-048);
j in-jokes with no fake in them (T3-031).
r readers' replies inside the staged joke's own thread (falling for it, groaning, booing, playing
  along, the poster revealing it): part of staging, not a second doing;
p themed play on the day with no fake claimed (a bread flood, a math thread, a pun, costumes).

## Decision

T3-001 is kept whole. Every member does one thing: the room's own April Fools jokes, staged by
its members, with the replies inside the joke's own exchange (falling for it, groaning, spotting
the tell, the maker revealing it). What varies, written out for B1e: the fake (trades,
retirements, leaks, releases, deaths, rules, bans, sub moves), the form (a single title, a
thread, a flood of posts, a persona or AMA, a format parody), who stages it (a member, a parent
on a child, a moderator posting as a member), and how far the replies go (playing along,
rating the effort, testing, objecting). Themed play with no fake claimed (bread, Snek, robot
pretence, Data, a pun) is the same doing at its lightest; it stays.

T3-095 beside it. B1b-02 split T3-095 off for members working out whether a page change or a game
update, made by the moderators or the company, was an April Fool, and left the theme that stages
its own joke and spots it to B1e. Legs B1b-08 to B1b-18 then co-placed such themes on any
spotting concept; earlier legs did not, and later legs did not always (aoe2 t01, terraria t05,
magictcg t04, muse t09). The full read gives the boundary: T3-095 is placed where the theme tests
a claim it did not stage itself: a change or announcement by the moderators, the company's or a
public figure's post, a news story, another room's material, another member's post whose making
the theme does not show, or real news taken for a joke. Where the theme stages the fake and its
readers spot it, the spotting is the reply side of staging: T3-001 alone. Borderline themes, with
a test of something staged elsewhere beside in-theme spotting, keep both (pokemon t03, frankocean
t03, anime t04, OnePiece t08, onepunchman t05, rush t08, totallynotrobots t03, monstercat t02,
alberta t01, darksouls t05).
Set aside: co-placing every theme whose replies spot a member's fake, the B1b-08 to B1b-18
practice. About forty more T3-001 themes would take T3-095, and the two kinds would cover the
same staging threads.
