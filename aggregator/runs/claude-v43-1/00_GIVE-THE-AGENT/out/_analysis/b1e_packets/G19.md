# Family G19  taunts and victories over rivals

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-025  placing rival marks together  [rare]

gloss: A room puts its mark beside a rival's and keeps both standing without a war.
must_show: The room's mark placed next to a rival community's mark on purpose, both defended together, the rivalry kept on the canvas without attacking.
must_not_show: Taunts traded (T3-050); a truce ending a fight (T3-149); a joint mark (T3-056).
decide_when_both: Rival marks side by side under a defence pact: T3-025 for the pairing, T3-150 for the pact when it has concepts of its own.
varies: One room's case: the Battle of Alberta kept on the canvas without a war.
is_not: Two fandoms finishing each other's lettering (T3-056).
vs T3-056: Do the rooms make one mark together (T3-056), or keep two rival marks side by side (T3-025)?
vs T3-149: Is a fight ended (T3-149), or two rival marks set together with no fight (T3-025)?
vs T3-050: Are taunts traded (T3-050), or the rivalry shown by marks placed side by side (T3-025)?

## T3-050  trading taunts with rivals

gloss: Members trade taunts, threats and mockery with a rival community over the canvas.
must_show: Taunts, threats or mockery aimed at a rival community over the canvas work: 'try your best', 'they will destroy you', jibes at a rival's scripts or logo.
must_not_show: Jokes about a people or nation (T3-142); blame laid for a loss after the close (T3-058); a community's own quarrel carried onto the canvas (T3-086).
decide_when_both: Taunts with ethnic jokes: T3-050 primary when taunting leads, T3-142 added when the ethnic jokes have concepts of their own.
varies: From friendly ribbing between schools to threats that an allied faction will destroy the rival.
is_not: Mocking a rival nation's people (T3-142).
vs T3-142: Is a rival community taunted (T3-050), or a people or nation mocked (T3-142)?
vs T3-058: Is a rival taunted as sport (T3-050), or blamed for a loss after the close (T3-058)?
vs T3-086: Is a rival taunted (T3-050), or the community's own internal quarrel fought (T3-086)?
vs T3-155: Is the rival mocked (T3-050), or saluted after the fight (T3-155)?

## T3-134  flooding rivals' threads with chants

gloss: Members flood a rival's thread with a chant or refrain: the Void's creed, a streamer's victory chorus, 'get gooned'.
must_show: A chant, refrain or slogan repeated by many members in another room's thread or in their own as a raid or victory chorus.
must_not_show: Taunts traded in ordinary replies (T3-050); the room's own in-jokes played among members (T3-031).
decide_when_both: A chant that is the room's own in-joke posted in a rival's thread: T3-134; posted among its own members: T3-031.
varies: From the Void's creed posted line after line in Green Lattice's thread to a streamer's fans chanting in chorus after a win.
is_not: A room's members playing their own catchphrase among themselves (T3-031).
vs T3-050: Are taunts traded in replies (T3-050), or one chant repeated as a flood (T3-134)?
vs T3-031: Is the refrain played among the room's members (T3-031), or used to flood a rival's thread (T3-134)?
vs T3-154: Is victory claimed in words (T3-154), or chanted as a chorus (T3-134)?

## T3-135  telling clashes as fandom lore

gloss: Members retell the canvas clashes in their fandom's own terms: as the game's battles, the show's factions, the band's season.
must_show: The canvas fight or its damage told in the fandom's own story terms: Autobots and Decepticons, battle scars, a game's release, a fictional war.
must_not_show: Fights cast as a nation's historic wars (T3-081); the room's in-jokes with no canvas clash (T3-031).
decide_when_both: A clash told in fandom terms with jokes: T3-135 when the clash is the subject, T3-031 when the joke is.
varies: From missing pixels called battle scars to a whole retelling as the game's Grand Battle.
is_not: A Polish room calling a border fight a new partition (T3-081).
vs T3-081: Is the fight cast as a nation's history (T3-081), or as the fandom's story (T3-135)?
vs T3-031: Is it the room's running joke (T3-031), or the canvas clash retold in fandom terms (T3-135)?
vs T3-073: Is the work's history recounted plainly (T3-073), or as fandom lore (T3-135)?

## T3-143  judging whether a joke offends

gloss: Members argue whether a taunt or joke about the canvas goes too far: racist, cruel or just a joke.
must_show: Argument over a joke's or taunt's limits: called racist or horrible, defended as only a joke, 'you can't make a joke without offending someone'.
must_not_show: The taunt or joke itself (T3-050, T3-142); objecting to April Fools posts (T3-032).
decide_when_both: A taunt and the argument over it: T3-050 or T3-142 when the taunting leads, T3-143 when the argument does.
varies: From a joke about a former president's death called horrible to a flag joke defended against a charge of racism.
is_not: Members telling the sub to stop its April Fools posts (T3-032).
vs T3-050: Is the taunt made (T3-050), or argued over (T3-143)?
vs T3-142: Is the ethnic joke made (T3-142), or its limits argued (T3-143)?
vs T3-032: Are April Fools posts objected to (T3-032), or a canvas joke's limits argued (T3-143)?

## T3-154  claiming victory over rival rooms

gloss: Members claim victory over rival rooms: outlasting them, defending against bigger subs, defeating them.
must_show: Victory claimed against named or implied rivals: a flag that outlasted a rival's, a small sub that held against bigger ones, rivals called defeated.
must_not_show: The finished work celebrated with no rival (T3-009); rivals saluted (T3-155).
decide_when_both: Celebration with a victory claim: T3-009 when the work is celebrated, T3-154 when victory over rivals is.
varies: From a county flag outlasting its neighbour's to nationalist boasts of defeating a rival nation.
is_not: A room celebrating its finished logo with no rival named (T3-009).
vs T3-009: Is the finished work celebrated (T3-009), or victory over rivals claimed (T3-154)?
vs T3-155: Is victory claimed (T3-154), or the rival saluted (T3-155)?
vs T3-134: Is victory claimed in words (T3-154), or chanted as a flood (T3-134)?

## T3-155  saluting rivals after the fight

gloss: Members salute their rivals once the fight is over: well played, good game, thanks for playing.
must_show: Respect or thanks addressed to a rival after the fight: 'well played', 'GG', 'thanks for playing', a loss conceded with good grace.
must_not_show: Taunts (T3-050); victory claimed (T3-154); greetings with no fight behind them (T3-122).
decide_when_both: Salutes mixed with taunts: the side with more concepts is primary.
varies: From a streamer's fans telling Estonia 'well played' to a member conceding the loss to the vegans.
is_not: Neighbours greeting each other with no fight between them (T3-122).
vs T3-050: Is the rival mocked (T3-050), or saluted (T3-155)?
vs T3-154: Is victory claimed (T3-154), or the rival saluted (T3-155)?
vs T3-122: Are greetings sent with no fight behind them (T3-122), or a rival saluted after one (T3-155)?
