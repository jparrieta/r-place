## 6. How the model runs here

Counts are of members with a current-ledger line, particular lines included (`out/_analysis/d5_elements.py`); they count any line of an op. Rates are compared with the 391 rooms outside F20 that have at least its median of 90.5 coded concepts. These rooms are smaller than the population (median 90.5 coded concepts against 101), and they are of two sizes: five members have fewer than 90 coded concepts, and three have fewer than 20. A small record holds few lines, so an element is more often absent in it, and the comparison of absences is weak here. With 10 members, one room moves a rate by 10 points.

## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A08: restricted in 6 of 10 members, against 127 of 391, an excess of 27.5 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 10 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A20 is next, at 18.0 points.

A08 runs from P11 (marking the work) to P12 (the record). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first marking of the finished work comes before the first record of what happened to it". Its mechanism: "Marking the work together turns the room toward keeping it: members ask for the final image, the timelapse, the list of what changed."

Four of the six restrict lines say that the marking and the record came together at the close, or that the record came first. r/stlouis: "Celebrating and recording survival can coincide in one finality statement rather than requiring distinct successive acts." The room's final post did both: "This is the final Blue Note that will forever be immortalized!" [stlouis/c0091]. r/Rainbow6: "Thanks and preservation occur in the same closing act without establishing that celebration caused a separate recording initiative." One comment held the record and the thanks: "The place has ended and this is how it will remain. Thanks for everyone who helped making this" [Rainbow6/c0613]. r/re_zero: "Archival saving can precede closure thanks rather than necessarily follow that celebration." A member posted the "Complete version for history" [re_zero/c0043] at hour 63.30. The thanks came at the close, ten hours later: "It was a lot of fun making this! Thank you to all who participated!" [re_zero/c0054]. r/sips: "Fate reports precede closing celebration and the final announcement combines recording with evaluation, so celebration need not initiate recording." A member celebrated at hour 56.82: "we did it guys. well done." [sips/c0066]. The loss was recorded at hour 62.29: "Its gone rip" [sips/c0070]. The final post joined the record to a verdict: "/r/place has ended, and this is our impact" [sips/c0083].

The other two restrict a cause, not the order. r/canucks: "Celebration and media sharing coexist without showing that pride generated the decision to preserve a record." r/hatsune: "Early appreciation precedes retrospective posts, but no cited passage makes appreciation the cause of preserving or modifying a record." The connection states an order only, so these two lines restrict the mechanism. The same reading of a cause into an order arrow was logged for A09 and A20. The three absent lines (r/azerbaijan, r/eder, r/tokyo) say that their records hold no celebration linked to a record.

In these rooms the close was the occasion for both acts. In r/stlouis, r/Rainbow6 and r/sips, a member who said the work was over marked it in the same post. Part of the excess may restate the cut. A08's restrict lines fall on P12, the element the key changes, and the coders who read the end as a step in the record also read the record's order against the marking.

This is description, not a test. A08 has 2 within-case datings, so its expectation in F20 is 0.028, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A08, shared lines only: restrict 3.
