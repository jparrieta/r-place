## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A02: restricted in 8 of 21 members, against 65 of 259, an excess of 13.0 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 4.8 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A17 is next, at 5.8 points.

A02 runs from R01 (the starter) to P01 (the call for hands). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first proposal or start of a mark comes before the first call for hands". Its mechanism: "A member who proposes or begins a mark gives the room something to answer, and calls for hands gather around it." Its bound: "Where the room calls for hands before anyone proposes what to make, the proposal answers the call instead."

By my reading of the 8 restrict lines, 7 say the start and the call came in one turn, so the two cannot be dated apart. r/clg's starter posted at hour 13.15: "[Fluff] /r/place CLG Logo Plan! Start at the top right corner at (325, 830) and fill in teal tiles! Discussion inside." [clg/c0011]. A reader answered at hour 16.91: "oke i'm in" [clg/c0012]. In r/stardewvalley at hour 48.48: "Hey guys, I wanted to make the sleeping chicken on r/place at (497,424). Can anyone help?" [stardewvalley/c0055]. In r/luxembourg at hour 22.09: "Please help. We are currently attempting to conver the Argentinian flag over to the Luxembourg flag on the r\place canvas! Any help is appreciated." [luxembourg/c0001]. In r/habs at hour 25.20 one post both lamented and located: "/r/place is missing a Habs logo, which is a tragedy" [habs/c0072], and it gave coordinates [habs/c0073]. The same holds in r/bostonbruins, r/luciomains and r/ockytop. The eighth line is r/undertale's: its keepers were recruited around a figure no one in the room had started (section 1).

The other side holds in other members. In r/OnePiece at hour 4.01 a member reported starting the room's lettering at given coordinates [OnePiece/c0003]. Within a minute another member called: "We will need more manpower, lets make it happen!" [OnePiece/c0004]. In r/unturned a post at hour 26.55 proposed: "Let's get Unturned on here" [unturned/c0019]. At hour 28.18 a comment in another thread called: "We need your help to put an Unturned zombie face in /r/place" [unturned/c0025].

This meets the key in part. The coders of these rooms read the model's stages arriving joined in one turn: at the start, a proposal that is also the call; at the marking, a celebration that is also the call to keep (the first group in section 3). A02's bound covers a call that comes before the proposal; it does not cover a proposal that is itself the call. I note it for stage D6 in `notes-D5.md`, beside the link.
