## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

The arrow is chosen by the rule logged at D5a-12: the arrow whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A20, restricted in 11 of 36 members, against 73 of 329; one member is 2.8 points, and the excess is 8.4. A20 runs from P01 (the room's calls) to P20 (the host's line). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first call to the canvas comes before the first line the host sub draws around canvas talk (a megathread, a removal by rule, a changed rule, a canvas sub, an objection to the flood, a dismissal of the canvas)". The account reads it as an order: "In 132 of 155 rooms, the room's calls come before the first line the host sub draws around canvas talk".

All 11 restrict lines say the same thing: the host's line in these rooms is not shown to answer a flood of canvas calls. It was the sub's ordinary rules, applied to canvas posts as to any other. r/gameofthrones' line: "Inherited scope enforcement can remove a canvas proposal without evidence that a flood of calls caused the intervention." r/bostonceltics': "A denied sticky request can reflect occupied sticky slots rather than a new moderation response caused by flooding."

r/gameofthrones shows both sides in one minute. A member proposed: "Can we do a Targaryen sigil on r/place?" [gameofthrones/c0149]. The sub's automatic moderator removed the post at once: "Your submission has been automatically removed because all post titles must begin with one hard-bracketed spoiler scope tag" [gameofthrones/c0150]. The call came first, as A20 states, and the line that followed was the sub's spoiler rule, not an answer to the canvas. r/bostonceltics shows the host helping, then not. At hour 27 a member asked, "Mods can we get a sticky post up for this?" [bostonceltics/c0030], and half an hour later the answer was "Just stickied." [bostonceltics/c0031]. At hour 57 another member reported: "lol, mods say we cant sticky a place thread since we have two already. I think ill be joining forces with a diff sub. nice try though" [bostonceltics/c0095].

A20 states an order. The restrict lines deny a cause, a flood of calls, that the arrow's own list names among the host's lines ("an objection to the flood"). As with A09 in F11 to F13, the coders read a cause into an arrow of order. In these rooms the host drew fewer lines than in rooms their size (section 6), and the lines it drew were its standing rules. The stage notes carry the wording item.

This is description, not a test. A20 has 3 within-case datings, so its expectation in F17 is 0.153, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A20: restrict 4, instantiate 1.
