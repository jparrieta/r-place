## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A05: restricted in 12 of 23 members, against 57 of 266, an excess of 30.7 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 4.3 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A18 is next, at 15.8 points.

A05 runs from P08 (borders and deals) to P07 (sparing). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first border talk, truce or deal comes before the first act of sparing a neighbour". Its mechanism: "A settled border gives the room a line to keep: members spare the neighbour's work by it and remind others of it." Its bound already says that sparing often comes first and tacit, before any talk.

By my reading of their `how` sentences, the 16 restrict and add-condition lines fall in two groups. Twelve say that a settled border spares a neighbour only where its terms reach the placers and they accept them. r/placetardis: "Settlement produces restraint only when placers receive and recognize the agreement, as missed plans and stops after discovery demonstrate." A member wrote: "ah I missed this. maybe the plans agreed on could be posted here so we can help facilitate." [placetardis/c0019]. Another answered: "Sorry, we're not very organized. I've been trying by to spread the word that we've talked to you, and we're okay if you cover our background." [placetardis/c0023]. r/buffalobills: "A deal provides grounds for restraint but does not ensure compliance when placers continue crossing and repairs remain necessary." Seven hours after the deal was announced (section 1), a keeper reported: "I am getting notifications from them that people are crossing over." [buffalobills/c0026]. r/harrypotter: "A boundary announcement guides restraint only where recipients receive and accept its terms rather than disputing its authority or content." A member warned: "We can't say that, actually. Many harry potter people are asleep right now and if too many of them don't like it, we'll have to concede to them." [harrypotter/c0211].

Four say that sparing came before any settled border. r/metroid: "Restraint can answer a neighbour's memorial appeal before any settled border or treaty exists." A neighbour asked the room to spare "our Puffle, which is meant as a memorial to our game that was closed down" [metroid/c0174], and a member answered: "Club penguin is too precious for this world." [metroid/c0177]. The other three are r/lifeisstrange, r/python and r/transformers. These four fall where the arrow's bound already allows.

The first group restates the key in another element. P05's telling clause says that keepers undo a planned change until they are told. These lines say that a settled border spares a neighbour only once its placers are told and agree. In both, the deal works only as far as its news and its acceptance reach. So the excess here is partly the cut seen from the neighbour's side: the same condition on P07 that the key states on P05.

This is description, not a test. A05 has 3 within-case datings, so its expectation in F26 is 0.098, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A05, shared lines only: restrict 11, add-condition 4, instantiate 4.
