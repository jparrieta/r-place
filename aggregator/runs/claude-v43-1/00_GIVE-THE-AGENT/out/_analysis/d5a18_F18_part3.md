## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

The arrow is chosen by the rule logged at D5a-12, as amended at D5a-14: the arrow whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size, if the excess reaches one member's share. It is A03, restricted in 3 of 15 members, against 24 of 292; one member is 6.7 points, and the excess is 11.8. A10 follows closely, at 11.2. A03 runs from P03 (building and reporting) to P05 (keeping). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first build report comes before the first act of upkeep or defence". The account's example is a room that said "It's done!" and a minute later "time for upkeep now".

The three restrict lines say that keeping began before the building was done. r/weezer's: "Protection begins while design and expansion continue, so the order cannot require total completion before any keeping work begins." r/flyers': "Upkeep began while the logo remained unfinished and a usable template was still awaited".

r/flyers shows both sides. At hour 32 a member asked for a plan: "Someone needs to make a template." [flyers/c0067]. The reply put keeping first: "That's above my paygrade. I'll try to maintain a nice orange mass till we can get something together." [flyers/c0068]. The logo itself was built at hour 67 (section 10). r/weezer shows the same at hour 7, with the work in progress, "my purple =w= in progress! Help me spread the weez" [weezer/c0002], and four hours later the defence: "im just trying to stop this green from killing us" [weezer/c0007]. r/swordartonline shows A03's order holding: the start at hour 37 [swordartonline/c0023], the victory at hour 40 (section 1), then the defence: "We always are. We just have to fight back faster than they can fight us." [swordartonline/c0028].

In F18, keeping runs on both sides of the finish. It starts before the work is done, and it goes on after the work is called done (section 3). A03 is instantiated here in 6 of 15 members, against 149 of 292 rooms this size. The finish these rooms judged was not a point after which keeping began; keeping ran through it.

This is description, not a test. A03 has 2 within-case datings, so its expectation in F18 is 0.043, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A03: instantiate 6, restrict 2.
