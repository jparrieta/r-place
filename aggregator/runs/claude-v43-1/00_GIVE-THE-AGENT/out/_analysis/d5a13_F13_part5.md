## 12. The form test

**The constructs.** P13 (carrying on) at T3-057 (the work remade elsewhere), under T4-028 (Transition).

**The relations the form would need.** A member, or the room, takes the mark or the canvas into another medium. Others ask for the file, add to the design, or teach the craft. Cost, copyright or credit limits what can be made. Across members these are shown in subsets. What was remade splits three ways: new work in 4, the room's mark in 4, the whole canvas in 2. Who acted splits two ways: one member alone in 4, others joining in 5. The relation the branch bends is the model's own, A09, and it turns on timing (section 7).

**The four organizing problems.** An empty mapping. Requests for a file (r/prequelmemes, r/romania) touch information, and a credit challenge (r/romania) touches reward, but in one or two members each, too few to bind. A remainder is supported: once the work left the canvas, members had to settle whose work a copy was. The whole canvas held other rooms' work and other companies' logos (r/globaloffensive), an app's debt to the canvas was challenged (r/romania), and a member asked leave to post new work beside the room's (r/parrots). The four problems treat the work as the organization's own; here that was in question.

**What varies among members.** The medium (a game, a phone, beads, bricks, a text), and whether one member or several acted. Each medium is a particular of one history. No subform candidate stands on keys.

**Result: a grouping, not a form.** The constructs can be named, but the members do not share one relation among them, and the key is a change inside one element, as in F11 and F12. Unlike F09, the branch states no relation between two constructs in every member's line.

## 13. What would make you doubt this is a form at all

I already report F13 as a grouping. Three things make me question even what it tracks.

First, the coders. T3-057 is "the work remade elsewhere", but 3 of its 9 lines are about new work made from the event, not a remake. The name's timing clause holds for 3 lines, and those 3 are one member's copy each.

Second, size, and one room. The median member has 239 coded concepts, against the population's 101. r/globaloffensive holds a third of the branch's coded concepts and most of its Keying (section 8). The canvas median area sits at the 100th percentile, the highest of the branches so far; I read that only afterwards, and it grounds nothing. Large rooms with large marks have more members, and more members to copy a mark.

Third, the ending, which says little with 9 rooms. F13's median organizing end is hour 74.88 against 72.02. The mean aftermath share is 0.0314 against 0.0200 over all rooms. Work is kept in 8 of 9 members, lost in 1, and silent in none.

What would make me read it as a form: the same relation shown in most members, for example a remake that brings others in to supply the file, the craft or the cost, with the question of whose work it copies settled in the same thread.
