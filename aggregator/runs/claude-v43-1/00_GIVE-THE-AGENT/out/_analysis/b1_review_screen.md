# Screen of the large kinds the PI asked to look past (B1b-20)

The stage card asks for a full re-read of a kind holding more than one second-order theme in
ten. None does: the largest, T3-009, holds 6.4%. The PI asked to judge the oversized kinds,
starting from four (T3-010, T3-009, T3-014, T3-001) and looking past them. Those four are read
whole, every member with its note and concept labels. The ten others above 140 members are first
screened: every member's label and its other kinds (`b1_kind_labels.py`), with the co-placement
counts (`b1_kind_overview.py`). A kind whose labels show a second doing that no kind holds, or
whose co-placement with one neighbour runs high enough to suggest a bin rather than a doing, is
then read whole like the first four, since a split is a re-read of every member (rule 10). A kind
whose labels show one doing with variation gets its variation and its boundary written here.

## T3-021 keeping watch and repairing the work (182 members, screened B1b-20)

stray or changed pixels, cleaning it to the template and taking shifts. Variation: the piece, the

The boundary with T3-005 fails in practice. `b1_both_acts.py` lists 76 second-order themes whose
labels name both acts (defending and repairing) but which hold only one of the two kinds: 38
only T3-005, 38 only T3-021 (`out/_analysis/b1_review_T3-005_T3-021_both_acts.txt`). 44 hold
both. The labels alone do not show which act each theme's concepts carry, so the 76 are left as
placed and listed for B1d, which rereads at the concept level; each should hold both kinds where

Weighed and set aside: merging T3-021 into T3-005. A stranger tells "raising alarms and fighting
the Void" from "keeping the mark clean and on-pattern"; the two kinds keep an attacker faced
apart from upkeep against drift, and the stage card's asymmetry says keep two when in doubt
(a merge later is arithmetic, a split later a re-read). The pair goes first in `confusions.md`
at B1e. Gate for B1e: T3-005 must show an attack answered (an attacker named, an alarm, a rally,
a fight); T3-021 must show upkeep with no attack answered (watching, cleaning, routine repair,
shifts); a theme with both carries both by rule 0. No move now.
