# Family G02  the sub's rules and usual posts

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-015  routing canvas posts into megathreads

gloss: Moderators or members gather the sub's canvas talk into one stickied thread, a megathread, and send other canvas posts there.
must_show: Canvas talk gathered into one thread inside the sub: a moderator stickying a megathread, members asking for one, posts redirected or removed with a pointer to it, the plan kept in one visible place.
must_not_show: Moving the planning out of the sub, to a chat or a new sub (T3-014, T3-090); removals under the sub's rules with no pointer to a gathering thread (T3-063); complaining about the flood without asking for a place to gather it (T3-075).
decide_when_both: Removing canvas posts and pointing them to the megathread is T3-015; removals resting on other rules (karma, titles, relevance) are T3-063. A theme with both in concepts of their own takes the larger as primary and the other under rule 0.
varies: From one moderator's sticky to dozens of posts redirected by hand; members asking for a thread that does not exist yet.
is_not: A moderator removing a canvas post because the canvas has ended (T3-063): nothing is gathered.
vs T3-063: Is the post sent to one gathering thread (T3-015), or removed or filtered under a standing rule with no such pointer (T3-063)?
vs T3-014: Is the gathering place a thread inside the sub (T3-015), or a chat outside it (T3-014)?
vs T3-090: One thread in the sub (T3-015), or a new sub made for the canvas work (T3-090)?
vs T3-075: Is one thread asked for so the work can go on in order (T3-015), or are members objecting that canvas posts crowd the sub (T3-075)?

## T3-017  carrying on ordinary sub talk

gloss: The sub's ordinary business goes on during the canvas week: its usual subject, news, help, jokes and quarrels, apart from the canvas and April Fools.
must_show: Talk on the sub's usual subject during the window (the game, team, show, place or hobby; its news, help and arguments), or ordinary side talk inside a canvas thread, with no canvas work and no April Fools joke as its point.
must_not_show: Talk about the canvas work (other families); April Fools jokes (G01); the room's running in-jokes played in the canvas talk (T3-031).
decide_when_both: Usual talk inside a canvas or April Fools theme is named in the room note unless it holds three or more concepts of its own; then T3-017 is added under rule 0.
varies: From patch questions and match threads to a sub's politics, help requests and feuds; from whole themes of usual talk to usual talk drifting into a canvas thread.
is_not: Members trading outside creators' April Fools releases as examples of the day done well: that is reacting to official April Fools (T3-048).
vs T3-031: Is it the room's running in-joke carried into the canvas talk or the day (T3-031), or the sub's ordinary subject going on (T3-017)?
vs T3-001: Is anything posted as a fake for April Fools (T3-001), or are the posts ordinary (T3-017)?
vs T3-185: Are members arguing the sub's posting rules (T3-185), or talking about the sub's subject (T3-017)?
vs T3-050: Does the sub's ordinary talk go on with no rival in it (T3-017), or is a rival community taunted (T3-050)?

## T3-051  pushing posts up the rankings

gloss: Members ask for upvotes so a post, an image or a joke climbs: up r/place or the front page so placers see it, or to the top of a search result.
must_show: A drive for votes: members asked to upvote a post so that it rises (to be seen by placers on r/place, to reach the front page, to make an image the first search result), or bargains of the form 'if this gets N upvotes'.
must_not_show: Calling members to place with no votes asked (T3-002); asking another sub for help (T3-062).
decide_when_both: A call to place that also asks for upvotes so the call is seen: T3-002 primary, T3-051 added when the vote drive has a concept of its own.
varies: From one request to upvote a coordination post on r/place to joke campaigns to own a search result; pledges promised for a vote count.
is_not: Offering to place a tile for an upvote (T3-110): the vote buys a tile.
vs T3-002: Are members asked to vote a post up (T3-051), or to come and place (T3-002)?
vs T3-110: Is a tile offered in return for the vote (T3-110), or are votes gathered with nothing given (T3-051)?

## T3-053  spelling slogans with post titles

gloss: Members post a run of posts whose titles, read down the front page or the sub's listing, spell a slogan or a chant.
must_show: Coordinated posts of single letters, words or lines timed so that the listing spells a phrase; plans to make the front page say something; praise for the result.
must_not_show: A vote drive for one post (T3-051); a chant repeated in replies without spelling anything across posts (T3-031).
decide_when_both: Posts timed to spell and upvoted so that they reach the front page: T3-053 for the spelling, T3-051 added when the vote drive has concepts of its own.
varies: From letters spelling a name down the front page to song lyrics chained one word per post.
is_not: Pushing one post up the rankings (T3-051).
vs T3-051: Is one post pushed up (T3-051), or a run of posts lined up to spell a phrase (T3-053)?
vs T3-031: Is the room's chant played in replies (T3-031), or spelled across the titles of many posts (T3-053)?

## T3-063  removing canvas posts by rule

gloss: Moderators, or their bots, remove, filter or flair canvas posts under the sub's standing rules, and members ask about or contest the removals.
must_show: Canvas posts handled under the sub's standing rules: removals for titles, links, karma, relevance, duplicates or politics; automatic filters; required flairs; members asking what the rules allow or contesting a removal.
must_not_show: Gathering canvas talk into one thread (T3-015); changing the rules themselves (T3-185); members objecting to the flood with no moderator act (T3-075).
decide_when_both: Removing with a pointer to a gathering thread is T3-015; removing under any other rule is T3-063; both with concepts of their own, the larger primary and the other under rule 0.
varies: From an automatic removal for an all-caps title to a moderator removing a post that ties the sub to fascism; members apologising in advance for breaking a rule.
is_not: A moderator stickying a megathread and asking for posts there (T3-015).
vs T3-015: Is the post removed under a standing rule (T3-063), or sent to one gathering thread (T3-015)?
vs T3-185: Are the rules applied as they stand (T3-063), or made, dropped or argued as a change (T3-185)?
vs T3-075: A removal or filter applied (T3-063), or members complaining about canvas posts (T3-075)?
vs T3-182: Posts removed for breaking the sub's rules (T3-063), or members shutting down bigotry in a chat (T3-182)?

## T3-075  objecting to the canvas flood

gloss: Members object that canvas posts crowd out the sub's usual talk, and ask for them to stop, be condensed or move.
must_show: Members complaining that r/place posts flood the sub, are spam or off-topic, or crowd out its news and subject; asking that they stop, be condensed into one post or be removed.
must_not_show: Moderators applying a rule (T3-063) or gathering posts (T3-015) with no members objecting; judging the canvas itself worthless (T3-037); objecting to April Fools posts (T3-032).
decide_when_both: Members objecting and moderators answering with a megathread: the larger part primary, the other under rule 0. A complaint that the canvas is pointless, not that it floods the sub, is T3-037.
varies: From one weary 'please stop' to threads arguing whether the canvas enlivened or wrecked the sub; refusals to join because of the spam.
is_not: A member calling the canvas a waste of time with no word on the sub's space (T3-037).
vs T3-037: Is the complaint that canvas posts crowd the sub (T3-075), or that the canvas is not worth doing (T3-037)?
vs T3-032: Are the unwanted posts canvas posts (T3-075) or April Fools jokes (T3-032)?
vs T3-015: Are members objecting to the flood (T3-075), or is the talk being gathered into one thread so the work can go on (T3-015)?

## T3-106  calling a network boycott  [rare]

gloss: Members answer a TV network's April Fools prank by calling for a boycott: piracy, cancelled subscriptions, complaints.
must_show: Calls to boycott or punish an outside company over its April Fools joke: withholding views, piracy, cancelling subscriptions, organising complaints.
must_not_show: Judging the official joke with no action against the maker (T3-048).
decide_when_both: A theme that judges the joke and calls a boycott in concepts of their own takes T3-048 as primary and T3-106 under rule 0.
varies: From one member's private piracy to a call for someone to organise a boycott.
is_not: Objecting to joke posts inside the sub (T3-032).
vs T3-048: Do members only judge the maker's joke (T3-048), or call for action against the maker (T3-106)?

## T3-110  placing a tile as payment

gloss: A member offers to place a canvas tile in return for an upvote or a favour.
must_show: A tile offered in exchange: 'one upvote and I place a tile', a pixel promised as thanks for help with something else, tiles counted as something a member can give.
must_not_show: Vote drives with no tile given (T3-051); extra accounts used to place more (T3-088); prizes for the room's helpers (T3-159).
decide_when_both: Counting alts as placing power is T3-088; offering the tiles in trade is T3-110.
varies: From a joke offer for one upvote to a pixel promised as thanks for advice.
is_not: Rewarding the room's helpers with prizes (T3-159).
vs T3-051: Is a tile given for the vote (T3-110), or are votes gathered with nothing given (T3-051)?
vs T3-088: Are extra accounts used to place more (T3-088), or tiles offered as payment (T3-110)?
vs T3-159: Prizes or gifts for the room's helpers (T3-159), or a tile itself as the payment (T3-110)?

## T3-133  recovering a hacked sub  [rare]

gloss: The moderators recover the sub after a moderator's account is hacked and used to change it.
must_show: A moderator account compromised and used on the sub, and its repair: announcing the compromise, restoring the sub, removing what the intruder posted, explaining it to members.
must_not_show: A takeover staged as a joke (T3-023).
decide_when_both: A hack staged for April Fools is T3-023; a real compromise is T3-133.
varies: From a one-line notice that a new moderator was hacked to removing the link the intruder posted.
is_not: Members playing with moderator powers handed out for April Fools (T3-093).
vs T3-023: Is the change a joke the moderators staged (T3-023), or a real compromise they repair (T3-133)?
vs T3-093: Members playing with powers handed out for the day (T3-093), or a stolen account undone (T3-133)?

## T3-140  moderators disowning the canvas work

gloss: The sub's moderators say the canvas work is not the sub's: members may go on, but not in the sub's name.
must_show: Moderators stating that the sub as such takes no part in the canvas work: no official project, no brigading in the sub's name, members free to organise on their own.
must_not_show: Moderators gathering or removing canvas posts without saying the work is not the sub's (T3-015, T3-063); a room separating its own plan from rogue placers (T3-188).
decide_when_both: A sticky that gathers the canvas talk and disowns the work is T3-015 primary with T3-140 under rule 0.
varies: From a moderator too tired to lead who leaves members to it to a statement that the moderators have nothing to do with any canvas activity.
is_not: A room disowning rogue offshoots of its own work (T3-188).
vs T3-188: Do the moderators say the sub has no canvas project at all (T3-140), or that placers acting against the room's own plan are not the room (T3-188)?
vs T3-015: Is the thread only gathering canvas talk (T3-015), or do the moderators also say the work is not the sub's (T3-140)?

## T3-184  pausing the sub's usual events

gloss: The sub puts one of its recurring events on hold for the canvas and resumes it after.
must_show: A recurring event of the sub (a game among members, a contest, a daily thread) held off because of the canvas, or resumed once the canvas posts end.
must_not_show: Usual talk going on unchanged (T3-017); a change to the posting rules (T3-185).
decide_when_both: A theme that pauses an event and changes rules: the change to rules is T3-185, the paused event T3-184.
varies: From holding a game round for the canvas to announcing that the regular programme is back.
is_not: Removing canvas posts so that usual threads stay visible (T3-063).
vs T3-017: Does the usual business go on (T3-017), or is a recurring event stopped or restarted around the canvas (T3-184)?
vs T3-185: Is a rule about what may be posted changed (T3-185), or an event of the sub paused (T3-184)?

## T3-185  changing the sub's posting rules

gloss: Moderators change the sub's posting rules around the canvas or April Fools, and members argue the change.
must_show: A change to the sub's rules or their enforcement meant to hold: allowing or banning a kind of post for a time, new flairs or filters, a rule dropped or added, members debating what the rules should be.
must_not_show: Applying the rules as they stand (T3-063); a rule change staged as a joke for the day (T3-023).
decide_when_both: Applying and changing in one theme: the larger part primary and the other under rule 0.
varies: From a day's allowance for canvas posts to dropping a rule against memes for good.
is_not: An automatic removal under an existing rule (T3-063).
vs T3-063: Is a standing rule applied (T3-063), or is a rule made, dropped or argued (T3-185)?
vs T3-023: Is the rule change a joke for the day (T3-023), or meant to hold (T3-185)?
