# Family G04  calling people in to place

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-002  calling members in to place

gloss: Members call the room's own members to come and place for its work: to start, build, finish or hold a picture, calls to placers who are not yet a group included.
must_show: Calls to the room's members, or to placers not yet gathered into a group, to come and place: 'come help', 'we need more people', 'everyone join', rallies to build, finish or repair a work, posts asking who is in.
must_not_show: Calls aimed at other subs (T3-062) or at another canvas room as a room (T3-084); welcoming canvas visitors into the sub (T3-044). The national flag as the room's mark carries T3-065; writing the sub's name carries T3-169.
decide_when_both: A call made mostly of coordinates or a template is T3-070, with T3-002 added when the call has concepts of its own; a call to defend a work under attack is T3-005 when the defence leads.
varies: From one 'come help me out' that draws no reply to repeated rallies through a long build; calls to start, to finish, to repair; calls to placers repeating a pattern before any group exists.
is_not: Calls to build the room's national flag (T3-065).
vs T3-062: Are the room's own members called (T3-002), or the members of other subs (T3-062)?
vs T3-005: Is the call to build or finish (T3-002), or to defend against an attack under way (T3-005)?
vs T3-033: Is a mark already chosen and hands called for it (T3-002), or a mark proposed (T3-033)?
vs T3-070: Is the call itself the doing (T3-002), or the coordinates and templates that tell placers what to place (T3-070)?
vs T3-104: Are people asked to place (T3-002), or those already placing cheered on (T3-104)?

## T3-044  recruiting canvas visitors

gloss: The room's mark on the canvas brings outsiders to the sub; members welcome them, advertise the sub on the canvas for it, or count the newcomers.
must_show: Newcomers led to the sub by its canvas mark: visitors saying they came from r/place, members welcoming them, the sub's name placed so that viewers find it, subscriber growth counted.
must_not_show: The room's own members finding its mark already placed (T3-082); former fans returning (T3-196); calling members to place (T3-002).
decide_when_both: Whose eyes find the mark: outsiders led to the sub go to T3-044; the room's own members finding work they did not know of go to T3-082.
varies: From a visitor saying the sign brought them in to members tracking subscriber stats day by day; welcomes in the room's own idiom.
is_not: A member of the room discovering that the room's mark is already on the canvas (T3-082).
vs T3-082: Are outsiders led to the sub by the mark (T3-044), or members finding their own room's mark already placed (T3-082)?
vs T3-196: Is the newcomer new to the community (T3-044), or a former fan coming back (T3-196)?
vs T3-169: Is the sub's name written to sign the work (T3-169), or to bring visitors in (T3-044)?

## T3-062  recruiting kindred subs to help

gloss: Members take the room's call into other subs, mostly kindred ones (sister, regional, related fandoms), to bring more hands to the room's work.
must_show: The room's call for hands carried into other subs or communities, or proposed: 'ask r/X for help', threads opened in other subs, recruiting from related communities.
must_not_show: Calls to the room's own members (T3-002); one canvas room asking another, as a group, to help or make room (T3-084); a hub sub gathering kindred rooms (T3-013).
decide_when_both: A request to an organised canvas group, room to room, is T3-084; a call for hands posted to a community, T3-062.
varies: From a suggestion to ask a neighbouring sports sub to threads posted across many subs by a dying faction.
is_not: Sending the room's members to help another room's work (T3-011).
vs T3-002: Are the room's own members called (T3-002), or other subs' members (T3-062)?
vs T3-084: Is another sub's membership asked for hands (T3-062), or another canvas group asked, as a group, to help with this room's work (T3-084)?
vs T3-013: Is help sought for this room's work (T3-062), or are kindred rooms gathered into one hub (T3-013)?
vs T3-011: Is help asked for (T3-062), or sent to another room's work (T3-011)?

## T3-104  cheering the builders on

gloss: Members and passers-by cheer those placing: praise, encouragement and good wishes while the work goes on.
must_show: Encouragement aimed at builders during the work: 'keep going', 'nice work team', 'good luck', praise from members or from outsiders who may not place themselves.
must_not_show: Calls for more hands (T3-002); celebrating the finished work (T3-009); thanking or crediting helpers afterwards (T3-072, T3-043).
decide_when_both: Praise while the work goes on is T3-104; once the work is called finished, T3-009.
varies: From a passer-by's 'good job to everyone chipping in' to members keeping each other company through a night's building.
is_not: Celebrating the finished mark (T3-009).
vs T3-002: Are people asked to come and place (T3-002), or those placing encouraged (T3-104)?
vs T3-009: Is the work going on (T3-104), or done and celebrated (T3-009)?
vs T3-043: Are builders encouraged (T3-104), or the makers credited (T3-043)?

## T3-129  making passers-by notice a demand

gloss: Members use the canvas to put a demand before passers-by: a wanted comeback, a new season, a sauce.
must_show: A mark planned or placed so that people viewing the canvas see a demand or announcement the room wants noticed by others (a company, a creator, Redditors at large).
must_not_show: The sub's name placed to bring visitors to the sub (T3-044); the room's emblem proposed as its mark (T3-033).
decide_when_both: A demand written over another room's art is T3-068 for the converting, with T3-129 added for the demand when it has a concept of its own.
varies: From a wish that random Redditors notice a band's wanted comeback to a giant request for a fast-food sauce.
is_not: A sub writing its own name for canvas visitors (T3-044).
vs T3-044: Is the mark meant to bring visitors to the sub (T3-044), or to make others notice a demand (T3-129)?
vs T3-033: Is a mark proposed as the room's emblem (T3-033), or as a message carrying a demand (T3-129)?

## T3-144  recruiting quietly to avoid attackers  [rare]

gloss: Members recruit only inside friendly communities and keep the work small, so that it draws no attackers.
must_show: Recruiting held back or kept inside friendly communities because publicity might bring attackers; a small presence chosen for safety.
must_not_show: Hiding the plan's words from rival readers (T3-116); calling for hands in the open (T3-002, T3-062).
decide_when_both: Keeping the call quiet is T3-144; keeping the plan's text from readers is T3-116.
varies: From weighing whether publicity would do more damage to recruiting through a few friendly chats.
is_not: Writing the plan in a code so that rivals cannot read it (T3-116).
vs T3-116: Is the recruiting kept quiet so attackers do not come (T3-144), or the plan hidden from rivals who read it (T3-116)?
vs T3-062: Is the call kept inside friendly communities for safety (T3-144), or carried to other subs to bring hands (T3-062)?

## T3-159  rewarding helpers with prizes

gloss: Members reward those who helped the work with prizes: gold, medals, game codes, giveaways.
must_show: A prize offered or given for work on the canvas or for the room's effort: Reddit gold, mock medals, game codes, giveaways.
must_not_show: Thanks or credit with no prize (T3-072, T3-043); a tile offered as payment (T3-110).
decide_when_both: Thanks and a prize in one theme: T3-159 when the prize has a concept of its own, beside the thanking kind.
varies: From gold offered before a feat to mock medals and a giveaway after.
is_not: A pixel promised as thanks for help with something else (T3-110).
vs T3-043: Is credit given in words (T3-043), or a prize given (T3-159)?
vs T3-110: Is a prize given to helpers (T3-159), or a tile itself offered as payment (T3-110)?

## T3-189  fearing newcomers the mark draws

gloss: Members fear what the canvas mark will bring into the sub: an influx of newcomers who do not read the rules or swamp the community.
must_show: Worry about the newcomers the mark draws: an influx that will swamp the sub or its site, newcomers who ignore the rules, the sub said to have gone downhill since.
must_not_show: Welcoming newcomers (T3-044); objecting that canvas posts flood the sub (T3-075).
decide_when_both: A theme that welcomes and fears the influx: the side with more concepts is primary, the other under rule 0.
varies: From jokes that the Foundation cannot risk discovery to complaints that the influx ruined the sub.
is_not: Members welcoming visitors who came from r/place (T3-044).
vs T3-044: Are the newcomers the mark draws welcomed (T3-044), or feared (T3-189)?
vs T3-075: Is the complaint about canvas posts crowding the sub (T3-075), or about the people the mark brings in (T3-189)?

## T3-196  drawing lapsed fans back

gloss: The room's mark on the canvas brings former fans back to the community; members welcome them or hope it will.
must_show: Former members or fans returning because they saw the room's mark; members hoping the canvas will revive the sub.
must_not_show: Newcomers led to the sub by the mark (T3-044).
decide_when_both: A theme welcoming both: the larger group of concepts decides; the other is added under rule 0 when it has three or more concepts.
varies: From one returning fan's fond memories to a sub hoping the canvas wakes it up.
is_not: A visitor who never knew the community (T3-044).
vs T3-044: Is the person new to the community (T3-044), or a former fan coming back (T3-196)?
