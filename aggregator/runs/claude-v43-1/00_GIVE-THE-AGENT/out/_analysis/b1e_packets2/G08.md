# Family G08  building and growing the mark

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-003  reporting one's own placing labour

gloss: Members report their own part in the placing: what they placed, fixed or defended, and when.
must_show: A member telling of their own placing: 'I helped', 'I put in my part', 'I placed my first pixel', what they fixed or defended, the hours they spent.
must_not_show: The progress of the room's work as a whole (T3-038); one member's lone tribute (T3-092); others credited (T3-043).
decide_when_both: A member's own share told inside a celebration: T3-009 primary when the celebrating leads, T3-003 when the member's share is the point.
varies: From 'I helped! Yay me!' to accounts of hours of repair; told during the work and after the close.
is_not: A member making a small tribute alone (T3-092).
vs T3-038: Is the room's work reported (T3-038), or one member's share in it (T3-003)?
vs T3-092: Is the share part of the room's work (T3-003), or a small piece the member made alone (T3-092)?
vs T3-043: Does the member tell their own part (T3-003), or credit others (T3-043)?
vs T3-009: Is the finished work celebrated (T3-009), or one's own part in it told (T3-003)?

## T3-026  announcing the mark is up

gloss: Members announce that the room's mark is on the canvas: 'we got on r/place', with a picture or its place.
must_show: Announcements that the room is on the canvas: 'we made it onto r/place', a screenshot or link showing the room's mark, others pointed to where it is.
must_not_show: Members discovering a mark of the room that they did not know of (T3-082); celebrating the finished work at or after the close (T3-009); reports of progress (T3-038).
decide_when_both: An announcement that is also a celebration: T3-026 while the work stands during placement, T3-009 when the finished work is celebrated.
varies: From a one-line post to a weekly roundup that names the mark.
is_not: A member finding that someone made the sub's logo without the room (T3-082).
vs T3-082: Is the room's own work announced (T3-026), or a mark found that the room did not know of (T3-082)?
vs T3-009: Is the mark announced as being up (T3-026), or the finished work celebrated (T3-009)?
vs T3-038: Is the mark's progress reported (T3-038), or its presence announced (T3-026)?

## T3-038  building and reporting the mark

gloss: Members build the mark and report its progress: started, half done, almost there, the next part.
must_show: Placing the mark and reports of its state: started, coordinates of the work in progress, half done, almost finished, the parts done and left.
must_not_show: Calls for hands (T3-002); the design argued (T3-004); the finished work celebrated (T3-009); repair after an attack (T3-021).
decide_when_both: Building with calls for hands or with repairs: the larger part primary and the other under rule 0.
varies: From a start at given coordinates to running reports until the close.
is_not: Restoring the mark after an attack (T3-021).
vs T3-002: Is the work placed and reported (T3-038), or are hands called for it (T3-002)?
vs T3-021: Is the mark built for the first time (T3-038), or restored after damage (T3-021)?
vs T3-009: Is the work under way (T3-038), or finished and celebrated (T3-009)?
vs T3-003: Is the room's mark reported (T3-038), or one member's own share (T3-003)?
vs T3-073: Is the first build's progress reported (T3-038), or what happened to the work once it stood (T3-073)?

## T3-041  extending the room's work

gloss: The room adds pieces to its work or enlarges it: a second picture, another album, a bigger logo, details around the first.
must_show: Proposals or work to add to the room's existing mark or to make another: a next piece, a bigger version, additions around the first.
must_not_show: The room's first mark proposed (T3-033); a member adding a personal piece to a shared mark (T3-060); spreading a faction's colour over others' work (T3-171).
decide_when_both: Additions argued as design: T3-004 when the existing mark's look is settled, T3-041 when a new piece or an enlargement is added.
varies: From a cake beside a logo to a run of album covers; enlarged letters; details added around a figure.
is_not: The room's first proposal of a mark (T3-033).
vs T3-033: Is the room's first mark proposed (T3-033), or more added beside or around it (T3-041)?
vs T3-060: Does the room extend its own work (T3-041), or does a member add a personal piece to a shared mark (T3-060)?
vs T3-042: Is growth pursued (T3-041), or set aside for upkeep (T3-042)?
vs T3-004: Is the existing mark's look settled (T3-004), or a new piece or enlargement added (T3-041)?

## T3-042  choosing upkeep over expansion

gloss: The room puts keeping what it holds before growing: maintain, clean, defend first, no new fronts.
must_show: Upkeep chosen over growth: calls to maintain, clean or defend before expanding; refusals of new projects or attacks because the room cannot hold more; designs scaled to what can be defended.
must_not_show: Concentrating effort on fewer of the room's pieces (T3-069); repairs as such (T3-021); reining in the room's own attacks (T3-076).
decide_when_both: Choosing among the room's own pieces is T3-069; choosing holding over growing is T3-042.
varies: From a refusal to anger another sub to a faction's 'clean first' against its expansionists.
is_not: Dropping one of the room's pieces to save pixels for another (T3-069).
vs T3-069: Is one of the room's pieces dropped for another (T3-069), or growth set aside to keep what it holds (T3-042)?
vs T3-041: Is growth pursued (T3-041), or set aside (T3-042)?
vs T3-021: Is the work repaired (T3-021), or upkeep argued as the room's priority over growth (T3-042)?
vs T3-076: Is the room's own attack on others stopped (T3-076), or upkeep put before expansion (T3-042)?

## T3-060  adding a personal piece

gloss: Members add their own piece to a mark many share: a word, a heart, a drug's name, a character.
must_show: A member or a small group adding their own part to a shared mark: their word, symbol or character placed into a common work.
must_not_show: The room extending its own work (T3-041); a lone tribute apart from any shared mark (T3-092); converting a neighbour's art (T3-068).
decide_when_both: A personal addition the room then adopts: T3-060 for the adding, T3-041 when the room takes it up as its own extension.
varies: From one drug's name in a molecule area to words and hearts added to a shared flag.
is_not: A member making a tribute alone, away from any shared mark (T3-092).
vs T3-041: Does the room extend its work (T3-041), or does a member add a personal piece to a shared mark (T3-060)?
vs T3-092: Is the piece added to a shared mark (T3-060), or made alone apart from one (T3-092)?
vs T3-056: Is a joint mark of kindred rooms made (T3-056), or a personal piece added inside it (T3-060)?

## T3-069  concentrating effort on fewer pieces

gloss: The room drops or pauses some of its pieces to put its pixels on one: one priority, one attack point, the rest abandoned.
must_show: Effort concentrated: one piece named the priority, others abandoned or paused, 'all focus on this', a design scaled down to fit the hands.
must_not_show: Upkeep chosen over growth with no piece dropped (T3-042); the room's strength doubted (T3-136); placers held to one template (T3-091).
decide_when_both: Concentrating and doubting in one theme: T3-069 when the room acts on the choice, T3-136 when it doubts.
varies: From abandoning a television to save a face to a faction's single attack point; pieces scaled down to the hands at hand.
is_not: Refusing a new front to keep the existing mark (T3-042).
vs T3-042: Is one of the room's pieces dropped for another (T3-069), or growth set aside to keep what it holds (T3-042)?
vs T3-136: Does the room put its pixels on fewer pieces (T3-069), or doubt it can manage any (T3-136)?
vs T3-091: Are the room's pieces narrowed to fewer (T3-069), or placers held to one design of one piece (T3-091)?

## T3-082  finding the room's mark placed

varies: From surprise at a logo someone made to members joining the work they found.
is_not: A visitor finding the sub through its mark (T3-044).
vs T3-044: Do the room's members find their own mark (T3-082), or are outsiders led to the sub by it (T3-044)?
vs T3-026: Is a mark found that the room did not know of (T3-082), or the room's own work announced (T3-026)?
vs T3-175: Is the room's own subject found drawn (T3-082), or another's sign adopted as the room's (T3-175)?

## T3-175  adopting a sign others began

gloss: The room adopts as its own a sign others began: a neighbour's character made a mascot, a stray symbol claimed.
must_show: A work begun by others taken up by the room as its own: a mascot adopted, a character given the room's nationality or creed, a stray symbol claimed.
must_not_show: Another's art converted by placing over it (T3-068); the room's own subject found drawn (T3-082).
decide_when_both: Adopting with a recolouring: T3-068 when the art is placed over, T3-175 when it is claimed with little or no change.
varies: From Squirtle made a Portuguese hero to a party parrot declared a comrade.
is_not: Painting the room's flag over a neighbour's work (T3-068).
vs T3-068: Is another's art recoloured into the room's (T3-068), or adopted with little or no change (T3-175)?
vs T3-082: Is the room's own subject found drawn (T3-082), or another's sign taken up as the room's (T3-175)?

## T3-187  handing the work over

gloss: A member who led the work hands it to the community: maintainership passed on, the project kept community-run, a leader stepping down.
must_show: A leader or organiser passing the work's care to the community, keeping it community-run, or stepping down from it.
must_not_show: A member quitting under strain (T3-181); one member put in charge (T3-101).
decide_when_both: A leader stepping down tired: T3-181 when strain is the reason, T3-187 when the work is passed on.
varies: From open-source maintainership passed to the community before a flight to a moderator retiring after a last tile.
is_not: A member quitting because the work became stressful (T3-181).
vs T3-101: Is one member put in charge (T3-101), or does the leader hand the work to all (T3-187)?
vs T3-181: Does a member quit under strain (T3-181), or pass the work on (T3-187)?
