## 13. What would make you doubt this is a form at all

I already report F24 as a grouping. Three things make me question even what it tracks.

First, the coders. The key is a restriction, a statement of what the record does not establish. Second, size. The median member has 144 coded concepts, against the population's 101. The canvas median area sits at the 69.7th percentile; I read that only afterwards, and it grounds nothing.

What would make me read it as a form: in most members, the argument settled into a choice the room kept, scripts used or refused, with the placements that followed shown in the record.
