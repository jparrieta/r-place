## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A07: restricted in 7 of 27 members, against 20 of 175, an excess of 14.5 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 3.7 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A08 is next, at 12.2 points.

A07 runs from P03 (the build and its reports) to P11 (marking the finished work). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first build report comes before the first marking of the finished work". Its mechanism: "A mark reported done gives the room a finished thing to point at, and the room marks it together: announcement, credit, pride." Its bound allows two exceptions: a mark found already made, and a work the close cut short.

By my reading of their `how` sentences, with one room model, 8 of the 9 restrict and add-condition lines say that the marking did not close the work. In four members a finish was declared and then corrected: r/OnePiece, r/pcmasterrace and r/lakers by their lines, and r/greece by its room model ("“We did it” receives demands for a border, square boxes and a move to correct the aspect ratio"), whose line says only that the report and the celebration came in one announcement. r/pcmasterrace: "Celebrating completion can be immediately followed by correction rather than establish a settled finished form". At hour 7.13 a member wrote: "We did it!" [pcmasterrace/c0381]. Within a minute another answered that a letter "is only two pixels wide (should be three), which means there is an extra pixel gap between the two R's" [pcmasterrace/c0382]. In r/lakers a member announced the logo at hour 57.12; eleven minutes later another answered: "Not quite done in that pic" [lakers/c0183]. In four more, members celebrated while repair or defence went on (r/babymetal, r/empiredidnothingwrong, r/gravityfalls, r/metalcore). In r/metalcore at hour 7.03: "Our labour as core-soldiers has ended. Good job guys." [metalcore/c0003]. At hour 21.09 another member wrote in the same thread: "No, the fight is not over. I volunteered as janitor and am now cleaning up some intrudors" [metalcore/c0023]. The ninth line, r/frankocean's, marks a work the room stopped short of finishing so as not to damage a neighbour's: "Although it's not 100% finished I think we should stop now cause we'll fuck up the wu tang logo if we go any further." [frankocean/c0105].

The first group meets the key. A finish declared draws a correction toward the design, and that correction is the change the keepers then repair away. r/OnePiece shows the whole run: "finally perfect", then "Not perfect", then the jaw's fix taken for vandalism (section 1). Its line on A07: "A completion announcement can elicit specific correction and renewed repair rather than agreement on a settled final image." The room models of r/OnePiece and r/greece each propose the same new step, which the second calls "returning a declared finish to correction". So the excess here is partly the cut seen at the moment of completion.

This is description, not a test. A07 has 2 within-case datings, neither in F27, so its expectation in F27 is 0.077, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A07, shared lines only: restrict 6, add-condition 2, instantiate 2.
