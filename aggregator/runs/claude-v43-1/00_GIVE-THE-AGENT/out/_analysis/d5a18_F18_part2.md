Members judged by survival, by whether the figure could be recognised, by whether damage suited the room, by a place on the final canvas. They revised the judgment: one member called the work a failure and four minutes later, after refreshing the page, called it good (r/kcroyals). And a "done" did not end the work. Members called the work finished and went back to repair, defence or more building in the same thread.

**Stage D4's name, read against the members.** The name is "success judged while upkeep continues". Its two clauses hold for different members: success judged in 7, upkeep after "done" in 5, both in 1. And "while upkeep continues" does not date the judging.
