## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A09: restricted in 12 of 30 members, against 38 of 239, an excess of 24.1 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 3.3 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A19 is next, at 9.9 points. A09 was also the arrow of F11, F12 and F13, the other branches on P13.

A09 runs from P12 (the record) to P13 (carrying on). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first record of what happened comes before the first act of carrying the work on". Its mechanism: "A record of the work gives the room something to carry: the image into the banner, the plan into the next canvas." Its bound already says: "Where a room planned its next canvas or its emblem while still building, carrying on comes before any record."

By my reading of their `how` sentences, three of the 12 restrict lines are on the order. Two fall inside that bound; the third adds a case it does not name. r/greyblob: "A final record is not a necessary precursor to successor activity, and later coexistence of preservation and invitations does not establish a causal link." Its members posted the blob on another canvas at hour 39 [greyblob/c0037], and the record came at the close: "For posterity" [greyblob/c0079]. r/thebluecorner: "Prior memorial recording does not explain every successor effort when a parallel corner is already offered during original placement cooldowns." That corner was offered at hour 39 (section 1). r/enterthegungeon: "Proposed reuse can prompt retrieval of the record, so a completed record need not precede the wish to reuse it." A member asked for the record in order to reuse it: "If someone has a picture saved that would be great." [enterthegungeon/c0099]. Another answered with a link, and the first wrote: "Thanks!" [enterthegungeon/c0102].

Eight restrict a cause, not the order. r/rotmg: "Successor builders reuse an old sprite, but they do not state that recording the original result motivated their move." There the order holds. A member posted the record at hour 73.13: "Place has ended! Here's what we ended with for" the room [rotmg/c0248]. Nearly seven hours later another wrote: "They made a new one and i've started. I need help though." [rotmg/c0278]. Later a member brought the old record to the new work: "Well anyway here's the sprite (taken from the /r/place thread): http://i.imgur.com/eoZ6hC3.png" [rotmg/c0286]. In r/naruto a member started "the build based on the template for r/place" [naruto/c0213]. The record was carried, as the mechanism says, but no member said the record made them go on. The connection states an order only, so these eight lines restrict the mechanism. The same reading of a cause into an order arrow was logged for A08, A09 and A20 in earlier branches. One more line, r/DestinyTheGame's, restricts the deployment: "Prepared images support a reuse proposal but technical constraints separate endorsement from demonstrated deployment."

This is the fourth branch on P13 in which A09 runs differently (F11, F12, F13 and F23). Here two of the three order lines fall inside the arrow's own bound, and most lines deny a cause. The excess may be what coders write about any room that carried its work on.

This is description, not a test. A09 has 2 within-case datings, so its expectation in F23 is 0.085, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A09, shared lines only: restrict 8, instantiate 2.
