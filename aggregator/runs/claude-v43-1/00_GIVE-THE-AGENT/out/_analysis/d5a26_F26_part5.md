## 12. The form test

**The constructs.** P05 (keeping) restricted at T3-010 (settling borders with neighbours), under T4-016 (Negotiated order), with P08 (borders and deals) and P07 (sparing), present in every member, and P21 (who may commit the room) in 13.

**The relations the form would need.** Two rooms agree a border, and the agreement changes one room's work (P08, P02). The room's keepers repair the change as damage until they are told (P05). Told, they stop and spare the neighbour (P07). Where the telling does not reach them, the change is lost. Where members know the terms and do not accept them, they undo the change on purpose, and some deny that the agreement binds them (P21). Across members these come in parts. By my reading of the coders' lines, the record does not show the clause's parts in 17 members, informed members contest the terms in 10, and mistaken repair is shown in 7. In members' cited words, told keepers stopped in at least five rooms, and in two the change was fought or lost where the telling did not reach. No member's passages show all of it.

**The four organizing problems.** The branch binds information: P05's clause is about who learns of a planned change, and at the border the news has to cross from one room's thread or chat to the placers of both rooms. It leaves division, allocation and reward free. A remainder is supported, as at F07 and F10: where members know the terms and reject them, the problem is agreement, not information. Here the agreement is between two rooms, and members of either room could refuse it. The four problems take one organization and its goal as given; these were two rooms with no common authority.

**What varies among members.** Whose change was undone: in 3 members the change undone is not shown to be one the room had agreed (r/lifeisstrange, r/metroid, r/poland), and in r/metroid it was a neighbour's. How the news travelled: by the thread, by a chat (r/stevenuniverse, r/python), or to a script's operator. Who refused: one member, a night crew, or members loyal to the template (r/monalisaclan). No subform candidate stands on keys: all 23 key lines lift through T3-010, and no finer node separates them. Telling through a chat is a particular of a few histories, and F16 tracks it apart.

**Result: a grouping, not a form.** The constructs and their relations can be named, and members' words show the relations in parts in several rooms. But the key is a restriction of one element at one theme, most of its lines say what the record does not show, and no relation between two constructs is in every member's line. Unlike F09, the branch states no such relation. The branch bounds one claim, F-026, on cases: at the border the telling clause needs the news to cross between two rooms, and it settles the change only where those told on both sides accept the terms.

## 13. What would make you doubt this is a form at all

I already report F26 as a grouping. Three things make me question even what it tracks.

First, the readers. The key lines are re-check lines, written when P05's clause was newly served to every room. Four of the 23 lines name the "added", "revised" or "new" mechanism (r/maryland, r/NewMexico, r/ravens and r/soylent). If readers tested the new clause wherever they saw a border, the branch partly tracks the readers' test of a new sentence, not what the rooms did. Their lines mostly say the clause is not shown, while the members' words show it in at least five rooms. F-026 rests on the cited words, which do not depend on the readers' habit.

Second, the cut and its cousins. The key restates P05's clause at one theme, and section 7's arrow, A05, restates it from the neighbour's side. F07 and F27 restrict the same clause at other themes. Read together, the three branches may track one change to P05, split by where the coders lifted their lines. These are large rooms: the median member has 135 coded concepts against the population's 101, and the canvas median area sits at the 95.9th percentile. I read that only afterwards, and it grounds nothing.

Third, the ending. F26's median organizing end is hour 72.22, against 72.02 over all rooms and 71.93 in F10. Its mean aftermath share is 0.0373, against 0.0200 over all rooms and 0.0145 in F10, but two members carry it (r/NewMexico, 0.333; r/placeportalpattern, 0.25), and without r/NewMexico it is 0.0226. Work is kept in 11 of 23 members, lost in 10 (43 per cent, against 31 per cent over all rooms) and silent in 2 (9 per cent, against 20). Members' records name the work's fate more often than most, and a loss more often. The survival figures come from the hours and words of the records, not from the coders' ops. They do not say why.

What would make me read it as a form: in most members, the change an agreement made, the keepers' knowledge of it, and its fate, all shown in the record.
