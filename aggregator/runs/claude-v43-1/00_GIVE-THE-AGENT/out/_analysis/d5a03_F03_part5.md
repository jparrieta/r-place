## 12. The form test

**The constructs.** K01, the April Fools frame, at T3-095 (the joke tested), under T4-001 (Keying). T4-003 (Sensemaking) is over-represented in the branch, and the test is a kind of working out what a thing is.

**The relations the form would need.** A claim is posted under the licence. A member doubts it. Members check it by its date, its source or its marks. The reading is repaired, or the claim stands, and a member who believed it comes back. r/ontario shows the whole sequence in one thread. Across members it is shown in part: 7 of 18 show two of its parts, the date with a repaired reading in 3, and every member holds one line.

**The four organizing problems.** The branch binds information. Members give each other what they need to judge a claim: the date, the source, the marks of a real card or a real part. It leaves division, allocation and reward free. The joker's satisfaction in r/ontario ("If I can just get one person, it was worth it.") is a particular, not a reward the room provides. I find no remainder beyond the information problem.

**What varies among members.** What the test used (source and marks in 8 members, the date in 5) and what it led to (a repaired reading in 5, troubleshooting in 3, a sincere exchange in 3). The form, if it were one, would hold these as its variants. No subform candidate stands on keys. Two members are particulars of one history: r/PUBATTLEGROUNDS, where members read what looked like a joke as a hack, and r/reddevils, where the test reached a ticket sale.

**Result: a grouping, not a form.** Of the three F01 subforms read so far, F03 comes closest. One practice, its evidence and its outcomes can be named, and one member shows them in order. But each member holds one line, the relations among the parts are shown in few members, and the practice binds no relation to the rest of the model. The test applied to the sub's own claims; in the members' codes, no canvas call was tested as a joke.

## 13. What would make you doubt this is a form at all

I already report F03 as a grouping. Three things make me question whether it tracks more than the coders' reading of reply threads.

First, the coders. Any sub with a fake post drew replies that doubted it. 354 rooms instantiate K01, and 18 carry this step. The branch may sort subs by whether a coder read the replies as a test.

Second, size, which here runs the other way. The median member has 158 coded concepts against 101 over all rooms, a smaller excess than in F01 and F02. The canvas median area sits at the 9th percentile: these members left smaller works than random groups of their size. Size does not drive this branch's canvas figures, and the canvas says nothing about the test.

Third, the ending. F03's survival figures are close to the population's: a median organizing end at hour 71.21 against 72.02, and work kept in 9 of 18 members against 347 of 705, lost in 6 and silent in 3.

What would make me read it as a form: K01's own clause, a canvas call tested as a joke, found in several members. I searched for it and found none.
