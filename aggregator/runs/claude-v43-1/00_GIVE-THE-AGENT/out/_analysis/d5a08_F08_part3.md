## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

A06 runs from P05 (keeping) to P14 (machine hands). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first act of upkeep or defence comes before the first script, bot or extra account". In 18 of 50 members a current-ledger line restricts it: the script came to build the work, and keeping was one of its jobs from the start. r/starcitizen shows both sides in one post at h+48.53. The building: "Every 5 minutes it will automatically colour in our logo!" The keeping: "The more people the better as there's less chance of people reverting our changes!" [starcitizen/c0218]. The coders' line: "Automation also supported initial construction and resistance to reversions rather than entering only after completed-work upkeep became overwhelming." The script then served a neighbour as well: "If we combine both logo in that script, we'll simultaneously build our logo and help keep theirs repaired. Pay them back for the help they're giving us." [starcitizen/c0220]. One member ran it with a reservation: "I know the script isn't the most honest way to get our logo on there, but thanks for putting it together." [starcitizen/c0228].

In these rooms the order A06 states, upkeep first and then scripts, holds for the scripts added under attack. It misses the scripts that started the work. As section 6 shows, that is true of rooms that run scripts in general, not of this branch alone.
