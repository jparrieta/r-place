# Family G12  attacking others' work

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-006  calling to deface rivals' work

gloss: Members call for, plan or carry out attacks that destroy, black out or erase another room's work.
must_show: Calls, plans or reports of attacks on another room's work meant to destroy or erase it: 'attack the Danish flag', 'wipe out Arizona', 'we must destroy the blue corner', strikes timed against a rival.
must_not_show: The room's work grown over a neighbour's space (T3-061); another's art turned into the room's own sign (T3-068); griefing for fun (T3-192); defending the room's work (T3-005).
decide_when_both: Replacing or attacking another room's mark, another nation's flag included: destroying or blacking it out is T3-006; turning it into the room's sign is T3-068; growing the room's work over the neighbour's space is T3-061. A theme with more than one: the larger part primary, the others under rule 0.
varies: From one member's 'let's attack' to timed strikes with coordinates; rivalries of sport, nation, fandom and colour.
is_not: Painting the room's colours or symbol into a neighbour's art (T3-068).
vs T3-061: Is the rival's work destroyed (T3-006), or is its space taken for the room's own work (T3-061)?
vs T3-068: Is the rival's work destroyed (T3-006), or turned into the room's own sign (T3-068)?
vs T3-192: Is the attack called for the room (T3-006), or a member's griefing for fun (T3-192)?
vs T3-005: Is another's work attacked (T3-006), or the room's own defended (T3-005)?
vs T3-128: Is the call the room's own (T3-006), or a streamer's relayed (T3-128)?
vs T3-076: Is an attack called for (T3-006), or reined in (T3-076)?
vs T3-067: Is a rival attacked for the room (T3-006), or a third room's work bargained away to a neighbour (T3-067)?
vs T3-085: Are flags attacked (T3-006), or weighed against the room's art (T3-085)?
vs T3-086: Is a rival's work defaced (T3-006), or the community's own quarrel carried into the marks (T3-086)?
vs T3-102: Is the attack called for by the room (T3-006), or pressed by a member against the room's word (T3-102)?
vs T3-171: Is a rival's work destroyed (T3-006), or the faction's colour spread over whatever lies in its way (T3-171)?
vs T3-176: Is the rival's work defaced by members placing (T3-006), or by a script planted on the rival (T3-176)?
vs T3-177: Is a rival's work defaced (T3-006), or a swastika drawn (T3-177)?
vs T3-178: Is a canvas work attacked (T3-006), or one person on another site (T3-178)?

## T3-061  pushing into a neighbour's space

gloss: The room grows its own work into space a neighbour holds: expanding over another flag or picture, taking a neighbour's ground.
must_show: The room's work extended over a neighbour's space, or proposals to take it: 'take over the Hungary flag', 'keep expanding west', 'wage war for space'.
must_not_show: Destroying a rival's work with no gain of space (T3-006); converting another's art into the room's own sign (T3-068); growing into empty canvas (T3-041).
decide_when_both: See T3-006.
varies: From a proposal to fight for room beside a cloud to a nation's flag pushed across its neighbours' flags.
is_not: Adding a second piece in empty canvas (T3-041).
vs T3-006: Is the neighbour's space taken for the room's work (T3-061), or the neighbour's work destroyed (T3-006)?
vs T3-068: Is the room's own work grown over the neighbour (T3-061), or the neighbour's art turned into the room's sign (T3-068)?
vs T3-041: Does the room grow into empty space or onto its own work (T3-041), or into a neighbour's space (T3-061)?
vs T3-010: Is the space taken (T3-061), or agreed (T3-010)?
vs T3-171: Is a picture grown into a neighbour's space (T3-061), or a colour spread as the aim (T3-171)?
vs T3-192: Is a work griefed for fun and owned as a joke (T3-192), or the room's work pushed into a neighbour's space for its own ends (T3-061)?

## T3-067  trading away another room's work  [rare]

gloss: In a deal with a neighbour, a room offers to remove a third room's work.
must_show: A third room's work offered up in a bargain between two rooms: 'I'll help get rid of the frog if we can keep ...'.
must_not_show: Help bartered with no harm to a third room (T3-151); a rival attacked directly (T3-006).
decide_when_both: The bargain is T3-151 when no third party is harmed, T3-067 when one is offered up.
varies: One room's offer only.
is_not: Trading help between two rooms with no third party harmed (T3-151).
vs T3-151: Is help traded between two rooms alone (T3-151), or a third room's work offered up in the trade (T3-067)?
vs T3-006: Is a rival attacked for the room (T3-006), or a third room's work bargained away to a neighbour (T3-067)?

## T3-068  converting a neighbour's art

gloss: Members turn another room's art into the room's own sign: a logo changed into theirs, a flag recoloured into the room's flag, a symbol rewritten.
must_show: Another's mark altered into the room's own sign or joke: 'turn their symbol into ours', 'change Finland's flag into ours', a logo rewritten with the room's letters.
must_not_show: Destroying it (T3-006); taking its space for a separate work (T3-061); adopting it unchanged (T3-175).
decide_when_both: See T3-006. A national flag chosen as target for political reasons adds T3-130 when that choice has concepts of its own.
varies: From a pokeball turned into a band's emblem to national flags recoloured into the room's flag.
is_not: Adopting a neighbour's character as a mascot with no change (T3-175).
vs T3-006: Is the other's art turned into the room's sign (T3-068), or destroyed (T3-006)?
vs T3-061: Is the other's art converted (T3-068), or its space taken for the room's own work (T3-061)?
vs T3-175: Is another's sign changed into the room's (T3-068), or adopted with little or no change (T3-175)?
vs T3-130: Is the converting the doing (T3-068), or the national politics read into the choice of target (T3-130)?
vs T3-071: Is the work taken in with consent (T3-071), or converted by placing over it (T3-068)?
vs T3-060: Does a member add their own piece to a mark many share (T3-060), or turn another room's art into the room's sign (T3-068)?

## T3-128  relaying a streamer's attack call  [rare]

gloss: Members relay a streamer's call for the audience to attack another work, and join it.
must_show: A streamer's call to attack passed on in the room: 'call to arms from Tyler1', a streamer named as leading an attack, members asked to join the streamer's army.
must_not_show: The room's own call to attack (T3-006).
decide_when_both: A streamer's call and the room's own in one theme: T3-128 when the streamer's call leads.
varies: From a one-line call to arms to a streamer's army joined by allied factions.
is_not: A room's own members calling an attack on a rival (T3-006).
vs T3-006: Is the call the room's own (T3-006), or a streamer's relayed (T3-128)?
vs T3-062: Are hands sought for the room's work (T3-062), or for a streamer's attack (T3-128)?

## T3-178  brigading one person off Reddit

gloss: Members propose or join harassing one person on another site: spamming an Instagram account, screwing with an actor on Twitter.
must_show: A named or pictured individual targeted on another site by members acting together.
must_not_show: Attacks on canvas works (T3-006); taunting a rival community (T3-050).
decide_when_both: A raid on a person within a taunting theme: T3-178 when the person is targeted on another site.
varies: From a joke request to screw with an actor on Twitter to a planned spam raid on one Instagram user.
is_not: Taunting a rival sub (T3-050).
vs T3-006: Is a canvas work attacked (T3-006), or one person on another site (T3-178)?
vs T3-050: Is a rival community taunted (T3-050), or one person harassed (T3-178)?
