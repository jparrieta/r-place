# The scheme: index

Rule 0. **Single placement, exception on the record.** A second-order theme carries one third-order theme: the one whose gate its material satisfies best; where more than one gate is satisfied, the theme's `decide_when_both` field decides. A second placement only where one cannot carry the material, and never silently: it needs its own distinct supporting concept, a written `why_not_one` in terms of what the single placement would fail to carry, a named dispensable one, and a line in `multiplacement_log.jsonl`. Three at most; beyond three, place one, record `item-is-a-bag`, and name every other theme the material satisfies under `also_present`, each with its own concept ids, so the cap drops no theme from the record. Every placement you make yourself names the concepts that carry each theme it places (`support_by_theme`, or `--codes` to the writer verbs). An unjustified extra is a defect. The log is a measurement: recurring `(primary, additional)` pairs are boundaries the scheme has not drawn, and stage C1 reads that table and the `also_present` record first. (J, 2026-10-11: the `also_present` record and the concepts behind a placement.)

Every family below has its own page (`<family>.md`) with each theme's full tests. Read this index whole; open a family's page before placing anything into it, and record the families you opened on the line you return (`families_opened`).

## G01  April Fools in the sub

- **T3-001**  staging April Fools fake news: Members post their own April Fools fakes in the sub as if true (news, leaks, patches, releases, claims) and play along with them, fall for them or reveal them.
- **T3-023**  running an April Fools takeover: The sub is turned into something else for April Fools, by its moderators or by the sub as a body (renamed, restyled, given new rules or a new subject, or one sub's joke raid on another), and members play inside it.
- **T3-032**  objecting to April Fools posts: Members object to the April Fools jokes in their sub as unfunny, stale, hurtful or in the way, and ask for them to stop or come down.
- **T3-048**  reacting to official April Fools: Members react to the April Fools jokes of the company, creator, channel or organisation the sub follows: waiting for them, judging them, comparing years, missing them.
- **T3-064**  spreading the room's joke elsewhere: Members carry their sub's joke or in-joke into other subs or sites, or show another sub what the room is doing.
- **T3-093** [rare]  playing with granted moderator powers: Every member is made a moderator for April Fools, and members play with the powers and with losing them.
- **T3-095**  testing others' April Fools claims: Members work out whether a claim they did not make is an April Fools joke: a moderators' or company's announcement, a public figure's post, a news story, another room's claim, or real news taken for a joke.
- **T3-097**  switching off the prank styling: Members get past the sub's April Fools look or rules: turning the style off, working around it, reporting what it breaks, asking for it gone, or the moderators switching it back.
- **T3-100**  keeping April Fools changes afterwards: Members ask that something made for April Fools outlive the day: the sub's flairs, styling or name, or a company's joke feature made real.

## G02  the sub's rules and usual posts

- **T3-017**  carrying on ordinary sub talk: The sub's ordinary business goes on during the canvas week: its usual subject, news, help, jokes and quarrels, apart from the canvas and April Fools.
- **T3-051**  pushing posts up the rankings: Members ask for upvotes so a post, an image or a joke climbs: up r/place or the front page so placers see it, or to the top of a search result.
- **T3-053**  spelling slogans with post titles: Members post a run of posts whose titles, read down the front page or the sub's listing, spell a slogan or a chant.
- **T3-063**  removing canvas posts by rule: Moderators, or their bots, remove, filter or flair canvas posts under the sub's standing rules, and members ask about or contest the removals.
- **T3-075**  objecting to the canvas flood: Members object that canvas posts crowd out the sub's usual talk, and ask for them to stop, be condensed or move.
- **T3-106** [rare]  calling a network boycott: Members answer a TV network's April Fools prank by calling for a boycott: piracy, cancelled subscriptions, complaints.
- **T3-110**  placing a tile as payment: A member offers to place a canvas tile in return for an upvote or a favour.
- **T3-133** [rare]  recovering a hacked sub: The moderators recover the sub after a moderator's account is hacked and used to change it.
- **T3-140**  moderators disowning the canvas work: The sub's moderators say the canvas work is not the sub's: members may go on, but not in the sub's name.
- **T3-184**  pausing the sub's usual events: The sub puts one of its recurring events on hold for the canvas and resumes it after.
- **T3-185**  changing the sub's posting rules: Moderators change the sub's posting rules around the canvas or April Fools, and members argue the change.

- **T3-114**  disputing the chat's authority: Members dispute whether the chat may decide for the room: who speaks there, who is left out, whether its plans bind the thread.
- **T3-116**  hiding the plan from rivals: Members hide the room's plan from rivals who read the thread: private messages, their own language, codes.
- **T3-132**  spying in a rival's chat: Members watch a rival room's chat from inside, or an outsider watches theirs, and report what they see.

- **T3-153**  meeting other rooms in chat: Members meet other rooms in chats (their own, the other's or a shared council) to talk terms, borders or joint work.

## G04  calling people in to place

- **T3-129**  making passers-by notice a demand: Members use the canvas to put a demand before passers-by: a wanted comeback, a new season, a sauce.
- **T3-144** [rare]  recruiting quietly to avoid attackers: Members recruit only inside friendly communities and keep the work small, so that it draws no attackers.
- **T3-159**  rewarding helpers with prizes: Members reward those who helped the work with prizes: gold, medals, game codes, giveaways.
- **T3-189**  fearing newcomers the mark draws: Members fear what the canvas mark will bring into the sub: an influx of newcomers who do not read the rules or swamp the community.
- **T3-196**  drawing lapsed fans back: The room's mark on the canvas brings former fans back to the community; members welcome them or hope it will.

## G06  strain, doubt and giving up

- **T3-181**  quitting the effort under strain: Members quit the canvas work themselves: tired, stressed, beaten by scripts or rivals, or at odds with the room.

## G09  defending and repairing the work
