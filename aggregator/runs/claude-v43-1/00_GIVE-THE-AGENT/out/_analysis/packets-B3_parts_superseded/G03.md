# Family G03  where the planning happens

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-014  moving planning into a chat

gloss: The room moves its canvas planning into a chat outside the thread (a Discord most often; IRC, Skype, Telegram, Slack), setting it up, sharing invites and sending members there.
must_show: A chat set up or named for the room's canvas work; invites posted or asked for; members sent there for plans, live direction or voice.
must_not_show: Reporting in the thread what the chat decided, as the theme's own doing (T3-152); talking with other rooms in their chats or a shared one (T3-153); a new sub for the work (T3-090); arguing which chat to use (T3-109).
decide_when_both: Moving into a chat and relaying its decisions: T3-014 primary, T3-152 added when the relay has concepts of its own; meeting other rooms there adds T3-153.
varies: From one member's new server to a sub's official Discord with a channel per project; voice chat; invite links that break and are renewed.
is_not: A room's members joining another room's chat to talk terms (T3-153).
vs T3-152: Are members sent into the chat (T3-014), or told in the thread what the chat decided (T3-152)?
vs T3-153: Is the chat the room's own planning place (T3-014), or where it meets other rooms (T3-153)?
vs T3-090: Is the new venue a chat (T3-014) or a subreddit (T3-090)?
vs T3-109: Is a chat simply set up and used (T3-014), or is the choice of chat argued (T3-109)?
vs T3-015: Does the talk move out of Reddit into a chat (T3-014), or into one thread of the sub (T3-015)?
vs T3-107: Does the planning stay in the thread (T3-107) or move into a chat (T3-014)?
vs T3-116: Is the plan moved because rivals are reading (T3-116), or for speed and order (T3-014)?

## T3-049  complaining the calls are scattered

gloss: Members complain that the room's calls and plans are scattered, duplicated or uncoordinated, and ask for one clear plan.
must_show: Complaints that the room lacks coordination: too many threads, coordinates or designs at once; no one keeping one plan up to date; members unsure where the work is.
must_not_show: Gathering the talk into one thread (T3-015) or onto one template (T3-091) as an act; doubting the room can manage the work at all (T3-136).
decide_when_both: A complaint answered in the same theme by a megathread or a template: the act (T3-015, T3-091) is primary when it has more concepts, T3-049 otherwise.
varies: From a request for one post that states the goal to a member admitting there is zero coordination; during the work and after it.
is_not: A member saying the room has too few hands for its design (T3-136).
vs T3-015: Is the scattering complained of with no gathering act (T3-049), or is the talk gathered into one thread (T3-015)?
vs T3-091: Are many plans complained of (T3-049), or is one template set for all (T3-091)?
vs T3-136: Is the room said to be uncoordinated (T3-049), or too weak or too few (T3-136)?
vs T3-188: Is the plan itself scattered (T3-049), or are placers acting against a plan set apart from the room (T3-188)?
vs T3-125: Is the lack of a plan complained of (T3-049), or stated as the room's way (T3-125)?

## T3-090  opening a canvas sub

gloss: Members open a separate subreddit for the room's canvas work, or ask for one, and send canvas talk there.
must_show: A new subreddit made for the canvas work (a place sub for the room or nation), asked for, or used: canvas posts redirected to it, members told to go there.
must_not_show: One thread inside the sub (T3-015); a chat (T3-014); a hub sub for many kindred rooms (T3-013); what the canvas sub becomes after the canvas (T3-111).
decide_when_both: A sub made for the canvas that later takes a lasting purpose: making it is T3-090, its future T3-111.
varies: From a request for a separate sub or filter to moderators sending every canvas post to a national place sub.
is_not: A sub that gathers many kindred rooms for a joint mark (T3-013).
vs T3-015: Is the canvas talk sent to a new sub (T3-090), or to one thread in the old one (T3-015)?
vs T3-014: Is the new venue a subreddit (T3-090) or a chat (T3-014)?
vs T3-013: Is the new sub for this room's work (T3-090), or a hub for many kindred rooms (T3-013)?
vs T3-111: Is the sub being opened or used for the canvas (T3-090), or given a purpose after it (T3-111)?
vs T3-197: Is a canvas sub opened for order (T3-090), or to keep the work out of the creator's way (T3-197)?

## T3-107  keeping planning in the thread

gloss: Members keep the canvas planning in the Reddit thread instead of a chat.
must_show: A choice or agreement to coordinate in the thread rather than in a chat, or a chat tried and failed so that the thread carried the work.
must_not_show: Moving into a chat (T3-014); arguing which chat to use (T3-109).
decide_when_both: A theme that tries a chat and returns to the thread: T3-107 when the return carries the work, T3-014 when the chat does.
varies: From a small room agreeing to stay in touch through the thread to a member recalling that a chat never coordinated anything.
is_not: Gathering canvas talk into a megathread (T3-015).
vs T3-014: Does the planning stay in the thread (T3-107) or move into a chat (T3-014)?
vs T3-109: Is the thread chosen over any chat (T3-107), or one chat argued against another (T3-109)?

## T3-109  arguing which chat to use

gloss: Members argue over which chat to organise in, or whether to use one at all: Discord, IRC, Slack, an official or an unofficial server.
must_show: A dispute or choice among chats: Discord against IRC or Slack, the official server against a new one, whether a chat suits the room at all.
must_not_show: Simply moving into a chat (T3-014); disputing whether the chat may decide for the room (T3-114).
decide_when_both: When the question is which venue, T3-109; when it is whether the venue's decisions bind the room, T3-114.
varies: From an argument over proprietary software to a choice between two servers; a Void member rejecting any chat for an uncoordinated Void.
is_not: Members disputing that a Discord may decide for the room (T3-114).
vs T3-014: Is a chat set up and used (T3-014), or is the choice of chat argued (T3-109)?
vs T3-114: Is the argument about which chat (T3-109), or about the chat's right to decide (T3-114)?
vs T3-107: Is one chat weighed against another (T3-109), or the thread kept over any chat (T3-107)?

## T3-114  disputing the chat's authority

gloss: Members dispute whether the chat may decide for the room: who speaks there, who is left out, whether its plans bind the thread.
must_show: Contest over the chat's standing: a Discord said to have no right to decide; the work said to belong on Reddit; members outside the chat said to be left out; the chat's decisions defended as binding; a chat seized or closed.
must_not_show: Choosing which chat to use (T3-109); relaying the chat's decisions with no dispute (T3-152); disputing who may speak for a room toward other rooms (T3-126).
decide_when_both: A dispute over who speaks for the room to outsiders is T3-126; over whether the room's own chat governs the room, T3-114.
varies: From a member asking why the Discord is an authority to outsiders seizing the room's server.
is_not: Members telling outsiders that no one can speak for the placers (T3-126).
vs T3-152: Are the chat's decisions only reported (T3-152), or contested or defended as binding (T3-114)?
vs T3-109: Is the question which chat (T3-109), or whether the chat may decide (T3-114)?
vs T3-126: Is the dispute about who speaks for the room to other rooms (T3-126), or about the chat's power inside the room (T3-114)?

## T3-116  hiding the plan from rivals

gloss: Members hide the room's plan from rivals who read the thread: private messages, their own language, codes.
must_show: A plan kept from rivals who read the thread: moved to private messages or a closed chat because of infiltrators; written in the room's own language or in a code.
must_not_show: Moving to a chat for speed or order with no rival in view (T3-014); spying in a rival's chat (T3-132); recruiting quietly so attackers do not notice (T3-144).
decide_when_both: A move into a chat made because rivals read the thread is T3-116; the same move for speed is T3-014.
varies: From asking members to write only in Hindi to letter-and-number codes in Russian; a plan passed by private message because rival fans got in.
is_not: Recruiting quietly to avoid attackers (T3-144).
vs T3-014: Is the plan moved because rivals are reading (T3-116), or for speed and order (T3-014)?
vs T3-132: Are rivals kept out of one's own plan (T3-116), or is the rival's plan read from inside (T3-132)?
vs T3-144: Is the plan hidden from readers (T3-116), or the recruiting kept quiet so attackers do not come (T3-144)?
vs T3-083: Is language used to keep rivals out (T3-116), or a gap bridged to deal with another room (T3-083)?

## T3-132  spying in a rival's chat

gloss: Members watch a rival room's chat from inside, or an outsider watches theirs, and report what they see.
must_show: Someone inside another room's chat or thread as a watcher: joining a rival's Discord to learn its plans, reporting what is said there, an outsider announcing that they are watching.
must_not_show: Meeting other rooms in chat to talk terms (T3-153); hiding one's own plan (T3-116).
decide_when_both: A visit that is both open talks and watching: T3-153 when terms are talked, T3-132 when the report is what was overheard.
varies: From one member undercover in the Void's Discord warning of an invasion to a member laughing at rivals panicking in their chat.
is_not: A diplomat serving in the open across many servers (T3-194).
vs T3-153: Is the visit open, to talk with the other room (T3-153), or hidden, to watch it (T3-132)?
vs T3-116: Is the rival's plan read from inside (T3-132), or one's own plan kept from rivals (T3-116)?
vs T3-087: Is an attack traced from the pixels and what others said (T3-087), or learned from inside the rival's chat (T3-132)?
vs T3-176: Is the rival watched from inside (T3-132), or harmed from inside by a script (T3-176)?

## T3-152  announcing what the chat decided

gloss: Members bring the chat's decisions back to the thread: the plan agreed, a change authorised, where to place next.
must_show: A report in the thread of what the chat decided or is doing: 'agreed on Discord', 'the Discord is working on it', the chat's next target, a change explained as approved there.
must_not_show: Moving into the chat (T3-014); disputing whether the chat may decide (T3-114); a decision put to the thread by vote (T3-066).
decide_when_both: The move into the chat is T3-014, the relay of its decisions T3-152; when both have concepts of their own, the larger is primary.
varies: From one line saying a change was agreed on Discord to running relays of the chat's orders during an attack.
is_not: A moderator putting a choice to a vote in the thread (T3-066).
vs T3-014: Are members sent into the chat (T3-014), or told in the thread what it decided (T3-152)?
vs T3-114: Is the chat's decision reported (T3-152), or its right to decide contested (T3-114)?
vs T3-066: Is a decision put to the room by vote or poll (T3-066), or a chat's decision reported (T3-152)?

## T3-153  meeting other rooms in chat

gloss: Members meet other rooms in chats (their own, the other's or a shared council) to talk terms, borders or joint work.
must_show: Contact with another room through a chat: inviting its members to the room's Discord to talk, visiting theirs, joining a shared server or council, passing a template or a request there.
must_not_show: The room's own planning chat with no other room in it (T3-014); watching a rival's chat in secret (T3-132); the deal itself, when that is the doing (G14, G15).
decide_when_both: Meeting in chat and the deal made there: the deal's kind (T3-010, T3-150, T3-151) is primary, and T3-153 is added when the meeting in chat has concepts of its own.
varies: From an invite to talk borders in the Green Lattice Discord to diplomats on a world council server.
is_not: A member spying in a rival's Discord (T3-132).
vs T3-014: Is another room in the chat (T3-153), or only the room's own members (T3-014)?
vs T3-132: Is the visit open, to talk (T3-153), or hidden, to watch (T3-132)?
vs T3-194: Is a member acting for the room toward another room as its envoy (T3-194), or are members meeting in a chat (T3-153)?
vs T3-010: Is the theme the place of the talks (T3-153), or the border or space agreed (T3-010)?
