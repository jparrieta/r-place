**The rule (G61, logged before any re-check line was ruled).**
1. A re-check line that repeats a first-return line of the same room exactly (the same element, op, cited codes, condition, statement and relation text, and observation state) takes the last ruling of the line it repeats. Its reason names that line, its decision and its kind. A NONPORTABLE repeat is a particular by G6. An ambiguous repeat is read.
2. Every propose line is read. A proposal that a candidate's statement or range states is held by that candidate and rejected (G59a). A line that repeats a D3a-55 proposal accepted for a candidate is held by that candidate.
3. Every other line is read by element, in packets of at most 100, under the G rule D3a used for its op (G1 to G60 as amended), against the revised statements. A candidate's lines are read against its statement and range.
4. No move. Where two accepted corrections meet (section 2), the element is re-examined in the leg, and the result is written here as a held proposal.
5. Rulings are batched from files (QD3-001) and checked by the batch adjudicator.
