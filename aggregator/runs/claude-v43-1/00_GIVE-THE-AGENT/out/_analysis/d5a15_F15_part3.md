## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

The arrow is chosen by the rule logged at D5a-12: the arrow whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A11, restricted in 29 of 42 members, against 211 of 386; one member is 2.4 points, and the excess is 14.4. A11 runs from P02 (the design) to P04 (the template). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first design talk comes before the first template (tested, not beaten: 0.556 against a null mean of 0.557)". The account's prose reads it as "Inside a thread, a posted template settles a design under argument".

The 29 restrict lines say one thing in many forms: a posted template settles the design only as far as placers receive the current version and follow it. r/uofmn's line: "A posted template settles practice only insofar as builders receive and follow its current version." r/philippines': "Posting a template does not settle the design for keepers following stale specifications or unaware of redesign decisions." r/criticalrole's: "A new template precedes a later report of two-link conflict, so posting settles design only if participants converge on the current version."

r/criticalrole shows both sides. A member posted one template to end competing attempts: "I just made a new template and edited the original post. Just make sure to follow that" [criticalrole/c0051]. Forty-eight minutes later another reported that the old link was still in use: "having two links is causing some confusion around the" letter I, "and were fighting ourselves" [criticalrole/c0056]. The template was posted, and it did not settle the design, because two versions were followed at once.

The account quotes r/udub for A11, and r/udub is one of the arrow's two within-case datings. r/udub is a member of this branch, and its own line restricts the arrow: "Explicit uptake reaches a promise to revise and upload the template, while delivery of the revision remains unverified by these concepts." The held proposal would withdraw A11, because 261 accepted restrict lines say that a template does not settle the design for good. F15's 29 restrict lines say the same. This branch is where the proposal's reason is plainest: here the template was revised, and each revision had to be settled again among the placers.

This is description, not a test. A11 has 2 within-case datings, so its expectation in F15 is 0.119, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A11: restrict 29, instantiate 1.
