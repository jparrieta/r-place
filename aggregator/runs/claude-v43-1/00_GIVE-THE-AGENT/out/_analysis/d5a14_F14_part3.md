## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. The amendment of D5a-14 applies here: with 7 members, one member is 14 per cent of the branch, and no arrow's excess reaches that. The largest is A16's: restricted in 2 of 7, against 45 of 280, an excess of 12.5 points. So by the rule's measure no relationship runs differently in F14. I describe A16, as description only.

A16 runs from P09 (attack) to P07 (sparing). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the room's first call or act against another room's work comes before its first sparing, refusal, apology or reining in". r/utahjazz shows it as stated. A member brought a plan to cover a work near the room's ball: "We're trying to build a knicks logo right next to the jazz ball on the place! We plan on going right over the face, but whoever made that art is defending it hard!" [utahjazz/c0090]. The room's members answered with restraint: "please do not destroy other's art." [utahjazz/c0094]. r/pitt shows the other side. The restraint came from the target's complaint, posted in r/pitt's own thread: "I'm all for you guys creating your logo but please stop destroying ours; we worked hard on it." [pitt/c0064]. Within the hour the moderator answered: "I've removed the post about attacking penn state" [pitt/c0065]. Its room model asks the model to "Allow the target's complaint to mediate restraint, instead of requiring an internal refusal immediately beneath the attack call". Four hours later a member proposed the attack again: "if we have the manpower to do more than defend we should go write 42-39 on the PSU logo" [pitt/c0132].

A16 has nothing to do with the key. The arrow nearer the key, A09 (the record before carrying on), runs here as the model states it more often than in F11, F12 or F13: 4 members instantiate it and 2 restrict it. In these rooms the thing carried on was a record, a screenshot of the mark made into the sub's picture or flair. r/pitt's line: "An available screenshot supplied the adoptable image and was corrected after adoption." Where the record is the emblem, the record comes first.

This is description, not a test. A16 has 4 within-case datings, so its expectation in F14 is 0.040; A09's is 0.020. Both are not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines: A16 restrict 2, instantiate 1; A09 instantiate 4, restrict 2.
