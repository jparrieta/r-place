## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

A10 runs from K02 (judging too few hands or too little room) to P10 (the move). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first doubt about the room's numbers or room comes before the first move after a loss". r/day9 is one of the two rooms on which A10's within-case dating rests, and there it holds. A member judged the spot lost before the move: "I wanted to keep fighting for the spot we've had, but it looks like this is a losing battle. I'll help move it if we can decide on where." [day9/c0023], at h+49.66. An hour later: "It's official: we are relocating the Day9 logo to below the crusader Kings logo" [day9/c0027].

In 27 of 54 members a current-ledger line restricts A10: the move came without a judgment of too few hands. r/Temple moved to avoid a fight it expected: "I'd rather not move but I figure their gonna just take the spot and we'll fight over it unless we move and then hopefully it just stays without too much upkeep" [Temple/c0020]. A member there conceded the spot to the faster room: "we figured you guys got organized quicker than we did so you deserve it lol" [Temple/c0033]. The coders' line: "Moving is explained by avoiding fighting and upkeep and by a neighbor's quicker organization, without an explicit judgment of too few hands." In r/UPenn the spot was simply taken: "Someone took the spot, moving to (212, 892)" [UPenn/c0002].

So the move had more than one source, and judging too few hands was one of them. The share of restrictions here (27 of 54) is close to that among other rooms of this size (110 of 263); this is how moves went in large rooms, not only in this branch. What sets F09 apart is what came after the move.

This is description, not a test. A10's expectation in F09 is 0.153 (2 within-case datings over 705 rooms, times 54), so A10 is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' shared current-ledger lines on A10: restrict 27, instantiate 7, add-condition 1.
