# Review of T3-009 celebrating the finished work (B1b-19-p2)

## Decision (written after all 299 members were read)

Kept whole; its internal variation is written out. Every member marks the room's own work as
achieved. Members differ in timing, object and form, not in doing:
- timing: provisional completion calls during the canvas (a figure, a letter, a flag done at
  hours 5 to 60, often turning at once to defence, clearing or the next piece), survival claims at
  the close (hour 73), retrospective pride days later;
- object: one figure or letter, the room's area, a nation's flags, the whole canvas;
- form: "we did it", chants, songs and anthems, toasts, memes, mock medals and game codes, the
  room's own idiom ("wavy", "majesty"), final images and timelapses.
Doings that come with the celebration and have concepts of their own are placed in their own
kinds as seconds: credit (T3-043), thanks to allies (T3-072), screenshots and timelapses (T3-105),
mourning (T3-029, T3-117), recounting (T3-073), reflection on cooperation (T3-157), victory over
rivals (T3-154), emblem (T3-156), personal pixel claims (T3-003), progress reports (T3-038),
finding the mark (T3-082).
Set aside: a kind for mid-canvas completion calls, because the doing is the same at an earlier
hour, and where the call turns into coordination the theme already carries T3-021, T3-038 or
T3-041; a kind for spotting the room's work on the canvas, because T3-082 and T3-026 hold it.
T3-157 beside T3-009 (39 members): the two placements record two doings in one post-close
thread. T3-157 needs a concept saying what made the work succeed or what it did for the
community; celebrating alone does not meet it. The thinnest five (sixers t05 c0134, starlightstage
t13 c0113, starbound t06 c0082 and c0084, superman t06 c0029, smite t10 c0404, c0431, c0432,
c0435) cite such concepts in their rule 0 lines, so all stand.
Leads for B1d, a second kind not yet placed: ice_poseidon t05, giivasunner t04, stardewvalley t07,
overwatch t21 and rickandmorty t06 show the room finding and showing its own work on the
canvas (T3-082 or T3-026); gameofthrones t11, nuclearthrone t05, furry t06 and miamidolphins t05
carry progress reports (T3-038, already placed for some).
No placement changed; no tier_history line (writing out variation is not one of its events).

Working file for the PI's request of 9 October. Every member printed whole with
b1_theme_members.py ($TMPDIR/k009.txt, 389 KB, cut into 16 parts by b1_split_print.py) and read.
299 members before the review; co-placed with T3-157 in 39, T3-043 in 27, T3-105 in 18,
T3-072 in 15, T3-117 in 14, T3-029 in 13.
Letters, added as found:
a marking the room's own work as achieved (we did it, pride, it survived, it is done);
b showing or admiring the finished picture (screenshots, quality praised);
r reflecting on what working together meant or why it worked (T3-157);
k crediting or thanking the makers (T3-043, T3-072);
m mourning or regretting (T3-029, T3-117);
v victory over a rival (T3-154).

More letters: n announcing a piece complete mid-canvas, a coordination signal (T3-026's ground);
h retelling the room's history after the close (T3-073); s spotting other rooms' work on the
finished canvas (T3-052, T3-018); e outside recognition (T3-147, T3-127).
