Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first agreement with another room (a border, a truce, a deal or a permission) comes before the first dispute over, or stated limit of, who may commit the room". r/buffalobills shows both sides. The agreement: "We have a Deal with Harry Potter to NOT CROSS into the crest on our left!" [buffalobills/c0026], at h+54.96.
