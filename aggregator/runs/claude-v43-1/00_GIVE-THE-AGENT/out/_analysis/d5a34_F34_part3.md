## 6. How the model runs here

Counts are of members with a current-ledger line, particular lines included (`out/_analysis/d5_elements.py`, `out/_analysis/d5_within.py`); they count any line of an op. Rates are compared with the 364 rooms outside F34 that have at least its median of 95 coded concepts. Half the members are smaller than every room in the pool, so an element can look idle here for size alone. With 22 members, one room moves a rate by about 4.5 points.

**The element that fits badly: P02.** P02 says the room settles what its mark will show. It does not say what bounds the choice. In these rooms the choice was bounded by the room's hands, upkeep, space and colours, and in the cited words of 10 members by the room's own capacity. Beside it these rooms' coders restrict the model less than coders of rooms of their size: 6.5 restrict lines a room, 8.9 per cent of their lines, against 8.1 and 11.0 per cent (the check logged at D5a-21). P05 is restricted in 14 of 22, against 230 of 364, about the same rate. A10, the arrow from too few hands to the move, is restricted in 7 of 22, against 149 of 364 (`out/_analysis/d5_arrow_restrict.py`). Its lines here say what the key says from the other side: a shortage of hands did not lead to a move, because the room sized its work, concentrated it, recruited or carried on (r/yugioh's line: "Shortage alone does not force relocation here because thin participation and overwrites are followed by recruitment, repair, and completion.").

**What these rooms do that the model has no place for.** 6 of 22 members carry 17 propose lines, against 170 of the 364 rooms of their size, a lower rate. They name ordinary sub life beside the event. In r/yugioh members planned decks and made joke cards for the day (six lines). In r/4chan members kept a birthday thread and argued over the day's merged boards (five lines). In r/starlightstage a real milestone of the sub ran beside the canvas (three lines). The gap is the one logged at D5a-01 (`defects.jsonl`, ordinary sub life).

**Where the room models and my reading disagree.** Three room models name the step in their own words. r/evex's model proposes a new element: "fitting ambition to available builders before losing a spot". r/penguins's model: "These passages make limited hands and occupied space conditions of the representational choice, rather than a design decision completed before building". r/crystalclear's model reads the same act differently: its member sized the design to the room, but the model holds K02 "unobserved as a judgment that the desired work exceeds available hands", because the member did not say the larger work was out of reach. I believe the reading of r/evex's and r/penguins's models: a room that chooses a small design because it is small has judged its hands, whether or not it says the larger work is impossible. The difference matters for D6, since K02 as written asks for a judgment that the room cannot do what it wants, and these rooms judged first and wanted less.

## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A16: restricted in 5 of 22 members, against 60 of 364, an excess of 6.2 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 4.5 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A17 is next, at 5.1 points.

A16 runs from P09 (an attack on others' work) to P07 (sparing, refusing, apologising, reining in). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the room's first call or act against another room's work comes before its first sparing, refusal, apology or reining in". Its mechanism says that a call to attack makes an answer due, and that a refusal "usually comes with a reason (23 of 38 read pairs, F-014), and the reason states the room's norm or the attack's cost". Its bound allows tacit sparing that comes first.

By my reading of the 5 restrict lines, each qualifies the reasoned refusal the mechanism describes. In r/4chan a moderator's call at hour 20.24, "Nuke Trans Road on /r/place" [4chan/c0057], drew refusals that gave no reason about the target: "implying we are your personal army" [4chan/c0058] and "Rule 8" [4chan/c0061]. A later call, "Let's Black out Asexual pride." [4chan/c0223], drew, twelve hours later: "Or let's not" [4chan/c0224]. In r/evex the member who floated an attack withdrew it in the same comment: "...What if we defaced the EVE logo by adding an X? We'd probably get slaughtered but it'd be amazing if we pulled it off" [evex/c0031], then asked, in small print, that no one do it [evex/c0032]. In r/PORTUGALCARALHO a proposal at hour 69.32 to extend the room's sea over a picture of ponies [PORTUGALCARALHO/c0040] was refused with a slur, not a norm: "Deixem la estar os poneis sossegados que aquela paneleiragem e contagiosa." [PORTUGALCARALHO/c0041] ("Leave the ponies in peace, that faggotry is contagious."). In r/detroitlions a member questioned one target, "But that sub has nothing to do with the Packers..." [detroitlions/c0099], while after the close another reported that a rival's request for peace had been ignored: "I paid no attention to it, lol." [detroitlions/c0155]. r/starlightstage's line concerns restraint asked of others with no attack call seen.

The other side holds in other members, and there the reason is the room's capacity as often as its norm. In r/palestine a member proposed at hour 35.63 to cover a neighbour's symbol "to show them who is boss" [palestine/c0090]. Two answers came within ten minutes. One gave a norm: "I don't support covering them up. They don't touch us we don't touch them. We can't be the aggressors. Right now we should focus on repairing the flag. It's at a decent size right now" [palestine/c0038]. The other gave a count of hands among its reasons, and linked to the neighbour's own thread [palestine/c0096]: the room, it said, barely had the manpower to defend its flag. In r/nlsscirclejerk a proposal to build "Right overtop of the Swedish flag" [nlsscirclejerk/c0014] drew: "Dont want to ruin the other flag tho" [nlsscirclejerk/c0016]. In r/AyyMD "We should fight back." [AyyMD/c0115] drew "That would not be good. Seems to be calm now." [AyyMD/c0116].

This meets the key in part. The same count of hands that sized these rooms' designs also held back some of their attacks: r/palestine could not spare hands, and r/aoe2 refused a second picture because it would cover two neighbours and lose two allies (section 1). Where the refusal had no such reason, the coders restricted A16. I note for stage D6 that capacity is a reason for restraint beside the norm and the cost that A16's mechanism names.

This is description, not a test. A16 has 4 within-case datings, none in F34, so its expectation in F34 is 0.125, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A16, shared lines only: instantiate 7, restrict 4.
