## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A07: restricted in 8 of 22 members, against 26 of 219, an excess of 24.5 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 4.5 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A10 is next, at 21.6 points.

A07 runs from P03 (the build and its reports) to P11 (marking the finished work). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first build report comes before the first marking of the finished work". Its mechanism: "A mark reported done gives the room a finished thing to point at, and the room marks it together: announcement, credit, pride." Its bound allows a mark found already made, and a work the close cut short.

By my reading of the 8 restrict lines and 1 add-condition line, 7 say the room marked its work without a finished or lasting mark to point at. r/nbaspurs shows it most sharply. At hour 56.22 a member posted: "WE DID IT! This was so fun. We finally got a permanent spot on the canvas after getting destroyed four times." [nbaspurs/c0123]. Two minutes later a neighbour answered: "Please do not do it here! We're the Anime_IRL Discord banner and we'd very much like it if you didn't do this!" [nbaspurs/c0130]. Two minutes after that the poster wrote: "We're moving it." [nbaspurs/c0136]. In r/foxes the thanks came before the work was done, and the credit outlived it. At hour 70.29: "Thanks a lot for all the help of you guys to help us place our fox!" [foxes/c0046]. At hour 70.68 the member who had the idea wrote: "As the original idea haver, Im so fucking proud if you guys. Thank you! <3" [foxes/c0054]. After the close: "Man I am so sad this didn't make it to the end after hours of working for it." [foxes/c0089]. In r/faeria a member reported after the close: "most likely bots started to erasing him so I just gave up" [faeria/c0066]. Another answered: "Valiant effort!!!" [faeria/c0067]. In r/seahawks: "In the end we got something that kinda looks like a 12" [seahawks/c0150]; then "yeah I had 3 accounts going at it for hours. wake up to see its over and looks terrible..." [seahawks/c0155]; then "the 12th man flag proudly raises and only flutters in the face of competition. We are strong even at the end" [seahawks/c0158]. The other three such lines are r/FloydVsVoid's, r/frankocean's and r/prequelmemes'. The two remaining lines ask which proposal produced the marked result (r/sadboys) and note hostility beside the celebration (r/chicago).

This meets the key in part. A room whose spot stood on another's leave could celebrate a "permanent spot" that lasted about five minutes, mark a work it stopped short of to spare a neighbour (r/frankocean), or credit a fox that a larger room then took. Where the space was held on leave, the finished, lasting mark that A07's mechanism points at was often not there.

A07 now runs differently in three branches in a row: F27, F30 and F31. Across the three, 18 rooms carry an A07 restrict line, 4 of them in two branches (r/pcmasterrace, r/FloydVsVoid, r/prequelmemes, r/frankocean). In the whole population 87 rooms carry one. The three sections 7 partly rest on the same rooms' lines. A07's bound covers a work the close cut short; it does not cover marking during the work, or marking a spot held on another room's leave. I note this for stage D6 in `notes-D5.md`.

This is description, not a test. A07 has 2 within-case datings, neither in F31, so its expectation in F31 is 0.062, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A07, shared lines only: restrict 7, instantiate 3, add-condition 1.
