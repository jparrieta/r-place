# Family G16  kindred rooms and colour factions

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-013  gathering kindred rooms together

gloss: Kindred rooms (a league's teams, a region's subs, sister fandoms) gather their canvas work in one hub sub or chat.
must_show: A hub for many kindred rooms used or proposed: 'everyone go to r/CFBplace', a regional place sub for several teams, one chat for a league, members sent to the hub for help.
must_not_show: A separate sub for one room's own work (T3-090); one joint mark made by two rooms (T3-056); calls for hands sent to kindred subs (T3-062).
decide_when_both: A hub that also makes a joint mark: T3-013 for the hub, T3-056 added when the joint mark has concepts of its own.
varies: From a college football place sub to a regional hub for a city's teams and a European chat server.
is_not: A room opening its own canvas sub (T3-090).
vs T3-090: Is the new sub for this room's own work (T3-090), or a hub for many kindred rooms (T3-013)?
vs T3-056: Do two or more rooms make one mark together (T3-056), or gather their separate works in a hub (T3-013)?
vs T3-062: Are kindred subs asked for hands for this room (T3-062), or gathered into a common hub (T3-013)?

## T3-019  tallying kindred rooms' works

gloss: Members count which kindred rooms (teams, schools, bands) made it onto the canvas and which were lost.
must_show: An inventory of kindred rooms' marks on the canvas: which teams, schools or subs made it, which were covered, where they are.
must_not_show: Decoding the finished picture in general (T3-018); remarking on the pictures beside the room's own mark (T3-052).
decide_when_both: A tally inside a reading of the final canvas: T3-018 when the whole picture is read, T3-019 when the kindred rooms are counted.
varies: From a guess that only two NBA teams made it to a list of hockey logos kept or covered.
is_not: Explaining what each picture on the finished canvas is (T3-018).
vs T3-018: Is the whole finished picture decoded (T3-018), or the kindred rooms' marks counted (T3-019)?
vs T3-052: Are pictures beside the mark remarked on (T3-052), or kindred rooms' marks across the canvas tallied (T3-019)?

## T3-056  making joint marks with others

gloss: Two or more rooms make one mark together: a shared design, a combined flag, lettering finished for each other.
must_show: A mark designed or built jointly by two or more rooms: shared borders made into a design, flags combined, each finishing part of the other's work, a joint emblem.
must_not_show: Help sent to another room's own work (T3-011); a member adding a personal piece to a shared mark (T3-060); national flags joined by a heart (T3-079).
decide_when_both: A joint mark of two national flags joined by a heart: T3-079; any other joint mark: T3-056.
varies: From a beanie and lettering traded between two fandoms to ANZAC flags built together before the Americans woke.
is_not: Sending pixels to a neighbour's defence (T3-011).
vs T3-011: Is help sent to another's own work (T3-011), or one mark made together (T3-056)?
vs T3-079: Are national flags joined with a heart (T3-079), or another joint mark made (T3-056)?
vs T3-150: Do rooms ally for defence or a fight (T3-150), or make one mark together (T3-056)?
vs T3-060: Is one mark made jointly by rooms (T3-056), or a personal piece added to it (T3-060)?
vs T3-013: Do two or more rooms make one mark together (T3-056), or gather their separate works in a hub (T3-013)?
vs T3-025: Do the rooms make one mark together (T3-056), or keep two rival marks side by side (T3-025)?

## T3-096  hailing the colour corners

gloss: Members of other subs cheer the colour corners and factions, or call people to their side.
must_show: Outsiders hailing or cheering a colour faction or corner, or calling others to its side, without being its own members.
must_not_show: A faction's own members professing loyalty (T3-174); spreading the colour (T3-171).
decide_when_both: A member of another sub who joins the faction and hails it: T3-163 for the joining, T3-096 for the hailing.
varies: From 'all hail blue corner' in a game sub to a streamer's sub calling its army to the purple side.
is_not: The Blue Corner's own members hailing their empire (T3-174).
vs T3-174: Do the faction's own members profess loyalty (T3-174), or do other subs' members cheer it (T3-096)?
vs T3-171: Is the colour spread on the canvas (T3-171), or cheered in talk (T3-096)?
vs T3-163: Does a member defect to the faction (T3-163), or only cheer it (T3-096)?

## T3-163  defecting from a rival faction

gloss: Members announce that they have left a rival faction and joined this one.
must_show: Defection or conversion between canvas factions: 'defector from the Blue Corner', 'converting from the Void', joining the lattice's army with a first pixel.
must_not_show: A faction splitting over doctrine (T3-190); outsiders cheering a faction (T3-096).
decide_when_both: A member leaving over doctrine to found or join a splinter: T3-190 when the split is the doing, T3-163 when the member's crossing is.
varies: From one defector's title post to convert stories told in a faction's thread.
is_not: A faction splitting into rival doctrines (T3-190).
vs T3-190: Does a faction split over doctrine (T3-190), or does a member cross from a rival faction (T3-163)?
vs T3-096: Does a member cross over (T3-163), or only cheer a faction (T3-096)?
vs T3-174: Is loyalty professed by members (T3-174), or a member's crossing announced (T3-163)?

## T3-171  spreading a faction's colour

gloss: A colour faction spreads its colour across the canvas: expanding the corner, covering others with its colour, tendrils and cores.
must_show: A colour, not a picture, spread as the faction's aim: calls to expand the corner, to cover the canvas in the colour, to grow tendrils, the colour treated as territory.
must_not_show: A room's picture extended into a neighbour's space (T3-061); a rival's work defaced (T3-006); loyalty to the faction professed in talk (T3-174).
decide_when_both: Spreading the colour over a rival's work: T3-171 when the colour's spread is the aim, T3-006 or T3-061 when the target is.
varies: From a corner that grows a few rows to a Void meant to consume the whole canvas; red spread as a political colour.
is_not: A room extending its logo into a neighbour's space (T3-061).
vs T3-061: Is a picture grown into a neighbour's space (T3-061), or a colour spread as the aim (T3-171)?
vs T3-006: Is a rival's work destroyed (T3-006), or the faction's colour spread over whatever lies in its way (T3-171)?
vs T3-174: Is the colour spread on the canvas (T3-171), or loyalty to it professed (T3-174)?
vs T3-041: Is the room's work extended (T3-041), or a colour spread for its own sake (T3-171)?
vs T3-096: Is the colour spread on the canvas (T3-171), or cheered in talk (T3-096)?

## T3-172  renewing the order's oaths

decide_when_both: Officers appointed under the order's rules: T3-172; officers voted in: T3-173.

is_not: A faction voting in a new leader (T3-173).
vs T3-173: Are offices filled by election (T3-173), or the order's rules and oaths laid down or renewed (T3-172)?
vs T3-101: Is a leader named for the work (T3-101), or the order's standing rules set (T3-172)?

## T3-190  splitting a faction over doctrine

gloss: A canvas faction splits over what it stands for: pure colour or art, erasing or cleaning, the void or coexistence.
must_show: Dispute over a faction's doctrine that divides it: members leaving for a purer version, splinter subs founded, the faction's past and true nature argued.
must_not_show: A member defecting from a rival faction (T3-163); a member quitting under strain (T3-181).
decide_when_both: A member leaving over doctrine: T3-190 when the split in the faction is the doing, T3-163 when the crossing to another faction is.
varies: From one eraser leaving for the black to a colour faction arguing whether it was once the void.
is_not: A member giving up because the work became stressful (T3-181).
vs T3-163: Does a member cross from a rival faction (T3-163), or does the faction split over its doctrine (T3-190)?
vs T3-181: Does a member quit under strain (T3-181), or over the faction's doctrine (T3-190)?
vs T3-179: Is a political ideology argued from the canvas (T3-179), or a faction's own canvas doctrine (T3-190)?
