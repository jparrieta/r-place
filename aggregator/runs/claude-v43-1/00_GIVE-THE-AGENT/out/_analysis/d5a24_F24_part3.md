## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A04: restricted in 13 of 27 members, against 67 of 248, an excess of 21.1 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 3.7 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A06, the arrow into P14, is next, at 14.5 points.

A04 runs from P05 (keeping) to P08 (border talk and deals). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first act of upkeep or defence comes before the first border talk, truce or deal". Its mechanism: "Keeping a mark against erosion brings the room up against the neighbours whose pixels erode it, and the room turns to talk with them." Its bound already records other paths, among them rooms that "talk with neighbours first, asking for space before they have a mark to keep".

The restrict lines say that border talk began before keeping: during building, or before it. r/sips: "Neighbour permission was discussed as a prerequisite to building, so border talk cannot locally be confined to an outcome of maintaining an existing mark." At hour 45.54 a member asked: "do we need to ask for permission from r/greenlattice before drawing?" [sips/c0034]. Twenty hours later another explained a delay: "we haven't really started yet, we need permission from the green lattice area" [sips/c0044]. r/hyperlightdrifter: "Negotiation begins during construction before reported maintenance, so upkeep cannot supply the exclusive initiating stage." A member reported: "We are officially approved by r/greenlattice so we shouldn't have to worry about them anymore!" [hyperlightdrifter/c0021]. The keeping came after: "Doing my bit to maintain our hero" [hyperlightdrifter/c0029], and, two hours after the approval, "All we have to do now is keep him alive and maybe give him a black border." [hyperlightdrifter/c0031]. r/scotland: "Border talk anticipates mutually costly expansion rather than requiring demonstrated erosion inflicted by the neighbour." A member wrote of a neighbour: "We're pretty equally sized subs so it would just be attrition if either tries to consume the other. Any ideas?" [scotland/c0043]. Five hours later: "I think it is all working out now, we don't go below the border line of our flag, they don't go above theirs." [scotland/c0044]. So in these rooms, talk with neighbours came early, about space before a mark was kept. This is the path the arrow's bound already names, and C5's reading of 40 rooms found the same: where border talk comes first, it mostly offers terms before any damage. The excess here may also be the coders' habit: these members' lines restrict more of the model than rooms of their size do (section 6).
