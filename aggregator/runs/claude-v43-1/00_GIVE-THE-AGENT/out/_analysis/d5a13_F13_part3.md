## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

The arrow is chosen by the rule logged at D5a-12: the arrow whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A09, as in F11 and F12. A09 runs from P12 (the record) to P13 (carrying on). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first record of what happened comes before the first act of carrying the work on". In 4 of 9 members a line restricts it, against 23 of the 131 rooms of their size. Three more members hold an absent line (r/parrots, r/romania and r/touhou).

The four restrict lines split two and two. Two say the remake came before the record (r/lego, r/lovelive). Two say no record is shown to have caused it (r/prequelmemes, r/warriors). r/prequelmemes' line: "Affection for the drawing and pleasurable participation are explicit motives for remaking, while an effect of preserving records is not established."

r/lovelive shows both orders in one room. At h+53.63, while the work was still being placed, a member posted the room's figure as a profile picture: "r/place Honk in profile pic form." [lovelive/c0034]. That use came before any final record. At h+81.00, after the close, a member made new fan art from the room's design: "and the hearts are using the r/place design we had. Any suggestions for improvement?" [lovelive/c0078]. Its room model reads the later passage as connecting "reused design to new work", while "the earlier profile picture prevents making final recording or collective closure a prerequisite". r/globaloffensive shows the order A09 states. Members proposed a weapon design made from the finished canvas, with its stages as the design's wear: "the fn would be the final result or something" [globaloffensive/c1143]. Its room model reads this as reuse of the recorded canvas, "supporting the move from record to prospective keepsake". The order turns on when the remaking began, not on what was remade: r/lego's wish for a copy of the whole canvas came before the record, as did the three early copies of a room's mark.

This is description, not a test. A09 has 2 within-case datings, so its expectation in F13 is 0.026, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A09: restrict 4.
