## 6. How the model runs here

Counts are of members with a current-ledger line, particular lines included (`out/_analysis/d5_elements.py`, `out/_analysis/d5_within.py`); they count any line of an op. Rates are compared with the 354 rooms outside F35 that have at least its median of 98 coded concepts. Half the members are smaller than every room in the pool, and three have ten coded concepts or fewer, so an element can look idle here for size alone. With 18 members, one room moves a rate by about 5.6 points.

**The elements that do the work.** The call (P01) is instantiated in all 18 members, the design (P02) in 17, and building, keeping and the questions about how to place (P03, P05, P24) in 16. Rooms of their size carry them as often. P10, the move after a loss, is instantiated in 10 of 18, against 158 of 354, a little more often.

**The element that fits badly: P15.** P15 says that a member answers a call "in one line that joins, assents, reports a part done or thanks, and the line asks for nothing back." It is instantiated in 13 of 18 members, against 336 of 354: 72 per cent against 95. The answers in these rooms asked for something back. The branch's key is a step added to the call, but what it changes most is the answer.

**The elements that sit idle.** P33 and P34 are absent in all 18 members, and C02, K07, P28, P30 and P32 in 16. Most are idle in the population too. Against rooms of their size, several are present much less often. P25, the work bringing new people into the room, is instantiated in none of 18, against 97 of 354, and absent in 15, against 147. P13, carrying the work on after the close, in 5, against 207. P17, coalitions, in 5, against 225. P18, a contest with a rival carried in talk, in 7, against 264. P23, the chat, in 9, against 289. K04, the room acting as a body it was before the canvas, in 8, against 296. P31, the argument whether covering others' work is fair, in 8, against 280. P22, the contest over the kept version, in 3, against 160. These rooms' records hold little about rivals, coalitions, chats and the work after the close.

**Restriction.** These rooms' coders restrict the model less than coders of rooms of their size: 6.4 restrict lines a room, 8.9 per cent of their lines, against 8.2 and 11.1 per cent (the check logged at D5a-21). P05 is restricted in 11 of 18, against 224 of 354, about the same rate.

**What these rooms do that the model has no place for.** 7 of 18 members carry 16 propose lines, against 165 of the 354 rooms of their size, a lower rate. They name ordinary sub life beside the event. In r/clashroyale members ran a game tournament, argued over the sub's new layout and played a letter game under a picture of the canvas (five lines). The gap is the one logged at D5a-01 (`defects.jsonl`, ordinary sub life).

**Where the room models and my reading disagree.** They agree with the key, and several name the gap in P15. r/polyamory's model records P15 absent because, of the written replies, "none satisfies the element's requirement that the line ask nothing back". r/seahawks's model keeps "I'm in" as P15 and the first volunteer's line as "making acceptance conditional and asking something back". r/stargate's model: "Acceptance need not be a bare line asking nothing back". r/clashroyale's model: "It adds operational onboarding and distinguishes usable canvas registration from merely sharing coordinates or art". r/tenma's model: "the adaptation adds orientation within the call". ## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A12: restricted in 8 of 18 members, against 83 of 354, an excess of 21.0 points. That is well above one member's share of the branch, 5.6 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A08 is next, at 6.1 points.

A12 runs from P01 (the call) to P15 (the bare answer). Its connection: "a one-line pledge answers a call in the same thread (form-pass finding FP-01; never counted)". Its mechanism: "Joining is the answer the call prefers, so it comes bare, one line that commits and gives no reason ('I'm in')". Its bound: it was never counted as an order, and it rests on the written, coded replies only.

By my reading of the 8 restrict lines, 6 say an acceptance asked for something, gave a reason or doubted the call. In r/stargate the answer asked for coordinates (section 1). In r/GuildWars2 "Sure." came with a request for a plan (section 4). In r/raocow a call at hour 24.22 asked whether the room had enough people, and a member answered: "Should we? Yes. Enough participation? No idea." [raocow/c0002]. Five hours later another asked: "Did it happen? I'd totally join in!" [raocow/c0004]. In r/borussiadortmund a member joined with a criticism: "No offence but I think we can make it look better than that. I'm going to start helping now" [borussiadortmund/c0026]. In r/littlewitchacademia one answer was bare, "i help" [littlewitchacademia/c0014], and another gave a reason: "Welp, with episode 13 delayed might as well help the cause here :)" [littlewitchacademia/c0027]. r/TheHulk's joiner gave a reason too (section 4). Of the other two lines, r/polyamory's records a refusal that gave a reason, too much canvas spam [polyamory/c0002], beside the answer that asked what to do; and r/clashroyale's accepts its bare replies but not the obligations the mechanism draws from them.

The other side holds in other members. In r/FlaggyFlag a member summoned three others by name, and one answered: "Sir, yes sir!" [FlaggyFlag/c0007]. In r/hatsune a post read: "A little miku standing in the corner of r/place. Let's help keep her there!" [hatsune/c0001]. A member answered: "All my dots are belong to Miku" [hatsune/c0002]. In r/wow: "LFM /r/place raid. FRESH run! Need all!" [wow/c0242]; "Count me in!" [wow/c0246]. In r/starbound: "Help us claim our spot on /r/place" [starbound/c0072]; "Support" [starbound/c0073].

This meets the key directly. The key and the restriction describe one exchange from its two ends: the key says the call needed a further turn, and the restriction says the answer was not bare. A12's mechanism treats the bare join as the preferred answer. In these rooms many willing members did not know what to do, and their answer was a question. I note for stage D6 in `notes-D5.md` that P15's "asks for nothing back" describes one form of acceptance, and that a willing answer that asks for a task is another; F-023's count (25 of 31 read acceptances bare) stands, and these rooms show where the other forms sit.

This is description, not a test. A12 has 3 within-case datings, and r/lebanon is one of them, so its expectation in F35 is 0.077, and it is not-testable-here (section 9). Members' current-ledger lines on A12, shared lines only: instantiate 8, restrict 7, add-condition 1.
