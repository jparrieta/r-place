## 7. The one relationship that runs differently here

By the rule logged at D5a-12, the arrow is the one whose restrict rate among the members most exceeds its rate among rooms of their size. It is A03: restricted in 4 of 30 members, against 25 of 318, an excess of 5.5 points. That is above one member's share of the branch, 3.3 points (the amendment of D5a-14). A09 is next, at 3.3 points.

A03 runs from P03 (building) to P05 (keeping). Its connection: "in a room carrying both, the first build report comes before the first act of upkeep or defence". Its mechanism: "Once a mark stands on a surface anyone can change, keeping it becomes the work: a finished part is something to lose."

The four restrict lines say that keeping began before the building was done. r/flyers: "Upkeep began while the logo remained unfinished and a usable template was still awaited". The relation bears on the key. The helpers' commonest condition was their own work first. A03's restrict lines say that keeping began before the building was done, and the instantiating lines say it went on after the finish.
