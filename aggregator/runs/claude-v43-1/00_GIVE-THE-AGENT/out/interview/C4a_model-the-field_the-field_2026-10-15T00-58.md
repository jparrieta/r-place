# Interview, stage C4, at the close (C4a)

The model read against the field: what the two sources share, what only the field shows, and where the rooms and the field disagree.

## What I did

- **My own model first.** I read `model-C3.md` again and wrote down the three claims a view of the whole event could most easily overturn:
  - rooms deal with other rooms after they begin to keep their work;
  - decided order is rare;
  - each room's order of steps is its own, not the event's clock.
- **Then the field, whole.** I read `field/model.md` and `field/check.md`. The field analyst worked from r/place and could not see my files.
- **The comparison in three lists.** I marked each disagreement as substantive, a difference of setting, or a difference of subject.
- **The changes.** I amended two declarations, entered three tentative entries, bounded one model sentence, and wrote the successor files.

## What I found

- **No claim was overturned.** The field supports the second claim. It holds one early buffer negotiation against the first, and one episode cannot test a count over 401 rooms. Its analyst could not find one shared progression across groups, which fits the third.
- **Five shared mechanisms.**
  - Members make agreements between rooms that they cannot make every placer keep.
  - A specified design makes the work possible to act on.
  - A request for space, answered with terms, changes a plan.
  - Members record the work so that it outlasts the canvas.
  - Members tell late proposers that placement has ended.

  The first is the strongest. A member in r/place writes "I don't speak for all rainbownians, but I agree to these terms". A member in flyers writes that one user's agreement "doesn't mean every lurker/person active on the sub agrees" [flyers/c0166].
- **Two mechanisms that only the field shows**, both entered as tentative entries:
  - Members dispute who may commit their room to an agreement (P21, with A21 from deals to the dispute).
  - After placement ends, members contest which version of the canvas and of the event is kept (P22).
- **Three sentences of mine were wrong.**
  - The model said that organizing for the canvas ended at hour 73. Placement ended; organizing around copies and records did not.
  - Standardization of output called the template the strongest coordination at scale. No test of mine ever supported that.
  - Boundary work said every room existed before the canvas, which my own count of 78 rooms already contradicted.
- **No finding changed.**

## What surprised me

[SquareSpiral/c0217]. No theme of mine names it, and I had written that organizing was over.
- **The field caught two errors in prose I wrote at stage C1 and never tested.** The tested parts of the model held. The untested sentences did not.

## What I doubt

- **The tentative entries rest on what the field marks provisional:** its explanation, not its exchanges.
- **P22's room side came from a keyword search after hour 73,** not from a theme. A reader could find many more cases, or fewer.
- **A21's direction comes from one field episode.** A dispute over who may speak for a room could come before any agreement, as a refusal to deal at all.
- **The field read my structure only in part.** Its silence on the rest is not agreement.
- **The field and I differ in what we count.** My counts are of first concepts in rooms. The field's episodes are selected. Where we agree, we agree on mechanisms, not on how common they are.
