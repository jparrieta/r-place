# Interview brief, A1c: the reading done

Target length: under 800 words. Written 2026-10-07.

## What I did

I read all 705 rooms of the analysed set in 19 blocks, in a seeded shuffle, and wrote one note per room. Every room was read through its room page, its hours line, every concept with its words, and its canvas fields. The rendered forest was read for 155 rooms, the coder's account for 174 and the brief for 98. That is less than section 4 asks. I logged the departure late, at this leg. The handback says what stage B1 should do about it.

I kept a lexicon of the rooms' own words (1,285 entries over 627 rooms) and a running file of what I kept seeing (535 lines). I left 2,478 leads for stage B1. In the last block I wrote the coordinating-turn rule and applied it to all 115,388 concepts with a saved script, which gave `organizing_end-A1.jsonl`.

## What I found

- The canvas was ahead of the talk. In 649 of 678 notes with a start hour, the room's linked artwork was being placed before hour 3. The median room's first coded turn is at h9.51. Many rooms found their work already there, or joined work others began. FederationPlace's first coded turn shows its badge finished. seventeen showed a mark it had organised on Discord before its first post.
- Neighbours fought and then made deals: borders, truces, alliances, help traded for space. Former rivals often ended as partners. Predators took help from Hawks and Wild fans. Russiaclan settled every border by alliance.
- One member's deal often did not bind the crowd. reddevils members broke the CFB truce. rpi members kept attacking WPI after their Discord agreed a move.
- Under the rule, the median room's last coordinating turn is at h72.02. 78 rooms end before hour 60, and 146 end after hour 73 with calls to rebuild.
- Restraint was a rule many rooms named for themselves: prequelmemes, yogscast, europe, predators and others.

## What surprised me

r/Summit1G. Most of its coded record (82 of 140 turns) is the chant of a raiding chat from another streamer. Canada also took its spot by force and then helped rebuild its logo elsewhere. I recorded no other room where an outside group wrote most of the record.

Placements before hour 0 surprised me too. 25 rooms' linked artworks show placements up to sixteen hours before the store's hour 0. I could not read the canvas manifest through any verb, so I filed it as an unsure defect.

## What I doubt

- The reading route. The notes rest on concepts and second-order themes. In most rooms they do not rest on the coder's account. Where a note names a turn as the room's end, the rule disagrees in 281 rooms by more than an hour.
- The coordinating-turn rule. It reads the first verb of the coder's label. It counts a question about why Discord was popular again as coordinating (reddevils/c0089), and it takes another room's ruined flag as the room's own loss. A hand check of 40 work_remained outcomes found about six doubtful.
- Calls after hour 73. I counted calls to rebuild on later canvases as coordinating. Whether that is "organizing" in the contract's sense decides 146 rooms' end hour.
- The onset contrast. The late side (61 rooms) is small and mostly tiny rooms. Its kinds (ask and move, find the mark already there, one member alone) come from the notes and are not counted.

## Three questions I would most like to be asked

1. Should the end of organizing count calls to rebuild after hour 73, or report them apart?
2. What would change in the notes if the coder's account were read for the 531 rooms read without it?
3. Is "the work was on the canvas before the room spoke" a finding about the rooms, or about how the canvas fields link artworks to rooms?
