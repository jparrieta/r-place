## What I doubt

- **Whether option (b) meets the stage's line.** The scope decision is made and printed with the claim everywhere D3 prints it. But P05's words in the model are still universal in form. If the line asks the element's own words to carry the scope, (b) does not meet it.
- **Whether my pairs measure distinct changes.** A pair joins two lines. It does not merge every line that states one change. So 499 may be too many.
- **Whether local validation can be read at all.** The labelers marked every room unresolved and every grade composes with residue, before and after. Their status does not separate the rooms.
- **Where my rulings drift.** In the blind check, add-condition, add-step and restrict lines moved between specification, elaboration, limit and particular. Instantiate and absent lines held. The class boundaries for the first group are where my rulings are least stable.
