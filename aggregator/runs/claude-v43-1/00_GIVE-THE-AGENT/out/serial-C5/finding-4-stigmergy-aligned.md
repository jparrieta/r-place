# Finding 4: Stigmergy and "aligned" (C5c-10, pass 13)

Target length: about 1,800 words. Problem 4 of the serial work, worked third in the revised order. J left the decision to me. He asked that it be argued from organization design and coordination, and tied to the contract's two questions.

Scripts: `c5c10_sparring_brief.py` (and the brief it wrote), `c5c10_aligned.py`, `c5c10_findings.py`, `c5c10_decl_amend.py` with `c5c10_decl_spec.json`, and `c5c10_f018.py`.

## 1. The question

There are two questions.
- **Stigmergy.** Is Stigmergy (T4-007), with one member since C5c-02, the right fourth-order theme? Its member is building and reporting the mark (T3-038).
- **"Aligned".** Should the dimension Coordination without command (D02) say that rooms aligned many hands, or only that they tried? The field's advice moved it to "tried" at pass 6.

How the question serves the study's two questions:
- **The theory question.** It asks how a scattered readership becomes something that can act, and how forms differ in how they coordinate.
- **The method question.** It asks whether a fourth-order theme composes upward, and whether a construct claims more than its evidence carries.

## 2. The rules, written first

The rules were logged with `--kind abductive` before I read the sources or any record (diary, C5c-10).

**The options, and the order they were named in:**
- **(a)** Keep Stigmergy with its one member.
- **(b)** Withdraw it, and place T3-038 in another D02 theme.
- **(c)** Return the two moved themes (T3-041, T3-060).
- **(d)** Merge it with Standardization of output, under Okhuysen and Bechky's "objects and representations". This was named after reading the sources.
- **(e)** Keep the theme, its member and its place, and name it for what its records show. This was named after the adversarial reader returned.

Each option was logged before it was weighed.

**The rule for the choice.** Choose the option that:
1. the members' records support;
2. the organization-design source admits (Moffett et al. 2021 or Okhuysen and Bechky 2009, with Grassé and Heylighen only as the construct's own sources);
3. keeps D02 whole, with every member in a theme and every theme grouping what it names;
4. serves the theory question and the method question.

On a tie, choose the option with fewer claims that lack support. The three-coder rule's outcome is not set aside on my belief alone.

**The rule for "aligned":**
- "The room aligned many hands" is said of the rooms whose own record reports the work it called for as built, with the count.
- "Tried" is said of the rest.
- If fewer than half of the rooms that call for hands report the work built, "tried" stays for the dimension.

## 3. What was read, with counts

**The sources, cited from the page:**
- **Moffett and Garnier 2021, p. 58.** Stigmergy "describes how the products of past efforts can channel the work of other individuals, sometimes much later".
- **Warglien and Sartoris 2021, pp. 63–64.**
  - They follow Grassé: "the construction itself is the stimulus".
  - In human settings, "stigmergy acts on the top of a large amount of planning and standardization".
  - In temporary organizations after a disaster, needs became observable "both by explicit signaling and by direct material observation" and triggered a "stigmergic response".
  - The response then "moved from completely decentralized and spontaneous to polycentric, from stigmergy and swarming to explicit signaling and (coarse grained) task routing".
- **Moffett and Garnier 2021, p. 71.** For humans, stigmergy is "a rare and invariably transitory phenomenon dependent on planning and standardization".
- **Okhuysen and Bechky 2009:**
  - p. 469: definitions of coordination share that people work collectively, that the work is interdependent, and that "a goal, task, or piece of work is achieved".
  - p. 474: objects and representations "coordinate by providing information" and offer "a common referent around which people interact, align their work, and create shared meaning".
  - p. 474, direct information sharing: "a common space for interdependent groups to post information that is vital for the current task", and "Posting information also makes the activities of the different groups visible".
  - p. 482: "a specific tool, such as a schedule, is sometimes treated as a plan and at other times is treated as an object".
  - p. 483: accountability can be achieved "through public status reports".
- **Grassé 1959, p. 75, checked.** "Plus n'est besoin de faire appel à des relations entre individus, ni à un plan suivi par les Termites". The page then advises not to rule out "une information entre ouvriers".

**The records.**
- *The three codings of 72 records (F-018).* By the code two of three coders gave, building and reporting the mark (T3-038) reports the work's state in 10 of its 14 bearing records. Extending the room's work (T3-041) and adding a personal piece (T3-060) are mostly proposals.
- *Two routes, carried apart (F-022).* Building and reporting the mark is carried by 237 rooms. Standardization of output's members are also carried by 237; the equal counts are a coincidence, and both were checked. 89 rooms carry both, about what independence would give (80). So 148 rooms report their mark's state with no template coded, and 148 share templates with no report coded.
- *The C3 mapping.* Stigmergy is mapped to information provision, and Standardization of output to task division. The other D02 theme mapped to information, Backstage decision-making, holds planning in chats, not posts in the thread.

**"Aligned" (F-021).**
- 370 of 705 rooms call for hands. 284 of these report a work up, and in 233 (0.630) the first such report comes after the first call.
- By room size, from the smallest quarter to the largest: 24 of 60, 55 of 90, 74 of 110, 80 of 110.

**The adversarial reader** (read-only). It was served the brief, which held the declarations, the member themes, the 72 three-coded records through `cite.py`, the mapping lines, the source line ranges, the options and the contract's questions. It got none of my notes. Its hardest reasons:
- **Against (a):**
  - A one-member theme fails the composition test by construction.
  - Grassé's page that sets proposals apart also sets relations between workers apart.
  - Warglien and Sartoris put hub-like explicit signaling on the far side of stigmergy.
  - Several of T3-038's M records are coordinates, which overlaps sharing coordinates (T3-070).
- **For keeping some form of the theme:** the Venice passage lets explicit signals trigger the stigmergic response. Records such as [AvoArmy/c0006] and [gameattack/c0031] read the trace.
- **Against (c):** the rule written before the tally. Its knife-edge (T3-060 at exactly half, all its P records from two rooms) is real, but it is not a reason to override the rule.
- **Against (d):**
  - Okhuysen and Bechky's own warning that a tool can be a plan or an object (p. 482).
  - Two of T4-008's members are not objects: messaging placers to stop, and complaining that calls are scattered.
  - Reports and templates create different integrating conditions.
  - A merged theme would have to give up one of its two universal problems.
- **On every option:** the record holds talk, so each option judges a construct about responding to the work using records of members talking about it.

## 7. How sure I am

- **The rename: qualified.** It claims less than Stigmergy did, and only what the records hold. Rules 2 and 4 decided it, weighed above rule 3's grouping. That weighting is mine, and the declaration says so.
- **What remains open.** The reader's hardest point stands on every option: the record holds talk. Direct information sharing is a construct about talk. Whether the posts changed what placers who never wrote did is not in the record.
- **"Aligned": qualified.**
  - 233 of 370 is a share with no null drawn. A report that a work is up is the room's own word, and it does not say who placed the pixels.
  - The gradient by size may partly reflect that larger rooms write more.
  - Okhuysen and Bechky's definition makes an achieved piece of work part of what coordination means (p. 469). By that definition, "tried" undersold the 233 rooms whose record reports the work up.
- **F-022: firm** as a count of what the coded record carries. It is soft as a claim about what rooms did, since an uncoded report may sit under another theme.
