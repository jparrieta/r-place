# Family G09  defending and repairing the work

The full tests of every theme in this family. A placement rests on the gate; the discriminators say what separates a theme from its nearest neighbours.

## T3-005  defending the work against attackers

gloss: Members rally to defend the room's work against an attack under way: alarms, calls to fight back and hold, reports of attacks staved off.
must_show: An attack on the room's work and the room's answer: alarms, calls to defend or fight back, holding the line, reports of an attack repelled.
must_not_show: Watching for damage and repairing it, with or without an attacker in view, where no fight is called (T3-021); naming or tracing the attacker (T3-087); pleading with the attackers (T3-139); attacking others' work (G12).
decide_when_both: Defence and repair: T3-005 when the attack and the fight lead, T3-021 when the watching and fixing do; tracing who attacked in concepts of its own adds T3-087.
varies: From one alarm to day-long defence against bots, raids and the Void; defenders outnumbered or victorious.
is_not: Cleaning stray pixels from the room's work with no attacker named (T3-021).
vs T3-021: Is an attack fought (T3-005), or the work watched and fixed with or without an attacker in view (T3-021)?
vs T3-087: Is the attack fought (T3-005), or its source traced (T3-087)?
vs T3-139: Do members fight the attack (T3-005), or ask the attackers to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-006: Is the room's own work defended (T3-005), or another's work attacked (T3-006)?
vs T3-081: Is the defence told as a fight (T3-005), or cast as a historic national war (T3-081)?
vs T3-011: Is the room's own work defended (T3-005), or another room's work helped or defended by the room's members, a pledge to protect it included (T3-011)?
vs T3-002: Is the call to build or finish (T3-002), or to defend against an attack under way (T3-005)?
vs T3-055: Are attackers fought off (T3-005), or the mark's shape changed against them (T3-055)?
vs T3-123: Is an attack fought off (T3-005), or a request to yield refused (T3-123)?
vs T3-124: Is the attack fought off (T3-005), or is fighting one option weighed against another (T3-124)?
vs T3-141: Is an attack fought off (T3-005), or the night covered (T3-141)?
vs T3-195: Is an agreement already made invoked, spread or said to be broken (T3-195), or the room's own work defended, the pledge named only as the reason for the defence (T3-005)?

## T3-007  urging repair over retaliation  [rare]

gloss: After an attack, members urge the room to repair its work rather than strike back.
must_show: A call to answer damage by repairing instead of retaliating against the attacker.
must_not_show: Repairing with no word on retaliation (T3-021); stopping an attack the room itself started (T3-076).
decide_when_both: A theme that fights and then urges repair: T3-005 primary when the fight leads, T3-007 added when the call against retaliation has a concept of its own.
varies: From one call to repair and coexist again to a member saying striking back is not the way.
is_not: Telling members to stop an attack the room began unprovoked (T3-076).
vs T3-021: Is the work simply repaired (T3-021), or repair urged instead of striking back (T3-007)?
vs T3-076: Is an attack the room started stopped (T3-076), or retaliation for damage it suffered declined (T3-007)?

## T3-008  messaging individual placers to stop

gloss: Members contact individual placers who damage the work, by private message or by name, and ask them to stop or tell them the plan.
must_show: Individual placers singled out and contacted: private messages to those placing wrong pixels, users named, a placer checked through their posting history and asked to stop.
must_not_show: Pleading with an attacking room as a whole (T3-139); tracing who changed the pixels with no contact (T3-087); banning the room's own vandals (T3-121).
decide_when_both: Tracing a placer and then messaging them: T3-087 when the tracing leads, T3-008 when the contact does.
varies: From friendly private messages explaining the plan to naming repeat defacers in the thread.
is_not: A post asking a whole room to stop its attack (T3-139).
vs T3-139: Is a room asked to stop (T3-139), or individual placers contacted (T3-008)?
vs T3-087: Is the placer only traced (T3-087), or contacted and asked to stop (T3-008)?
vs T3-121: Is the placer asked (T3-008), or banned (T3-121)?

## T3-021  watching and repairing the work

gloss: Members keep watch over the room's work and repair it: fixing changed and stray pixels, cleaning, keeping it intact over hours.
must_show: Upkeep of the work: watching it, fixing changed pixels, cleaning stray ones, routines of repair, reports of damage to be fixed. Repair during a raid counts here when the fixing leads and no fight is called.
must_not_show: An attack fought as such (T3-005); the mark reshaped against tampering (T3-055); watch through the night arranged as shifts (T3-141); a script that repairs (T3-028).
decide_when_both: Fight and upkeep: see T3-005. Upkeep with night shifts: T3-021 primary, T3-141 added when the shifts have concepts of their own.
varies: From a morning routine of cleaning a logo to hours of watching one small figure.
is_not: Running a script that restores the work (T3-028).
vs T3-005: Is the work watched and fixed (T3-021), or an attack fought (T3-005)?
vs T3-141: Is the work kept (T3-021), or kept through the hours others sleep, in shifts (T3-141)?
vs T3-055: Is the mark restored as it was (T3-021), or changed in shape against tampering (T3-055)?
vs T3-038: Is the work first built (T3-038), or kept and restored (T3-021)?
vs T3-028: Is it kept by hand (T3-021), or by a script (T3-028)?
vs T3-007: Is the work simply repaired (T3-021), or repair urged instead of striking back (T3-007)?
vs T3-042: Is the work repaired (T3-021), or upkeep argued as the room's priority over growth (T3-042)?
vs T3-073: Is what happened told (T3-073), or the work watched and repaired (T3-021)?
vs T3-146: Is a hate symbol or slur covered (T3-146), or the work repaired of other damage, obscene drawings included (T3-021)?

## T3-022  relocating after losing the spot

gloss: After the work is erased or overrun, the room moves it: a new spot, a smaller version elsewhere, starting again.
must_show: The work moved or restarted elsewhere because it was destroyed, overpowered or crowded out: new coordinates after a loss, 'we've moved', 'we will have to find someplace else'.
must_not_show: The first choice of a spot (T3-020); weighing whether to move (T3-124); moving because a neighbour asked for the space (T3-039).
decide_when_both: Weighing and then moving: T3-124 for the weighing, T3-022 for the move; both with concepts of their own, rule 0.
varies: From one quick move after an erasure to a run of moves and shrinkings as the canvas fills.
is_not: Moving aside when a neighbour asks for room (T3-039).
vs T3-020: Is a spot chosen in the first place (T3-020), or a new one found after a loss (T3-022)?
vs T3-124: Is the move weighed (T3-124), or made (T3-022)?
vs T3-039: Does the room move because its work was lost (T3-022), or because a neighbour asked for the space (T3-039)?
vs T3-054: Does the work move to a new spot on the same canvas (T3-022), or to another canvas (T3-054)?
vs T3-029: Is the lost work grieved (T3-029), or moved or restarted elsewhere (T3-022)?

## T3-139  pleading with attackers to stop

gloss: Members ask the room or group damaging their work to stop or spare it: 'please don't destroy our logo'.
must_show: Requests addressed to an attacking room or group to stop, spare the work, or let it live.
must_not_show: Fighting the attack (T3-005); messaging individual placers (T3-008); asking a host room for space before any damage (T3-035).
decide_when_both: Pleading inside a border deal: T3-010 primary when the deal leads, T3-139 added when the pleading has concepts of its own.
varies: From a polite request posted in the attacker's own thread to angry demands that a faction end its expansion.
is_not: A request to a host for room before any damage (T3-035).
vs T3-005: Do members fight the attack (T3-005), or ask the attackers to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-008: Is a group or room asked (T3-139), or individual placers contacted (T3-008)?
vs T3-035: Is space asked for (T3-035), or an attack asked to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-046: Is the fairness of covering others' work argued (T3-046), or the covering asked to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-076: Are the room's own attackers stopped (T3-076), or another room's attackers asked to stop (T3-139)?
vs T3-010: Is a line or space settled between neighbours (T3-010), or another room's attackers asked to stop (T3-139)?
