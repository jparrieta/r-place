# Boundaries ruled once (stage B4 section 5), packet 1

Ruled at B4a-01 before the packet's first item, for every sibling pair on which five or more of the packet's items split the two readings' primaries (`b4_prepare.json`), and for two defects in the scheme met on the way. Each item the ruling decides carries the pair in `boundary_ruling`. The item list of each is written by `b4_boundary_lists.py` when the packet is ruled, and the paragraphs go into `adjudication_report.md`.

**T3-001|T3-023 (20 items in packet 1).** The discriminator: "Is the joke the sub's own changed state (its name, look, rules, subject or a staged takeover)? T3-023. Is it a member's post claiming something false inside the sub? T3-001." Ruled: the joke's stage decides. The sub may be changed for the day in its name, look, rules or subject. That change counts whether the moderators made it or members flooding the sub in one format made it "as a sub acting together" (T3-023's `must_show`; conflict CC-001). Then it is T3-023, and members' posts inside it are playing along, which T3-023's `decide_when_both` keeps there. Members' own false claims in an unchanged sub are T3-001. An item with both acts, each with concepts of its own, takes the larger as primary and adds the other (`genuine-joint`). Where neither the concepts nor the note show whether the sub itself changed, the test cannot be applied: `scheme-ambiguous`, field `must_show`, the sentence "or by a sub acting together".

**T3-017|T3-031 (16) and T3-001|T3-031 (5): the canvas condition.** The scheme gives two answers for an in-joke played with no canvas in it:
- **T3-031 requires the canvas.** Its `must_show` reads "The room's own humour carried into canvas talk", and its discriminator against T3-017 asks "Does the sub's ordinary talk go on with no canvas in it (T3-017), or is the joke played around the canvas work (T3-031)?"
- **Two discriminators do not.** T3-017's asks "Is it the room's running in-joke carried into the canvas talk or the day (T3-031)", and T3-001's places "the room's ordinary in-jokes and memes on any day" in T3-031.

Ruled: T3-031's own tests govern, since the theme sits in the canvas family G20. An in-joke with canvas in it is T3-031. An in-joke with none is the sub's ordinary talk, T3-017, whose gloss names "its usual subject, news, help, jokes and quarrels". Where the joke is April Fools play, the gap below applies. Every item decided on this pair is `scheme-ambiguous`, field `discriminators`: the disagreeing discriminator is the sentence stage C1 must repair.

**T3-001|T3-048 (13).** The discriminator: "Whose joke is it: the room's members (T3-001), or the game's company, the show's creator, an official channel or organisation (T3-048)?" Ruled: the source shown in the concepts decides. Where the source cannot be read from the surviving words (a removed body, a bare title), the test cannot be applied: the ruling is `unresolved`, `scheme-ambiguous` on that discriminator.

**T3-023|T3-095 (11).** The discriminator: "Are members working out what the sub's change is and whether it is real (T3-095), or playing inside it (T3-023)?" T3-023's `decide_when_both`: "Working out what the change was in three or more concepts adds T3-095." Ruled: playing inside the change leads unless most concepts work it out. T3-095 is added at three or more concepts of its own, and is primary when most concepts work it out.

**T3-001|T3-095 (10).** The discriminator: "Did members of this room stage the claim being tested (T3-001), or did it come from elsewhere, the moderators, a company, a public figure, the news, another room (T3-095)?" T3-095's `decide_when_both`: "If the room's members staged the claim, T3-001 even when readers test it." Ruled: the claim's maker decides. A test of a member's own fake is T3-001, and a test of anyone else's claim is T3-095. Where the claim's maker is not shown, the ruling is `unresolved`, as at T3-001|T3-048.

**T3-001|T3-017 (8).** The discriminator: "Is anything posted as a fake for April Fools (T3-001), or are the posts ordinary (T3-017)?" T3-017's `decide_when_both` adds usual talk inside an April Fools theme only at three or more concepts of its own. Ruled as written.

**T3-015|T3-063 (7).** The discriminator: "Is the post sent to one gathering thread (T3-015), or removed or filtered under a standing rule with no such pointer (T3-063)?" Both themes' `decide_when_both` say: "both with concepts of their own, the larger primary and the other under rule 0." Ruled as written: removals with a pointer count toward T3-015.

**T3-048|T3-095 (6).** The discriminator: "Is it still open whether the announcement is a joke (T3-095), or is the joke known and members judge it (T3-048)?" Ruled: the larger share decides. T3-048's `decide_when_both` says "When most concepts ask whether an announcement is real, T3-095."

**T3-032|T3-097 (5).** The discriminator: "Are the jokes themselves bad or unwelcome (T3-032), or is the joke look in the way of reading and voting (T3-097)?" T3-032 needs three or more concepts of objection. Ruled as written. Met in the packet: T3-032's `must_show` ("asking members or moderators to stop them or take them down") and T3-097's ("requests to switch back") both take a request to end the sub's joke look or rule, and T3-032's `must_not_show` sends only readability complaints to T3-097. Applied: complaints about the sub's own joke look or rule, and requests to end it, go to T3-097; objections to the jokes as jokes, or to joke posts, go to T3-032. Items whose split turns on that overlap are `scheme-ambiguous`, field `must_show` of T3-032.

**T3-063|T3-185 (5).** The discriminator: "Is a standing rule applied (T3-063), or is a rule made, dropped or argued (T3-185)?" T3-185's `decide_when_both` says: "Applying and changing in one theme: the larger part primary and the other under rule 0." Ruled as written: a moderator's exception for the day, or a rule changed for the joke, counts as a rule made. A rule change staged as the joke itself is T3-023.

**A gap: April Fools play that is not a fake posted as true.** Some items hold holiday prompts, imagined pranks, foolish-pick games, and pranks on family told to the room. These are not posted as true, so they fall outside T3-001's `must_show` ("post something false as true"). They are not a change to the sub (T3-023), the maker's joke (T3-048), a test (T3-095), an objection (T3-032), a workaround (T3-097) or a keep request (T3-100). T3-017 excludes them: "no April Fools joke as its point". The scheme has no theme for them. Ruled `unresolved`, `scheme-ambiguous`, field `must_show` of T3-001, and carried to stage C1 as a missing theme. Where an in-joke of the room carries such play into the canvas talk, it is T3-031 by the canvas condition above.

**A gap met in the packet: another sub's April Fools change.** Members talk about a change another sub made to itself for the day (r/chicagobulls in an Iowa State design, r/Texans looking like a Katawa Shoujo sub, r/space borrowing the room's CSS). T3-023's `must_show` begins "A change made to a whole sub for April Fools" and does not say whose sub. T3-048 takes a joke by "the maker the sub is about", and another sub is no maker. T3-064 takes the room's own joke carried elsewhere by members. Ruled `unresolved`, `scheme-ambiguous`, field `must_show` of T3-023, quoting that sentence. Two cases differ. One sub changing another for the day is T3-023, which its `must_show` names ("one sub taking over another for the day"). A community's Discord server is not the sub, so a change to it is not T3-023 either.

**A gap met in the packet: moderators applying standing rules on the joke day (conflict CC-004).** T3-063's `must_show` begins "Canvas posts handled under the sub's standing rules". A moderator removing an April Fools post, or a duplicate or repost on the joke day, under the sub's ordinary rules is not a canvas post. T3-032 is members objecting in three concepts. No theme takes the moderators' act. Where such removals are the whole item, the ruling is `unresolved`, `scheme-ambiguous`, field `must_show` of T3-063. Where they sit beside a placed theme, they are named in the ruling's why and not placed.

## Pairs met in the packet on fewer than five items

These pairs were not ruled before the first item. Each was decided by the tests as written, and the items carry the pair in `boundary_ruling` so the decision can be found.

**T3-023|T3-051: upvote bargains to change the sub (conflict CC-007).** T3-051's `must_show` names "bargains of the form 'if this gets N upvotes'". A post promising that the sub will become Club Penguin-, toaster- or Communist-themed at N upvotes is that bargain. It is T3-023 only where the change was made, as at warframe t06, where the banner did change. A bargain whose change was never made is T3-051 alone.

**T3-001|T3-051: a promise for upvotes.** The same test decides it. A promise traded for upvotes ("for every upvote this gets I'll go one day without shitposting") is T3-051, not a fake posted as true.

**T3-031|T3-048 and T3-023|T3-031: the canvas condition met beside other G01 themes.** The canvas condition ruled above applies. Play with the maker's joke content, such as the Super Adventure Box terms or infantile mode, is T3-048 by whose joke it is. A fandom mash-up played as the sub's theme for the day is T3-023. T3-031 needs the canvas in either case. These items are `scheme-ambiguous` on T3-017's discriminator, as on the T3-017|T3-031 pair.

**T3-023|T3-048: whose change.** A named organisation's reveal (1RIT's) with no change to the sub in the words is T3-048. T3-023 needs the sub itself changed.

**T3-023|T3-100: a change brought back.** Members reviving the room's April Fools look from two years before, and asking the moderators to restore it, play inside the look together (T3-023, members acting together) and ask for it back. T3-100's "stay after the day" does not say whether it reaches a change returned years later. That item is `scheme-ambiguous` on T3-100's `must_show`.

**T3-023|T3-185: was the rule meant to hold?** A moderators' ban on one kind of post "until further notice", enforced with jokes, may be a real rule or the day's joke. T3-185's `must_not_show` ("a rule change staged as a joke for the day") turns on that, and the members themselves could not tell. That item is `scheme-ambiguous` on T3-185's `must_not_show`. It was placed T3-023 because the play inside the rule reads as the joke.
