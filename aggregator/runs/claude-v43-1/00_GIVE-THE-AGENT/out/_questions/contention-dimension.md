# Open distinction: is contention a dimension of its own?

Opened at C2a-01 (stage C2, pass 1). Two readings of the dimension partition stay open across pass 1.

## Reading 1, kept at pass 1

Attack (T4-020 Repertoire of contention) and rivalry (T4-021 Rivalry) sit in D04 Order without law beside restraint, deals, reciprocity and territory. Attribution (T4-019) sits in D03 Maintenance. Restraint and attack are two moves in one order between rooms. The pair test called the pairs of settling borders (T3-010) with truces (T3-149) and with bartering help (T3-151) joints, not lumps.

## Reading 2, the rival

Attribution, Repertoire of contention and Rivalry form a contention dimension. It would answer a question no member answers alone: how a room turns a change to its work into a fight, and how it keeps one going.

## What pass 1 found

- No arrow runs from attribution to restraint or to attack. P06 to P07 holds in 0.452 of rooms against a null mean of 0.456. P06 to P09 holds in 0.194 against 0.408, reversed. The hinge the interview proposed does not show in the order of first concepts.

- The onset contrast keeps the difference in calls to deface rivals' work at held size: 0.41 of early rooms against 0.08 of late rooms.

## What would decide it

Reading 2 is preferred if pass 2 grounds an arrow into or out of attack as a room's own work, with a mechanism that does not run through border talk. One candidate is rivalry carried in from outside the canvas, leading to the room's first attack. Reading 1 stays if attack is told best as one move in the order between rooms: an attack answered by talk, or talk broken and answered by attack, within one pair of rooms.
