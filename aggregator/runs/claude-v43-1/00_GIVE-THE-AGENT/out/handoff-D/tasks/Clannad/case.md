## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: a small Clannad group kept shared marks alive through calls to protect, border responses, and cooperation, then documented and celebrated their survival.

The competing account written before search was that the thread mainly records expansion and antagonism rather than cooperative protection. If that were stronger, the record should contain explicit urging to attack or take other groups' space, rejection of cooperation, or reports that the shared marks failed to survive.

The strongest contrary item is the direct reply “we have killed their borders” (`dfrgtgv`). Together with proposals to expand upward for better letters (`dfrbef4`) and to expand against the void (`dfrh7ut`), it means the case cannot be described as cooperation alone. The record-wide searches tested attack/destroy/kill/eat/expand/take-over/invasion language, cooperation/help/defence/protection/pact language, and failure/loss/finished/final/intact language. Every returned item was read. The relevant results contained no explicit call to initiate an attack or take over another group's space, no rejection of cooperation, and no report that the marks failed; the other matches were ordinary Clannad uses of words such as “finished” and “help.” The cooperation claim (`dfrpf3e`) and mostly-intact outcome (`dfsniti`) therefore survive, but only alongside a retaliatory border claim. Null results apply only to this 11-thread window with a single r/place thread and cannot establish how participants acted elsewhere.
