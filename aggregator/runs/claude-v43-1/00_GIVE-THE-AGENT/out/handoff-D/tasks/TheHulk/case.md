## Relations — what set off what

The Yoshi Egg border agreement is an evidenced response: `62xz6p` asks for discussion about two overlapping squares, and 15.3 minutes later `dfpwttr` answers, “I am okay with your proposed border.” The subsequent offer “I can help you guys preserve hulk as well” at `dfpwwzd`, 3.3 minutes after that answer, explicitly follows the agreement in words as well as reply structure.

At `62y10b`, the same speaker supplies a participant explanation rather than an independently established cause: “The name was too easily griefed. Building Green Lattice around HULK now.” The supported claim is that this participant presented repeated griefing as the reason for switching from the name to lattice; the record does not establish that every builder shared that reason.

Relocation is likewise linked in the speaker's own words at `631voy`: “It looks like we are losing this fight to the rebel Croatians. New HULK is being built down to the right of old HULK.” This supports the participant's explanation that losing that fight prompted the new build. It does not show that “Croatia” acted with one mind: `dfqo5xg` says the flag makers did not support destruction, `dfqoj3j` promises “Croatians will help,” and `dfqojto` credits Croatians with keeping old Hulk up.

Some relations are narrower call-and-answer practices. “Hair needs fixing” at `632ock` receives “My bad. Fixing it now” at `dfqthdc` after 4.9 minutes. The r/marvel plea at `dfry607` receives “Good call! Hopefully that helps” at `dfryjbn` after 7.5 minutes; that answer supports approval of the plea, not a claim that r/marvel supplied help. Similarly, the sequence from the general defence call at `637r1e` to “I'm doing what I can” at `dfrxq8t` 15.6 minutes later shows a stated response, but later visual improvement cannot be assigned to that one response.

The post at `6382up` follows canvas closure by about one minute and is followed by refusal at `dfs04z1`. Contrasting Croatian threats, promises, apologies, and assistance come from different speakers; they do not establish that the community changed its mind.

## Counter-reading

My reading before search was: this small room kept a Hulk on the canvas by repeatedly calling people back to repair it, working out borders and relocation with neighbours, and distinguishing helpful Croatians from attackers rather than treating the conflict as one undivided war.

A plausible competing account was that Hulk survived mainly because Croatian actors took control of relocation and defence while the Hulk room was largely passive. If that account were right, the record would contain repeated Croatian claims that they built, protected, or saved Hulk, together with few concrete passages in which Hulk participants organise or perform the work themselves.

The strongest contrary item is `dfs23w9`: “Especially when we relocated and in the last hour when you all help kick the trolls back!” It directly credits the Croatian interlocutors with consequential help in both relocation and final defence. Searches for Croatian help, protection, building, saving, relocation, defence, and kicking trolls also returned `631luy`, `dfqoj3j`, `dfqojto`, `6324lz`, `dfqntpy`, and `dfqpsew`; each returned item was read. `dfqsqh0` was also returned because it mentions Croatia near another group's relocation, but its “kind souls” concern Yoshi Egg, not Hulk.

The contrary evidence changes the emphasis: outside Croatian help was not incidental, and the survival account must include negotiated relocation and final-hour assistance. The passive-room account does not survive. Direct local organisation and work appear repeatedly at `62wi6s`, `6303xl`, `631voy`, `632ock`, `637r1e`, `dfrxq8t`, and `dfry607`. The evidence therefore supports interdependent defence, not a self-sufficient Hulk room and not a Croatian takeover.

## Saturation

The last stretch adds post-event congratulations and apology, but not a new way of organising the pixel work.

This is record saturation, not a claim about all historical activity. Four post bodies are deleted while their titles survive, one further item is deleted, and the pre-week archive supplied no items.
