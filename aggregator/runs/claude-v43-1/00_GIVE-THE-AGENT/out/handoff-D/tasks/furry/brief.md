## Evidence base

The corpus contains 3,692 items in 452 threads. I read 334 complete item bodies from 85 threads. All seven calendar days had items, all seven were touched, and there were no empty days. Every served item body was complete. (state)

Most threads and items remained unopened. Titles were sampled across the full index and by day. The giveaway thread `62adcf` was opened through item 15 of 73, and `Furry Worries` through item 20 of 51. Their served item bodies were complete, but their remaining comments were left unopened. (state)

## Vernacular

`Fursona` names a character that can represent its maker. `Sona` is a shortened form used without explanation.

> I would eventually like to make a fursona that represents me. (`61dwui`)

> I think that making a hime just comes naturally with making a sona. (`dfdubea`)

Members also call themselves `furs`. A `furmeet` is presented as a way to meet them after talking online.

> Hello furs! I hope you are all having a good day/night, and if not, I hope this thread can provide some help. (`61jxeg`)

> Agree with the furmeet suggestion.  Helps if you talk online first (`dfib843`)

In art offers, a `ref` is a reference drawing or sheet for a character.

> If you'd like a basic ref drawn, please comment below with your reference sheet or an explanation of your character. (`dfkzlik`)

`Iron Artist` is the name of a flexible personal production challenge. The artist in the opened thread used it for commission work.

> Iron Artist is a personal challenge for some graphic artists, usually includes them producing a greater than usual quantity of images over pre-defined period of time. (`dfll3oq`)

## Data problems

The 143 deleted or removed items hide content that cannot be recovered from this corpus, and 107 of 3,293 recorded parent pointers are unresolved. Claims about reply structure are therefore limited to threads whose relevant parent links resolved. (header)

Coverage was broad by day but shallow relative to 452 threads and 3,692 items. No command output was truncated, no served body had an unfinished continuation, and no active day was missed. Absence and uniqueness claims in this brief are bounded to the opened sample. (state)
