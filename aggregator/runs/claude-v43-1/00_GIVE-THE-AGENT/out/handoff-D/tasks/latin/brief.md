## Vernacular

`Verbum Diei` means the recurring word-of-the-day post. It gives a headword, definition, grammar, classical example, translation, and derivatives, then asks readers for favorite, hated, or funny words.

> For the English speakers amongst us, this thread marks a Multiloquous Monday; it's specifically for (relatively informally) conversing or composing in Latin! (`61owg1`)
