# r/GreenCorner

## Chronology

The room begins as a newly named green place rather than as a settled command structure. In the opening episode, from `62nnkh` at d0 03-31 19:55 to `62oj1x` at d0 03-31 22:10, participants hail “Slime,” invent a “Church of the Great Green Box,” ask people to “constantly fix miscolors,” try to write `/r/GreenCorner` on the canvas, and repeatedly advertise Discord. The lettering proposal in `62o8yv` explains its own recruitment bind: “we can't direct people here until we manage to write it! It's a bit of a catch 22.”

The next episode, from `62on21` at d0 03-31 22:28 to `62pzyr` at d0 04-01 02:23, combines defense, outward attack, and extensive bargaining. A moderator post says “DEFEND THE GREEN CORNER!” in `62oopr`; other posts call for going north, merging with Green Lattice, or attacking Blue. At the same time neighbours ask Green to spare the RIP TOM memorial, a name, a tree, and other art. Green participants answer the RIP TOM request by saying “I will not overrun any of your message, but I will build around the box” in `dfo9oug`, and `62pzyr` turns that bargain into a broader rule: “attacking any art is strictly prohibited.” This restraint coexists with expansion proposals; it does not erase them.

The room also tries several ways to choose and coordinate action. Between `62pr0k` at d0 04-01 01:36 and `62sarp` at d0 04-01 12:00, participants hold leader elections, claim ambassador roles, negotiate in Discord, and dispute whether pixels should protect allies or remain at home. In `62phh0`, the call to make a second green area is answered 1.3 minutes later by “We don't have enough forces to divert our efforts”; the proposer accepts that 3.3 minutes later with “Oh ok” in `dfob8a6`.

The loss episode runs from `62qq9x` at d0 04-01 04:56 to `62toxe` at d1 04-01 16:19. `62qq9x` opens with “Blue is beginning to eat us!” and asks for “alliances. Anything with green or not blue.” In the same thread, `dfoqmqh` says “the battle is lost” 4.2 hours after the opening. Posts then call for total reclamation, report a Blue invasion, seek replacement corners, and expose surrender pitches to Blue as a “Trojan horse” in `dfonog0`. By `62toxe`, Green Lattice is welcoming “survivors” as “refugees,” while `dfp68ou` describes joining Purple after displacement.

The final episode, from `62ud56` at d1 04-01 18:04 to `dft486b` at d3 04-04 08:58, alternates between negotiated recovery and remembrance. `62ud56` announces a reciprocal Red treaty. In `62vokr`, the self-described president offers three choices—keep replacing, expand, or move—and reports that the poll chose continued fighting from a small square. The later post `62w1f5`, 58 minutes after the poll post opened, explicitly says “After the poll” and announces the same small-square plan. The sequence then turns between “RIP Greencorner” in `62yj69`, another attempted comeback in `62youk`, “Never surrender!” in `62yury`, and the moderator memorial “Gone but not forgotten” in `636yt9`. The last reply, `dft486b`, is a Green Lattice taunt rather than evidence that this room reversed its own view.

## Relations — what set off what

Several links are supported by participants' own words, not merely by reply order.

In `62oqq9`, the Red envoy explains the alliance request by saying Blue is “viciously conquering territory” and Red is losing pixels; the proposed response is to “put our differences aside, and unite against the common enemy, blue.” This is the envoy's explanation for the request, not independent proof that Blue caused every alliance.

The Red alliance also activates a remembered grievance. `dfobqgz` asks whether Green is eager to side with its “original destroyers” and predicts “Red will turn on you again.” Its direct reply `dfoc3u9`, 8.8 minutes later, accepts the remembered “greenocide” but says to “put the past behind us and tackle the blue menace first.” The exchange establishes contrasting strategies within the same moment; it does not establish that the community changed its mind.

Resource scarcity produces an explicit redirection in `62phh0`: `dfob33v` answers the proposed northern front with “We don't have enough forces to divert our efforts,” and `dfob8a6` accepts the answer. A different contrast remains unresolved in `62pi9y`: the post says Red must be protected “at all costs,” while `dfom8bb` says to let Blue and Red spend pixels fighting because “It gives us time to expand.” These are competing positions, not a supported collective decision.

The election dispute is also explicit. `dfoyfld` objects, “YOUR HAVING FUCKING ELECTIONS WHILE WE'RE GETTING ATTACKED”; `dfoynue` answers 6.2 minutes later, “A principle of the Green Corner is democracy, always.” This supports the relation between the objection and the defense of voting. It supports no broader causal claim about whether elections improved defense.

`62w1f5` names the poll as its source—“After the poll, we've determined that we will keep fighting”—so the announced small-square strategy is participant-attributed to the poll in `62vokr`. Because the edit time of the result inside `62vokr` is unavailable, the 58-minute opening-to-opening interval is only the observed post sequence, not the duration of deliberation.

Other sequences have no demonstrated causal link. The calls for alliance in `62qq9x` precede the defeat statements and refugee offers in `62toxe`, but the corpus does not show that the Reddit calls produced either the loss or the migration. Likewise, adjacent replies in the forest show who answered whom; adjacency alone is not treated as causation.

## Posts against comments

The post layer is directive and declarative: defend (`62oopr`), expand first (`62p88p`), protect Red (`62pi9y`), reclaim everything (`62r2ri`), vote (`62sarp`), or keep fighting after the poll (`62w1f5`). The comment layer often supplies limits that the posts omit.

The clearest contradiction is in `62phh0`: the post proposes opening a new northern front, while `dfob33v` says there are not enough forces and redirects effort to the existing corner. In `62pi9y`, the post says to protect Red “at all costs,” while `dfom8bb` argues the opposite—let Red and Blue fight so Green can expand. In `62sarp`, the post presents another election as routine, while `dfoyfld` condemns holding it during an attack; `dfoynue` then defends voting as a standing principle.

Comments also complicate apparent consensus around alliances. The Red diplomatic post `62p2qt` proposes peace, but `dfobqgz` invokes Red's earlier destruction of the green home; its reply `dfoc3u9` argues for temporary cooperation despite that history. Conversely, the RIP TOM request in post `62p0na` receives both accommodation in `dfo9oug` and the contrary report in `dfo922v` that “some” were still trying to overrun it.

Some claims live only in one layer. Leadership claims and strategic proclamations are mainly posts (`62r40e`, `62ud56`, `62vokr`), while acceptance, refusal, suspicion, and tactical qualification often appear in comments (`dfob8a6`, `dfom8bb`, `dfonog0`). That distribution is descriptive; it does not show that posters and commenters were stable opposing groups.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that GreenCorner treated green as a shared home to defend through upkeep, alliances, negotiated restraint, and repeated recovery; after losing it, participants carried the home into retreat and remembrance.

Before searching, the competing account was that Green was mainly a license for unchecked expansion, not a bounded home defended through bargains and restraint. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain participants endorsing overrunning neighbours or art and rejecting borders, treaties, or protected symbols.

The search covered the record for language around “overrun,” “destroy,” attacks on art, unrestricted or bounded expansion, border respect, and “inferior colors.” It returned 13 items, each read individually. The strongest contrary item is `dfo922v`: “There are some who are still trying to overrun you.” That changes the account by making restraint contested rather than universal. It does not overturn the reading, because the same item labels the over-runners “domestic terrorists” and says Blue connections “cannot be ruled out”; `dfo9oug` promises not to overrun the memorial, `62pukr` proposes “border respect” and bounded expansion, and `62pzyr` says attacking art is “strictly prohibited.” The search also found direct anti-Blue destruction (`dfo6tqv`) and “inferior colors” language (`62q8iy`, `62qagy`), so restraint applied unevenly: it was strongest toward named art and negotiated neighbours, not toward enemy colors.

No searched item explicitly rejected all borders, treaties, or protected art. This null result is limited to the supplied 97-hour record and the tested vocabulary; it cannot establish that no such view existed, especially where Discord discussion is unseen and bodies were removed.

## Saturation

The thematic record does not settle early. Initial identity and defense are followed by alliance bargains, border and art negotiations, elections, defeat, refugee offers, renewed recovery, and finally memorialization. Later material mostly repeats defense and recovery, but `636yt9` adds the durable practice of memorializing a corner described as gone. The instrument computes the location of the last new first-order code; I do not estimate it here.

The Reddit record is structurally strong: every one of the 86 threads was disposed, all 243 in-window items were served, and the header reports every recorded reply parent present. Its main substantive limit is venue movement. Discord invitations in `62oe81`, `dfocmrf`, `dfodvz6`, and `62r40e` show discussion, peace negotiation, invitations, and a leadership claim moving off-platform, but not the decisions inside those servers. The other limit is deletion: 27 items have deleted or removed text, including 19 posts whose titles survive. Surviving titles and replies preserve some conduct, but not the missing bodies.

## Selection

I read the empty pre-week briefing, then read `openings.md` in full before triage, as the case-lead instrument requires. The openings showed that the subreddit itself was organized around the Green Corner r/place effort. I then went through every thread in corpus order. Short windows were served whole; the nine longer threads that displayed triage windows were explicitly opened in full. Every thread touched the community's r/place conduct, including hostile posts, jokes in the faction's register, diplomacy, and retrospective mourning, so all 86 were retained as `coded`. No thread was excluded as out of scope.

## Other communities named

Other communities appear as prospective allies, enemies, neighbours, or refuges rather than as a single stable coalition. GreenCheckerboard proposes holding a line against Blue in `62o1pl`; Red offers a short-range alliance in `62p2qt`; Purple, OSU, the Carrot Farm, and Green Lattice appear in distinct aid or coexistence proposals (`62pouf`, `62q6wa`, `62q6nn`, `62toxe`). Metalcore, TreePlace, Weezer, and Smash ask for named art to be spared (`62p0na`, `62pxaq`, `62q5x2`, `62umhm`). These pointers show what this room was asked to do; they do not establish what the other communities did beyond the quoted speakers' claims.

Discord and Strawpoll play different roles. Discord is repeatedly offered as the place for planning or negotiation (`62oe81`, `dfocmrf`, `dfodvz6`, `62r40e`); Strawpoll carries a leader vote from `62pr0k`. What returned to Reddit was an invitation, a claimed leadership outcome, or an announced decision. The deliberation and vote records themselves are not visible.

## Absence

Deleted and removed bodies are concentrated in short posts whose surviving titles still issue calls, attacks, or announcements, such as “We need people to constantly fix miscolors” in `62o23c`, “DOWN WITH THE GREEN!” in `62otyl`, and “Start expanding the green corner!” in `62ptju`. I coded those titles as surviving public conduct and did not reconstruct their bodies. In longer exchanges, deletion sometimes interrupts a chain, as in `62pqq3`, but surviving replies still show the offer to rebuild a damaged name and the later Discord invite.

One flagged automatic account, sneakpeekbot, appears under `62p2qt`; its generated subreddit preview was treated as platform setting and not coded. No missing-parent structure appears in the supplied record. The absence claims above are therefore limited to this archive: complete reply linkage cannot recover removed text or off-platform talk.
