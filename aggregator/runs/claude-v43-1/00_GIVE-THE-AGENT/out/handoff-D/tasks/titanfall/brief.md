## Evidence base

The corpus contains 1,490 threads and 19,308 items. I served 44 threads and 422 unique items, all 422 complete, across all seven active days; there were no empty days. I left 1,446 threads unopened, sampled rather than exhausted the title index, and paged only part of several large served threads. This is a broad sample, not a basis for corpus-wide absence or uniqueness claims. (state)

> My only criticism is that it says "Press C to deploy Tick" instead of "Press C to deploy Spicy Boy." (`dfftasq`)

Titan names also mark player identities. One participant calls themself a “Northstar main” while comparing weapons and balance.

> As a Northstar main I'm finding it difficult lately to not get pissy about the spamminess of those two (`dfd3p4f`)

The free-weekend thread explicitly glosses one abbreviation for newcomers.

> **Last Titan Standing** or LTS is a titan-only round-based TDM (`dflivcm`)

## What I could not tell

The opened sample does not establish a community consensus on how to treat newcomers. One post asks experienced players to coach them.

> Encourage the new players and try to give them tips when they spend the whole round running on the ground. (`627aoa`)

Another participant rejects any duty to make the free weekend easier.

> No. It's not my job nor anybody else's that plays this game to sell it to newcomers. (`dfkeghx`)

I also could not verify the origin of “Spicy Boys,” assign stable social roles beyond the visible accounts described above, or decode every abbreviation that appeared without explanation. These limits reflect the opened sample rather than claims about the full corpus. (state)

## Data problems

The supplied archive contains 635 deleted or removed items and 644 unresolved parents among 18,007 recorded parent pointers. Those gaps limit claims about missing remarks and some reply chains. (header)

No reader response was truncated in transit, and every served item counted as complete in the final receipt. The main limitation is coverage: 422 of 19,308 items across 44 of 1,490 threads, with some large threads intentionally left partly paged. (state)
