## Data problems

Deleted or removed bodies and 509 unresolved parents limit reconstruction of some exchanges. The record supplies 1,417 deleted or removed items, and their missing text was not inferred. (header)

Several served threads began before the window and therefore lacked their opening posts in this corpus. Large threads were only partly opened even though every individual item served from them was complete. (state)

The 431 complete items cover all active days but only 75 of 1,358 threads. Claims about absence, uniqueness, and recurrence are therefore bounded to the opened sample. (state)
