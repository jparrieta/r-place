# r/SquareSpiral

## Chronology

The supplied week begins without an ordinary-week baseline: the pre-event archive is empty. The openings table nevertheless shows that this venue itself begins as an r/place room. In the first episode, from `62oga4` at d0 03-31 21:57 through `62p64i`, people welcome recruits, send them to Discord, invite pixel placers by private message, patch “rogue pixels,” expand, and speak of the design as a “monument to love.” This account comes from batch 01’s slice chronology and the forest; the lead read the openings before dispatch but not the raw slice.

From `62p7c3` through `62u906` at d1 04-01 17:47, recruiting becomes visible construction and diplomacy. People propose a canvas sign, make treaties and border requests, and shorten R/SQUARESPIRAL to R/SQSP. The post `62rncy` gives two reasons in the participant’s own words—time needed for the full sign and “Concerns raised by neighbors to the north”—and says the shorter proposal “was adopted.” The poll post `62u906` then reports 26 votes for the short sign, five for the long one, and four indifferent. That is a reported decision, not evidence that every builder agreed: `62soz9` records an all-night builder saying the change wasted the work.

From `62ugeb` through `62wza5` at d1 04-02 01:30, the room alternates “EXPAND, REPAIR, RESPECT ARTWORK, MAKE FRIENDS” with emergency defense. The Void’s approach produces calls to allies and a direct fight order at `62w9rn`; 2 hours 15 minutes later, `62wza5` says the attack has been beaten back and assigns cleanup and “anti-Pikashit” work while deferring whether expansion should end. These are batch 01’s closing events.

Batch 02 opens with infrastructure and limits. From `62x3g9` through `62ziyf`, members add flair, poll for a core, negotiate buffers and permissions, and tell one another to defend the sign and center. `62xev6` reports a 23–6–2 vote for a 109-by-109 core while still allowing peaceful expansion; `62ziyf` later declares the “Era of Expansion” over. This is a descriptive development in posted policy. It does not establish a single community-wide change of mind, because comments and later posts keep proposing expansion.

From `630rzu` through `632qea`, accusations against Finland, Brazil, Argentina, and Touhou mix with denials that organized allies are responsible. As the room says it is being overrun, `631qt4` at d2 04-02 19:41 announces that the core is moving, gives a red center at 140,740, and sends coordination to Discord. The smaller core is then defended against new artwork; `632qea` at d2 04-02 22:29 calls the sign the room’s only easy way to recruit. Batch 02 therefore closes on a diminished design still being actively organized, not on an outcome.

Batch 03 inherits an attack already underway. From `632rtw` at d2 04-02 22:36 through `63477m`, people attribute rapid loss to a scripted botnet, hold inner squares, broadcast the saved center, switch to red-only patching, mourn, and announce “WE ARE COMING BACK.” From `6348p5` through `6352nz` at d2 04-03 06:28, rebuilding becomes a dispute over retaliation and limits. The moderator-backed plan in `6352nz` assigns a 41-by-30 footprint, reserves Brazil’s space, says “The era of expansionism is over,” and asks for “no retributions.”

From `636crp` through `637i61` at d2 04-03 15:31, participants fit the rebuilt spiral among Tango, Brazil’s beam, and neighboring art. `63726p` says expansion should become “a background for art”; 1 hour 11 minutes later, `637i61` reports a confirmed plan to change the spiral’s shades where it merges with the Brazilian beam. That interval is an observed sequence. The corpus does not say the first post caused the agreement.

Placement ends at `638317`, d3 04-03 17:03. Thirteen minutes later `63869x` thanks the people who recruited, fought, made friends, and rebuilt. From `638bku` through `63d4bk`, people create a Steam group, make jokes and art, reproduce the spiral in beads, and start spirals on successor canvases. The final visible cleanup discussion remains open: `63d4bk` asks for outside visibility, and its reply `dftdeo0` at d3 04-04 14:11 argues that imperfections should remain as “battle wounds.”

## Relations — what set off what

Finnish repair explicitly sets off a peace proposal in `62phzn`: “the Finnish flag seems to be steadily repairing itself” is immediately followed by “we should make peace and respect their progress.” This supports the relation because the participant names the observation as the reason for the proposed response.

Time and northern concern explicitly set off the short-sign proposal in `62rncy`. Joshblake names both the time required for the full sign and neighbors’ concern before saying “I went ahead and created /r/SqSp.” This is the participant’s own explanation for the redirect and reduced design.

The Void attack supports a response-and-release relation within batch 01. `62w9rn` says the tendrils have reached the eastern edge and orders “Fight them back”; `62wza5` later says “We have won a great victory over the Void” and “can now refocus” on cleanup. Both endpoints state the link, and the measured interval is 2 hours 15 minutes.

Territorial loss explicitly sets off relocation in batch 02. The title and body of `631qt4` call moving the core and building a wall the “Only way to stay a spiral before being overrun.” The same post later drops the wall during the Void attack and focuses on the red spiral. This is a changing plan within one edited post, not proof of settled centralized control.

The “CURRENT VOID INVASION” explicitly sets off red-only patching in batch 03. `633i8f` says Discord High Spiral Command chose one color “to preserve” the spiral and orders “ALL SPIRALERS” to use red. The item supports an issued response; it cannot establish that all placers complied, because Discord activity and pixel histories are not present.

One participant supplies a causal interpretation of recovery in `dfrbsf4`: “the absence and desolation of the void” created the push to bring the spiral back. This is that participant’s explanation, not an independently established causal relation. Likewise, `636i4g` credits the Void with revealing weaknesses and strengthening alliances; the corpus supports that retrospective claim as speech, while the observed sequence alone would support only attack followed by rebuilding and negotiation.

The detailed conquest plan in `632a79` is followed 9 hours 16 minutes later by `6352nz`, which ends expansionism and prohibits retribution. Neither item says the later policy was caused by the earlier plan, so this is reported only as a sequence.

Ending placement explicitly sets off thanks in `63869x`: “now that Place has ended” introduces preservation and gratitude. Post-event deprivation also explicitly sets off successor building: `63akgc` opens “r/place-deprived?” and says people “have started another great spiral.” No causal relation in this account crosses a slice boundary; inherited sequences across 01|02 and 02|03 are described in Chronology only.

## Posts against comments

[batch 01] The posts say the room should seek peace, go behind finished art, and use a shorter sign; the comment layer says some people still want to cross Finland and that the sign change discarded an all-night build. The contrast is disagreement among speakers, not evidence that the community collectively changed its mind. The late counter code on `dfo4pt2` preserves the direct comment “I say we go over it,” while `dfo8m5d` answers a similar expansion proposal by refusing to “ruin others work.”

[batch 02] The posts say the “Era of Expansion is over” (`62ziyf`) and then announce a small, movable core (`631qt4`). Comments say the proposed core can be too small and argue for later growth; other comments defend it as “all we have left of our community” in `dfqqaec`. The layers agree on pressure and the importance of a core but differ over its size and whether defense permits renewed expansion.

[batch 02] Posts accuse neighboring flags of attack; comments and visiting posts repeatedly separate organized allies from unaffiliated placers. `630xuq` denies that r/Suomi is enlarging Finland, `630zbw` says the northern expansion is not Touhou’s doing, and `6310t8` says Brazil is trying to repair the situation. This supports a recurring attribution dispute, not a finding about who actually placed the pixels.

[batch 03] The post `634nw4` says “NO neighbours, no alliances” and “SPIRAL AWAY!” The direct comment `dfrbsch` proposes limits three minutes later, and the author’s reply `dfrcb2o` says “you’re right” before proposing background spiraling “without major infringements.” Here the comment layer directly reverses the poster’s stated position, but one person’s revision does not establish a room-wide change.

[batch 03] Post-event posts say the spiral survived and was preserved; comments largely add labor and memory rather than contradicting that claim. Their additions name patchers, Discord assistants, late helpers, and battle scars. Claims about decisions on Discord appear in both layers, but Discord deliberation itself appears in neither.

## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: SquareSpiral grew through recruitment and expansion, then repeatedly narrowed toward defending and rebuilding a bounded artwork through negotiated coexistence, while many placers and off-platform decisions remained outside centralized control.

Before searching, I specified the competing account this way: crises may instead have converted the room’s peace language into sustained conquest. If my reading were wrong, the corpus should contain post-crisis calls to “spiral away,” conquer, reject alliances, or overrun art that persist rather than being limited, answered, or withdrawn.

The strongest contrary item is `632a79`. It is not a stray aggressive word: it lays out “Phase two: Spiral and Destroy” and “Phase three: Spiral and Conquer,” including creating chaos and taking land. The search also returned the early “go over it” reply `dfo4pt2` and the post-crisis “NO MERCY” passage in `634nw4`. These findings change the account by making conquest a concrete, recurring alternative inside the room; “peace” cannot describe a uniform practice or settled consensus.

The alternative does not overturn the developmental reading. In the strongest later test, `dfrbsch` answers “NO MERCY” with a limit after three minutes, and `dfrcb2o` has the author accept that answer and propose expansion without major infringements. One hour 43 minutes after `634nw4`, `6352nz` announces a negotiated footprint, “no retributions,” and an end to expansionism; `63726p` later says “Don’t destroy art.” The main query returned eight items across the complete corpus, and a follow-up search for limits and reversals returned six; every returned item was read individually. Five late codes were appended at step 5 and added to the existing planning or artwork themes.

## Saturation

Thematic saturation was not reached before the final slice. Themes first founded in batch 01 were: recruiting and gathering Square Spiralers; building, expanding, and patching; setting tasks, roles, and boundaries; making the spiral sacred, funny, and worth remembering; making and defending the sign; asking neighbors for peace, borders, and help; deciding what art to go behind or over; rallying against attacks; moving coordination beyond the thread; and making appeals through surviving titles. Batch 02 first adds separating allies from randos and repairing blame, shrinking/relocating/rebuilding the core, and thanking or recounting spiralers’ work. Batch 03 first adds carrying the spiral on after Place. Later slices repeat and extend earlier practices, but the genuinely new post-event continuation practice means the full-window reading was necessary.

The record is whole at the thread-disposition level: all three coders read their assigned event threads in full, all 219 threads were disposed, and the forest gate passed. It is not whole as evidence of conduct. Discord receives recruitment, crisis command, and negotiation, but only what returns to Reddit is visible. Anonymous polls, private messages, linked images, deleted or removed bodies, and successor canvases also hide parts of action. The edited in-window post `63cz6v` contains later updates; only its general successor-server direction is treated as in-window conduct. The final-cleanup request and successor-canvas trajectories remain unresolved at the record’s edge. Because one coder read each arbitrary slice, there is no agreement measure, and no reader held the whole raw week; cross-slice synthesis is therefore weaker than a single-reader account.

After the counter-reading I reviewed the grouping again. No further regrouping was needed: the late conquest and reversal codes extended “setting tasks, roles, and boundaries together” and “deciding what art to go behind or go over.” The recorded regrouping had already separated immediate attack-rallying from changing and rebuilding the core, neighbor diplomacy from judgments about artwork, and direct pixel work from rule-setting.

## Other communities named

Participants treat Finland/r/Suomi, Brazil, Argentina, placehearts, Rainbow Rift, Touhou, Dwarf Fortress, bopp, Furry groups, and placeportalpattern as neighbors with whom borders, non-destruction, color changes, or mutual help can be negotiated. The Void/Black Hole is chiefly treated as an attack to resist, though later participants also credit it with enabling rebirth. Other named groups arrive as claimants to room inside the spiral, making the subreddit a place where outsiders seek “planning permission.” The pointer record preserves one naming anomaly: `63d4bk` says `/r/thefinalcleanup`, while the linked and discussed community is `/r/thefinalclean`.

Discord differs from a merely named neighbor: recruitment, command, and negotiation partly move there, while plans and reports return to Reddit. Steam, pxls.space, killtheidols.com, and Place 2 become ways to maintain contact or continue building after the canvas closes. Their internal activity is not visible and is not inferred from links alone.

## Absence

Deleted and removed bodies are concentrated in some conflictual openings, so initiating accusations can disappear while replies survive. The account uses surviving titles and replies but does not reconstruct missing claims. Image and link posts can show that an appeal or artifact was presented, not what an unseen image depicted. Machine messages from preview, mention-helper, phone-battery, and donger bots were treated as platform setting. The empty pre-week archive supports no baseline for ordinary community practice, and absence in these partial/off-platform records cannot establish that contrary conduct never occurred.
