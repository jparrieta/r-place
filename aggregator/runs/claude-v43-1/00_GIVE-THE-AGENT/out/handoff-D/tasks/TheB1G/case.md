# r/TheB1G — within-case account

## Chronology

The event first appears in this record at `d1 04-01 23:47`, when `62wfyx` asks, “Anyone want to help me put together a small B1G logo below them?” The opening episode runs from `62wfyx` to `dfpozpj`: members split pixels between school marks and the conference mark and call for “co-ordination and Non-Aggression Pacts.”

A building-and-fitting episode then runs from `62y6b6` to `dfrcrua`. Members establish a common design, choose the “1/I” first “so that people can organize easier” in `dfpy0dy`, repeatedly adjust borders and locations, and negotiate proposed space for Iowa, Indiana, Maryland, and Gonzaga. The practice is not simply drawing logos: `dfqwpuy` tells an Iowa proposal to be deleted because it would “cover up Argentina's stuff,” while `dfrcrua` says RURURU has “100+ people” maintaining its space and advises Iowa to look elsewhere.

An overlapping defense-and-alliance episode runs from `631rkn` to `dfriywc`. The initiating post `631rkn` frames a choice between fighting Venezuela and retaining the B1G colors. Replies turn that attack into arguments for conference cooperation: `dfqn15r` jokes that they could succeed “If we stop fighting each other for ten minutes,” and `dfqn8ex` proposes playing only defensively to do “the least to sabotage each other.” This cooperation remains disputed rather than settled. `635068` later says “Forget past agreements,” while `dfrgyrv` answers that Temple stayed within its boundaries and “we don't need to make enemies with them.” These are contrasting positions by different speakers, not evidence that the whole room changed its mind.

Automation becomes a distinct practice inside that defense. At `d2 04-03 02:05`, `dfr47uz` shares a bot running nonstop for Purdue, Illinois, and Minnesota and invites pull requests for more B1G logos. At `d2 04-03 03:24`, `dfr86x9` asks people to download and run the bot themselves and add their schools to its target. Indiana's repeated difficulty is partly explained by a participant in `6352t2`, who says that defended areas made it “pretty much impossible to gain a foothold” and recounts an eighth or ninth attempt. That is the participant's explanation; the record supports repeated failed attempts but does not independently establish that scripts caused every failure.

Near the event's end, calls for defense and missing-school space intensify from `636qrq` through `6380cb`. PSU asks the conference for help against Finland and the Chicago Bears in `636qrq`; `dfrtfxl` later thanks helpers after “we managed to make them leave.” Indiana continues sharing code and asking any team with free people for help in `6380cb`. At `d3 04-03 16:19`, `637t5m` asks “Discord?” and edits the post to say r/place has ended. The record shows the ask immediately before that announcement; it does not show that the event ending caused the Discord proposal or that an operating server followed.

After placement ends, a preservation dispute runs from `638d93` to `dfs3scg`. People make cleaned-up versions, request pixel corrections, and debate fidelity. `dfs3lam` refuses to insert Indiana because it would be “disingenuous and not true to our end result.” By contrast, `dfsfra8` says “Test is over, pencils down” and rejects any cleanup at all. Finally, at `d3 04-04 03:34`, `63brvv` announces newly carved B1G space and “all 14 teams this time”; `dft5gwh` clarifies that this is on `r/PlaceTwo`. That is a continuation on a clone, not additional placement on the finished 2017 canvas.

## Relations — what set off what

Several relations are supported by participants' own wording. In `dfr1o8t`, the speaker recalls that “we worked together to make the B1G, and to defend it from venezuela” and uses that history to say the schools “should come together as B1G bros.” This supports a participant's own explanation that earlier joint building and defense warranted later cooperation. It does not establish that every member accepted that inference.

The redesign instructions show a direct response across threads. `dfqyohz` names the move in `632sbr` and tells UMD to adjust its design because “the B1G logo is moving down by one square.” The later design adjustment is therefore explicitly tied to the earlier move, rather than inferred from chronological adjacency.

The cleaned-image practice carries its own stated cause. In `638enr`, the author says that “a few of us saw some vandalism in the closing hours,” then says, “I took a screengrab ... and decided to fix up our corner.” This is a participant-stated reason for that cleanup. The competing reply `dfsfra8` rejects the repair on the different principle that the final version should remain final.

Other developments are only sequences. `637t5m` asks about Discord and then announces that r/place ended; no passage says one caused the other. Likewise, the PlaceTwo attempt `63brvv` follows the cleanup disputes, but `dft5gwh` only identifies the new venue and does not say those disputes caused the move.

## Posts against comments

The post layer repeatedly proposes decisive action while comments narrow, contest, or redirect it. The post `635068` says “Forget past agreements” and claims the area as B1G territory; its comment `dfrgyrv` says Temple respected its boundaries and argues against making enemies. The post `6374gs` petitions to remove Notre Dame; `dfrwem5` opposes covering college logos, and `dfry2gt` asks the room to stop “picking fights.” These are visible disagreements, not a demonstrated collective reversal.

The same contrast appears around venue and memory. The post `633w9m` creates `r/placeTHEB1G` as a possible dedicated room; `dfr5avi` says the existing subreddit is quiet enough and “it'll be fine,” and `dfr5yco` concludes they can stay. The post `638enr` presents a repaired final image; `dfsfra8` says the final canvas admits no cleanup. In each case the post opens a practice and the comments negotiate its limits.

Some claims live mainly in comments. Detailed bot installation and requests to add schools appear in `dfr47uz`, `dfrepm4`, and `dfr86x9`, while link-post titles such as `6340fe` chiefly issue preservation calls. Conversely, the repeated missing-school proposals are often launched as posts (`632ud7`, `63359y`, `6352t2`, `6380cb`) and then constrained by comments about occupied space and existing agreements.

## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: members made a conference project by fitting school marks together, repeatedly turning rivalry and attacks into shared defense, then disputing how faithfully to preserve the finished result.

The competing account recorded before search was that conference unity never displaced rivalry and coordination was only temporary cover for taking space. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain explicit calls to break agreements, remove neighboring college marks, or resume attacks despite peace claims.

I searched the complete record for the case's own phrases around forgetting agreements, getting rid of others, covering work, attacking one another, peace, and picking fights. The search returned nine items, all of which were opened through the reading tool. The strongest contrary item is `635068`: “Forget past agreements, this is the B1G corner and our territory.” `6374gs` likewise petitions to “rid” the corner of Notre Dame, and `dfqvzls` says of an unclear target, “They are weak. Let's get rid of them”; because its parent is deleted, the target cannot be recovered.

The contrary reading changes the claim: shared defense was contested and conditional, not settled conference unity. It does not erase the cooperative practice. `633kod` explicitly says “don't attack each other,” `dfqwpuy` protects Argentina's existing work, `dfrwmic` calls covering a college logo “a dick move,” and the newly coded search item `dfry2gt` rejects “picking fights.” The record therefore supports coexisting practices and positions, not a community-wide conversion from rivalry to peace.

## Saturation

Evidence coverage is strong for the bounded Reddit record: all 32 threads and all 373 in-window items were read, and all 31 event-related threads have live passage codes. It is not complete for conduct that moved elsewhere. The record points to school subreddits, a UIUC megathread, a possible Discord, and PlaceTwo; it shows what returned here but not the decisions made inside those venues. Thirty bodies are deleted or removed, and three context items fall after the window, so absence cannot establish that no other coordination occurred.

Practice saturation is uneven across time. The early and middle record repeatedly returns to fitting logos, negotiating borders, stopping intraconference attacks, and defending shared work. Later threads add genuinely new practices—cleaning and authenticating the final image, rejecting cleanup, and restarting on PlaceTwo—rather than only repeating settled themes. The last addition therefore extends the case from live-canvas coordination into how participants preserve and continue it.
