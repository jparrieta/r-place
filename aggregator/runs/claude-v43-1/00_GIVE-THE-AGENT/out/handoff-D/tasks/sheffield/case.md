## Posts against comments

The posts and comments tell complementary rather than contradictory stories. The post layer names a project and an outcome: `632zky` says “Putting Sheffield on r/place.. 5 minutes at a time!” and `6386r1` says “Good job everyone who helped out!” The comment layer supplies nearly all of the work between those endpoints: night watching (`dfr79r9`), pixel priorities (`dfriof3`), colour polling (`dfroy1f`), the Apollo objection (`dfrqtvv`) and the one-pixel compromise (`dfrrw6p`, `dfrtgha`, `dfrudgx`).

## Counter-reading

My reading is that Sheffield participants used one long thread to keep and redesign a city mark, negotiated a one-pixel compromise with Ace Attorney, and then celebrated that the mark survived.

Before searching, the competing account was that the room was chiefly trying to take neighbours' space in a canvas “battle,” rather than collaboratively maintaining and redesigning its own mark. If my reading were wrong, I expected repeated calls to attack or take over neighbouring work, refusals of the Apollo compromise, or celebration of destroying it.

The strongest contrary item is `dfrqtvv`. An Ace Attorney participant says “you're taking over poor Apollo” and asks Sheffield to stop removing his sleeve. This changes the account by making clear that the blue-border redesign did encroach on neighbouring art and was experienced as a takeover. It does not overturn the broader reading: `dfrrw6p` responds with a one-pixel solution, `dfrtgha` recruits help for it, and `dfrudgx` confirms and thanks the move.

Across the complete supplied record, a search for `attack|take over|destroy|scrub|battle` returned `dfriof3` (“scrub us out”) and `dft0gj2` (“battle”), both already coded and both describing Sheffield as defending or remembering. A search for `apollo|sleeve|move the rose|respect|agreed` returned `dfrqtvv`, `dfrrw6p` and `dfrtgha`, the objection and compromise sequence already coded. A final search for refusal or destruction terms (`refus|won't|will not|destroy`) returned no items. That null result is scoped only to these terms in this 97-hour supplied record; it cannot show that no hostile placement happened, especially because pixel actions themselves and several deleted comments are not recoverable.
