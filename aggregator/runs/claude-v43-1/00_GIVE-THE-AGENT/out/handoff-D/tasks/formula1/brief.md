## Evidence base

The reader served 37 of 1,597 threads and 447 of 48,609 items. All 447 served items were complete. Bodies from all seven active days were touched, and there were no empty days. The title index was sampled across the whole week and in day slices, rather than exhausted. I deliberately left 1,560 threads and 48,162 items unopened. No opened item remained partial. These limits make this a broad sample of a very large room, with extra depth around the Australian Grand Prix weekend, policy disputes, technical discussion, memes, and late-week moderation. (state)

## Data problems

This is a small body sample from a large archive: 447 complete items across 37 of 1,597 threads. Most bodies and most of the title index remained unopened, so claims of absence or uniqueness are bounded to the opened sample. (state)

The archive records 2,659 deleted or removed items and 965 unresolved parents among 47,175 recorded parent pointers. Those gaps limit reconstruction of some exchanges, and I did not infer missing content or direct conversation where a parent was absent. (header)

All seven active days received body coverage, and all served items were complete. (state)
