## Evidence base

The corpus contains 21,727 items in 1,068 threads. I was served 63 threads and saw 450 unique items, all 450 complete. All seven calendar days contained items and all seven were touched; there were no empty days. Most thread bodies were deliberately left unopened, and the title index was sampled rather than exhausted. No served item remained partial. (state)
