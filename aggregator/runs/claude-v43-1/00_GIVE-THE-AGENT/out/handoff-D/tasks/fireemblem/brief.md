## Evidence base

The corpus contains 14,762 items in 480 threads. I was served 412 complete items from 47 threads, with no partly served item left open. All seven active days were touched, and there were no empty days. Most material was deliberately left unopened. Titles were sampled across the index; selected discussion, serial, playthrough, tournament, and moderation threads were read completely. The long general-question thread was read only at its first body page. No shell response was truncated. (state)

## Vernacular

“EPFE” is explicitly defined as “Everyone plays Fire Emblem.” Its post describes a shared place for playthrough updates and general discussion.

> EPFE (That's Everyone plays Fire Emblem, abbreviated...acronymed?). (`61bzwa`)

> Basically this is a place for people to post their playthroughs of FE so we don't clog up the Front page with them, as well as a place to discuss FE stuff. (`61bzwa`)

“PMU” names a playthrough format in which commenters supply characters and classes. The opened material shows how it works but does not spell out the initials.

> This is going to be a blind playthrough of Revelations, and I want you guys to PMU! (`61bkhx`)

> You may pick a single character and their class (including either an A+ pairing or an S Pairing as a means to acquire said class). (`61bkhx`)

“THE LIST” is EPFE's roster of ongoing playthroughs. Joining it requires posting progress in text, images, or video.

> Also, just ask if you want to be on **THE LIST**, just please post some sort of progress on it, whether text, image or video. (`62hyi1`)
