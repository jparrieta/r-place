# r/AprilKnights — within-case account

## Chronology

The room first named the live event at d0 03-31 17:14. A moderator described a place “where you can post a square to draw a picture” and immediately said “Join us and the discussion on our discord” (`62mjnb`). Fourteen minutes later, the first strategy post named “Spread the Red” and was edited to say “We are moving to the Red Corner to assist them against the Blue Corner” (`62mn20`). This opening episode runs from `62mjnb` to `dfo24gz`: it combined learning what the event was, calling affiliated subs, testing a first square, then explicitly leaving the Netherlands flag alone because “We have moved on to assisting the Red Corner” (`dfo24gz`).

From d0 03-31 18:33 through d1 04-01 18:15, the room recruited people and tried to organise colour work across a multireddit and Discord. “Calling All Knights” asked listeners to assemble and perhaps coordinate a drawing (`62n322`); an invite then expired (`dfo4ily`), while a multireddit link was repaired until a reply confirmed “There we go, that works” (`dfo0gf8`). The colour campaign was not one settled line. One post demanded a temporary anti-Blue truce (`62ore4`), Purple promised not to cover anything but Blue (`dfo8v6d`), and three successive Peace Treaty posts asked people to follow rules (`62qiyr`, `62qj2b`, `62qj6d`). The episode ends with a moderator asking for tiles for a subreddit box “for posterity,” while otherwise prioritising defense and red (`62tr8r`).

From d1 04-01 18:56 through d2 04-03 11:01, the event recruited the Knights’ older forms while their concrete project narrowed around the Button and sign. The oath thread invited old members to renew and new warriors to join (`62uoqz`); one Purple participant asked to help in future events (`dfsc31o`) and was welcomed because the first grandmaster “was a purple” (`dfsdu9l`). The United Red Front invitation proposed defeating Blue, Green and Purple (`62v1xf`), but a moderator answered that the Knights already had cross-colour allies (`dfpei02`) and that their mission was to “maintain peace for all colours” (`dfpgvz6`). Meanwhile the room began “drawing 2015's TheButton” (`62vl41`), later made building it under the sign the new strategy (`62xgzx`), lost the sign and promised to rebuild (`dfqle8d`, `dfqlf75`), and shifted to defending the Button (`dfqei74`). The later peace with Sweddit moved the sign under the Button (`631tkr`); a subsequent update says Jerma moved, Overwatch was beaten, and “Now we're building our letters” (`dfr66gn`).

From d2 04-03 07:04 through d3 04-04 03:38, participants saved, limited and then interpreted the result. A screenshot presented the logo and Button “in mint condition” (`6357d5`). A proposed extra project was rejected because maintaining the Button already exceeded their numbers (`dfrj8e0`), and its proposer conceded, “You're probably right” (`dfrjag9`). After placement ended, a post at d3 04-03 17:41 said both works remained and “We did it!” (`638coh`); the later retrospective thanked allies and recounted “72 hours” of pixel work (`639m5h`). The room then moved directly into candidacies and voter registration because “the April Fools Event [was] over” (`639ymb`, `63a4o3`), redefined elections so they would not interrupt the next event (`63a4qy`), explained that the Knights return each April to push the admins’ experiments (`dfsz4ht`), and finally opened a new subreddit for fixing the finished canvas (`63bsil`).

## Relations — what set off what

The first square becoming a Netherlands flag elicited “WE CAN'T LET THAT HAPPEN” (`dfnwu4y`), but a later turn by the same moderator explicitly says the group had moved to the Red Corner and therefore “The flag will remain as it is” (`dfo24gz`). This supports a participant-stated redirection, not a claim that the flag itself caused the wider Red Corner strategy.

The lost sign produced a direct rebuilding response: “it's completely gone” (`dfqle8d`) is answered 33 seconds later with “we've got plans to rebuild” (`dfqlf75`). The reply text supplies the link; the reply position alone does not.

The World of Tanks request to avoid overlap (`62wu81`) was followed 6.4 hours later by a moderator asking, “Did we nuke your art and not respond?” and offering help (`dfpzk7i`). This is a supported response because the later passage names the possible harm. It does not establish who removed the tank.

The move into elections is participant-explained rather than inferred from timing: both election posts say, “With the April Fools Event over it is time to begin the election progress” (`639ymb`, `63a4o3`). The policy post further explains that future elections will occur after the event so there is no “confusion in the middle of the event when time is of essence” (`63a4qy`).

Several other developments are only sequences. The temporary anti-Blue truce (`62ore4`) precedes the Purple alliance request (`62q85f`), and the defense-against-Sweden post (`630d2p`) precedes the Sweddit peace announcement (`631tkr`), but no cited passage says the earlier post caused the later one. They are contrasting positions and subsequent settlements, not evidence that the whole community changed its mind.

## Posts against comments

The opening strategy post says “make everything red” (`62mn20`), while its comments ask for affiliated help (`dfntbp9`) and ultimately leave a Netherlands flag intact (`dfo24gz`). The post layer supplies a clear slogan; the comment layer shows selective redirection rather than universal application.

The United Red Front post calls for defeating Blue and Green and purging Purple (`62v1xf`). The comment layer answers with existing Purple and Green ties (`dfpei02`), a peace-for-all-colours mission (`dfpgvz6`), and a Red Corner representative who is “not currently in favor” of directly attacking Blue (`dfph1ru`). This is a genuine contrast between speakers, not proof of collective conversion.

“Mission Accomplished” appears in a post (`62w9yc`), while later comments say the sign was “completely gone” and then promise rebuilding (`dfqle8d`, `dfqlf75`). The post records one accomplished moment; the comments record subsequent loss and renewed work.

The Sweddit peace announcement says a coordinated sign location is settled (`631tkr`), but its comments expose a second overlap with Jerma, a warning about Overwatch bots (`dfqo1gz`), and a later claim that Jerma moved (`dfr66gn`). The post layer announces agreements; the comments show the continuing work needed to enact them.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that the Knights used r/place to rebuild the Button and their sign, renew the order, and negotiate enough room and allies to keep those works alive; Spread the Red opened the week but did not remain its only rule.

If that reading were wrong, the corpus would instead contain repeated participant calls to destroy non-Blue work, reject compromise, and keep red expansion ahead of the Button, sign and allied obligations.

The strongest contrary item is the United Red Front opening post, which proposes defeating Blue and Green, purging Purple, and making red “rein supreme” (`62v1xf`). A search scoped to *destroy*, *purge*, *attack*, *fight*, *blue*, *spread* and *expand* returned 32 items. Read-back confirmed a direct “REDGUARD MUST DESTROY BLUE BLOB!” (`dfnttvq`) and a later hope to “defeat the other enemies … and expand outwards” (`dfpgygm`). These findings require the account to retain a real expansionist and destructive strand.

They do not overturn the reading. The same United Red Front speaker reports sending non-aggression pacts to Green and Purple before asking for reconsideration (`dfpgygm`), while the Red Corner representative rejects a direct Blue attack (`dfph1ru`) and the Knights’ moderator names existing Purple and Green ties (`dfpei02`). The evidence supports simultaneous, contested positions; it does not support a causal claim that the room collectively abandoned one for another.

## Saturation

The record is structurally whole for the supplied window: every thread was read in full, parent pointers were present, and the forest exposed uncoded stretches rather than hiding them. Nineteen deleted or removed bodies limit the precise content of several posts, especially the repeated treaty and Sweden-defense submissions; surviving titles and replies support only the actions stated here, not reconstruction of missing arguments.

The practice set did not settle early. Later material added renewing the order, moving immediately into elections, explaining annual purpose, and opening a post-event repair subreddit (`62uoqz`, `639ymb`, `63blhy`, `63bsil`). Discord clearly carried planning: Reddit receives invitations, results and references to decisions, but not the discussions themselves (`dfp7mpy`, `dfqlnb4`, `dfsj83n`). Saturation is therefore strong for visible Reddit practices and limited for how off-platform choices were made.

## Selection

I read `openings.md` before triage, as required, to locate the room's week without treating it as evidence. I then read every corpus thread in order and in full. Forty-two threads were retained because they concerned the live event, its immediate aftermath, or an established community practice visibly recruited by it. The pre-window “Current Info” thread `62ishp` was excluded because its only in-window turn, `dfnm4pd`, was the bare response “Oh” to pre-window clue checking and did not show event conduct inside the window.

## Other communities named

The room treats other communities as possible labour, neighbours and constraints rather than as a single enemy bloc. Red Corner receives early help (`62mn20`); Purple offers and receives cooperation (`dfo8v6d`, `dfpei02`); World of Tanks negotiates an overlap (`62wu81`); Parahumans asks about Galaga (`62zul0`); Jerma is asked to redirect (`dfqnqs8`); and Sweden moves from attacker to credited ally (`630d2p`, `631tkr`, `639m5h`). Discord is the main off-platform venue named for coordination, but the record shows only what participants bring back.

## Absence

Deleted and removed bodies are concentrated in otherwise legible exchanges. The treaty survives through three titles (`62qiyr`, `62qj2b`, `62qj6d`), while the removed anti-Blue-plan body leaves only its title and the reply about farming carrots (`62r9wm`, `dfor9rz`). The Sweden-defense post body is removed, but its surviving replies show a lost sign, a move to Button defense, an apology and acceptance (`dfqei74`, `dfqemvz`, `dfqeoxp`). These traces support limited descriptions of what remained visible; they do not support reconstructing the missing proposals.
