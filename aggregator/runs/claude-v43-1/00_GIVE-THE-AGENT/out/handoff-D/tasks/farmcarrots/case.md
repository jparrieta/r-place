## Chronology

The supplied record does not show this room at the instant the canvas opened. Its first episode begins at H+11.68, d0 04-01 03:40, when `62qd9u` supplies “The Base Carrot Image.” Five minutes later the room explains its own creation: carrots had “no place to coordinate,” so “This sub was made so users can talk to others and discuss ideas and where to grow carrots” (`62qdtx`). The founding practice is therefore not just admiring carrots. People share a base, ask about shading and colours, propose a sign and large builds, call themselves “humble farmers,” and begin negotiating with approaching work. The episode from `62qd9u` through `dfp9y9a` ends with a rainbow visitor returning a revised route and hoping to make rainbow carrots.

The second episode, from `dfp9y9a` through `dfqwag4`, combines making carrots with managing crowded neighbors. Participants offer templates, recruit pixels, announce national and hybrid carrots, negotiate protection for Boo, Sir Graham, Tampa Bay, Cave Story and the S, and move some plotting to Discord. They do not share one settled stance: some call for taking neighboring work, while others apologize, refuse attacks, or seek reciprocal help. The farm sign becomes a sustained object of work and argument. A post first says vandals made it read “Fart Carrots” and that this “has to be changed” (`62vecp`); later, a different post announces an M-to-T truce in exchange for a sticky link, maintenance, and border building (`632e47`). This is a documented negotiated development, not evidence that every speaker changed position: a comment still objects to the joke, while another accepts it because “it takes too much manpower to keep fighting on the sign” (`dfqtnu7`).

The third episode crosses the slice boundary [crosses 01|02], from `632zv7` through `637kui`. It opens at H+55.26, d2 04-02 23:15, with “We’re under attack” (`632zv7`). Posts and comments redirect defense among the Carrot Parrot, a green line, remaining carrots and proposed replacement sites; neighbors continue asking for land and truces. At d2 04-03 04:58, one participant already doubts the farm can return but says it had been “great fun planting carrots together all over the field of blue” (`634pv3`). Later efforts defend the German carrot and start a replacement carrot. `637kui` then considers payback and a placing script, while its edit also says the script might “protect[] the sole carrot left.” This stretch is assembled from the batch-02 account and forest rather than a single lead reading of the full slice.

Placement ends at 2017-04-03 17:00 UTC. The fourth episode therefore begins after closure, with “A carrot survived!!!” at d3 04-03 17:18 (`6386vb`), and runs through `63bu0a` at d3 04-04 03:47. People memorialize the original farm, thank farmers, preserve screenshots, turn a taunt into a celebrated reply thread, and ask whether the practice can continue. At d3 04-03 22:45, `63a98g` says, “Join us in farming carrots on PlaceTwo!” The final item shows “Our beloved crop after growing an impressive ~34 and a half carrots!!” (`63bu0a`). This is an observed sequence from mourning and survival talk into invitations elsewhere; the record does not establish that mourning caused the migration or that the migration endured.

## Relations — what set off what

The clearest supported causal relation is the room’s creation. The same speaker says the carrots had “no place to coordinate” and immediately explains, “This sub was made so users can talk to others and discuss ideas and where to grow carrots” (`62qdtx`). That is the participant’s own explanation, not an inference from chronology.

An approaching rainbow directly sets off route negotiation. The opening asks, “What would you all prefer?” (`62uqwa`); 3.5 minutes after a farmer’s reply, the visitor answers with a “New plan” that bounces around Starry Night and the German flag before heading toward a few carrots (`dfp9y9a`). The reply relation and the words together support the link.

The loss of a carrot to the Tampa logo directly sets off a request for reciprocal labor: “Since you harvested one of our carrots,” the reply asks the logo builders to help the farm when finished (`dfplhhr`). This supports the request’s stated reason. It does not show whether Tampa builders later supplied that labor.

The Blue alliance is given as a reason for a background decision. A participant reports, “We discussed it over in our discord, and we agreed to keep the blue. The alliance is important to us” (`dfqtjid`). This establishes the participant’s explanation for the decision and that deliberation occurred off Reddit; it cannot establish how that Discord discussion reached agreement.

The M/T fight directly sets off the announced sign compromise. `632e47` says, “After battling for the past day with /r/fartcarrots, we came to a truce,” and states the exchange of the T for a sticky link, maintenance, and border work. The follow-up explanation says fighting the sign used “too much manpower” that could go to the border (`dfqtnu7`). The corpus supports why these speakers accepted the bargain. It does not show that the bargain caused lasting compliance; the next slice opens amid wider defense, and the Bronco truce and later defense follow across the boundary without a passage proving that the sign deal caused them [crosses 01|02].

After closure, the PlaceTwo invitation follows survival, mourning and thanks. The sequence is visible between `6386vb` and `63a98g`, but no passage says the memorial talk caused that move. It is therefore a sequence, not a causal relation.

## Posts against comments

The layers do not tell one uniform story. Early posts set a public non-destruction line: the founding post says not to destroy collaborative work (`62qdtx`), and the moderator post calls the group “humble farmers” who will not intentionally attack (`62qyzm`). In batch 01, a comment nevertheless says “Attack the S!” (`dfpjne2`). Other comments actively enforce the opposite position, saying simple farmers should stay out of attacking and harming others (`dfplb9l`). This is a descriptive contrast among speakers, not evidence that the whole community first endorsed and then rejected attack.

The sign argument develops in posts as well as comments. The post layer moves from calling “Fart Carrots” unacceptable (`62vecp`) to announcing the negotiated T (`632e47`). Comments show both dissent and the practical explanation about manpower (`dfqtnu7`). In batch 02, the post layer itself contains the sharpest exception to the non-attack line: `633tqr` says farmers “have to attack the intruders” and names four targets, framing attack as defense of the farm. Thus “wholesome” is a recurrent rule and self-description, not a complete description of conduct.

Operational revision lives especially in comments. The rainbow opening asks for a preference (`62uqwa`), while the revised path appears in the reply `dfp9y9a`; the Discord background decision appears in `dfqtjid`; and the platypus exchange closes with “We do not attack:) thank you for moving!” (`dfrtuez`). Posts more often announce projects, threats and agreements. Comments show route changes, practical reasons, apologies and negotiated closes. Batch 01 supplies most of these detailed exchanges; batch 02 supplies the post-event defense, loss and continuation layer.

## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: r/farmcarrots turned spontaneous carrots into a room for humble-farmer building, coexistence and defense, while pressure exposed real arguments about attack and authority and closure redirected remembrance and farming toward new canvases.

Before searching, the plausible competing account was that humble and wholesome language mainly covered a room whose sustained practice was deliberate attack and territorial destruction. If my reading were wrong, the corpus should contain repeated, accepted plans naming targets and coordinating destruction or revenge, rather than isolated attack calls alongside refusals, apologies, truces and defensive redirection.

I searched the case’s own vocabulary—attack, destroy, take over, payback, revenge, raid, enemy and conquer—and opened all 42 returned items with the prescribed item reader. The strongest contrary item is `637kui`: it asks whether anyone has “interest in payback” and considers adapting the opposing placing script. That changes the account by making retaliation, including possible automation, a real practice rather than mere overheated talk. The same edited item later narrows the script idea toward protecting “the sole carrot left,” so it does not establish sustained offensive coordination. `633tqr` is the strongest direct attack instruction, saying farmers “have to attack the intruders” and naming targets.

The contrary account does not displace the main reading. The search also returns the founding ban on destroying collaborative work (`62qdtx`), an apology that attributes conversion to “renegade farmers” afraid of expansion (`dfpojje`), a refusal to attack while offering spare tiles (`dfplb14`), and the negotiated “thank you for moving” close (`dfrtuez`). Many other hits describe the farm or neighbors as being attacked, not farmers organizing attacks; one returned item is bot-generated link text and is setting rather than conduct. Four uncoded passages surfaced by the search were appended as late codes `c0191`, `c0192`, `c0193` and `c0194`. The resulting account keeps the non-attack identity as a contested practice, not a fact about every participant.

## Saturation

The thematic pattern is mostly settled in batch 01 and extended in batch 02. The themes first founded in batch 01 are: sharing carrot pictures and designs; planning and making carrots together; calling themselves humble farmers; defending and restoring threatened carrots; negotiating room with neighbors; moving farm decisions to Discord; turning things into carrots and carrot jokes; fighting over what the farm sign should say; asking the room to choose; choosing backgrounds and alliances to protect carrots; arguing over whether farmers should attack; celebrating and thanking carrot work; and mourning and remembering the farm. Batch 02 repeats most of those practices under greater loss and defense pressure. Two practices first appear in batch 02 because placement has ended: carrying carrot farming onto new canvases, and answering being wrecked with carrot jokes. Later evidence therefore adds a post-event phase rather than merely repeating the live-canvas account.

The record is a complete disposition of the supplied census—168 threads and 682 in-window items were read in full by the two fleet coders—but not a complete view of conduct. Removed bodies hide the terms of several neighbor requests and threats. Discord is directly linked in `62x4d3`, and Reddit receives a reported decision in `dfqtjid`, but the deliberation, participants and alternatives there remain unseen. PlaceTwo invitations are visible, but activity after this window is not. One coder read each slice, so there is no agreement measure, and no reader held the whole week. Cross-slice relations are consequently weaker: the defense continuing from `632xux` to `632zv7` is carried from separate slice accounts and the forest, not from a single reader’s full-thread memory.

## Selection

The gate chose branch B because the manifest held 682 in-window items, above the threshold of 500 (673 items and 72,417 characters by people). I read the empty briefing and the chronological openings before the gate. The fleet then read two whole-thread slices in corpus order: batch 01 covered 128 threads and batch 02 covered 40. Every thread was retained as coded because this subreddit was created around the carrot farm and every opening concerned the canvas, conduct around it, post-event remembrance, or continuation of that practice.

Before consolidation I did not read raw threads or search. I reviewed each slice’s appended timeline and lexicon facts at collection and struck none: the timeline additions were item-linked edit reports, and the lexicon additions were unglossed terms with first-use ids. After the final slice, I consolidated the pool, built the forest, and passed the fleet audit before grouping codes in slice order. Raw items were opened only after step 4 for the counter-reading and checkable quotations. The grouping was reviewed again against the forest and counter-reading; no code was moved. The instrument’s grouping records state the review, and late codes were added to the already-open neighbor-negotiation and attack-argument themes.

## Other communities named

Other communities appear in different practices, not as one alliance network. The Rainbow Road visitor asks the farm to choose a route and returns with a revised plan (`62uqwa`, `dfp9y9a`). The Blue Corner is invoked as protection and as the reason to keep a blue background (`dfqtjid`). Tampa Bay, Cave Story, Mordhau, SSBM, the Denver Broncos, a platypus builder and others negotiate space, restraint or reciprocal labor; `dfplhhr` explicitly asks Tampa builders to repay a harvested carrot with farm help. Placehearts supplies a gift and later an offer of land. AvoArmy asks fellow farmers for pixels. `r/fartcarrots` differs by moving from sign antagonist to an announced conditional maintenance partner (`632e47`), though the record does not establish that the bargain held.

After closure, PlaceTwo and another Place-like subreddit are destinations for continued carrot farming rather than evidence about the finished canvas. `63a98g` invites the farmers to PlaceTwo, and `63bchs` promises protection there. These are visible continuation attempts; their durability lies outside the window.
