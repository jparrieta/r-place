## Chronology

The room enters the record already treating the canvas as work that must continue while people leave: the episode from `62ucxe` to `62zhgr` opens with “kann nur noch mobil pixeln” and closes with a proposal to “Mit Belgien fusionieren.” In between, the slice account shows people directing defence, handing work to a Nachtwache, moving coordination to Discord, and negotiating the flag’s borders. This opening stretch comes from batch 01, whose raw threads I did not read as lead.

From `62zhw8` to `6322uj`, participants turn the flag into a sequence of projects and relationships. The opening asks whether there is still interest in a “Bundesadler”; later work includes the Belgian sausage, skyline and repairs, and the closing post proposes a heart with Spain because the two are now neighbours. The comments also expose the maintenance underneath the designs: eagle pixels “werden leider sofort immer wieder gelöscht” in `dfq6cen`. This episode crosses batches 01 and 02 and is reconstructed from their slice accounts, pooled codes and the forest.

From `6323bv` to `637unu`, making motifs and helping allies runs alongside arguments over expansion, targets and authority. The episode begins with a proposal to place BTS above the flag and ends with “Unsere belgischen Freunde stehen unter Beschuss, lasst uns helfen!” Participants build and revise a dove, accommodate a trophy by moving a dog, debate the 7:1 motif, and dispute whether Discord decisions bind Reddit participants. Expansion was not merely an analyst’s category: one comment campaigns “EXPANSION! SAGT ‘JA’ ZUR PLACE-HERRSCHAFT!” in `dfri7yf`, while another answers the language of Lebensraum by urging an “artistische highroad” in `dfrnzjh`.

The closing episode, from `637w4g` to `63f530`, begins with an explicit objection that 7:1 “ist jetzt aber nur schlechter Stil” and moves through the canvas ending, thanks, preservation, successor canvases and retrospective study. `63854i` says “Place ist offiziell vorbei!” and recalls peace treaties and cross-national work; `63dm71` then asks participants to reflect on Discord, Reddit, bots, specialisation, cooperation and conflict. The last item returns an outsider’s appraisal: `63f530` calls the German-French EU flag “one of the most beautiful things that happened.” This final stretch comes from batches 03 through 06 and their slice records.

## Relations — what set off what

Participants themselves explain why timed coordination mattered: `dfq24p1` says work must be coordinated so that “schnell ein erkennbares Ziel entsteht.” That statement supports a relation between announcing a shared target and concentrating pixel work; it is stronger than the mere fact that project posts precede completion comments.

The Belgian border develops as an explicitly connected episode. `dfq5s7b` calls for contacting the Belgians and finding “eine gemeinsame Lösung” rather than fighting. A revised plan then returns from Discord and is edited to “WE DID IT” in `630ldd`; `630r6g` directs people to execute the Belgium sausage merge and says “Belgien hilft!”, and `dfqhth1` reports “Die Wurst ist fertig.” The later turns name the same negotiated project, so this is supported as coordination becoming joint work, not inferred from adjacency. ## Posts against comments

In batch 01, posts often present designs as available collective projects, while comments reveal that builders cannot assume agreement or persistence. The Bundesadler post asks for interest; the comment layer reports its eye and beak being immediately deleted in `dfq6cen`. The layers therefore differ between proposing a visible object and describing the repair struggle required to keep it.

In batches 01–03, posts announce border projects and urgent instructions, while comments contest how those decisions were reached. The post layer directs the Belgian merge in `630r6g`; the comment layer distinguishes Belgium as agreed while calling Spanish and Italian painting unagreed in `dfq8aub`. The same contrast sharpens around Discord in batch 03: `dfrknyk` says the arrangements were made there, but `dfrl9sj` answers that a Reddit action “sollte daher auf reddit koordiniert werden” and warns of fragmentation.

In batch 03, the post layer offers the 7:1 judgment that there is no majority in `637w4g`; its comments do not simply reverse that position. `dfryaij` agrees with the criticism but raises the practical problem that overpainting alone will not work. This is a difference between normative opposition and implementation, not evidence that the community changed its mind.

In batches 04–06, posts preserve, restart and study the event, while comments retain more personal evaluations of what the work meant. The farewell post `63854i` describes collective treaties and cooperation; its comment `dfs11aj` credits what was accomplished to “Organisation und Effektivität.” The latter is a participant’s explanation, not an independently demonstrated causal effect.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/placeDE repeatedly turned proposals into coordinated builds through cleaning, project sequencing and neighbour deals, while disputes over territory and Discord authority remained visible.

If that reading were wrong, the corpus would instead contain sustained approval of expansion as the priority, acceptance of Discord or scripts as unchallenged authority, and repeated statements that announced projects never became coordinated work.

I searched the full searchable record for the room’s expansion vocabulary, Discord/Reddit decision language, failed or uncoordinated work, and explicit completion reports, and read the returned items rather than treating hit counts as findings. The strongest contrary item is `dfrwhgz`: it says Discord decisions made while people are at work prevent too many from speaking and concludes, “Ich werde mich sicherlich an diesen Sachen nicht beteiligen.” Expansion advocacy is also direct in `dfri7yf` (“PLACE-HERRSCHAFT”) and `dfq7nyd` (“Wir brauchen Lebensraum”). `dfqmapa` supplies the strongest pro-authority version, calling official Discord decisions democratic and linking a vote. These items make expansion and venue-based exclusion consequential strands rather than marginal noise.

The alternative does not displace the whole reading. `630ldd` reports a revised negotiated plan and “WE DID IT”; `dfqhgrb` says “We did it!”, `dfrsg4n` says a shared method worked, and `dfs11aj` names organisation and effectiveness when reflecting on the finished event. The search also found genuine failure—`dfrnfye` calls Italian-flag work “noch etwas unkoordiniert”—so completion was uneven. The surviving account is therefore narrower: coordinated projects were repeatedly achieved, but authority was contested, participation was uneven, and some people withdrew from work decided in venues they could not access.

## Saturation

Substantive saturation was late rather than early. The first slice founded practices around sequencing projects, moving decisions between venues, cleaning and night watch, neighbour deals, territory, motif building, technical work, celebration and preservation. Batch 02 added distinct practices of policing what belonged on the flag and choosing whether to attack or overwrite opponents. Batch 05 first added memorial-making, and batch 06 first added restarting and studying place after it ended. Later slices therefore did not merely repeat settled themes; the closing record still introduced new practice. Step 5 added contrary codes within existing themes rather than requiring a new theme.

Evidentiary saturation is stronger for this Reddit window than for the community’s full activity. Every disposed thread in the supplied window was read and coded by the fleet, but no single reader held the whole week. Discord conversations, strawpolls, shared documents, private messages, bot administration and image contents are only visible where participants bring them back into Reddit. Deleted or removed bodies cannot be reconstructed, and successor-canvas activity continues beyond this window. The account is therefore saturated for recurring practices in the supplied Reddit record, not for all coordination around the canvas.

## Selection

The recorded gate selected branch B. I read the briefing and all openings before the gate, using the openings as a map rather than evidence. Six coders then read whole-thread slices in corpus order; all dispositions were coded and none was excluded as out of scope. I did not read raw threads while the fleet operated. After consolidation I read the slice cases and pooled codes in slice order, then the forest, and only after themes were complete used the permitted counter-reading search and item reads.

This design has three limits. No reader held the whole week, there is no agreement measure because one coder read each slice, and slice boundaries are arbitrary with respect to exchanges. The Belgian negotiation and build is the principal relation carried across batches 01–03, supported by turns naming Belgium, negotiation, the returned plan and its completion. The account also carries the shift from final-building disputes in batch 03 to closure and retrospection in later batches, but treats that as chronology rather than community-wide change.

## Other communities named

Belgium is most often treated as a neighbour with whom conflict should become negotiated shared work: `dfq5s7b` asks for “eine gemeinsame Lösung,” and `630r6g` says “Belgien hilft!” France appears as a model for a peace process in `dfq5s7b` and later as the partner in the EU flag praised by `63f530`. Spain and Italy are both neighbours and contested painters: `dfq8aub` contrasts agreed Belgian action with Spanish invasion and Italian overpainting, while `dfrnfye` asks for help with uncoordinated Italian-flag work. `dfrknyk` says an arrangement with `r/farmcarrots` was made on that community’s Discord. BTS appears in the post `6323bv` as a proposed German connection, showing how cultural affiliation could be offered as a claim on flag space even when it did not become a central shared practice.

## Absence

Removed and deleted material remains absent; I did not reconstruct bodies from replies, links or later descriptions. Surviving comments sometimes show the shape of what cannot be seen—correction, bot templates and references to Discord votes—but those traces establish only what speakers report. Image-led proposals also leave the visual design partly outside the textual record. Accordingly, the account can describe proposing, revising, accepting or resisting those objects where participants say so, but cannot independently recover every template, poll result, moderation action or automated instruction.
