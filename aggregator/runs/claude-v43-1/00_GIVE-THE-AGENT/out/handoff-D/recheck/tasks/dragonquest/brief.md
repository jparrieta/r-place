## Vernacular

`DQ` is the routine short form for Dragon Quest, followed by a Roman numeral for an entry. `DQX` and `DQ XI` refer to the tenth and eleventh main games, while platform names such as 3DS, PS4, and Switch often stand without explanation.

> Not only is DQX an MMO (`dffi1tu`)

Series groupings also work as shared shorthand. People say `Loto Trilogy` and `Zenithian Trilogy` without defining them.

> since you just started the Zenithian Trilogy (`dfe861l`)

> the loto twist is far more effective having played I and II already (`dfep596`)

Game locations, monsters, systems, and bosses also enter ordinary speech without glosses, as in `Slime Hill`, `alchemy`, and `Rhapthorne`.

> due to getting way too carried away in Slime Hill (`62a1dg`)

Members call an enthusiast a `DQ fan`.

> I met a DQ fan at work today. (`623goi`)
