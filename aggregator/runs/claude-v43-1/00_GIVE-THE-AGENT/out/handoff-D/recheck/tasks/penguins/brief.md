## Data problems

The main limitation is coverage: 425 complete items were served from 45 of 142 threads, leaving most corpus bodies unopened and several very large threads only partly paged. All seven active days were covered, and no served item remained incomplete after following the one explicit continuation marker. (state)

The archive itself contains 137 deleted or removed items and 432 unresolved parent pointers, so some conversations cannot be reconstructed and sibling replies cannot safely be treated as replies to absent content. There were no empty days, and the archive’s counts describe the supplied record rather than independently proving historical collection completeness. (header)

Linked images, videos, and off-corpus pages were deliberately not fetched. That makes image-led memes and comics less legible than text exchanges; the comic titles show the gap but not the missing visual content.
