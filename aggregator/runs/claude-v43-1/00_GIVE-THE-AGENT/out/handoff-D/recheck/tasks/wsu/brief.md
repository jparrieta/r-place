## Vernacular

> meet a few hundred of my fellow future Cougs! (`61dkgy`)

“Wazzu” is understood but marked as less official than “WSU.” A former Student Ambassador says the training barred both “wazzu” and “dorm”:

> we were explicitly told not to use "wazzu" and "dorm". (`dfgg7em`)

“Residence hall,” or simply “hall,” is the official-facing term. Campus shorthand includes “CDD” for Community/Duncan Dunn and “UREC” for the recreation center:
