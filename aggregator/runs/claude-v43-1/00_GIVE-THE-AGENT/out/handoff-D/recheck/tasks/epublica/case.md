## Relations — what set off what

One causal claim is explicit but belongs to a participant: metalpotato says “este proyecto surge para evitar la inútil guerra que estaba en marcha” (`dfqat6y`). This supports reporting the new flag as their own response to the zona azul fight; it does not establish that every painter left that fight.

Several neighbour-facing links are supported by the words of both turns. SpaceX asks them to “hold off on changing the color of the flames” because of bot resources (`dfqi7zt`); 59 seconds later metalpotato answers “Thanks, I'll tell the others!” (`dfqi9ps`). Later, a SpaceX participant asks whether a planner will join a webchat “to discuss the border” (`dfqtjx0`); 82 seconds later metalpotato replies “On my way” (`dfqtma4`). These are answer-and-response relations, not conclusions drawn from reply adjacency.

A second supported relation is contained within the participant's own explanation: after “nos han quitado el espacio” (`dfrercl`), metalpotato proposes another design. Likewise the rocket redesign is described as allowing them to work “por detrás” and leaving more room (`6318po`). These are participants' explanations of why the plan changed. The record supports the reported sequence and explanation, but cannot independently establish what happened on the unseen canvas.

The closure of Place is followed 20.82 hours later by a proposal to continue the subreddit (`63871r`, `63eb2x`). The later post itself says Place was the “excusa perfecta” for a group the author had wanted to create (`63eb2x`), which supports that author's explanation of the transition. Supportive replies from `dftdllj` and `dftlqza` show multiple people endorsing continuation; they do not establish that the whole community changed its mind.

The corpus does not show that the peace appeal itself caused all attacks on the other Spanish flag to stop. Facu7701 says “ya no tenemos problemas” after describing private-message redirection (`dfqhjhg`), but this is that participant's outcome report, not an independently supported causal relation.

## Posts against comments

The post layer presents a consistent public programme: stop the zona azul “guerra” (`62wl1o`), avoid bothering larger communities (`62wmg6`), grow behind or around neighbours (`62zae1`), and preserve peace between Spanish projects (`630v3d`). The comment layer often shows that programme being enacted: a participant corrects a yellow Hoodr pixel after learning of the agreement (`dfqb9na`), another apologises (`dfqdv8n`), and the organiser goes to the SpaceX webchat (`dfqtma4`).

There is also a direct contradiction: the posts say not to interfere and seek peace; the comment layer contains AnisDelMono's allegation that “muchos de aquí” were “continuamente atacando” the other Spanish flag (`dfqjip1`). Metalpotato does not deny purple repainting; he says he messages such painters and “no puedo prohibir nada” (`dfqjpxt`). Later, pelospeludez calls people working on the lower flag to go after another one and then return to the upper flag (`dfr0prc`), while metalpotato warns this could bring retaliation to “ésta” (`dfr0tss`). This is a contrast between an organising norm and contested conduct, not evidence that the room collectively reversed its position.

Some claims live only in one layer. The posts carry the detailed route and border rules; comments carry most evidence of actual repair, recruitment, neighbour help and disagreement. Claims of a “perfect” result appear in both a post (`63871r`) and comments (`dfs3vha`, `dftcxu2`), but remain participants' assessments because linked images were not inspected.

## Counter-reading

My reading is: a small group built its republican flag by redirecting conflict into its own project, negotiating routes around larger automated neighbours, and then treating the shared result as the start of a lasting subreddit.

The competing account recorded before searching was that cooperation is mainly the organisers' retrospective story. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain sustained calls to overwrite neighbours, reject agreements, or abandon the main flag for the zona azul fight.

I searched the complete supplied record for attack, sabotage, war, overwriting, vandalism, going “encima,” rejection of agreements, and zona azul/lower-flag language, then opened every returned item through `read item`. The strongest contrary item is `dfqjip1`: AnisDelMono says “muchos de aquí” had been “continuamente atacando” the flag beside Rocket League and the Placehearts. `dfr0prc` is the strongest direct aggressive proposal, calling painters toward another lower flag before returning upward. These items change “the group cooperated” into the narrower claim that organisers and several participants advocated, negotiated and repaired cooperation amid conduct they could not fully control. The central reading survives because the corpus also contains explicit agreements and answers—not just retrospective praise—including `dfqi7zt` with `dfqi9ps`, `dfqtjx0` with `dfqtma4`, and the mistake/apology exchange `dfqd3bh` with `dfqdv8n`.

The searches found no item in the supplied window explicitly rejecting the Hoodr or SpaceX agreements after learning of them. That null result is limited to this small, partial Reddit record: off-platform discussion and the identities or intentions of canvas painters are not visible.

## Saturation

Record saturation: all 26 threads and all 149 in-window items were read in full. The corpus does not look truncated, but seven bodies are deleted or removed, several plans survive only as link-post titles, and the images themselves are unseen. The SpaceX webchat and the constitutional-flag Discord moved discussion outside this record; Reddit receives summaries, but the live deliberations cannot be checked.

Practice saturation: later event threads mostly repeat the established work of revising routes, respecting borders, recruiting help and maintaining the flag. The post-event edge adds a genuinely different practice—turning a temporary Place coordination group into a lasting political subreddit—and the final PlaceTwo title extends the flag beyond the closed canvas. The record therefore supports the event practices strongly but does not show whether the proposed longer-lived community stabilised inside this window.

## Selection

I read the empty pre-week brief and then all 26 openings before triage, as the case-lead instrument requires. All other threads show r/place conduct, immediate retrospective work, or the direct post-event continuation of the Place-created group.
