## Vernacular

“Robust” is an adjective for a player, tactic, or object that is effective in a fight; “unrobust” is its opposite. In one weapons discussion, disarming an opponent is itself called the most robust weapon.

> The most robust  weapon is disarm though. Take their weapon, push them dow, hit them with something heavy. (`dfd3u4o`)

“Greytiding” or “grey tiding” names assistant-style theft, break-ins, slipping, and other disruptive play short of the speaker's idea of outright antagonism. One participant defined their own version through stealing station equipment.

> Your right, to be honest, the only real grey tiding I commonly do is just steal everything that is important. (`dfm3d7p`)

“Cargonia” is cargo spoken of as its own bloc, with “hail cargonia” as the greeting; “bwoink” is an administrator contacting a player, while “adminbus” describes overt administrator intervention.

> Just join cargo, yell "hail cargonia" to the other cargo people, and they will teach you. Cargo solidarity is strong. (`dfh2p9b`)

> I highly doubt an admin would bwoink you for that, unless you mean they went the adminbus route. (`dfgcnsi`)

“Spessmen” is the game/community's joking name for its people, and clown talk turns “honk” into a greeting, exclamation, and identity marker.

> Bad spessmen jokes! (`61to4t`)

> Hello fellow honkers. (`61yy4y`)
