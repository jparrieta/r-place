## Counter-reading

The one-sentence reading before search was: the record shows one author asking for help with a specified logo and later using two posts to thank participants and celebrate success; it does not itself show participants coordinating in the subreddit.

A plausible competing account was that the subreddit visibly coordinated the logo effort here. If the cautious reading were wrong, the corpus should contain replies or wording about volunteering, assigning pixels, changing the location or plan, or defending the logo.

The strongest item against the cautious reading is `632v02`, because its “Any help would be greatly appreciated” request and later thanks to “everyone that participated” attribute the result to more than the author. Even so, this item contains no reply or description of what another participant did. Searches across both corpus items for volunteering, assigning, pixels, coordination, defense, changes or moves returned no items; searches for solitary work or failure also returned no items. A broader query for the case’s own words returned only the already-coded `632v02` (“help”) and `638658` (“We did it”), and both items were reread in full. The alternative therefore sharpens the finding—collective participation is the author’s claim—but does not turn that claim into visible in-subreddit coordination. Because the record is only two posts, the null results establish no more than the absence of such material from this supplied corpus.
