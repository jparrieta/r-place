# r/hatsune — within-case account

## Chronology

The openings show an ordinary stream of Miku artwork, source links, and a music video around a compact r/place episode. The event first becomes visible at `62xk3w`, d1 04-02 03:32, when the post asks people to “help keep” a little Miku on the canvas. The first episode runs from `62xk3w` to `dfq2xny`: people give “all my dots,” ask whether to box Miku in, report “we somehow did it,” celebrate having “representation,” and thank those who helped. A taskbar threat enters within the same thread at `dfq2w3a`, which says she is still standing behind a border but “might die soon.”

The second episode, from `dfq6au1` to `dfqv0kj`, turns keeping the original sprite into bargaining and relocation. `dfq6au1` says the Windows 95 taskbar is in her path and joins “protect her at all cost” to proposing a treaty. The edited post `62zmro` later reports an agreement and sends a future representative to Discord. By `dfqjmbn`, the group is presented with two options “with how much power they have”: relocate or become a taskbar item. In `631zsv`, that becomes “we can't fight the expansion we'll just be eaten”; the replies make a smaller Miku, share templates, and move building discussion to Discord.

A third episode, from `635gow` to `dfrvikb`, combines renewed defense with a more official campaign. At d2 04-03 08:18, `635gow` asks for defense against people attacking Miku; `dfrhyx6` promises to continue protecting the sprite. `dfri6iu` answers that the sprite is supposed to move under the deal. Moderator posts at `635ld8` and `635ug4` label an official stance and official campaign, although the former's body is removed. The megathread `63643u` announces completion, a protected desktop-icon deal, relocation instructions, and an official point of contact. Its comments do not simply accept the post: `dfrlhh4` says not to move until a build spot is ready, `dfrll24` says to hold the picture while conceding its border, and `dfrvikb` later reports that the picture is being bulldozed.

The ending episode runs from `dfs0tqe` to `dfsa42z`. At d3 04-03 17:26, after placement has ended, `dfs0tqe` says “It's over!” and mourns the Vocaloid logo and Miku's skirt while judging the result “not bad.” A promise to build a new one at `dfs0xcq` is corrected 4.7 minutes later by `dfs165l`: “r/place is over.” The exchange closes with “fun while it lasted” at `dfs730q` and, at `dfsa42z`, relief at no longer living in five-minute intervals alongside pride in a “(smol) mark.”

The final retrospective episode runs from `63bqp0` to `dftgnvs`. Two posts titled “The Ideal Place” appear after the event. One has a deleted body; the other prompts comments about Miku stepping onto a barely readable osu logo, more resolution being available, and laughter. These are post-event readings and jokes about the finished image, not further pixel placement.

## Relations — what set off what

The corpus directly supports the taskbar threat setting off protective diplomacy. In `dfq6au1`, the same speaker says the taskbar is “in her path,” immediately says “We must protect her,” and then says a treaty was proposed “to find a way to not destroy her.” This is a participant's own explanation, not an inference from thread order.

It also supports unequal power setting off relocation work. `dfqjmbn` introduces the two relocation options “with how much power they have”; `dfqk3jq` asks whether the group lacks the manpower to stay; and `dfqk6he` answers “against the taskbar? no way.” In the later post `631zsv`, the participant makes the reasoning explicit: “we can't fight the expansion we'll just be eaten.” The smaller-design call at `dfqp6b2` is answered 2.1 minutes later by `dfqp9i8`, “yes im working on that at the moment.” The causal claim rests on what participants say about power and work, not merely on this sequence.

The defense request at `635gow` receives a participant explanation that changes what defense should mean: `dfri6iu` says the sprite “is supposed to move” because a deal is outlined on r/placestart and r/Vocaloid. `dfri9qp` asks where the deal is, and `dfrj1yw` supplies the link 41.1 minutes later. The text supports the deal being invoked to redirect defenders; it does not establish that the deal caused the attack.

The megathread supplies a supported response from damage to rebuilding elsewhere. `dfrvikb` says, “The picture is being bulldozed”; the direct reply `dfs0932`, 1.6 hours later, answers, “It's okay, it has to be rebuilt at a different location.” Likewise, the correction at the end is explicit: `dfs0xcq` promises “we're building a new one,” and `dfs165l` answers 4.7 minutes later, “where? /r/place is over.”

The official stance `635ld8` at H65.00 and official campaign `635ug4` at H66.21 follow the defense request `635gow` at H64.32. The record does not state that the request caused the moderator posts, so this is an observed sequence only. Different speakers also disagree about when to move—`63643u` directs work to the new location while `dfrlhh4` says not to move until a spot is ready—but that contrast does not establish that the community as a whole changed its mind.

## Posts against comments

The posts establish the public arc: `62xk3w` asks people to keep Miku there; `62zmro` announces a treaty; `631zsv` announces the taskbar-icon move; `635ld8` and `635ug4` make the stance and campaign official; and `63643u` centralises plans. The comment layer supplies most of the labour, doubt, correction, and closure.

The clearest contradiction is inside the megathread. The post `63643u` says the group is “giving up the current space” and directs people to start the new location; the comment `dfrlhh4` says “we should [not] move until we see a spot ready to build in,” while `dfrll24` says the picture should stay for now. This is a disagreement among speakers over timing, not evidence that the entire community reversed a settled view.

A descriptive development also separates the layers: the post `63643u` says “Miku with Leek is complete,” whereas the later comment `dfrpo1g` reports “Leek seems to have been removed.” Because the comment comes 2.3 hours after the post, it supports a change in canvas condition, not a simultaneous contradiction.

The official status exists only in posts (`635ld8`, `635ug4`), and the removed body of `635ld8` prevents recovering what the stance contained. The end-of-event correction and evaluation exist only in comments: `dfs165l` states that r/place is over, and `dfsa42z` joins relief with pride in the group's smol mark. The post-event “Ideal Place” image is a post-layer claim, while the osu and resolution jokes are comment-layer interpretations (`dfsxykb`, `dft72ik`).

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/hatsune combined defense with negotiation against a stronger taskbar project, then reluctantly redesigned Miku as a smaller taskbar icon, and finally turned the ending into mourning, relief, and jokes about the mark that remained.

The strongest competing account was that the taskbar deal was welcomed as a route to visibility and displaced defense early. If my reading were wrong, the corpus should contain members positively choosing or celebrating the move before pressure or damage, or rejecting continued defense because moving was preferable rather than because they lacked power.

The strongest contrary item is `dfqkuau`: after working through what “Vocaloid” meant, the commenter says a taskbar application would make Miku “more visible,” “which would be nice.” This changes the account by adding a desired benefit to relocation; reluctance was not pure loss. It does not overturn the main reading. In the same exchange, `dfqk3jq` asks about lacking manpower and `dfqk6he` answers “no way,” while `631zsv` says they will be eaten if they fight. A search scoped to positive move language returned three items: `dfqkuau`, the unrelated ordinary-art comment `dfr42qm` (“nice”), and the post-event `dfsa42z` (“Nice to leave our (smol) mark”). A second search scoped to the case's pressure vocabulary returned eleven items, including protection calls at `dfq6au1`, `635gow`, and `dfrhyx6`, power and manpower at `dfqjmbn` and `dfqk3jq`, “can't fight” at `631zsv`, and bulldozing at `dfrll24` and `dfrvikb`. Every returned item was read. No late code was needed because the substantive returned passages were already coded; the null result is limited to these vocabulary-led searches in this small record and cannot show that nobody privately preferred the move.

## Saturation

Record saturation is partial. All fifteen threads were disposed and every event thread was read whole, but six items have deleted or removed text. The most consequential loss is the removed moderator body at `635ld8`; its surviving title establishes an official stance but not its substance. The deleted body and reply in `63bqp0` similarly limit the first “Ideal Place” post. Surviving adjacent threads and comments still show the campaign, relocation, ending, and retrospective joke, but they cannot reconstruct those missing words.

Practice saturation is reasonably strong for this compact case: building and defense appear early, negotiation and relocation recur across several threads, and the final threads add ending and retrospective joking rather than another organising practice. Off-platform saturation is weaker. `62zmro`, `dfqv0kj`, and `63643u` move or point discussion to Discord; templates and plans return, but the negotiations and building talk inside those chats are unseen. The record also has only one side of relations with r/placestart, r/Vocaloid, r/Berserk, and r/thebluecorner.

## Selection

As required for a branch-A case lead, I read the briefing and the complete openings file before triage. I then read the corpus header and all fifteen triage windows in chronological order. Threads whose short records were returned whole were disposed from that reading; the four longer event-touching threads—`62xk3w`, `631zsv`, `63643u`, and `63buv8`—were opened in full. Nine threads were kept because they showed building, defending, negotiating, officially organising, ending, or recounting r/place. Six were excluded as ordinary artwork/source routines, a music video, a Sony-store video, or an unrelated reply. No excluded thread met the ten-item citation threshold.

## Other communities named

r/placestart is treated as both a threat and negotiating partner: `dfq6au1` names its taskbar plan, `62zmro` announces an agreement, and `dfrj1yw` points to its diplomacy thread. r/Vocaloid is an ally and party to the deal in `63643u` and `dfri6iu`. A visitor from r/Berserk at `dfqmpvg` raises a possible spatial accommodation, but the confused replies do not establish an agreement. r/thebluecorner matters conditionally in `dfrll24` because its claimed space affects the proposed relocation. The osu logo appears only as an object of a post-event joke at `dfsxykb`; the record supports no organisational relation with that community.

## Absence

The six deleted or removed items are visible as absences rather than reconstructed. In `62xk3w`, deleted turns sit beside surviving proposals to start Rin and later treaty talk, but their content is unknowable. At `635ld8`, the moderator title and later “please go ahead and add it” survive while the official stance itself does not. The corpus contains no direct account from the taskbar, Vocaloid, Berserk, bluecorner, or osu participants, and no text from inside the named Discord chats.
