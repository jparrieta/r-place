## What I could not tell

The opened sample does not establish an expansion or origin for “Zekken”; it only establishes that the term names a numbered weekly series.

> Peace signs and smiles! - Weekly Zekken 21 (`61ze59`)

It is also too early to call Weekly Alice a durable series. In that thread, a commenter says earlier attempts had failed, while the poster promises to try and sets a Sunday reminder; the opened body is installment #1.

> we have had several attempts to start a reoccurring Alice, and almost all of them seemed to have failed. (`dfgfzyh`)

> I'll try to make it not fail then. Let my determination kick in! . . . And maybe set an alarm every Sunday~ (`dfgifaz`)

The 41 served threads are enough to identify visible high-volume voices and several roles, but not to assign stable roles to most of the archive's 351 distinct authors. The same coverage limit means that no claim of a unique joke, unique format, or universal response style is warranted beyond the opened sample. (header) (state)
