Its reply `dfqgvk8`, 31.9 minutes later, says “ok, ya estoy en discord” and makes red and movement to the right “la prioridad.” The later speaker expressly accepts and restates the priority, so this is stronger than reply adjacency.

- `6354ne` asks for defence of Bolivia and `dfrm8a1` asks for the location 5.3 hours later. The sequence supports a request for actionable detail, but it does not support the causal claim that the appeal produced pixel defence.

Several important sequences do not carry a supported causal link. ## Posts against comments

The comment layer carries most of the operational work: explaining the automaton in `dfqed23`, recalculating proportions in `dfqe961` and `dfqezml`, reporting a nearly complete border in `dfqh1gv`, and repairing chat links in `dfqfw7d` and `dfqgzjo`.

Three contradictions or qualifications are explicit. The post `630e7a` proposes asking Rainbow Road for space; the comment `dfqiita` says Rainbow Road already has plenty of space and proposes extending toward Spain instead. The post `630hyh` presents a newly made Gran Colombia Discord; the comments say another room already exists (`dfqcuby`), demand clarity about “CUAL ES CUAL” (`dfqcvpf`), and recommend joining the other room (`dfqd6ux`). The post `630uyj` labels its invitation “Chat Oficial”; the comment `dfqgvre` says it does not work, after which `dfqgzjo` reports repairing it. These differences show proposal, challenge, and repair in the two layers. They do not establish that the whole community reversed a settled position.

The claim that Discord “tiene mas tiempo creado y hemos discutido varios temas” lives only in the comment `dfqcsh2`; the corpus cannot inspect those discussions. Conversely, the coordinate orders in the post `630lyb` receive no visible comment-layer confirmation.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that this room assembled and maintained a Gran Colombia flag by using Reddit for public calls, tactics, link repair, and neighbour-facing proposals while shifting detailed coordination into Discord.

If that reading were wrong, the corpus should instead contain participants saying that nobody was coordinating, that the plan or flag had been abandoned or lost, that division between Discords prevented action, or that proposed border agreements gave way to fighting.

The strongest contrary item is `dfqcvpf`: “AL FIN CUAL ES CUAL!!!! DEJEMOS EL DE ESTE POST COMO OFICIAL.” Its exasperation confirms competing rooms and uncertainty over which was official. `dfqkfzq` likewise asks whether another Discord is being used because the linked one does not work, and `dfqgvre` says the official link “no funciona.” These items make the account less tidy: coordination was repeatedly fragmented by venue choice and access failures. What survives is the narrower claim that participants worked to coordinate, because the contrary items themselves demand an official room or a working link, and replies such as `dfqfw7d`, `dfqd6ux`, and `dfqgzjo` supply, select, or repair one.

The searches covered this case’s own words for no coordination, abandonment or loss, Discord confusion, failure, separation, fighting, invasion, and war. Every returned item was read. No item explicitly says that the flag or plan was abandoned, that nobody was coordinating, or that Discord division prevented further action. That null result is limited to 78 in-window items, 14 deleted or removed bodies, and discussions that participants say occurred off-platform; it cannot establish absence in the wider community or in Discord.

## Saturation

For the central practices, later passages mostly repeat and elaborate what appears by the dense d2 period: taking space, working out the tricolour, negotiating borders, and maintaining Discord access recur across separate threads. The very late request in `6354ne`, however, opens a distinct practice—asking this room to defend the Bolivian flag—so the set of practices is not fully saturated before the record thins. The post-event `dfs6mgf` repeats Belgian joking rather than adding a new form of conduct.

The record is internally whole as supplied: every one of its 22 threads was read in full, and all replies whose parents should be present are present. It is not socially complete. Fourteen bodies are deleted or removed, including five posts; the body of several organising posts is unavailable, and Discord carries discussions and decisions the corpus cannot see. Those limits are material to the details of coordination but do not erase the surviving Reddit calls, answers, invitations, and repairs.
