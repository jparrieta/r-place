## Relations — what set off what

The clearest links are stated answers, not merely neighbouring posts. When `dfp6kl6` asks for ten pixels beside the MCRN logo, `dfp7627` replies 14.4 minutes later that they “would not dare to touch it,” and `dfp7i3z` repeats the promise after 22.6 minutes. When `dfpad26` says a border would spoil Latvia's proportions, `dfpb5ga` answers 19.7 minutes later “Sutinku” (“I agree”) and proposes a joint border. These are supported responses because the later passages explicitly accept the earlier requests.

The Latvian help sequence is similarly explicit. `62vw5n` asks for workforce to repair Latvia's flag, and 37.9 minutes later `dfpibhf` answers “padedam latviams” (“let's help the Latvians”). In `62z1ie`, `dfq34rn` responds after 1.4 minutes that they had thought Latvia was attacking but are “going to help now.” That is a participant's own explanation for changing action, not evidence that the room as a whole changed its mind.

The defence instructions form a direct exchange: `dfqlqoa` asks “Instructions on how?” and `dfqlvov` answers 3.0 minutes later by telling them to fix wrong-coloured pixels. A longer sequence follows `dfqot7y`'s demand for Belarus's six pixels: 235.6 minutes later `dfqztbl` says “your flag is safe now.” The wording supports a repair sequence, but the corpus does not identify which placements caused the outcome, so it does not establish that any single Reddit comment saved the flag.

One causal claim belongs to a participant. `dfq241f` says “šis mūsų darbas paskatino” other countries to mark their flags with hearts—their work “prompted” the others. The record preserves that explanation but contains no observations of those other groups' decisions, so the relation is reported as their claim rather than an established effect.

Several developments only follow one another. The shared Discord proposal in `dfp7pd6` precedes later Discord-made plans in `dfpezjz`, but nobody says the first Reddit comment caused those plans. Likewise, the aggressive “consume your hard work” line in `dfpidtn` precedes extensive Baltic assistance; sequence alone does not show that the community rejected it or changed its collective mind.

## Posts against comments

The posts say Lithuania and its neighbours need help: `62vw5n` requests workforce, `62z1ie` asks people to make the banner, `63130c` asks people to defend small flags, and `63533l` asks Lithuania to restore Estonia rather than make a third Lithuanian flag. The comment layer largely supplies the doing—pixel instructions in `dfpibhf`, a commitment to help in `dfq34rn`, repair instructions in `dfqlvov`, and the soldierly acceptance in `dfrjvty`.

There is one sharp contradiction: the posts say to coordinate space and help neighbours, while the comment layer in `dfpidtn` says Lithuania needs “more land than a stupid show” and threatens to “consume your hard work for Lithuanian pride.” Other comments also grumble that Estonia has too many flags (`dfpl377`), but these are positions voiced by particular speakers, not proof of a community-wide turn against cooperation.

The exception is the post title `631q3h`, which says the Baltic server has 125 members and “Čia vyksta visas strategavimas” (“all strategy happens here”); its body is removed. Conversely, the hammer-and-sickle post `6301wf` supplies only the surviving question, while the comments carry the competing readings—minimising the image in `dfqcf24`, politicising it in `dfqdqb2`, and calling its makers edgy teenagers in `dfqnvho`.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/lithuania used its flag as a base for making room with nearby builders and helping Baltic flags, while much detailed planning moved to Discord and decisions returned to Reddit.

Before searching, I specified that, if this were wrong, the record should instead contain repeated calls to take neighbours' land for Lithuanian pride, refuse help to Latvia, Belarus or Estonia, or attack their flags—not merely an isolated objection or joke.

The strongest contrary item is `dfpidtn`: “we need more land than a stupid show” and “i try to consume your hard work for Lithuanian pride.” It shows that cooperative accommodation was contested and prevents treating it as consensus. It does not overturn the reading, because the returned items also include `dfq34rn` changing from “Latvians were attacking us” to “I am going to help now,” and `dfrjucw` and `dfrjuid` asking neighbours to cooperate against an anticipated attack. The “pavogti” hit in `dfoyjs5` is marked with “:D” and offers Latvia Austria's completed flag as a joke; two “užim” hits in `dfpcifa` and `dfpcwyh` are translations telling Lithuania not to occupy a neighbour's needed space. `dfpl377` is a real hostile description of Estonia but urges Latvia not to surrender rather than refusing Baltic help.

The search covered the complete supplied record for the case's own words around consuming, stealing, attacking, refusing, occupying and giving up, returning eight items; all eight were opened through the reading tool. It found no repeated practice of refusing Baltic help. That absence is bounded by this Reddit record: removed passages and the unseen Discord discussion mean it cannot establish what all participants did or thought elsewhere.

## Saturation

The practice reading is broad but not fully settled before the record ends. Flag shaping, neighbour negotiation, Baltic help and Discord coordination recur across several threads; later defence exchanges repeat the established practice of reallocating pixel work to neighbours. Yet the late Greek/Turkish “mission” and Grand Duchy exchange introduces a distinct mock-military practice near the end, so the supplied window does not demonstrate complete thematic saturation.

The evidence record is also incomplete. Twenty-eight items have deleted or removed text, including nine posts; surviving titles sometimes preserve the prompt, but deleted reply chains in `6301wf` could hide positions in its political dispute. Planning visibly moved to Discord—`dfq3a80` says “all the planning's done there”—and Reddit receives plans, invitations and decisions but not the deliberation inside. Two sneakpeekbot items are machine-generated setting rather than community conduct. The forest also shows uncoded stretches where links, greetings or automatic output did no additional practice. These limits weaken claims about consensus and causes, while the repeated, directly quoted requests and answers still support the described Reddit practices.
