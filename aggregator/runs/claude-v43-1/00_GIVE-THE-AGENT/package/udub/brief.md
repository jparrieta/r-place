## Vernacular

“UW” is the usual short name. Campus branches appear as “UWB” and “UWS,” while fields and departments appear as “CSE,” “EE,” “INFO,” and “BioE.” One student described a move between branches in those terms:

> I transferred to UWB and everything changed for the better. (`dfl2fw9`)

Student groups are often named by initials. “FASA” receives a direct gloss:

> FASA is the Filipino American Student Association (`dfg82j8`)

“HMB” is used for the band after its full name appears:

> Husky Marching Band (`dfgfbgm`)

“RSO” is used without expansion for a campus organization:

> There's also another RSO on campus called Stage Notes (`dfhbsld`)

“Panopto” is treated as familiar lecture technology rather than explained:

> Panopto is an introverts dream. (`dflw0fs`)

## Recurring formats

The clearest repeated format in the served threads is a practical question followed by personal experience, course detail, or direct advice. A wait-list post explicitly offers an AMA:

> I was put on the wait list in 2015 and got in. If you are in the same boat AMA. (`61x48s`)

Course questions often receive concrete recommendations:

> I recommend the class to anyone who might be even slightly interested in Atmospheric Sciences. (`dffs2r7`)

Open prompts about the start of the quarter draw short jokes and shared complaints:

## Register

Speech is casual, compressed, and often profane. Replies can be warm and practical, with offers to continue in private:

> You're welcome to PM me if you'd like someone to talk with (`dfkz260`)

Advice is often direct rather than formal:

> Find something that you really care about, and let them know why you need to go to UW to do this thing. (`dfi2ztp`)
