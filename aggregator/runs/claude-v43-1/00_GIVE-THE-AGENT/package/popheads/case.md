## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: Popheads briefly concentrated its visible canvas work on a small RAWM saxophone that participants brought to a recognizable completion point, but limited manpower constrained expansion and, after reported destruction, the organizer declined to rebuild; a separate late lettering call drew no visible response.

If that reading were wrong, the record would contain participant words reporting a rebuild, continued defense, surviving or completed `r/popheads` lettering, another completed community design, or renewed coordination after `dfrqs3u`.

I searched the full supplied record for rebuilding, survival, defense, project and lettering references, and participation or completion, and read every returned item. Most broad hits used those words for music or unrelated community matters. The strongest contrary item is `dfqm5ji`, “done!”, reinforced by `dfqqn81`, “Looking good so far guys!” It changes any account of simple failure: the sax was successfully built or visibly brought to a participant-recognized completion point before it failed to endure. The search returned no item reporting a rebuild, later defense, or completed lettering. Within this partial record, the durability-and-scale conclusion survives; absence cannot establish that off-record activity never occurred.
