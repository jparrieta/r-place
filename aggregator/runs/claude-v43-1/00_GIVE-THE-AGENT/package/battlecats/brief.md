## Vernacular

Players use `uber` as a cat class, and `uber carry` for relying on those units to clear a stage.

> In your case just uber carry it... use anti-zombie/tanky ubers (`dffh3v1`)

`TF` names a unit form or upgrade state. The record sometimes places the abbreviation beside an explicit evolved form, but it does not expand the letters.

> No. Thief TF is Goemon. Maglev is evolved Express Cat, the unit you get from River Styx. (`dffipjq`)

`Meatshield` appears as a verb for a defensive battle action.

> Alien Nyandam can murder your army if you don't meatshield and control Aliens well. (`dfet0xl`)

Players say `roll` for a choice among named gacha sets, often with a guaranteed draw under discussion.

> Should i roll 11-Guaranteed on Dragon Emperors, Easter Carnival or wait for another? (`dfh2rtx`)
