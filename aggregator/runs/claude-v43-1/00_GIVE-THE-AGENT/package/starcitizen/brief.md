## Vernacular

Members are addressed as “citizens,” and “the verse” appears in greetings and farewells. The text also uses `o7` as a friendly sign-off. These forms occur in more than one opened thread.

> Hey there citizens!!! (`62bbrh`)

> Welcome to the verse. (`dfleaoy`)

> See you in the verse and fly safe o7 (`dflezvy`)

“Avocados” is the local slang for Evocati testers. This meaning is stated inside the opened discussion, so it does not rely on an outside expansion.

> There is an elite group that you can be invited to if you do ALLOT of bug hunting called the Evocati. The common slang for this is the avocados. (`dfd3gj2`)

“Baby PU” names the limited playable area then available.

> We have a "baby" PU currently... It's basically the local area around a gas giant where you can fly and play with others. (`dfd5bli`)

The buying register includes `CCU` as an upgrade action and “melt” as recovery of package and upgrade value as store credit. That use is visible in a question and its direct answer.

> If I CCU from a Freelancer (via the game package) to a Freelancer DUR; will I still be able to melt and retain the full value of the original game package, plus the difference paid in the CCU to the Freelancer DUR in store credit if I decide to upgrade to another package in the future? (`dfk7jne`)

> Yes, you will get the full value of the package and any upgrades applied. (`dfkbi7k`)
