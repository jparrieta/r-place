## Vernacular

People shorten Morrowind to “MW” when contrasting it with Skyrim.

> For an rpg, MW. For an acton game, Skyrim. (`dfhw5xf`)

They use “TR” for Tamriel Rebuilt, and “vanilla” for the base game.

> Tamriel Rebuilt "just" adds new land to vanilla Morrowind. (`dffabx3`)

> For the most part, the only effect TR has on the base game is that a recall spell will occasionally transport you off-Vvardenfell. (`dfgmlr4`)

“OP” describes an extremely strong build or tactic, while “outlander” can work as a playful form of thanks.

> Thieves are either very weak or hilariously OP. (`dfd7gtp`)

> Thanks, outlander. (`dfl7msb`)

## Register

Advice is usually direct and detailed, but it can close with blunt encouragement.

> Sneak is ass in early game though :/ good luck! (`dfdjnyb`)

Help exchanges can be brief and warm: a user supplied a named mod, and the requester replied in the game’s own idiom.

> Here you go, it's called "Chargen Revamped v2.95". (`dfl7gyb`)

> Thanks, outlander. (`dfl7msb`)
