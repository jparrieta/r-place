## Vernacular

Random outcomes are personified as “RNGesus” or the “RNG Gods”: they can smile on a run, while a favorable background means a sacrifice has “paid off.”

> RNGesus smiled down on me today. (`622ufq`)

> It means the goats I sacrificed to the RNG Gods are finally paying off! (`62h15b`)

“OP” marks a setup as exceptionally strong. “Crew kills” or “crewkills” means winning by killing the enemy crew rather than simply breaking the ship.

> Holy shit boy, that is what I call OP (`dfju22y`)

> Perfect, then you have plenty of fights ahead to get intense crewkills using fire and anti-bio beam (`dflggmw`)

“Golden” crew or skills can puzzle a newer reader; in this thread another user glossed “golden things” as second-tier skill upgrades.

> So what's all this talk about golden things? (`dfgwogn`)

> Second tier skill upgrades (`dfgzihq`)

## Recurring formats

New-player troubleshooting appeared in multiple separate threads. Posters named the difficulty, hours played, the point where a run failed, and then asked for diagnosis; replies usually asked for screenshots or specifics before giving long, system-by-system advice.

> Ok I'm really trying to get this game, but I can't win even on easy. Pointers for a newbie? (`61nh2d`)

> I Need Help. I Have 50 Hours In The Game, And Yet To Win On Easy. (`61v2a3`)

First victories were another repeated format. The post often downplayed an Easy win, while replies treated it as worth celebrating and then added advice for the next run.

> Wooo. Just beat the game on easy. (`624k1d`)

> Just beat the boss for the first time, though on easy (`62ekjw`)

> Very well done, congrats on the first victory, man. I'm rooting for you in the future, I'm sure you'll have more in no time. (`dflvwsq`)

Loadout-choice threads formed a third visible format: a poster described several weapon or augment combinations, and commenters argued over safety, fun, and scrap value.

> LRS + Preigniter seems like the *safest* option, even if SRA + Preigniter is stronger. (`dfg1qu0`)

The opened evidence did not establish a named daily or weekly thread, a fixed posting schedule, or a ritual with a reliable cadence. (state)

## Register

The prevailing register in the opened newcomer and victory threads was casual and encouraging. Welcome and congratulations could sit beside warnings that harder modes would “screw” the player.

> So welcome to FTL, enjoy Normal and Hard totally screwing you over in sector 1 and 2. (`dfjv7s6`)

Disagreement was direct but usually stayed on the game mechanics. One sharp rebuke was answered with an explanation of the speaker’s stake in the advice rather than an escalating fight.

> Don't be a dick (`dfhci6k`)

> I'm not. I wrote the guide, and I'm genuinely wondering about this. I want people to be able to read the guide then beat the game. (`dfhcn9s`)

Technical disagreements also turned into play-acting with the game’s systems. In this exchange, a dispute over Mind Control became jokes about hacking and boarding the other person’s brain.

> We're going to have to disagree here. I think you're really underestimating Mind Control, but I don't feel like debating the point right now. (`dflhemt`)

> That's because I hacked your debating ability and boarded your left cortex. (`dflhgnw`)

> Ah. Then allow me to counter with Mind Control. (`dflhiqx`)
