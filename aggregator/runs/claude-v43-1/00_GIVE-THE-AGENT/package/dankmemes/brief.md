## Evidence base

The corpus contains 36,430 items in 5,495 threads. I received 480 complete item bodies from 49 threads, touching all seven active days. No day was empty, no served item was partial, and no active day was untouched. Most threads and items were deliberately unopened. Titles were sampled across the week, selected threads were read in full, and most other represented threads supplied only a sampled item. (state)

## Vernacular

The clearest local contrast is **dank** against **normie**. A repeated sticky asks readers to grade each post on that scale.

> #Upvote this comment if this is a **DANK MEME**. Downvote this comment if this is a **NORMIE MEME**. (`dfd4ded`)

Commenters use **updoots** for votes and replace letters with the boxed B glyph. Both appear in one compact line.

> 137 updoots for the good 🅱u c c (`dfhvx6k`)

The substitution can extend across a whole phrase rather than mark one stable word.

> This 🅱eme is ol🅱er than the 🅱inosaurs (`dfkufqr`)

**Needs more jpeg** is both an image-quality taunt and a bot command in the opened threads.

## Register

The usual register in the opened threads is brief, ironic, slang-heavy, and often profane. **Normie** can work as a direct rebuke or a teasing insult.

> Downvote for the red box. Fucking normie (`dfi6oau`)

> Haha your dad is a normie (`dfd5o9b`)

Replies also correct meme logic and provenance as if those details matter to the joke.

> yes but if you haven't noticed this meme usually has both sides very simplified for the meme-effect (`dfd4qxl`)

The tone is not uniformly hostile. In one direct exchange, a failed joke receives reassurance.

> Why do I always fail at making jokes :( (`dfhvpne`)

> Don't feel bad man this is hilarious (`dfhyxt4`)

## Jokes with a history

The opened sample contains references that assume prior meme history, but it rarely supplies origins. One commenter places the loss of "rares" at a named event in 2015. The supplied week does not explain that event.

> There are no rares left, they all disappeared in the pope crash of 2015 (`dfhg5xn`)

Participants also judge a format by claimed provenance. This records the criterion used in that exchange, not an independently established history.

> Behind the meme has not made a video about it, plus it came from 4chan, therefore very danko memeo. (`dfd5o08`)

I could not establish the origin or shared meaning of these allusions from the served bodies. (state)

## Moderation and its visible traces

Moderation is highly visible in the archive: 5,305 items are moderator-distinguished, 3,797 are sticky-marked, and 4,083 are deleted or removed. (header)

Automated removals state concrete submission rules. Rule 6 requires a direct meme-image link, while another template excludes social-media, video, and audio links.

> Unfortunately it has been removed because per Rule 6, your submission must be a link to a meme image. (`dfgfxht`)

> All links to social media as well as video & audio websites are no longer allowed in /r/dankmemes. (`dfjzp6l`)

New or inactive users are told to build comment history before submitting.

> Spend some time getting to know /r/dankmemes and then you'll be welcome to make submissions. (`dfcsk9x`)

Moderation is also part of the joking register. In one thread, a commenter deliberately bundles trigger phrases and asks the bot to respond; AutoModerator answers with a stock insult.

> Mods are fags, needs more jpeg, cuck. Any thoughts automoderator? (`dfn3y65`)

> It has been said that, given enough time, ten thousand monkeys with typewriters would probably eventually replicate the collected works of William Shakespeare. (`dfn3y6e`)

## Data problems

The archive contains 1,217 unresolved parents among 31,244 recorded parent pointers and 4,083 deleted or removed items. Those gaps prevent reconstruction of some exchanges, and I did not infer missing content. (header)

Coverage is small relative to the corpus and uneven by day because one complete large thread contributes many d3 items. All seven active days are represented, and all 480 served items are complete. (state) No shell response was transport-truncated.
