## Relations — what set off what

The corpus supports several local response relations because later passages name or answer earlier ones. In the queen-beast exchange, `dfq1ie5` asks the flag to stop cutting through the beast, `dfq1vqs` offers cooperation if the “poop” stops, and `dfq93qr` says the request will be taken back to “my people.” The participants’ own words establish negotiation; the reply tree alone does not.

Mistaken repair also prompts explicit correction. `6349gx` describes approved hearts as an attack, and `dfr85o7` answers, “Those hearts at the cross are supposed to be there,” linking to the Discord-plan post. Likewise, the northward call in `6351xg` is answered by `dfrhi1b`: “We never wanted to go upwards, but more importantly we're not about expansion.” These are descriptive response relations. They show corrections within exchanges, not that the whole community changed its mind.

After closure, `dfsq2mp` denies that the Void specifically targeted the trans flag. Ten minutes and 10 seconds later, `dfsqkse` answers that `/pol/` weaponized the Void; 2m 26s later, `dfsqp4h` responds, “Oh, I didn't know about that.” This supports a participant revising one point after another participant’s explanation. It does not establish the historical truth of either external claim.

The proof post `63bfui` follows the opening of `638ugi` by 7h 27m 28s and says the author had been trying to tell the story because “there are people who would deny it was actually /pol/.” That is the participant’s own explanation for documenting evidence. The corpus does not show that the particular `638ugi` exchange caused the later post, so the case records the measured sequence and the author’s stated broader reason separately. No causal relation is carried solely across a fleet-slice boundary; repeated cross-slice practices establish continuity, not causation.

## Posts against comments

Posts make the week legible as urgent collective work: defend, rebuild, advance, preserve, or accept a plan. Comments more often expose uncertainty and limited control. The post `62r8wo` warns of a subreddit dedicated to destroying the flag, while the visitor in `dforqf1` says, “It's not us attacking you. It's 4chan,” and describes both projects contesting the same spot. This is a participant’s competing explanation, not a verified attribution.

The sharpest post/comment contrast concerns expansion. `6351xg` says “Advance to the north!”; the comment `dfrhi1b` says “we're not about expansion” and asks people not to attack Chrono Trigger’s area. Elsewhere the post `632aa3` rejects coexistence with the Winged Wheelers, while comments and other posts repeatedly seek compromises. These contrasting speakers show internal disagreement. They do not demonstrate a community-wide reversal.

At the retrospective edge, posts and supportive comments often describe a targeted hostile attack. The comment chain `dfsq2mp` → `dfsqkse` → `dfsqp4h` instead distinguishes the Reddit Void from `/pol/` and produces a limited acknowledgment. Thus the post layer supplies clear public plans and accounts; the comment layer supplies corrections, dissent, reported outcomes, and qualifications that those plans did not automatically command every placer.

## Counter-reading

The case reading tested was: participants kept a sprawling trans flag legible by combining repair with negotiated coexistence, using Reddit for public calls and Discord for plans returned to the room; after closure they made survival into a shared win and tried to continue elsewhere.

Before searching, the competing account was: the room was principally expansion-first and its coexistence language had little practical force. If that reading were wrong, the record should contain explicit, community-owned calls to overwrite agreed neighbouring art, reject treaties or returned Discord plans, and prefer expansion over repair—not merely alleged false flags, outsiders, or isolated dissent.

Searches across the supplied record looked for expansion through or over art, destruction or removal, rejection of agreements, “go under,” coexistence, and treaty language. Every returned item was read. The strongest contrary item is `632aa3`, whose surviving post text says a Winged Wheelers claim that the art can “co-exist” is met with “The feelings not mutual.” Other contrary items include `633bdt` asking for help to “remove this... thing,” `6351xg` calling for a northern advance, and `6355qd` proposing expansion to crush a forming Void. These items require the case to retain expansion, erasure, and explicit anti-coexistence positions rather than describe consensus.

The competing account does not displace the main reading. Direct answers repeatedly constrain those positions: `dfrhi1b` says “we're not about expansion”; `dfrl6v7` recalls a “longstanding agreement” to pass behind the queen; and `637rwj` says expansion through or under Chrono Trigger art is not endorsed. A narrower search for explicit instructions to ignore, reject, break, or refuse a treaty, agreement, or Discord plan returned no items in the supplied record. That null is limited to those terms and this partial textual record: deleted bodies and unseen Discord deliberation mean it cannot establish that no such rejection occurred. The late item `dfrhi1b` was coded as `c0331` and added to the crossings theme. The reading therefore survives as a recurring, contested practice—not a shared belief held by every speaker.

## Saturation

Thematic saturation is uneven in a meaningful way. Batch 01 first establishes steering the flag, negotiating crossings, defending and rebuilding, sorting blame, making alliances, moving plans through Discord, celebrating, restoring protectors, disputing fair play, adding marks, and joking. Batch 02 first adds making the flag fight mean support and belonging. Batches 03 and 04 deepen these already-open practices without requiring a new practice theme. Batch 05 adds two genuinely time-bound practices—recounting and defending the finished story, and starting again on successor boards—because placement had ended. Later slices therefore mostly repeat and elaborate settled practices until closure changes what participants can do.

Evidence saturation is weaker. Fleet coders read all assigned threads, but no single reader held the whole week, and one coder read each arbitrary slice, so there is no agreement measure. Discord invitations, decisions returned to Reddit, archived 4chan material, and successor-board announcements are visible; the underlying deliberations and later outcomes are not. Deleted and removed bodies interrupt disputes, image-only posts support only title-level acts, and some replies continue outside the window. The Reddit record is sufficient for the practices stated here but not for complete attribution of attacks, durability of treaties, or outcomes on successor boards.

## Selection

The lead read the complete openings table before the gate for orientation and coded nothing from it. The fixed gate selected branch B, so five fleet coders read whole-thread slices in corpus order. Of 264 threads, 262 were disposed as coded and two as out of scope; the scheduler’s slice preflights and the lead’s pooled audit passed. The room is event-specific, so requests, jokes, image-title reactions, immediate retrospectives, and successor-board attempts were retained when they were things participants did around or in response to r/place.

What remains on Reddit is access work, instructions, warnings, and reported decisions; the deciding talk is often unseen. After closure, `6391ro`, `63c6un`, and `63eqti` point to new pixel boards, but the corpus shows attempts to move rather than their results.

## Absence

Removed and deleted bodies were not reconstructed. Their surviving replies sometimes show what was being answered, but not the missing speaker’s exact act. External images were not interpreted; image and link posts were coded only where titles or participant descriptions supplied citable words. Recognizable preview-bot text was treated as platform setting rather than community conduct.

The pre-week brief is explicitly empty because the supplied archive contains no items. Accordingly, the account cannot say what ordinary community life preceded the canvas or what it “returned” to afterward. It can only show that, within this window, placement gave way to thanks, argued recollection, and attempted work on other boards.
