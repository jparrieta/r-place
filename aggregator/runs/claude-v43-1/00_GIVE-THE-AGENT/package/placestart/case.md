## Chronology

The supplied room begins late in the event, already oriented to making a Windows Start button. At d1 04-02 02:30, the opening of `62x9y1` asks, “So, where to *start*?” and supplies a grid and coordinates. The first episode runs from `62x9y1` to `62ykgm`: people join, correct colours and borders, publish templates, and declare “WE DID IT!!!!” at d1 04-02 08:09. The measured span is 5h 38m 51s. This is completion of the button, not yet completion of the project.

Expansion is already being proposed during that work. At d1 04-02 05:50, `62y48i` asks, “Can we make a whole task bar down the bottom of the canvas?” Within 3h 10m 24s, `62ypu5` opens the first sustained objection to destroying art. From `62y48i` through `6304ux`, the room shifts from one button to templates, scripts, a growing grey bar, windows, a clock, and repeated requests for neighbouring work to survive. This is an observed overlap and sequence: the record does not show that the completion celebrations caused the expansion.

From `62yrvz` through `633qpd`, collisions with existing work become the central practice. Some visitors ask the bar to stop or go around them; others seek a window, tab, icon, border, or shifted position. Participants summarize their own answer as “We are relocating art*” in `dfq56u6` and “We talk to the subs who created the art we're overlapping” in `dfq5841`. The practice varies from incorporating the Tom Searle memorial and framing Starry Night to disputed proposals for Monster Hunter, Blue Corner, Happy Happyists, Club Penguin, osu!, and many smaller pieces. A retrospective post, `63119w`, contrasts “dark times ahead if we just planned to bulldoze everything” with “we all worked together to talk with those people.” That is the participant's explanation of the turn to negotiation; it does not prove that every placer followed the resulting plans.

From `6306mx` through `637y6x`, templates and diplomacy develop together. Reddit becomes an intake desk for preservation claims, while Discord carries negotiations that return as reported agreements and new templates. Scripts are part of the same practical problem: `dfqwbgb` asks template makers for a bitmap “we can use it in the maintenance bot,” but comments also describe outdated scripts overwriting newer plans. This period does not show a settled design. It shows repeated revisions, manual repairs, and disagreement over who could make a treaty for whom.

The late main-canvas episode runs from `636a34` to `638br4`. Templates 4.1–4.3, preservation requests, defence calls, and pressure to reach the clock continue until placement ends. At d3 04-03 17:04, `6383ab` says, “Thank you for being awesome, this was incredible.” Celebration and blame then coexist with repair of final images. Four minutes and 19 seconds later, `dfrzvou` reports, “According to discord it is officially being continued on” another canvas. From `dfrzvou` through `63dofl`, participants invite one another to pxls.space, plastuer, and Place 2.0. The record shows this sequence and the invitations; it does not show that closure caused the migration, that one venue became authoritative, or that a replacement bar was completed.

## Relations — what set off what

Threats and objections sometimes produce explicit answers. In `dfqsr3p`, a French-flag participant warns, “If you refuse we will declare war and ally with the many other works of art you are systematically destroying.” The direct reply `dfqszgb`, posted 4m 50s later, answers, “There is common ground between us I assure you.” This supports a relation between threatened resistance and an attempt to reopen negotiation. It does not establish that the parties reached an authorized agreement; `dfqqxyf` later says, “There was no compromise. Whoever you talked to does not represent us.”

The Blue Corner episode shows both an agreement and its internal limit. Moderator post `635674` announces, “We are in agreement with Blue Corner.” The later comment `dfrgk2s` directly returns to that border and says, “I am all for diplomacy, but the taskbar must look complete and frankly, I will not obey the current treaty.” The supported relation is that the announced boundary drew an explicit refusal. The corpus does not support saying the community collectively reversed its position: different speakers express the agreement and the refusal, while other threads continue to negotiate and repair.

Regret about overwriting also develops into explicit acknowledgement, but the safest claim is sequence rather than causation. In `dfr349y`, a former helper says the bar “has become too destructive.” In the same discussion, 6h 26m 28s later, `dfrh5bf` says, “I apologize, I was one of the ones paving over the blue guys,” and proposes coordinated integration. The later speaker explains their own change after “snapping me out of that”; this is not evidence that the whole room changed its mind.

The end of placement directly occasions thanks and continuation proposals because the later turns name the ending as their premise. `6383ab` announces the end and thanks participants; `dfrzvou` then names replacement canvases “so we can continue.” By contrast, the several replacement-venue posts merely follow one another. No passage links pxls.space, plastuer, and Place 2.0 into one coordinated migration, so they remain parallel invitations.

## Posts against comments

Posts foreground plans and legible objects: “official” or “current” templates, treaties, windows, tabs, alerts, calls for defence, and requests from other communities. Comments reveal whether those objects governed practice. The sharpest contradiction is `635674` versus `dfrgk2s`: the post says there is an agreement and a stopping point; the comment says the treaty will not be obeyed because the bar must look complete. This is a post/comment contrast, not proof of a later collective reversal.

The ending has the same split. Post `6384qf` says, “THANK YOU EVERYONE! This was a great success!” In the comment layer, `dfsda8g` says bot scripts were distributed on Reddit and Discord and calls that “unfair,” while conceding, “I'm glad that at least some of the works were saved.” The post layer therefore emphasizes coordination and success; the comments preserve accusations about automation, lost work, and who had authority to dictate diplomacy.

Some claims live mainly in one layer. Short image and link posts carry design proposals in their titles, so the record establishes that a template or icon was proposed but not its unseen pixel detail. Operational disputes—outdated scripts, ignored treaties, missing representation, and whether a design was actually being followed—are most visible in comments. Discord decisions are reported in both layers but cannot be checked against the off-platform discussion itself.

## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: r/placestart turned rapid success with a Start button into an expanding taskbar, managed collisions through templates, Windows-style incorporation, treaties, and Discord diplomacy, but never made those arrangements binding on all people or scripts; closure brought celebration, blame, final repair, and several attempts to restart elsewhere.

If that reading were wrong, the corpus would instead contain sustained explicit coordination to erase neighbours or claim the entire bottom while rejecting accommodation, rather than isolated boasts and refusals alongside continuing bargains and redesign work.

I searched the case's own vocabulary for “entire bottom,” “taskbar will not stop,” “Manifest Destiny,” “bulldoze,” “pave,” “destroy everything,” “kill,” and refusal to obey a treaty. The 32 returned items were inspected by item, with the strongest candidates read in full. The strongest contrary item is `dfrgk2s`: it professes diplomacy but refuses the Blue Corner treaty for the sake of a complete taskbar. `dfr87r1` adds, “The entire bottom is our God given right!! Manifest Destiny!” and `dfrjndj` says, “We can have the entire bottom of the screen if we want.” These are not merely outsiders' accusations; they establish an internal maximalist current.

A narrower search for explicit rejection of diplomacy or compromise returned only `dfqqxyf` and `dfrypjw`. Both contest whether France or Iceland had a representative compromise; neither coordinates a general rejection of accommodation. The null is limited to those explicit phrases in this supplied Reddit record and cannot establish what was said in Discord or removed bodies.

The counter-reading changes the account by removing any implication that diplomacy governed the group or that integration was consensual. What survives is the coexistence of practices: maximalist claims and treaty refusal sit beside repeated templates, bargains, preservation requests, apologies, and accommodation work. The strongest contrary item was coded late as refusing the treaty so the bar could look complete; it belongs with pressing expansion and did not require regrouping the themes.

## Saturation

The Reddit-side assignment is structurally complete: all 426 threads were disposed, 424 as coded and two unrelated links as out of scope, and every selected thread was served whole. This does not make the historical record whole. Deleted and removed bodies interrupt disputes; image-only designs cannot be independently described from text; and Discord, scripts, linked documents, and replacement canvases are visible only through what participants bring back.

Practice-level saturation arrives early but has one meaningful late exception. Thirteen themes are founded in batch 01; defending incorporated work, attacking or replacing other work, and playing with taskbar jokes first appear in batch 02. Batches 03–06 extend existing practices rather than opening new themes. Batch 07 adds one genuinely new practice—ending Place and trying to restart elsewhere—because the event could not produce that practice earlier. Later slices therefore repeat and complicate a settled taskbar repertoire, while closure changes what participants can do.

Branch B weakens cross-slice relations: no reader held the whole week, one coder read each slice, and exchanges crossing a slice boundary were divided between readers. The forest restores reply sequence but not a single-reader memory of the week. Claims above that cross boundaries are consequently limited to explicit quoted recalls, measured sequence, or slice accounts; adjacency alone is never treated as causation.

## Selection

The case lead read the empty pre-week brief and all openings before the gate. The gate selected branch B, so seven fleet coders read whole-thread slices in corpus order; the lead did not read raw threads until the counter-reading stage. A thread was kept when it showed conduct around r/place: building, requesting, negotiating, criticizing, defending, celebrating, recounting, or continuing the project after placement. The two exclusions were an unrelated Gotthard Tunnel link, `6361d5`, and an unrelated snake video, `637alx`.

Purpose-built event discussion makes the selection unusually inclusive: 424 of 426 threads were coded. Critical and hostile threads remain in the case because opposition to this community's canvas conduct is itself part of what happened. Image posts and deleted-body posts were retained when a surviving title performed a request, proposal, warning, or celebration.

## Other communities named

Other communities appear as neighbours, petitioners, treaty partners, sources of labour, threatened works, and opponents. r/metalcore's memorial recruits help back after incorporation; France and Blue Corner expose disputes over representation and borders; Monster Hunter, Dark Souls, Adventure Time, Earthbound, Club Penguin, osu!, RBTV, and many smaller groups request windows, tabs, icons, relocation, or simple non-destruction. These are this room's claims about relations, not an independently reconstructed atlas of the canvas.

Discord is the principal off-platform venue. Participants seek invites, direct negotiators there, report agreements back, and use templates with a maintenance bot. What still returns to Reddit is a request, claimed outcome, revised template, or call for labour. What cannot be seen is who participated, how a decision was reached, or whether people and scripts followed it. After the event, pxls.space, plastuer, and Place 2.0 are proposed as continuations, but their subsequent activity is outside the record.

## Absence

Removed and deleted content is distributed across planning, criticism, and retrospective disputes. Surviving titles and replies often preserve the direction of an exchange, but missing bodies were never reconstructed. The record also lacks the content of linked images, private messages, Discord talk, script execution, and later drawing on replacement canvases.

The supplied pre-week brief contains no items, so no claim can connect the taskbar to a pre-existing r/placestart routine. Bot-produced previews and helper messages were treated as platform setting. Human decisions to summon, use, criticize, or maintain automation were retained, including direct maintenance-bot evidence; generic accusations of bots were not converted into facts about who operated them.
