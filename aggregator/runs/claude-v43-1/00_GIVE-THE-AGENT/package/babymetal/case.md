## Counter-reading

My one-sentence reading is: the room turned a BABYMETAL mark into sustained shared work—building, defending, negotiating, helping neighbors, and converting the finish into celebration and welcome—while its separate Fox Day prank remained distinct.

Before searching, I recorded this competing account: visible cooperation may have been mostly inward-facing defense. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain participants refusing outside help, abandoning alliances, rejecting newcomers, or insisting that they protect only their own mural.

The strongest contrary item is `dfrj2e0`: “Yeah, abandoned keeble to protect sg and keeble is in trouble. but sg comes first.” `dfrg1du` likewise calls the own mural the “top priority” and hopes some people save pixels for it; `dfr7f22` is willing to defend other art but declines “a fight that is not our fight”; `dfrj3vh` says the priority will “always be BM and Sakura Gakuin.” These findings change an unqualified cooperation reading: help was real but conditional, and participants sometimes withdrew it when projects competed.

What survives is equally explicit. `dfp8vnc` requires mutual respect for Rainbow Road without an alliance. `dfqwjxy` says, “we can’t just abandon” a neighbor after making nice, and `dfrcfs0` urges help so neighbors do not give up the buffer. `dfrsku8` honors a non-aggressive treaty despite wishing Manningface would leave. Cooperation therefore combined neighborly obligation, strategic buffering, restraint, and a stated own-project priority; no single motive explains every exchange.

A separate search for newcomer rejection returned six items in the search vocabulary. Reading them and the two necessary parents found concert and festival talk, bot or banter language, and one disliked neighbor—not an in-scope rejection of a newcomer. This null result is limited to those returned items and this partial Reddit record; it cannot establish that rejection never occurred elsewhere or off-platform.

## Absence

Removed material is uneven rather than total. The body of the neighbor-thanks post `631nba` is removed, but its surviving title and replies still show thanks, suspicion, explanation, apology, and appreciation of strangers’ help. Other proposal bodies, including `62pawe`, are gone while replies preserve negotiation. The body of `6385b3` is deleted, but its title still says the group “held out until the end.” Deleted comments inside large threads cannot be reconstructed and may hide reasons for local changes.

The visible record does not show Discord deliberation itself, the contents of linked images or scripts, whether every offered pixel arrived, whether every recoloring finished, or whether the final newcomer remained active. Search found no in-scope newcomer rejection in the returned items, but a scoped null in a Reddit record with removals and off-platform movement is not evidence of universal welcome. Surviving text is sufficient for the practices claimed above; it is not sufficient for unseen motives, complete alliance histories, or community-wide causal effects.
