When the canvas opened, r/uvic was still carrying on its ordinary campus-help conversations: `dfnkv9d` tells a hoodie seeker to ask the HINF Course Union, while `dfnq9qj` continues a course-recommendation exchange. The first r/place conduct appears 32.22 hours after opening, at d1 04-02 00:13, when the post `62wkwn` asks people to “Help out uvic on /r/place.”

The first episode runs from `62wkwn` at d1 04-02 00:13 to `dfqrb8o` at d2 04-02 21:38, an instrument-measured 21h 25m 43s. Members locate the mark, offer and request help, repair SFU's “F,” try to expand the UVic logo, report that others keep undoing the move, and warn that the logo is being taken over. This is not only defence: `dfpr94d` says “Let's give [UBC] a hand,” and `dfpo9hr` offers help “from SFU.” Near the episode's end, `dfqqqif` says UVic is “being invaded again,” but `dfqrb8o` corrects that account: “Nope, we're working on” the planned redesign.

Rivalry becomes explicit inside that episode. At d2 04-02 16:22, `630j9x` says r/UofT is trying to replace UVic and calls for defending “our honour.” Design work then becomes the dense centre of the record. The bounded design episode runs from `631oj2` at d2 04-02 19:30 to `dfqzmnf` at d2 04-03 00:33, 5h 2m 17s. People correct spacing, circulate a fixed version, debate light versus medium blue, mock up lowercase letters and two-pixel type, and reject some proposals after seeing them. The clearest local rule is stated by `dfqyxk1`: “Before attempting anything we should decide.” After `dfqzeiz` argues that the existing design fits the school colours, `dfqzmnf` agrees and says the proposal will be removed “to avoid confusion.” A separate exchange at `dfqyl8w` calls r/place “battle-of-the-bots” and urges scripted upkeep; `dfr7tt6` asks for an ELI5 rather than claiming that a script was actually made.

The final episode begins eight minutes after placement ended, with `63849e` at d3 04-03 17:08 celebrating “our final logo” after “72 hard fought hours,” and ends at `dfsy9dv` at d3 04-04 04:50, 11h 41m 57s later. This is retrospective talk, not further pixel placement. Participants mock OBC's bad pixels and UofT's finish, credit SFU and UBC for helping, and recount the ending. In `dfs2lr4`, one participant supplies the record's own explanation of development: “we all worked together pretty well towards the end” although “there was a lot of conflict early on.” That is one participant's account, not evidence that the whole community changed its mind. By the end of the window, ordinary campus questions again occupy the room: `63etdr` asks to message CS majors and `63f68j` compares “UVic VS. sFU for CS.”

## Relations — what set off what

The strongest supported response chain begins when `dfpuc60` warns that SFU and UVic are under pressure and says “we will need your support too.” After 34m 31s, the direct reply `dfpvfmj` says “I fixed your F”; 2m 8s later, `dfpvi1o` answers “Thank you” and says “We are defending you.” The wording as well as the reply structure supports a request–repair–thanks relation.

Design exchanges also show proposals producing tests and withdrawals. `dfqvl9u` asks to make the I in UVIC lowercase; 1m 9s later, `dfqvn6x` says this would also require a lowercase C and reports that an MS Paint mock-up “looks kind of ugly.” Separately, `dfqzeiz` says the existing design already uses the school colours and “looks good the way it is”; 4m 47s later, `dfqzmnf` explicitly agrees and says the proposal will be removed to avoid confusion. These are supported responses because the later passages name and answer the proposed change.

Reciprocal help is likewise explicit. `dfqvbnw` says west coast Canadian schools “need to stick together”; 57 seconds later, `dfqvdcl` answers that UVic had helped defend UBC against r/polandball, and `dfqvqpa` thanks them. The reply positions show sequence, while “help,” “defend,” and “thanks” establish the relation.

Some important order is only order. `630j9x` calls for defence against r/UofT; 25h 27m 6s later, `dfs244d` calls UofT's poor finish “karma for their earlier vandalism.” The later passage recalls earlier vandalism, but the corpus does not show that the earlier post caused this later judgement. Likewise, warnings of takeover at `dfqbg1j` and `dfqqqif` precede further design work; they do not prove that the warnings caused that work. The forest establishes adjacency and elapsed time, not causation.

## Posts against comments

The posts foreground broad calls and finished objects: “Help out uvic” in `62wkwn`, “defend our honour” in `630j9x`, “spruce up” the logo in `631oj2`, and “our final logo” in `63849e`. The comment layer shows how those claims were enacted: repairing a letter, choosing blues, rejecting lowercase type, asking for decisions, thanking neighbours, and keeping score afterward.

The layers also contain explicit contrasts. The post `630j9x` presents defence against r/UofT as honour; the comment `dfrs6p5` instead complains that “UVic doesn't get pride.” In `62wkwn`, `dfpz4py` tries to move the logo upward while `dfq07hx` says “I'm happy where it is.” These are descriptive contrasts between speakers, not evidence that r/uvic collectively reversed a position.

The proposed-change post `633bqs` says the updated logo is finished but could look nicer. In comments, `dfqze7s` prefers what already exists, `dfqzeiz` invokes the school colours, and `dfqzmnf` agrees that the proposal should be removed to avoid confusion. Because the opening body's author is deleted and the later agreeing account is named, the record supports a proposal being withdrawn in the discussion, not a claim that one identifiable person or the whole community changed its mind.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that UVic members chiefly made and defended a shared logo through repeated small corrections and reciprocal help with SFU and UBC, then described early conflict giving way to cooperation.

The competing account tested before search was that the conduct was mainly rivalry and unilateral defence, with help only incidental. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain refusals to assist neighbours, calls to attack SFU or UBC, claims that those neighbours harmed UVic, or later accounts that conflict rather than cooperation defined the ending.

The strongest contrary item is `630j9x`, which says “r/UofT is trying to replace us” and calls for defending “our honour.” It requires the reading to retain rivalry and threat rather than portray the event as uncomplicated cooperation. The search also returned `dfs244d`, which calls UofT's finish karma for “earlier vandalism.” Yet that same item says it was “cool” that SFU and UBC helped, and `dfs2lr4` explicitly places conflict “early on” and cooperation “towards the end.” The alternative therefore qualifies but does not overturn the reading.

The broad search returned 18 items. Every returned item was opened; four matches in admissions or campus-politics material (`dfqlbs7`, `dft3bdf`, and the post-window items `dftoqmy` and `dfu6xun`) use the same words in unrelated contexts and do not bear on r/place. Two targeted whole-record searches found no refusal to help and no passage pairing SFU or UBC with attacking, replacing, taking over, vandalising, betraying, or being an enemy. That null result is limited to those expressions in this 97-hour corpus; it cannot establish what happened outside the record or inside the linked images.

## Saturation

The practice record is coherent but visually incomplete. Six threads show mobilisation, reciprocal help, detailed design negotiation, a brief proposal for scripted upkeep, and retrospective score-settling. The last retained event thread adds the distinct practice of taking stock after closure rather than merely repeating design work. No later in-window r/place thread follows it; the remaining openings return to campus questions. The single scripting exchange is thin evidence: `dfqyl8w` urges a script and `dfr7tt6` asks how, but nobody reports building or running one.

The corpus is not a view of the canvas or of linked images. Several opening posts are image or link posts whose titles survive, so the account can establish that people proposed or celebrated designs but not independently describe those images. Three uncoded deleted comments sit inside retained threads (`dfpvqui`, `dfqjzxa`, and `dfr92ef`), and the body of `633bqs` is deleted while its title and replies survive. Their absence may hide individual turns, but surviving replies preserve the main design and cooperation practices. No retained passage says organising moved to an off-platform venue; links circulate locations and mock-ups, not a separate decision room.

## Selection

I read the briefing and the complete openings table before triage, so I knew the week mixed ordinary campus help with a cluster of canvas posts. I then followed the corpus in order. Twenty-one short or selected threads were read in full; the longer library-lights thread `632l27` and campus-politics thread `63bbmv` were triaged and excluded because their windows were about other matters. Six threads were retained because they directly showed r/uvic members asking for help, defending, redesigning, maintaining, or recounting their r/place mark: `62wkwn`, `630j9x`, `631oj2`, `632vm0`, `633bqs`, and `63849e`.
