## Vernacular

In one explicit terminology thread, participants distinguished a `stan`, an intensive favorite, from a `sasaeng fan`, a person described as crazy or overly obsessive.

> When you're a fan of a group, you generally like them and their music. When you 'stan' a group, they're typically your ultimate/all-time-favorite group who you watch all their videos for, know all the members and their personalities, most likely own many albums and merchandise from them. (`dfklynu`)

> Within kpop at least, there's already a term for crazy/overly-obsessive fans: sasaeng fans. (`dfklynu`)
