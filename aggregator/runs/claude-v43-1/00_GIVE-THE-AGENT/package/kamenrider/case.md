## Counter-reading

My reading is that a small r/kamenrider effort adapted its Mighty plan under pressure, used scripts, negotiated with neighbors, and ended by valuing a partial remnant.

If that reading were wrong, this corpus would instead contain refusals to stop or move, approval of covering neighbors, or sustained calls to attack other groups rather than negotiated limits, shared space, and rebuilding offers.

The strongest contrary item is `dfrh51b`. It proposes sharing a spot with r/Mariners, but also calls rivals “bullies” and asks to “kick” them out. `dfq39k0` urges the group to “assert our dominance,” while `dfrg52r` and `dfs8yo9` curse r/haskell and the Royals. These items change the reading from uniformly cooperative to adaptive cooperation amid real antagonism. They do not overturn it: the same `dfrh51b` offers shared placement, `dfrhqgy` receives a promise not to cover the flag in `dfrijjb`, and `dfrx22d` receives acceptance of KC's rebuilding offer in `dfrzez0`.

A broad search for stopping, covering, dominance, attacking, takeovers, contest, and hostile language returned 30 items; after every item was opened, the r/place hits were already represented in the coded account and the remainder concerned ordinary Kamen Rider stories or merchandise. A focused search for refusing to stop or move, not caring, continued covering, or attacking neighbors returned only `dfqpyti`, an unrelated statement that something “won't stop the Decade memes.” Thus the focused search found no contrary r/place item. Its scope is this 97-hour, partly removed record, so the null result cannot establish that refusals never occurred.

## Saturation

**Practice saturation.** The first canvas thread establishes recruiting, relocation, pixel choices, contest, negotiation, and post-event remembrance. The later `634flo` thread adds genuinely new practices—giving script instructions, answering a flag-boundary complaint, handling private requests to stop or move, and accepting rebuilding help—rather than merely repeating settled codes. The short `637o3r` thread adds one further practice, explaining Mighty as the room's “half-mascot” to an outsider, while repeating contest and alliance. The separate Strega thread adds non-canvas joke recognition and mockery. Later retained material therefore still brought new practice; this small case does not show a long tail of pure repetition.

**Record saturation.** The central canvas episode is visible from the first call through the post-event remnant, but the record is not whole in every respect. `637o3r` has a removed body, so r/Kanye's actual request is unknown; `dfq24e3` is deleted; and the corpus header reports 43 deleted or removed items overall. Discord-hosted images return a sprite, planned product, and final snapshot through links, but they do not show whether organising occurred on Discord. `dfrbd9y` only suggests contacting the Mariners' Discord. Some threads continue beyond the window, and the corpus itself says reply parents are not universally present. These gaps limit claims about off-platform decisions and outsiders' requests, but surviving exchanges are sufficient to show practice around the event.
