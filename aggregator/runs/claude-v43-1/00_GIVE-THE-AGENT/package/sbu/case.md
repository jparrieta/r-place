## Chronology

When the canvas opened, r/sbu mostly continued its ordinary traffic of course, housing, admissions, and registration questions. The event first became visible here in an artist-led pride-icon episode bounded by `62tebs` and `dfp3b9a`: at d0 04-01 15:32 the post asked, “Any artists willing to start something?”, and 59.5 minutes later its author directed “fellow Seawolves” to (400, 700) to “join the fight.” The record gives no pixel plan, colour plan, or further replies for that first attempt.

The next retained episode follows roughly 32.4 hours later, bounded by `633j37` and `dfrvrez`. At d2 04-03 00:55 Shaaaaan said “SBU needs to get its logo on there,” supplied a Python script that would place a tile every five minutes, set (38, 961) as the location, showed a target image, and said it needed at least 100 runners. Comments then turned the proposal into practical work. Rockon101000 asked where to paste it in `dfr6no6`, answered “Windows 8” in `dfr78rk`, and reported “I think I got it working” in `dfr8ti9`. Other participants asked the logo to move, warned of a nearby void, and disputed whether scripted placing was legitimate. The location did not stay settled: in `dfrlgof` Shaaaaan reported “downsized and moved” above the start button, while `dfrvrez` later preferred the area by gogh.bmp because it seemed “less contested.”

A linked duplicate-thread episode, bounded by `635exj` and `dfrhasi`, began at d2 04-03 08:04. Its body is deleted, but its surviving title advertises a “Script to create SBU logo on /r/place.” Shaaaaan answered that “We should stay united” and explained that the image and location in “the other post” were outdated because the logo was now elsewhere and smaller. This is evidence of consolidation around the revised script, not evidence that the deleted opening body said anything beyond its title.

After placement ended, an account-making episode runs from `dfs6ojv` to `639hk0`. At d3 04-03 19:18 Le_Phoque said “/r/place is shut down” and pointed to a substitute that “wants to make the logo again.” In `dfs7s2u`, a participant celebrated that “~20 of us managed to carve our initials into the canvas,” even though Jigglypuff partly covered one apparent location. Shaaaaan corrected that account in `dfs9z8k`: “We abandoned that location and moved to another one. Look to the left of the Van Gogh.” At d3 04-03 20:48, the surviving title of `639hk0` gathered “the various attempts of an SBU logo” into a gallery. The week therefore ends not with a single uncontested image, but with participants celebrating, correcting, and preserving several attempts.

## Relations — what set off what

The first pride-icon thread and the later script thread form a supported sequence, not a supported causal relation. `dfp3b9a` sends Seawolves to (400, 700); about 32.4 hours later `633j37` proposes a scripted logo at (38, 961). Neither passage says the first attempt caused the script, so the script only follows it in this record.

Several relations inside the script episode are explicit in the words:

- In `dfrblus`, bugolf says a void is opening and proposes that “we should downsize or move.” Shaaaaan answers 6.9 hours later in `dfrlgof`, “downsized and moved. Redownload the script.” The later action directly answers the earlier proposal.

- In `dfrkxyx`, define-Pi warns that the script will interfere with the r/placestart taskbar. Shaaaaan answers 6.5 minutes later in `dfrl3vb` that it will stop “exactly one pixel above it” and that the logo is now only 13 pixels tall. This supports a response relation; it does not show that the warning caused the earlier downsizing.

- In `dfroi5s`, aklancher31 says the new position enters r/smashbros territory and urges a compromise before retaliation. `dfrovx0` then says, “you are kinda interfering in our work there,” and 42.8 minutes later `dfrqom8` says r/smashbros will take the “cheaters” down “legit.” The text supports warning, complaint, and threat in sequence. It does not establish that r/sbu accepted the proposed negotiation.

- The second script post is explicitly tied back to the first: `dfrhasi` names “the other post,” says its image and location were not updated, and calls “We should stay united.” The participant’s own explanation is that outdated information accompanied a move to a smaller logo; the corpus does not expose the deleted post body well enough to say who created the duplication or why.

- Shutdown explicitly prompts a proposed continuation: `dfs6ojv` says “/r/place is shut down” and immediately offers pxls.space as a substitute that wants “to make the logo again.” The pointer establishes the invitation, but the record does not show anyone taking it up.

- In the final exchange, `dfs9i1d` laments that Jigglypuff is partly covering the logo. Shaaaaan replies 9.5 minutes later in `dfs9z8k` that “We abandoned that location and moved to another one,” after which `dfsa3w9` answers “Looks great!” The correction explains the apparent failure; it does not prove when or because of what the move occurred.

## Posts against comments

The posts present ambition and durable objects; the comments show the instability and work needed to sustain them. `62tebs` asks artists to begin a pride icon, while its only human comment, `dfp3b9a`, converts that general request into coordinates and a “fight.” `633j37` presents a complete-looking plan—script, target image, coordinates, download instructions, and “at least 100 people”—while the comment layer contains setup questions, import errors, requests to move, downsizing, territorial complaints, and repeated location changes.

There is one explicit numerical tension between layers. The post `633j37` says, “We need at least 100 people running it for this to work”; the later comment `dfs7s2u` says “~20 of us managed to carve our initials into the canvas.” The comment is a participant’s retrospective success claim, not an independently verified measure, and “our initials” may describe a revised or smaller mark. It nevertheless qualifies the post’s stated requirement rather than confirming it.

The post layer alone preserves two late practices because their bodies are gone: `635exj` names another script thread, and `639hk0` names a gallery of attempts. The comment layer alone shows the practical script help (`dfr6no6`, `dfr73iv`, `dfr78rk`), the inter-group space conflict (`dfroi5s`, `dfrovx0`, `dfrqom8`), and the correction about the final location (`dfs9z8k`).

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/sbu made its r/place presence through a script-led, repeatedly relocated SBU logo, with a small group adapting amid competing claims and then celebrating and preserving its attempts.

Before searching, I set out this competing account: the room’s SBU mark was chiefly a manual “fight” by artists, while the script was only a disputed side effort. If my reading were wrong, the record should contain sustained coordination of individual pixels or colours, rejection of the script by SBU participants, or a separate artist-led logo effort.

The strongest contrary item is `dfrqom8`, where a r/smashbros speaker calls the script users “cheaters” and promises to take them down “legit.” It makes the legitimacy dispute consequential and prevents describing scripts as uncontroversial. The earlier `62tebs` also genuinely asks for “artists,” and `dfp3b9a` calls participants to “join the fight,” so the script was not the room’s only idiom of action.

Searches for artist/manual/script-rejection and pixel/fight language returned those items plus script-specific uses of “pixel” in `dfrkjjr` and `dfrl3vb`, an unrelated college-choice use of “legit” in `62v75f`, and an unrelated textbook “solution manual” in the after-window item `dfu2t7t`. Reading every returned item found no sustained colour-by-colour or hand-placement plan and no SBU participant rejecting the script. The script-led reading therefore survives, but only as the best-supported practice in a small record; the earlier artist call and outsider accusation remain real counterevidence rather than being absorbed into it.

## Saturation

Practice saturation is limited. The large script thread repeats recruiting, setup, moving, and bargaining practices, but the final retained thread `639hk0` still adds a distinct act—preserving the attempts as a gallery. Later retained material therefore did not merely repeat settled themes.

Record saturation is also limited. Two selected opening bodies (`635exj` and `639hk0`) are deleted, two comments in the central script thread are deleted, and the archive reports 18 deleted or removed items overall. GitHub, Imgur, a Discord, and pxls.space are only glimpsed. GitHub returns the script and Imgur returns the proposed image, but repository activity, Discord decisions, and any move to pxls.space are not visible. The corpus is adequate to show the public r/sbu sequence, but not to reconstruct all coordination or verify what appeared on the canvas.

## Absence

Deleted text matters most in the two late posts. `635exj` retains only the title “Script to create SBU logo on /r/place”; `639hk0` retains only the title describing a gallery. Their surviving titles support posting those objects, but not claims about their missing contents. Inside `633j37`, deleted comments at two branches cannot be reconstructed. Their surviving replies show that one preceded Shaaaaan’s defence of scripting and another preceded no coded practice, but reply position alone supplies neither wording nor causation.

No moderator-distinguished or sticky item appears in the supplied record, and the case offers no evidence that moderators organised the logo. SmallSubBot’s automatic link under `62tebs` was treated as setting, not community conduct. The public record also contains no confirmed manual colour plan, no visible acceptance of the proposed r/smashbros compromise, and no evidence that anyone acted on the pxls.space invitation; these are scoped absences in this partial public record, not proof that the activities never occurred.
