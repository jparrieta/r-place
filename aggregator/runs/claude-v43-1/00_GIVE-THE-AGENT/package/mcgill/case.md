The second episode, `62p86r` through `dfr0pbo`, began at d0 04-01 00:00 with “Building a martlet in /r/place - come chat with us on discord too!” (`62p86r`). The opening linked a shared image to a second venue. ## Relations — what set off what

The opening call for a coordinated campaign (`62npdx`) received “GATHER THE FORCES!” (`dfo0jrn`) and a list of candidate motifs (`dfo53lh`). These are direct replies answering the request, so the exchange supports a relation between recruitment and proposing what to draw. It does not establish which proposal caused the later martlet build.

The warning that a laser beam was heading through the work and its offer of space (`dfqybkf`) was directly answered with “Join us on the discord to strategize!” (`dfqynca`). When that participant said the earlier invite was expired (`dfqyvsu`), the reply supplied a fresh invite (`dfr0of4`). The later turns explicitly answer the earlier problem; this supports a repair of the coordination channel, not merely adjacency.

The question “did we do this??” (`62quoe`) prompted the correction “No we did [this]” (`dfous0u`), followed 35 seconds later by “pls halp” because the actual work was a mess (`dfousju`). The correction identifies the object; the request then recruits repair. The words support those two relations, but not a claim that the mistaken image caused the damage.

The Void bargaining is supported by what each turn says. “Y u kill red chiken” (`dfp9c9w`) elicited an explanation that the Void needed a tendril (`dfp9dm6`); an offer to let the chicken live inside a tentacle (`dfp9f1o`) elicited the counter-offer that a larger chicken would be strangled (`dfp9hlf`); refusal to negotiate (`dfp9k1n`) elicited “red chicken will remain severed” (`dfp9mkj`). This is an exchange of positions. It is not evidence that the community as a whole adopted any one position or changed its mind.

The Acadia helper’s question about the white dot (`dfrn6s5`) was answered by identifying the eye (`dfrnfyc`), after which the same helper admitted the mistake (`dfrnvhp`). This supports a correction producing a stated change in that participant’s action. It does not support a broader claim that all mistaken defenders were corrected.

Across threads, recruitment, building, damage, defence and celebration follow one another, but no passage says that one thread set off the next. Those are observed sequences. Likewise, the April Fools mascot play and the canvas chicken overlap in vocabulary, but the record does not show that the subreddit styling caused the group to draw or defend the canvas image.

## Posts against comments

The posts present compact public claims; comments expose the work and uncertainty beneath them.

The post `62npdx` says a coordinated campaign is needed; its comments do not yet coordinate pixels, but offer “martlet? samosa? construction?” (`dfo53lh`). The contrast is between a confident call and an unsettled choice, not between action and refusal.

The post `62p86r` says a martlet is being built and invites Discord participation; its comments later say “The chicken needs help” (`dfpbrm9`), negotiate help against a laser beam (`dfqybkf`), replace an expired invite (`dfr0of4`), and update coordinates (`dfr0pbo`). The post supplies a stable banner while the comment layer shows maintenance and channel repair.

The post `62quoe` is uncertain and apparently points to the wrong image; the comment layer corrects it (`dfous0u`) and immediately asks for repair (`dfousju`). Here the comments overturn the post’s implied identification.

The display post `6345r1` presents a finished “contribution”; comments show continuing attack (`dfrj2yv`), mistaken friendly maintenance (`dfrn6s5`), design disagreement about “feet” versus “tufts” (`dfs2fnm`, `dfsatto`), and finally congratulations after closure (`dfs5pdt`). The post says what the object is; the comments show that keeping and interpreting it were still contested.

The success post `6386t3` says “We did it Reddit!” Comments qualify that accomplishment by locating other universities (`dfsjv7r`, `dfsk4iw`) and explaining the considerable maintenance effort (`dfsk3mz`). This is a descriptive contrast between headline success and comment-level labour, not evidence that commenters rejected the success claim.

The April Fools posts work differently. `62qgya`, `62tjjr`, `62v956`, `62va1w`, and `630qcs` launch jokes, while comments praise, test, extend or mock them. Their adjacency and shared play show participation in the room’s joke; they do not document canvas conduct.

## Counter-reading

My reading is that r/mcgill moved from recruiting and linking a McGill canvas effort, through correction, bargaining and defence, to celebration and recounting, while separately performing its own April Fools jokes around Red Chiken and campus life.

If that reading were wrong, the record would instead contain people disclaiming McGill authorship, failing to recognize the design, or describing isolated and mistaken help rather than shared building and defence.

The strongest contrary item is `dfrn6s5`: “I go to Acadia so i have been helping out but Ive been fighting taking out that white dot.” It shows that at least one defender was not from McGill and was actively undoing a correct detail. The reply and admission—“I have been making a huge mistake. Carry on.” (`dfrnvhp`)—show repair, but also make the coordination look porous and fallible rather than seamless or community-bounded.

The search scanned all 934 record items. A query for disclaiming language returned seven hits; the only case-relevant one was the correction “No we did [this]” (`dfous0u`), which distinguishes McGill’s work rather than disclaiming participation. A query for mistakes, wrong details or whether something belonged returned twenty hits; the case-relevant pair was `dfrn6s5` and `dfrnvhp`. A query for authorship and being the “only uni” returned eight hits; the case-relevant items were the initial authorship question (`62quoe`) and the post-event comparison question (`dfsjv7r`). No returned item said that McGill participants had not organised or maintained a canvas image. That null result is limited by the small set of canvas threads and by the Discord activity the corpus cannot show. The competing account therefore changes the claim from seamless McGill coordination to mixed, sometimes mistaken coordination involving outsiders; the documented calls, live links, coordinates, repair requests, negotiations and defence still survive.

## Saturation

The practice set broadened through the record rather than repeating one settled action: recruitment appeared first, cross-venue coordination followed, the room’s own April Fools play added a separate practice, bargaining and correction appeared during conflict, and celebration/recounting appeared only after closure. The last genuinely different practice was post-event celebration and explanation in `6386t3`, so the record reaches closure of the event but not early thematic saturation.

The record is structurally adequate for the visible subreddit activity but not whole for coordination. Discord is explicitly used for strategy (`dfqynca`), and only refreshed invites and coordinates return here; decisions inside that venue cannot be seen. Forty-nine record items have deleted or removed text. In retained threads, the removed bodies of `62unut`, `62uwzc`, and `630qcs` survive through their titles and replies, which preserve bargaining or joke reception but not the missing openings’ full wording. The corpus also contains context beyond the window and three unresolved parent links overall; neither can establish what happened outside the supplied period.

I read the briefing and all 84 openings before triage, then read every thread through the instrument in corpus order. ## Absence

The corpus does not identify who placed particular pixels, so claims about the finished image remain participant claims and surviving titles, not direct observation. It shows no passage linking the subreddit’s Red Chiken styling causally to the canvas image. It also shows no community-wide decision to accept the Void’s terms: contrasting speakers bargain, but no collective settlement is recorded. There is no coded moderator intervention in the canvas exchanges. Removed openings limit what can be said about who initiated the two Void threads, although the replies preserve the positions taken.
