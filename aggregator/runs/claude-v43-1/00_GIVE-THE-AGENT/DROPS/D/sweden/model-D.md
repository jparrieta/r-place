## D01 — Critical mass

Recruitment concerned making and holding a mark: a starter announced a blue dot and told others to follow, while another call asked for backup [R01][P01][c0005][c0001]. Starting and calling occupied the same turn, not separately observed stages [A02][c0005]. Joining could be brief and concrete: an operation scheduled for 16 received an answer promising an alarm just before 16 [P15][A12][c0144][c0146]. That supports acceptance, not a general obligation on readers to answer or evidence about silent participation [A12][c0146]. The Dala horse call received a report of switching from blue flag pixels to helping the horse, supporting reported uptake rather than a measured recruitment effect [A01][c0701][c0709].

Limits were articulated as competing fronts and limited space: one participant urged doing one thing at a time; another proposed shrinking a design to 26 pixels [K02][c0440][c0437]. Separately, an early comment proposed abandoning territory and regrouping at (600, 80) [P10][c0002]. Insufficient hands causing that retreat, and completion of the proposed move, remain unestablished [absent A10] (observation: not-testable)[P10][c0002][c0440].

## D02 — Coordination without command

Designing and building included proposing a flag, beginning it and dividing a king image into ten-pixel squares; participants circulated overlays and revised plans [P02][P03][P04][c0004][c0005][c0098][c0409][c0416]. The starter praised free organisation and declined to endorse competing plans, but others reported Discord votes and proposed teams [C01][c0006][c0025][c0727]. Sweden cannot exemplify an absence of decided organisation: decisions coexisted with contested adherence, without refuting C01's population-level claim about most rooms [C01][c0006][c0025][c0727].

Approval did not make a design recognisable to every placer. During Operation Dalahäst, one participant apologised for mistaking the horse for Danish interference; another reported switching from blue to helping it [P04][P06][c0704][c0709]. The organiser said an already approved plan was brought back to Sweddit to get everyone cooperating, while a moderator redirected discussion elsewhere [P04][P20][c0718][c0715]. Asked how information would spread, the moderator named SwedenOnPlace [P04][P20][c0716][c0717]. This requires a finer step inside template coordination: carrying decisions back to placers whose maintenance would otherwise erase them; approval or a posted plan is not sufficient settlement [P04][A11][c0703][c0708][c0718].

Automation was offered as an image-plotting script and an automated attack on Lilla Bält [P14][c0044][c0052]. Neither offer establishes that upkeep pressure caused automation, how many people ran it, or its achieved effect; a fullscreen script is not itself a placement bot [absent A06] (observation: not-testable)[P14][c0044][c0052][c0600].

## D03 — Maintenance

Participants prioritised repairing sabotaged lettering over expanding and handed care to allies while going to sleep [P05][A03][c0045][c0053]. These passages establish upkeep of placed parts, not a universal moment when building ended and maintenance began [A03][c0045]. Horse apologies and reports of keeping its location blue show friendly maintenance counteracting a build [P06][c0704][c0710][c0712]. A participant also reported that most wanted to retain Arch's logo but some did not; the percentage remains that speaker's estimate, not a measured distribution [P06][P07][c0407].

Night coverage explicitly supported reciprocal protection: one participant explained that Maryland's flag remained in exchange for repairs while Swedes slept; a Maryland participant reported repairs every five minutes [P16][A13][c0588][c0590]. These are an explanation of the bargain and a report of work, not independent verification of every term's performance [A13][c0588][c0590]. A Dutch truce proposed joint defence, but the proposal does not establish that shared upkeep strain produced an operating coalition [P17][absent A19] (observation: not-testable)[c0126].

## D04 — Order without law

Diplomacy included an envoy proposing a Dutch–Swedish border, a truce proposal and a condition that Dutch participants attack Danes [R02][P08][c0085][c0126][c0131]. A reply expressly described repairing a Dutch pixel as goodwill, supporting a local negotiation-to-help connection but not proof that a completed border agreement caused it [P16][A14][c0126][c0132]. Preserving the Dutch alliance was explicitly a reason for refusing northward expansion [P07][A05][A17][c0573]. These negotiations do not independently establish a general upkeep-to-negotiation mechanism [absent A04] (observation: not-testable)[c0085][c0126].

Hosting was not merely equal-neighbour reciprocity. Marylanders asked their 'colonial overlords' for permission to add a tag; another participant questioned whether that addition was sanctioned [P19][c0492][c0558]. Permission could be withheld: a response reported a Discord no vote and advised finding another space; elsewhere diplomatic talks and a building permit were demanded [P19][c0575][c0629].

One participant said Maryland had asked nicely; another explained the time-zone repair bargain [P07][P16][c0587][c0588]. These sibling replies offer different participant explanations, not evidence that kindness developed into calculation [P07][P16][c0587][c0588]. A defence of Americans who asked nicely and had nowhere else to go received the answer that the big must be kind [P07][P19][c0688][c0689]. Permission, unequal room and obligations of protection supplement reciprocity [P19][P07][c0629][c0688][c0689].

Tentative P21 is instantiated: a participant explicitly rejected the inference that having a forum meant agreeing with its alliances [P21][c0027]. Tentative A21 is supported in this limited form: alliance decisions were the stated object of dissent; no blanket rejection by the room or complete negotiation sequence is shown [A21][c0027].

## D05 — Collective identity

National and subreddit language supplied resources for action: a call announced the return of the age of great power; another response invoked welfare [K04][c0122][c0414]. A participant called Nordic hostility brotherly love/hate and 'bara käbbel'; that is their interpretation, not a demonstrated explanation of every attack [K03][c0150]. A proposal to eliminate Overwatch because r/sweden liked TF2 supplies the narrower same-turn link between an existing preference and a target [C02][c0729].

Identity was not a uniform motive: the participant rejecting alliances said painting over others' art made them lose Swedishness [K04][P21][c0027]. Later attachment to Maryland and the Dutch could be celebrated without proving every member changed position [K04][P11][c0771][c0819].
