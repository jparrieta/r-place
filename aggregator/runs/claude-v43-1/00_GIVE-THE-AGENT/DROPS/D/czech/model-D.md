# r/czech: adapted room model

## D01 — Critical mass

The initial call was already about defending an existing mark: “Someone started to draw our flag” and everyone should take care of it against griefers [P01][c0002]. A respondent reported “On it” and starting repairs after the night [P03][P05][A01][c0005]. This supports a call answered with reported work, not a measured increase in placers or proof that the call initiated construction [A01][c0002][c0005]. Bare acceptance is directly visible when “Help me secure the northern border” receives “Roger that” in `62yo19`, coordinates `9` → `9.1`; it does not establish what silent readers did [P15][A12][c0050][c0051].

Recruitment also ran back from the picture to a participant: “I saw the flag and as a half Czech American, I'm joining in” [new: joining after seeing the flag][t01][c0004]. This is a visibility return into participation, not evidence of becoming a subreddit subscriber or of lasting membership [T4-025][c0004]. Explaining the event was another entry task: “What is that picture game I see here?” received the r/place link in `632iob`, `0` → `1` [new: explaining the picture game to late arrivals][t11][c0192][c0193]. Explanation was not demonstrably complete: a different participant still did not know what it was about after the end [T4-003][c0279].

Judgments of inadequate capacity were specific rather than a single room-wide verdict: an outline was judged too late, while a visiting solo artist doubted making any mark at all [K02][c0220][c0096]. The latter is not evidence that the Czech collective gave up [K02][c0096].

## D02 — Coordination without command

The starter role appears retrospectively: Trax1 claimed the first pixel, and FHayek thanked them for the foundation pixel [R01][c0024][c0025]. That recognition does not recover the original act or show that this person's initiative caused the first recruitment post; the starter-to-call mechanism remains not-testable here [absent A02][c0024][c0002].

Design meant more than choosing a flag: members specified the wedge slope, the whitest white, a border, beer and the word CZECH [P02][c0013][c0019][c0021][c0022][c0108]. Plans and practical instructions tried to align hands, but the mere circulation of a plan did not settle the design: one participant found the pattern hard to follow, and the direct answer defended it as “more correct” [P04][A11][c0010][c0046][c0047][c0048]. The adapted mechanism requires maintaining a shared, usable current view as well as posting a template; refreshing was urged after someone reported wasting half an hour [A11][P04][c0058][c0066].

Coordination also crossed venues: a Czech-Slovak Discord invitation, a report of discussing a plan on Discord, and a later Discord-hosted plan link sit alongside public threads [new: carrying plans between chat and public threads][t09][c0158][c0075][c0233]. These are observable relays, not access to the chats' internal decisions or proof that everyone followed them [T4-010][c0075][c0233].

Machines were part of both maintenance and redesign: one participant reported three self-repairing bots overnight; another answered doubt about Dutch bots by saying “We are using bots too” and that updating was possible [P14][c0196][c0209][c0210]. “They are going to change the bots. We could start on the Czech part” brings bot targets into design coordination, without demonstrating completed changes [new: revising bots alongside redesign][t10][c0211]. The record shows bots doing upkeep, but not that manual capacity first became insufficient and therefore caused their adoption [absent A06][c0196].

The description “chaotically organized only via subreddit” is a participant's account in one exchange, not a timeless constitution [C01][c0118]. A Dutch vote reported in the Czech thread is evidence about that partner's decision, whereas the Czech moderator's later posting rule is local decided order [T4-011][c0142][c0255][c0269]. The cross-room frequency asserted by C01 cannot be tested with this one room [absent C01][c0118][c0255].

## D03 — Maintenance

The standing mark was treated as something to lose from the outset: the first explanation named griefers, and a later respondent reported fixing overnight damage [P05][A03][c0002][c0005]. Defense could take priority over extending it: “Maybe we should stop expanding and focus on protecting the current shape” [P05][c0073]. This is repeated keeping of an existing work, not evidence of a clean construction phase followed by an exclusively defensive phase [A03][c0002][c0073].

Attribution was a consequential intermediate task omitted from the standing model but covered by candidate P06: a suggested counterattack was edited to “they're not attacking, I'll stop my ferocious counterattacks” [P06][c0139][c0140]. Later, the claim that there was no truce was answered by distinguishing organized efforts protecting the flag from the apparent threat in `635zh2`, `3` → `3.1` [P06][c0230][c0231]. Protection and damage could therefore coexist without establishing that the organized ally had repudiated the agreement [P06][P08][c0229][c0230][c0231].

## D04 — Order without law

Negotiation did not arise only because hostile neighbours eroded the Czech mark: Czech growth was itself named as consuming Mindcrack's work, and a visitor asked it to expand the other way [A04][P08][c0017][c0052]. The local account adds expansion prompting appeals and border bargaining, rather than assuming an exclusively defensive route into diplomacy [new: negotiating because one's own expansion crowds neighbours][t06][c0017][c0052][c0054]. Priority could be disputed and revised: “We started before Czech decided to expand” received “I won't encroach on your stuff anymore. My apologies” in `62zd7d`, `1.1` → `1.1.1` [P07][c0101][c0103].

Border talk could explicitly open a help offer: someone working toward agreement with the spiral received “I'll help out with the top border if you're going to go ahead with this plan” in `62yo19`, `13.1.1.1` → `13.1.1.1.1` [P08][P16][A14][c0060][c0061]. Aid also rested on prior help and available capacity, not just shortage triggering requests: a Gameboy maker invoked having stopped fighting, while a Czech helper promised Texas repair only if their own flag was all good [P16][A13][c0127][c0197]. The Slovak request received a promise of troops because D2 was finished; this is an explicit availability explanation, not a measured transfer of pixels [P16][c0067][c0068].

Pacts and shared works included the Czech-Slovak road, a defense pact proposal, and a Czech-Dutch shared-heart plan [P17][c0090][c0184][c0131]. “I think many of us are already doing that” answered the defense-pact proposal in `6321w2`, `0` → `1`: formalizing a coalition could acknowledge existing cooperation rather than initiate it [A19][c0184][c0185]. Envoy work is visible in the invitation “you can be the diplomat if u want” and in carrying a proposal to the Dutch, without evidence of elected or exclusive authority [R02][c0119][c0252].

Tentative P21 fits only the narrower compliance problem: Dutch participants acknowledged would-be annexers on their own Discord while saying others would prevent them [P21][c0294][c0295]. This is not an explicit Czech rejection of a representative's authority [P21][c0294][c0295]. The warning, denial and distinction between factions show contested attribution, but do not establish that making the agreement caused this dispute; tentative A21 remains not-testable as a causal arrow [absent A21][c0138][c0143][c0294][c0295].

Some cooperation also depended on asymmetrical control of space, which the standing model needs candidate P19 to express [P19][c0250][c0251][c0258]. A proposed new Slovak flag received “I wouldn't do this without their approval,” then “Let's take it to them, then!” in `636bk6`, `2` → `2.1` → `2.1.1` [P19][c0250][c0251][c0252]. Friendship did not itself confer permission to allocate Dutch space; the later reply explicitly said the Dutch “have that space” [P19][P17][c0258]. Asking to put the subreddit name above the flag likewise went through Dutch friends [P19][c0262]. This was a constraint on those proposed additions, not proof of Dutch authority over every Czech pixel [P19][c0251][c0262].

Relocation must retain its actor: a visiting maker said they were moving to Rainbow Road and asked for help, receiving “Count me in!” [P10][P16][c0105][c0106]. A separate offer to move the Czech flag instead received an objection that the Dutch flag was gigantic and greedy [P10][c0234][c0236]. Neither exchange demonstrates the Czech room responding to insufficient hands by abandoning its own spot, so that room-level A10 remains not-testable [absent A10][c0096][c0105][c0236].

## D05 — Collective identity

National history supplied a named target, not merely decoration: reversing “'68” was proposed against Russia, and a hammer-and-sickle attack was justified by “murder, totality and occupation for many Czechs” [K03][K04][C02][c0008][c0122]. These instantiate historical antagonism in particular calls, not evidence that every participant shared that politics [C02][c0122][c0124].

Shared identity could also connect partners: a motorway proposal invoked brotherly nations, while Pat and Mat were proposed to celebrate Czech-Dutch friendship and explained as a cartoon many Dutch people cherished [K04][P17][c0090][c0188][c0190]. Interpretation remained contestable: Heineken above the flag was read as ridicule, answered as tradition, and then met with a proposal for both Heineken and Pilsner [K04][P02][c0180][c0181][c0182]. These different speakers' positions do not establish that the whole community changed its mind [K04][c0180][c0181][c0182].

## D06 — Host framing

Canvas talk could make the ordinary room hard to follow: one returning participant reported countless posts and no clue what was happening [P20][c0245]. The moderator gathered new submissions into a megathread “To keep this sub civil and organised,” then directly redirected heart and design proposals there [P20][A20][c0255][c0268][c0269][c0270][c0271]. This supports managing the proliferation of canvas submissions, not a claim that a particular initial call caused the rule [A20][c0245][c0255]. Opening r/placecz was another offered coordination venue; the invitation alone does not show it replaced the main room [P20][c0157]. April Fools fakes or a host-sub takeover are unobserved here, not established as unnecessary or absent merely because these coded exchanges concern the canvas [absent K01][c0157][c0255].

## D07 — Imprinting

After the close, the room congratulated builders and neighbours thanked it for the alliance [P11][c0275][c0277][c0286]. A claim about the quality of a shared result is not proof that gratitude ended all prior antagonism [P11][c0286][c0240].

Recording included a timelapse and a newer ending picture [P12][c0267][c0280]. The congratulatory ending post at hour 73.65 was directly answered at hour 73.76 with “This is a very old picture haha”; a newer picture was posted at hour 76.63 [A08][P12][c0275][c0276][c0280]. Tentative P22 applies only to this minimal correction of the version being circulated: there is no coded request to clean a shared copy or argument about preserving damage as history [P22][c0276][c0280]. Those stronger forms remain unobserved, not disproved [P22][c0276][c0280].

Continuation was proposed even before closure through an all-year version, then after closure through a call to remake the Czech flag on a knockoff [P13][c0201][c0288]. A respondent called repetition pointless, whereas the proposer later reported painting the whole flag [P13][c0291][c0292]. The appropriate restriction is proposal and individual continuation, not collective adoption or institutional survival [P13][c0288][c0291][c0292]. These exchanges do not show that preserving a record caused the move to a replacement canvas; A09 remains not-testable rather than an established final link [absent A09][c0267][c0288][c0292].

## D08 — Contention

Attack calls ranged from erasing Russia to invading Texas and disguising the hammer and sickle [P09][c0043][c0151][c0123]. Calls for general flag defense and later attack calls coexist, but the text does not establish that the initial mobilization caused those attacks [absent A15][c0001][c0043]. The model's attack-to-restraint response is clearer: “Some people worked on that” answered the hammer-and-sickle attack call in `6301mc`, `0` → `3` [A16][P07][c0121][c0124].

In `631d37`, the Texas invasion call was answered by a Texan's prior defense of both flags and by an existing truce; the caller then answered “Damn, now I'm sad!” [A17][A05][c0151][c0152][c0153][c0154]. This establishes invocation of an agreement to oppose the call and the caller's disappointed response, not proof that every placer obeyed [A05][c0153][c0154]. Restraint could also use the value of others' labour rather than a treaty [P07][A16][c0124].

Rival talk included a taunt that the Czech flag would fall and a reply insisting “No it won't” [P18][c0213][c0214]. An insult could distinguish a rogue negotiator from Dutch people generally: “I love the Dutch people but I hope you personally fall of your bicycle tomorrow” [P18][c0240]. Such talk does not itself establish a preceding executed attack or the attack-to-taunt mechanism of A18 [absent A18][c0213][c0240].
