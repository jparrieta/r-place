## D01 Critical mass

The invitation was initially larger than the means available: “why don't we all work together” proposed a sentence visible to the world [P01][c0068], and a later starter assumed the active subreddit could readily establish a logo [P01][c0149]. The judgement subsequently reversed within particular attempts, not necessarily across the whole community: one organiser called the site a “losing battle” without enough support [K02][c0166]; another said a team of about ten was needed [K02][c0217]. Even the eventual last-chance attempt received “Either I'm in the wrong spot or no one else is helping” [K02][c0320]. Successful completion therefore cannot be read backwards as evidence of consistently abundant participation [K02][P11][c0320][c0270].

Bare acceptance did occur: “I'd be down to help with whatever we decide” answered the original invitation [P15][A12][c0069]. “AYE AYE CAPTAIN!” answered a specific redirection [P15][c0169]. A competing commitment also furnished an explanation for declining: a member was “too busy expanding the void” [A12][c0078]. These instantiate the proposed answer forms, not the model's claim that every reader owes an answer or its aggregate frequency claim [A12][c0069][c0078].
