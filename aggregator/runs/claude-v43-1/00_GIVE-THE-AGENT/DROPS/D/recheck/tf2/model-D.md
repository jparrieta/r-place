# r/tf2 — D3 room model

## D01 — Critical mass

Canvas work began as specific invitations: “paint our logo next to our fellow Dota,” then requests for help finishing Engineer and later for reinforcements against the void [P01] [c0041] [c0169] [c0375]. A proposed Jerma face combined a call to “band together” with its author's report of a first pink pixel; the revised starter role fits without requiring proof that others joined [R01] [A02] [c0194] [c0195]. The first logo call did receive a coordinate-bearing response and a later completion report, supporting call-to-building within that thread [A01] [P03] [c0041] [c0044] [c0054].

Bare joining, assent and contribution reports occur: “lets do it,” “Agreed,” and “I contributed!” [P15] [A12] [c0058] [c0359] [c0123]. Other responses take different forms: “I WILL CONTRIBUTE, GIVE THE COORDINATES” requests further information, while a Texas aid call receives “No” without explanation [P01] [A12] [c0129] [c0321] [c0322]. These examples qualify the local response repertoire without refuting or testing the model's probabilistic claim about ordinary acceptance and refusal [A12] [c0058] [c0129] [c0322].

Sensemaking accompanied mobilization: people asked what was being drawn, received “seduce me,” asked what kept appearing, and were told “/r/place” [P24] [c0112] [c0113] [c0202] [c0203]. A neighboring logo was mistaken for Super Monday Night Combat and identified as CS:GO; this is identification work, not itself diplomacy or recruitment [P24] [c0262] [c0263] [c0264].

Participants judged turnout poor, available room nonexistent, or their logo aesthetically outclassed [K02] [c0045] [c0274] [c0283]. Inferiority prompted a fancier proposal rather than withdrawal, while occupation of the initial square explicitly explained moving left [K02] [P10] [c0283] [c0042]. Neither passage establishes the specific mechanism “too few hands, therefore move”; that arrow remains untested, rather than contradicted by an aesthetic response outside its stated trigger [absent A10] [c0045] [c0042] [c0283]. Harsh pixel instructions are visible, but departure under strain or a repaired interpersonal quarrel is not established by those instructions or apologies for mistaken placing [absent P32] [c0333] [c0312].

## D02 — Coordination without command

Representation developed through lettering, Nopegineer, an extended neck and successive logo proposals [P02] [c0096] [c0133] [c0208] [c0283] [c0351]. Practical instructions specified colours and shape, placed the figure before NOPE, and postponed the border until completion [P04] [P03] [c0132] [c0133] [c0134]. Such priorities and a demand for one “clear direction” establish deliberate ordering, but this single room cannot validate the comparative count or “most rooms” claim [C01] [absent C01] [c0133] [c0354] [c0414].

Templates did not settle the design merely by being published [A11] [c0329] [c0330] [c0331]. One pattern was superseded by a “Final final” version while a contributor admitted wasting pixels fixing the old star [P04] [A11] [c0330] [c0331]. Standardization here required retiring an outdated post, directing people to the current sticky or Discord, specifying the shifted location and stopping obsolete work [P04] [A11] [c0297] [c0327] [c0334]. This retains the local restriction on template-as-settlement, even though the model's range already allows competing versions [A11] [c0330] [c0331].

The new chat candidate directly fits invitations to the art channel, a halt while a nonconflicting design was made in a new server, and a design brought back with coordinates and a starting direction [P23] [c0288] [c0296] [c0352]. These are Reddit-side reports of decisions and invitations, not access to how the chat decided [P23] [c0352]. Private messages to pixel replacers aimed to recruit them to the redesign; they do not establish concealment from rivals or infiltration [P06] [absent P33] [c0285].

Automation was proposed and reported but contested: “No scripts. No bots. Just our bare hands” received “We already have a script,” followed by refusal to sink to “the cheater's level” [P14] [c0357] [c0363] [c0364] [c0365]. This is disagreement between speakers, not a collectively settled policy or independently verified execution [P14] [c0363] [c0364] [c0365]. Individual schedules also mattered: one maker deferred further logos until evening and described working on a prettier design when home [K05] [c0361] [c0362].

The claimed cooldown exploit must not be counted as demonstrated additional placements [K06] [c0226] [c0227]. Its reporter was unsure whether changes were client-side because they could not see them on a phone; the reply explained that extra tabs hide a timer but pixels revert [K06] [P24] [c0226] [c0227]. The supported process is reporting and correcting a suspected site effect, not establishing a successful bypass [K06] [c0226] [c0227].

## D03 — Maintenance

Preservation therefore supplies a stated motive for script recruitment, but timer exhaustion and uptake by recruits remain unverified; the manual-repair advocate also rejected automation [A06] [c0370] [c0363] [c0365].

The revised repair element now directly explains keepers undoing a planned redesign as damage [P05] [P06] [c0286] [c0289] [c0312]. One member replaced about six pixels before checking the subreddit; a reply admitted the same mistake and promised support, in `631f8e:3.2>3.2.1` [P06] [c0286] [c0289]. Another alarm was edited to “I WAS WRONG, DO NOT FIX,” and its author acknowledged having worked against their own subreddit [P05] [P06] [c0310] [c0311]. Information could reverse mistaken defense, but not every reversal was an information failure: a different reply knowingly rejected the improved logo because it would intrude on CS:GO and Dota [P02] [P07] [c0305] [c0306]. This limits application of the mistaken-keeper explanation to reported misunderstanding, not all opposition to a proposal [P05] [P06] [c0286] [c0306].

The void episode contains explicitly linked destruction, repair and restoration reports [P05] [c0373] [c0390] [c0393] [c0394]. The same poster called NOPE lost at H68.17 and celebrated it almost fully restored at H70.22; the account supports successive reports, not a community-wide change of opinion or knowledge of the attackers' initiating decision [P05] [P03] [c0377] [c0394].
